## OFFICIAL CONFIRMED

- Adaptive Difficulty is an **Experimental** option in the Sep. 5 “New Update Out Now” Steam announcement.
- It is optional and defaults to **OFF**.
- Toggle path: `Game > Game Settings > Adaptive Difficulty`.
- It can be toggled on or off.
- Sustained winning increases enemy damage, health, and break resistance.
- Sustained deaths reduce those values and apply a limit to the maximum share of player health one attack can remove.
- Difficulty changes gradually return toward normal balance over time.
- Sustained success can push enemies beyond the original highest standard difficulty. Do not infer infinite scaling.
- Night Mode does not become easier when Adaptive Difficulty scales downward.
- The official announcement does not provide exact multipliers, a numeric cap, or a complete interaction table with every mode/build.

## COMMUNITY REPORTED

- Players are discussing Adaptive Difficulty with Night Mode and boss damage.
- Some players report that higher break resistance changes the feel of parry/break-oriented play. This is an observation, not an official multiplier or universal result.
