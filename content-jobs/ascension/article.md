Short answer — how to get Ascension (Discover the secret of the Mango)
1. Collect Mether's Flesh from the hidden side room in the Sanguine Caverns lower route.
2. Travel to the Outskirts of Nochte Beacon in Fainweald, enter the nearby stream/cave, and find the giant fish and the small altar/cup beside it.
3. At that altar choose Offer Item → Mether's Flesh. The interaction teleports you to Mango Sanctuary.
4. Follow the tunnel in Mango Sanctuary and pick up / interact with the large golden Mango. The Ascension trophy/achievement should unlock on that pickup.

Step-by-step route (follow these steps in order)
1. Reach the Sanguine Caverns route. If your run hasn’t opened the caverns, use the Stonebled Gate Beacon route referenced in the Sanguine Caverns guide (/mortal-shell-ii/sanguine-caverns/).
2. From either Pulsing Caves or Sanguine Shoreline (sources differ on the exact starting interior anchor), backtrack into the lower cavern route: follow the visible hole/ledges, bridge area, and explosive enemies as local landmarks.
3. Find the hidden side entrance in the lower cavern. Sources describe the obstruction variably as stalagmites, a spiked wall, or a hidden barrier beside exploding statues/torches—clear or hit the visible barrier or explosive/statue triggers until the secret entrance opens.
4. Enter the side room and take Mether's Flesh from the podium/altar area beside a chest or sarcophagus. Confirm the item is in your inventory before leaving that area.
5. Travel to the Outskirts of Nochte Beacon in Fainweald.
6. From the Outskirts of Nochte, look for the ruined archway/stream that leads into a torch-lit cave; follow the stream or stay left through the archway until you reach the cave with the large fish (described by sources as a statue, NPC, or big blue fish).
7. At the small altar/cup beside the fish, interact and select Offer Item → Mether's Flesh. The offering triggers an automatic transport (current guides describe the fish/creature swallowing the Harbinger as that transition).
8. After being transported, follow the short tunnel to the Mango Sanctuary. In the open area, interact with or pick up the large golden Mango (Nerd's Chalk notes it sits near a red tree). The trophy/achievement should unlock when you pick up the Mango. Wait for the platform notification before leaving.

What the guides agree is and isn’t required
- Required (supported by checked sources): Mether's Flesh in your inventory; the offer at the small altar beside the giant fish near Outskirts of Nochte; picking up the golden Mango in Mango Sanctuary.
- Not documented by the checked sources as required: any specific boss kill, a fixed Ova total, a fixed character level or Shell, a particular weapon or Tarstone, or that Mether's Breath is universally required. If corruption/Ova blocks a route in your save, consult /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ for general guidance—the sources do not prove a universal Ova gate for Ascension.
- Platforms/achievement listing: Steam Global Achievements lists Ascension in the game’s achievements but the public Steam table omits the secret description. Third‑party sites (XboxAchievements, TrueAchievements) report the platform trophy title as “Discover the secret of the Mango” and list it as Bronze in some indexes; use those as cross-checks but note Steam’s public row hides the text.

Conflicts and source uncertainty preserved (do not treat these as resolved facts)
- Starting Beacon/route naming differs across guides (Cleansed Gate / Pulsing Caves / Sanguine Shoreline); the Sanguine Caverns guide in the repository uses Stonebled Gate → Pulsing Caves → Sanguine Shoreline. Use local landmarks, not a single cardinal beacon name, if your save differs.
- Barrier description varies: stalagmites, spiked wall, or hidden entrance beside exploding statues and a torch. Clear or hit the visible obstruction—don’t expect a single universal prompt name.
- Fish identity varies in source descriptions: statue, NPC, or large blue fish. Treat it as “giant fish/altar interaction” at the Outskirts of Nochte water cave.
- Missable status: sources disagree about whether the Mango is permanently one-run missable. Some pages call the chain “easy to miss” and advise picking up the Mango; no developer patch notes checked establish a permanent lockout. Recommended precaution is to complete the chain before performing the irreversible Send Ova / NG+ action (/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/).
- Item spelling: most repository and route sources use Mether's Flesh; third-party pages sometimes use Mother’s/Mather’s variants. Use Mether's Flesh in the guide and be aware of alternate spellings in other walkthroughs.

Practical tips and confirmations
- Always verify Mether's Flesh is in your inventory before approaching the fish altar; if you reach the fish without it, the teleport/offering will not trigger according to sources.
- Wait for the trophy/achievement popup after picking up the Mango inside Mango Sanctuary. If no popup appears, confirm you offered Mether's Flesh and did pick up the Mango—do not assume the teleport alone completed the trigger.
- The trophy is tied to discovering/picking up the Mango. Third-party pages report the Mango can be a wearable cosmetic or provide a passive health effect, but those post-pickup behaviors are not consistently confirmed across primary trophy/location sources—treat cosmetic/health reports as source-reported possibilities, not universal facts.

Common mistakes
- Leaving the Sanguine Caverns hidden room without confirming Mether's Flesh in inventory.
- Reaching the Outskirts of Nochte fish without the item and assuming the teleport would work anyway.
- Assuming the fish must be killed or that the offering starts a boss fight; sources describe an automatic transport rather than a combat check.
- Ignoring the trophy popup: some guides recommend verifying the platform notification inside Mango Sanctuary before leaving, because sources differ on missability.
- Relying on any single route name (Cleansed Gate vs Pulsing Caves vs Sanguine Shoreline) — use the local landmarks described above if your save’s beacon names differ.

What happens next
- On successful completion you should receive the Ascension trophy/achievement (platform popup) when you pick up the golden Mango in Mango Sanctuary.
- If the trophy doesn’t appear: verify you offered Mether's Flesh at the fish altar and that you actually picked up the Mango. If a route is blocked by corruption/Ova in your save, consult /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ and /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/ for broader progression advice.
- Safest practical precaution from the sources: finish this chain and confirm the trophy before using Send Ova to start New Game Plus (/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/), since third‑party coverage describes it as potentially missable even though checked developer notes do not document a permanent lockout.

Patch/version note
- Checked official patch notes (Cold Symmetry Balance Patch 1 and Hotfix 2.0) do not list any changes to Ascension, Mether's Flesh, the fish interaction, or Mango Sanctuary. That does not guarantee the content can’t change in future updates—this is a version-bound observation only. See /mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/ for the patch context.
