# Item 50 — Ascension / Mether's Flesh / Giant Fish research

Research date: 2026-08-24

Scope: Item 50 only (`mortal-shell-ii/ascension`). This file is the evidence boundary for the single article-writer call. No map coordinates are established, no shared files are changed, and no new content opportunity is proposed.

## Answer-first conclusion

Ascension is the hidden Bronze trophy/achievement whose platform description is **“Discover the secret of the Mango.”** The repeatable core sequence supported by multiple current guides is:

1. Reach the Sanguine Caverns route and collect **Mether's Flesh** from a hidden side room in the lower cavern route.
2. Carry Mether's Flesh to the **Outskirts of Nochte** area in Fainweald.
3. Enter the nearby water/stream cave, find the large fish or fish statue and the adjacent altar/cup, and choose **Offer Item → Mether's Flesh**.
4. The interaction transports the player to **Mango Sanctuary**; current guides describe the fish/creature swallowing the Harbinger as the transition.
5. Follow the short tunnel through Mango Sanctuary and interact with/pick up the giant golden Mango. The trophy/achievement should trigger on that pickup.

The action sequence is the important answer. There is no documented boss kill, combat check, weapon, Shell, exact level, or fixed Ova total attached specifically to Ascension in the checked sources.

## Location and route notes

### Mether's Flesh: Sanguine Caverns hidden room

The stable location is a hidden side room in the Sanguine Caverns route. The repository's existing Sanguine Caverns guide uses these local route anchors: **Stonebled Gate → Pulsing Caves → Sanguine Shoreline**. It describes Pulsing Caves as the interior checkpoint reached after the bridge route and the lower cavern as the route toward Sanguine Shoreline.

Cross-checked source details:

- AllThingsHow says to begin near **Sanguine Shoreline**, backtrack through the short cave, bridges, and enemies, then enter a hidden cavern and collect Mether's Flesh.
- 100% Guides gives the clearest action clue: from Sanguine Shoreline, backtrack through the short cave and bridge area; in the next cave, clear the exploding enemies on the left, hit the stalagmites so they disappear, and collect Mether's Flesh on the other side.
- PatchCrazy starts from **Pulsing Caves**, follows the hole down through ledges, then describes three exploding statues beside a torch and a spiked wall that must be hit to open the secret entrance; it places Mether's Flesh on a podium beside a chest.
- Nerd's Chalk describes the lower cavern, a more enclosed altar-like room, and a sarcophagus/chest interaction for the item, but its page alternates between “Mother's Flesh” and “Mether's Flesh.”

Safe article route:

1. If the Sanguine Caverns gate is not open, use the existing **Stonebled Gate Beacon** route. The exact wider story order is not established as a universal Ascension prerequisite.
2. From **Pulsing Caves** or **Sanguine Shoreline**, use the lower cavern route and the visible hole/ledges, bridges, and explosive enemies as landmarks.
3. Find the hidden barrier/entrance near the explosive enemies and torch. Sources describe the obstruction as stalagmites or a spiked wall; do not invent a precise button or geometry beyond “hit the barrier when the path is blocked.”
4. Enter the side room and take Mether's Flesh from the podium/altar area beside the chest or sarcophagus.
5. Confirm the item is in the inventory before leaving. AllThingsHow explicitly warns that it is the offering for the next step.

Do not use a cardinal direction or map coordinate. Source route bearings and starting Beacon labels do not agree, while the local page's named Beacons and physical landmarks do.

### Giant Fish / Outskirts of Nochte interaction

The stable delivery area is near the **Outskirts of Nochte Beacon** in Fainweald. Source descriptions vary in the landmark wording:

- AllThingsHow calls it a giant fish statue near the Beacon.
- 100% Guides calls it a fish NPC in a large cave under a structure on the mountain southeast of the Beacon.
- Nerd's Chalk routes through a ruined structure/archway, follows a stream, and identifies altar-like platforms with torches overlooking the water.
- PatchCrazy describes entering the water through an archway, staying left until a torch-lit cave, then finding a big blue fish with a small altar on its left.

Safe article route:

1. Travel to **Outskirts of Nochte Beacon**.
2. Look for the archway/ruins and the stream or water route leading into a cave; use the torch and the large fish as the confirmation landmarks.
3. At the small altar/cup beside the fish, interact and choose **Offer Item**.
4. Select **Mether's Flesh**. The fish/creature transition is intended progression; it is not a boss encounter that must be fought.

The repository already uses Outskirts of Nochte as a named Beacon and uses the Sanguine Caverns Beacon names above. Link to `/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/` for general Beacon behavior rather than editing that page.

### Mango Sanctuary and final trigger

After the offering, current guides agree that the player is transported to **Mango Sanctuary**, a hidden destination not reached by the normal route. Follow the tunnel to the open sanctuary and interact with the large golden Mango, described by Nerd's Chalk as being near a red tree. The pickup/harvest is the action that completes the secret and should trigger Ascension.

Practical confirmation: wait for the platform trophy/achievement notification before leaving. If it does not appear, first verify that Mether's Flesh was actually offered and that the Mango was picked up, rather than assuming the transport alone was sufficient.

## Prerequisites and progression boundary

Confirmed practical prerequisites:

- Access to the Sanguine Caverns route and its relevant Beacons/landmarks.
- Access to the Fainweald route containing Outskirts of Nochte.
- Mether's Flesh in the inventory when using the fish-side altar.

Not established as universal requirements:

- A specific boss kill or boss order.
- A fixed number of Ova/Ovum.
- A fixed character level, Shell, weapon, sidearm, Tarstone, or combat build.
- Mether's Breath as a direct item requirement for the secret itself. It may be useful for route access/travel depending on the save, but the checked Ascension sources do not prove a universal Breath gate.

If corruption blocks the needed route in a particular save, link to the existing [Ova and corruption guide](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/) and [Beacons and fast travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/) without converting their broader progression guidance into an Ascension-specific hard prerequisite.

## Missable, one-time, and uncertainty boundaries

- The secret is optional, hidden, and easy to overlook because the trophy description does not name either Mether's Flesh or the fish.
- Current third-party coverage conflicts on the strength of the missable claim. AllThingsHow calls the chain easy to miss; a separate AllThingsHow trophy roundup places Ascension in a missable-trophy table; another AllThingsHow version says the Mango spawns once per run; Nerd's Chalk advises picking it up rather than leaving it. No checked developer patch note establishes a permanent lockout or a universal one-run rule.
- Safest practical advice: complete the chain before choosing the irreversible **Send Ova** action that starts New Game Plus, and confirm the trophy before leaving Mango Sanctuary. This is a precaution, not proof that Ascension is permanently missable.
- If a player reaches the fish without Mether's Flesh, the checked sources say the teleport will not fire. Keep the item in the inventory until the offering is accepted.
- Source naming conflicts include Mether's / Mather's / Mother's Flesh, giant fish / fish statue / fish NPC, and Cleansed Gate / Pulsing Caves / Sanguine Shoreline as the starting Beacon. Use the plan's **Mether's Flesh**, the local in-game Beacon labels, and the physical fish/altar landmarks.
- Do not state that the player must kill the fish or that the fish is a boss. The sources describe an automatic transport sequence.

## Mango reward / practical insight

The trophy is tied to discovering the Mango, not to equipping it afterward. 100% Guides calls the result a “cool hat,” and PatchCrazy reports that the Mango can be worn as an active mask/cosmetic or as a passive item with a health effect. The exact post-pickup behavior and numeric health value are not cross-checked by the stronger trophy/location sources, so the article should present the cosmetic/equipment behavior as source-reported and omit universal percentages.
