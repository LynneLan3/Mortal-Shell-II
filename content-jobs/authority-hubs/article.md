How to use this hub

1. New players: open the Beginner Guide (/mortal-shell-ii/beginner-guide/) first. It is the recommended starting point linked from the homepage and Guides hub.
2. Use the Interactive Map (/mortal-shell-ii/map/) to locate shells, bosses, and upgrade resources as you follow a route.
3. Consult Shells (/mortal-shell-ii/shells/) and Weapons (/mortal-shell-ii/weapons/) pages when choosing builds.
4. Follow the Walkthrough / Routes (/mortal-shell-ii/routes/world-progression/) for suggested progression order and to find key areas in sequence.
5. When preparing for replay or late-game, read NG+ (/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/) and Tarforge (/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/).

Suggested route (fast reference)

1. Read the Beginner Guide (/mortal-shell-ii/beginner-guide/).
2. Open the Interactive Map (/mortal-shell-ii/map/) and mark your immediate objectives.
3. Prioritize a shell and a weapon page to focus your build (/mortal-shell-ii/shells/, /mortal-shell-ii/weapons/).
4. Use the Routes page (/mortal-shell-ii/routes/world-progression/) to follow a world-progression plan.
5. Consult Bosses (/mortal-shell-ii/bosses/) before major fights.

Common mistakes

- Skipping the Beginner Guide: many early-game questions are covered there — start with it to avoid confusion.
- Ignoring the map: locations matter for progression and upgrades; use /mortal-shell-ii/map/.
- Jumping between sources without a route: pick a world-progression route on /mortal-shell-ii/routes/world-progression/ and follow it.
