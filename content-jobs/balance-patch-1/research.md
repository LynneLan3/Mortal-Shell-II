## Verified facts

### Economy
- Shell-map reveals: Gloom instead of Glimpses
- Refund: Glimpses spent on Shell-map reveals → Shell Keeper's alcove in Blackmarrow
- Enemy Gold drops increased; Mammon enemies drop double Gold
- Tarforge Smelt Equipment Gloom cost reduced 75%

### Weapons (confirmed patch facts)
- Great Martyr's Blade attack damage +20%
- Troubadour's Lute attack damage +100%
- Caged Hystrix / Triarch Repeater: minimum Resolve requirement to fire removed
- Axatana / Black Needle / Clockwork Scythe: improved light-attack tracking
- Guard can be used while walking

### Tarstones buffed (no exact percentages in official note)
- Parasitic Stone, Grudge Stone, Bulwark Stone, Clerik's Stone, Squall Stone

### Boss/enemy (named changes only)
- Lost Child: base damage -10%, max health -15%
- Monolith: base damage -19%, max health -10%, some attack timings adjusted
- Mace Cultist HP -21.1%, Caerinid Spider HP -33.3%, Infested Stalker HP -52.4%, Rusted Knave HP -20.8%
- Beta HP restoration framing except Caerinid Spider in Magdalena area
