Quick answer
- Beacons are checkpoints: resting at one restores Health and Resolve, moves your respawn point, and respawns regular enemies while bosses you’ve killed remain defeated (AllThingsHow).
- You can fast-travel between Beacons early via the Marrow Keep upper mechanism after the prologue; a second, much faster method (Mether’s Breath) unlocks after you cleanse enough corrupted Beacons and return Ova to the Gloom Siphon/Ova Nursery (AllThingsHow, GamesRadar, GameSpot). Exact Ova thresholds are reported differently by sources — use the in-game unlock prompt as authoritative.

How to use and unlock Beacon travel (step-by-step)
1. Finish the prologue and reach Marrow Keep. Before this point you must travel on foot (AllThingsHow, GameSpot).  
2. To use the Marrow Keep upper mechanism (longer travel sequence):  
   1) Rest/settle the Keep hub, pass the Gloom Siphon, take the elevator to the upper mechanism.  
   2) Open the upper mechanism’s Beacon list and pick any activated Beacon to travel to it (AllThingsHow).  
3. To unlock Mether’s Breath (direct fast travel):  
   1) Cleanse corrupted Beacons (enter the short Beacon dungeon/route, reach the crystal/Ovum, pick up the Ovum, return to the surface to finish the cleanse).  
   2) Return to the Ova Nursery / Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep with your collected Ova.  
   3) Check the Gloom Siphon / your inventory / Beacon menu for the Mether’s Breath unlock prompt or item — use the in-game prompt/item as the authority for whether you have unlocked it (AllThingsHow, GamesRadar).  
4. After Mether’s Breath is unlocked: select the Mether’s Breath option from a Beacon menu to travel directly to any discovered/activated Beacon without first returning to the Keep. GamesRadar reports that destinations only need to have been discovered and rested at, not cleansed.  
5. Using Mether’s Breath to clear corruption: when Mether’s Breath is active you can clear black corruption tendrils/walls by holding the interaction prompt while travelling — this is a travel convenience and can reduce backtracking on corruption-blocked routes (AllThingsHow).

How to cleanse a corrupted Beacon (ordered)
1. At the surface Beacon, open the menu. If the menu shows a Cleanse option, begin the attached route.  
2. Enter and run the short Beacon dungeon/route to the crystal/Ovum.  
3. Claim/pick up the Ovum/Ova in the final room.  
4. Return to the surface Beacon and finish the cleanse; the menu will expand and nearby corruption growths may disappear (AllThingsHow).  
5. Confirm the changed icon/cleared corruption and the expanded menu as the better completion checks rather than merely visiting the surface Beacon.

Launch-scope Beacon counts and the source limits (do not treat these as a single universal total)
- LootMap documents a 19-marker overworld layer (nine Fainweald, four in the Marrow Keep belt, six Mammon) — this is the overworld map marker scope (LootMap, GameSpot).  
- AllThingsHow supplies a 49-name launch checklist and reports 46 cleanseable Beacons because Marrow Keep, Widow’s Overlook, and Outskirts of Mammon are pre-cleansed at launch. Treat the 49-name list as a source-backed checklist, not as an “official file” that every guide will present identically (AllThingsHow).  
- Do not use “19” as the total without stating that it refers to the overworld marker layer; do not present a single Beacon count without naming the scope.

Practical route anchors and suggested circuits
- Early Fainweald (practical early circuit): start at the pre-cleansed Widow’s Overlook, go north to Blackridge Pass, then to Mushroom Village and Mushroom Village Gate. Cleanse Mushroom Village Gate to open access to Glutted Mire. After Mether’s Breath is unlocked, One-Legged Wolf and the nearby trial altar are practical follow-ups (GameSpot).  
- Northern / western Fainweald: use Stonebled Gate (through Stones of Remembrance graveyard and bone-gate route) to unlock Sanguine Caverns access; Gloomshade Grove sits on a lower hidden route near the Grisha encounter; Citadel of Penance’s Beacon sits by its main gate.  
- Mammon belt (suggested circuit): Outskirts of Mammon (pre-cleansed) → Abandoned Outpost → High Lord's Courtyard → Gate of Mammon → Castigator's Keep → Gate of Infinity → The Silent Steps → Abbey Entrance → Sester’s Gate. This ordering is a practical circuit reported by GameSpot, not a mandatory sequence.  
- Dungeon / post-boss sweep: after defeating a dungeon’s boss, sweep the arena/exit area — some final Beacons appear only after the boss is down. AllThingsHow reports six rich post-boss Beacons at 26 Ova each; treat that as reported by the checklist, not universally verified in every play session.

Practical tips and priority rules
1. Always rest at a newly found Beacon even before you have Mether’s Breath — it creates a checkpoint and the place will become a travel destination later (AllThingsHow, GamesRadar).  
2. When a Beacon menu shows Cleanse, finish that route and claim the Ovum before leaving; the menu expansion and cleared corruption are the best confirmation.  
3. Cleansing yields Ova and removes nearby corruption gates; it is useful but—according to GamesRadar—cleansing is not required to travel to a Beacon once you’ve already discovered and rested at it.  
4. If your Ova tracker is one short for the Mether’s Breath unlock, first check for a post-boss/arena Beacon, then verify which Beacons are only tagged/rested vs cleansed.  
5. No shell or weapon is listed as required for Beacons in the checked sources; plan for survivability, healing, and minimizing long detours before a checkpoint rather than chasing a “best” Shell for travel.

Conflicting or uncertain claims to preserve
- Ova threshold: sources report widely different totals (GamesRadar: 4 returned; GameSpot and AllThingsHow: 5 additional / roughly 7–8 total depending on starting stock; LootMap: about 5–6; older pages: 8 or 10). Use the in-game unlock prompt/item as your authority (AllThingsHow, GamesRadar, GameSpot, LootMap).  
- Beacon count/scope: 19 = overworld marker layer (LootMap); 49 (46 cleansable) = broader launch checklist including dungeons (AllThingsHow). State the scope when you quote a number.  
- Names/order: some sources differ in labels (Disciple’s Grotto vs Disciple’s Pass, etc.). Use the region/landmark context if names vary between sources.  
- Post-boss reward reporting: the checklist reports six rich post-boss Beacons at 26 Ova each (AllThingsHow); this is a source report, not independently verified here.  
- Missability: the launch checklist reports no permanently missable Beacon (AllThingsHow); do not conflate this with beta-era single-save warnings.

Common mistakes
- Assuming a Beacon must be cleansed to be a fast-travel destination — sources report you only need to have discovered and rested at it once Mether’s Breath is unlocked (GamesRadar).  
- Publishing a single Ova threshold as universal — multiple launch sources disagree; follow the in-game unlock prompt.  
- Treating the 19 overworld markers as the total number of Beacons without stating the scope — the broader checklist names 49 Beacons with 46 cleanseable at launch (LootMap vs AllThingsHow).  
- Skipping a post-boss arena sweep after a major fight — final Beacons can appear only after a boss; this is the quickest place to find a missing Beacon if your tracker is short (AllThingsHow).  
- Relying on hotfix or community reports as definitive changes to Beacon mechanics — Balance Patch 1 and Hotfix 2.0 did not change core Beacon rules in their official notes; Hotfix 2.0 specifically fixed an equip/UI lockup when using the Slayer Seal in the Beacon menu (official Steam posts). Community reports of teleport crashes are user reports, not official rule changes.

What happens next (after unlocks and cleanses)
- Once Mether’s Breath is present you can Mether-travel directly between discovered/rested Beacons without always returning to Marrow Keep (AllThingsHow, GamesRadar).  
- Mether’s Breath can clear black corruption tendrils/walls if you hold the interaction prompt, easing route navigation.  
- Resting at Beacons continues to serve as the respawn point and restore resource node; regular foes respawn when you rest, while killed bosses remain defeated (AllThingsHow).  
- If any Beacons remain uncleaned, you can finish them during post-final-boss free roam before selecting New Game+; the launch checklist reports none are permanently missable (AllThingsHow).

Patch boundary and stability notes
- Balance Patch 1 (official Steam dev post) adjusted many systems (shell-map reveal costs, balance, weapon economy, etc.) but did not document Beacon-count, cleansing-rule, or Mether’s Breath-threshold changes. Keep Beacon mechanics tied to launch-build sources (official patch notes).  
- Hotfix 2.0 (official Steam dev post, Aug 18) fixed a Beacon-menu UI lockup when equipping the Slayer Seal and applied stability/performance fixes; it does not list changes to Beacon routing or cleanses. Community reports of teleport crashes exist but are user reports, not official rule changes.
