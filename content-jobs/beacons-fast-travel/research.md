## Evidence summary

### What a Beacon does

- Beacons function as checkpoints: the checked launch checklist describes resting, restoring Health and Resolve, moving the respawn point, and respawning regular enemies. Bosses remain defeated after a rest. Source: AllThingsHow's full Beacon checklist.
- A Beacon can be **tagged/activated** for the travel list without being cleansed. The checked sources consistently separate reaching/resting at a Beacon from completing its attached cleanse route.
- A corrupted Beacon is entered through a short dungeon/route. Cleansing ends when the player reaches the crystal/Ovum, claims the Ovum, and returns to the surface. The post-cleanse menu is broader than the bare checkpoint menu and nearby corruption growths can disappear.
- Cleansing is therefore not the same as tagging: tagging adds a destination/checkpoint; cleansing is the completion action that yields Ova and can open a gate or nearby blocked loot.

### Fast-travel layers and prerequisites

1. Finish the prologue and reach Marrow Keep. Before that, travel is on foot.
2. The first travel method uses the mechanism at the top of Marrow Keep. The route described by AllThingsHow is: settle the hub, pass the Gloom Siphon, take the elevator, then choose an activated Beacon from the upper mechanism. This method includes the longer travel sequence.
3. The faster method is **Mether's Breath**. Cleanse Beacons, collect the Ovum/Ova, return to the Ova Nursery/Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep, and check the inventory/Beacon menu for the unlock.
4. Once Mether's Breath is present, select it from a Beacon menu to travel directly to discovered/activated Beacons without returning through the Keep. GamesRadar says the destination only needs to have been revitalized and rested at; it does not need to be cleansed.
5. Mether's Breath also clears black corruption tendrils/walls when the player holds the interaction prompt. This is both a route prerequisite and a travel convenience, not a map coordinate system.

The exact Ova threshold remains uncertain. Current launch reports say four returned Ova (GamesRadar), five additional Ova / roughly seven to eight total depending on starting stock (GameSpot and AllThingsHow), or around five to six in LootMap's summary. Older local page sources also reported eight or ten. The safe player instruction is to return to the Gloom Siphon after meaningful Ova progress and use the in-game unlock prompt/item as the authority; do not publish one number as universal.

AllThingsHow publishes a 49-entry launch checklist and says 46 are cleansable because **Marrow Keep, Widow's Overlook, and Outskirts of Mammon** are pre-cleansed. LootMap independently describes **19 overworld map markers** (nine Fainweald, four in the Marrow Keep belt, six Mammon) and explains that dungeon interiors carry additional Beacons. These are compatible scopes, not evidence for 19 total: use “19 overworld markers / 49 total named Beacons / 46 cleanseable” only with the scope stated.

## Route notes and nearest useful anchors

- **Early Fainweald:** GameSpot routes from the default/pre-cleansed Widow's Overlook to Blackridge Pass north of it, then Mushroom Village and Mushroom Village Gate. Mushroom Village Gate must be cleansed for access to Glutted Mire. After Mether's Breath opens corruption, the One-Legged Wolf route is a useful follow-up near the trial altar between the village branches.
- **Northern/western Fainweald:** Stonebled Gate is reached through the Stones of Remembrance graveyard and bone-gate/chasm route; cleansing it opens Sanguine Caverns. Gloomshade Grove is a hidden lower route near the Grisha encounter. Citadel of Penance's Beacon sits by its main gate. Outskirts of Nochte sits at the shallow swamp edge; Nochtean Gate is in front of Prisoners' Domain.
- **Mammon:** Outskirts of Mammon is the default/pre-cleansed regional anchor. GameSpot's sequence continues to Abandoned Outpost, High Lord's Courtyard, Gate of Mammon, Castigator's Keep, Gate of Infinity, The Silent Steps, Abbey Entrance, and Sester's Gate. The order is a practical circuit, not a mandatory progression order.
- **Dungeon/post-boss:** Treat dungeon Beacon names as interior route anchors. After a major dungeon boss, sweep the arena/exit area again because the final Beacon can appear only after the boss. AllThingsHow reports six rich post-boss Beacons at 26 Ova each; retain this as source-reported and do not invent a universal boss-to-Beacon coordinate map.
- **Unfound Path / Disciple's Pass:** The checklist gives branch anchors including Faithful's Shelter, Brether's Court, Obsidian Vault, Bridge of Procession, Empty Cradle, and Chamber of the Forsaken. Existing local boss pages provide direct branch context for Monolith, Orrem, and Malborn Offspring.

## Prerequisites, missables, and practical player guidance

- Prioritize reaching/resting at a new Beacon even before Mether's Breath: it creates a checkpoint and future travel destination once the direct method is unlocked.
- When a Beacon menu shows Cleanse, finish the attached route and claim the Ovum before leaving. A changed icon/cleared corruption and the expanded menu are better completion checks than merely having visited the surface Beacon.
- Cleansing is useful for Ova and nearby corruption-gated routes; it is not required for ordinary travel to a Beacon that has been activated and rested at, according to GamesRadar.
- The checked launch checklist reports no permanently missable Beacon: skipped Beacons can be cleaned during post-final-boss free roam before choosing New Game+. This is a source-backed launch claim, not a guarantee for every future patch or mode; keep the page wording qualified.
- If the tracker is one short, check post-boss arena/exit Beacons first, then distinguish “tagged/rested” from “cleansed.”
- Do not treat the Open Beta's seven-Beacon objective as the full launch total. The local beta reference and current launch sources describe different scopes.
- No shell or weapon is required by the Beacon sources. Build advice should remain practical—carry healing, avoid a long detour before a checkpoint, and use the nearest rested Beacon—rather than naming a “best” Shell without evidence.

## Patch notes / version boundary

- **Balance Patch 1** (official Steam developer post, PC first) changed shell-map reveal costs, enemy/boss balance, weapon economy, seals, Tarstones, and general stability. It does not list a Beacon-count, cleansing-rule, or Mether's Breath-threshold change. Keep the page's Beacon mechanics tied to the launch-build sources and label old beta instructions separately.
- **Hotfix 2.0** (official Steam developer post, August 18) lists stability/performance/crash fixes and specifically fixes equipping the Slayer Seal from the Beacon menu causing a UI lockup. It does not document a change to Beacon routes, count, cleansing rewards, or travel prerequisites. Mention it only as a Beacon-menu reliability note, not as a routing fix.
- A Steam community report mentions teleport crashes after a hotfix, but it is player feedback rather than a patch specification; do not present it as a confirmed universal bug or as a Beacon rule.

## Conflicting or uncertain claims to retain

1. **Ova threshold:** 4 returned; 5 additional; 7–8 total; 5–6; and older 8/10 reports appear across launch guides. The page must not pick one fixed number without an in-game prompt.
2. **Beacon count scope:** 19 is the LootMap overworld-marker layer; 49/46 is the broader launch checklist including dungeons. Do not call 19 the total or imply every named interior Beacon appears on the world map.
3. **Names/order:** Some sources use nearby/variant labels such as Disciple's Grotto versus beta-era Disciple's Pass, and the travel-menu order is not an official published data file. Preserve the source spelling and use the player's landmark/region when names differ.
4. **Post-boss reward:** 26 Ova and six rich post-boss Beacons are reported by launch checklists. Keep “reported” unless independently verified in-game for the current build.
5. **Missability:** Current checklist says none are permanently missable before New Game+, while beta-era single-save warnings refer to the beta objective. Do not merge those rules.
