Short answer first
- Beta save/progress does NOT transfer to the full release. Do not expect story progress, levels, inventory, weapons, Shells, currency, collectibles, map or dungeon state from your Open Beta session to be restored.
- The only documented launch benefits from participating in the Open Beta are: (A) the Flayed Harbinger cosmetic, and (B) an optional Prologue skip in the full game for qualifying Beta players. These are unlocks/entitlements at launch, not a restored Beta save.

What specifically carries over (the bounded benefits)
1. Flayed Harbinger cosmetic
   - Verified: A developer-marked Steam reply says playing the Open Beta for more than 30 minutes automatically unlocks The Flayed Harbinger; the PlayStation Open Beta listing also lists the Flayed Harbinger as an Open Beta bonus.
   - Boundary: The numeric >30-minute threshold is stated in the Steam developer reply; that exact time was not independently confirmed for consoles in the checked sources.

2. Optional Prologue skip
   - Verified: The PlayStation Open Beta listing says players who progress beyond Marrow Keep in the Open Beta unlock the option to skip the Prologue in the full game. The Steam developer reply also confirms qualifying Beta players can skip the Prologue.
   - Boundary: The skip is an option in the full game (not an automatic transfer of Beta progress). The exact menu label, button, or spawn details after skipping were not published in the checked first-party sources — see the dedicated skip-prologue guide for current practical details: /mortal-shell-ii/skip-prologue/.

What does NOT carry over (explicitly documented)
- Official PlayStation Open Beta copy lists the following as resetting at launch: currency, weapons, Shells, collectibles, and other items discovered during the Open Beta.
- The developer-marked Steam reply states save data will not transfer.
- Boundary: Other save flags (quests, map completion, dungeon state, exact level/XP) were not claimed as carried forward in the checked first-party sources; treat those as not transferred unless a first-party source says otherwise.

If you want the entitlements: short steps
1. Play the Open Beta.
2. To secure Flayed Harbinger:
   - On Steam: follow the developer guidance — play the Open Beta for more than 30 minutes (developer-marked reply). That should automatically unlock the cosmetic for the full game at launch.
   - On consoles: PlayStation’s listing indicates simply playing the Open Beta qualifies; the exact numeric time threshold for consoles was not verified in the checked sources.
3. To qualify for the Prologue skip:
   - Progress in the Open Beta past Marrow Keep (PlayStation listing). If you meet the eligibility, the full game will offer an optional Prologue skip at launch.
4. Launch the full game at release to claim the cosmetic and/or use the Prologue skip option where available.

Platform notes and uncertainty
- The Steam developer reply and the PlayStation listing overlap on the two benefits but differ in wording. Do not treat Steam’s >30-minute statement as a verified console rule.
- No platform-specific exception to the “no save transfer” rule was verified in the checked first-party sources.
- Steam-specific troubleshooting reports (re-adding the Open Beta to your Steam library or partial-download workarounds) exist in community reporting and secondary coverage; they are Steam-only steps and are not evidence that saves actually migrated into the full game. See GamesRadar’s writeup for those Steam-specific reports if you need troubleshooting context.

Common mistakes
- Expecting the Beta save to resume in the full game. Developer answer: “save data will not transfer.” Do not expect story progress, inventory, or currency to be preserved.
- Treating the Prologue skip as a full save migration. It is an optional launch entitlement for qualifying Beta players — it does not mean your Beta map, items, or dungeon progress were preserved.
- Quoting the Steam >30-minute threshold as a confirmed console rule. The numeric threshold is from a Steam developer reply; consoles were not independently confirmed to require the same number.
- Using Steam-library re-add steps as proof of save transfer. Those are troubleshooting reports for Steam only and are not first-party confirmation of save migration.

What happens next (at launch)
- If you met the documented Beta criteria, expect to find the Flayed Harbinger cosmetic unlocked in the full game at launch and an option to skip the Prologue if you qualified. Neither restores Beta progression or inventory.
- If you did not meet the documented criteria, you will start the full game like other new players; Beta progress and items are not restored.
- For the precise in-game menu wording, trophy effects, and the decision consequences of skipping the Prologue, see /mortal-shell-ii/skip-prologue/.
