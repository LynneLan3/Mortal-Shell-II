Quick answer
- Blackmarrow Keys are used in Marrow Keep to open Shade chests located around the elevator‑room area. The local working model is eight Blackmarrow Keys and eight Shade chests; five candidate routes for keys are known and three exact key locations remain unconfirmed. This is the current local evidence model (the original launch coverage included an unresolved eight‑vs‑nine key count), not an independently verified complete list.

What a Blackmarrow Key opens
1. Use: One Blackmarrow Key opens one unopened Shade chest in the Marrow Keep elevator‑room area. The key is consumed on use (page‑level evidence).
2. Reward: Each chest grants a cosmetic Shade tied to a Shell you already must have unlocked. After opening the chest, choose the Shade via Change Shades at a Beacon.
3. Important dependency: Opening the chest is a cosmetic unlock only — it does not unlock the Shell body or provide combat stats. If the matching Shell is not already unlocked, the UI interaction may fail; confirm Shell unlocks before spending a key.

Where to get Blackmarrow Keys — route steps
(Each route shows the Beacon you should fast‑travel to, the prerequisite(s) per local sources, the in‑game steps in order, the reward, and an evidence confidence note.)

1) Abandoned Outpost / The Collector (Abandoned Outpost Beacon) — low-to-medium confidence
   Steps:
   1. Activate/fast‑travel to Abandoned Outpost Beacon.
   2. Enable Night Mode first (see Gloombound Flame / Thestus guidance).
   3. Head south of the Beacon to find The Collector merchant.
   4. Purchase a Blackmarrow Key from The Collector for 1,500 Gold (this price and merchant lead are recorded on the existing key page).
   Reward: One Blackmarrow Key.
   Evidence note: Purchase price and merchant are page‑level evidence in the existing Blackmarrow Keys page; the purchase restock behavior is not documented.

2) Shrine of Sorrows (Mushroom Village Beacon) — medium-to-high confidence
   Steps:
   1. Fast‑travel to Mushroom Village Beacon.
   2. Enable Night Mode as required by the Shrine sequence.
   3. Enter the Shrine of Sorrows route (the cocoon‑like entrance).
   4. Collect the Moonlight, Sunlight, and Starlight Sonatas and complete the mounted‑head / frozen‑world sequence.
   5. Make Alluring Mushrooms, obtain the Basement Key, and finish the basement ritual as described on the Shrine page.
   6. Claim the Shrine reward — a Blackmarrow Key (and Tooth of Nochte is listed alongside it).
   Reward: One Blackmarrow Key (+ Tooth of Nochte).
   Evidence note: The Shrine walkthrough on its dedicated page documents this full prerequisite → reward chain and links back to Blackmarrow Keys.

3) Desolate Keep (Marrow Keep adjacent secret route) — low-to-medium confidence
   Steps:
   1. Carry the Slayer Seal in your inventory (do not equip it if you care about achievements; see Slayer Seal page).
   2. Reach the Desolate Keep hidden/secret route related to Marrow Keep (the existing key page ties the key to this route).
   3. Locate the key room along that route (page gives the connection but no precise turn coordinates in the local evidence).
   Reward: One Blackmarrow Key (per the existing page).
   Evidence note: The Slayer Seal → inventory prerequisite is supported by the Slayer Seal page, but the Desolate Keep key room itself lacks independent confirmation in this checkout.

4) Abbey Entrance (Abbey Entrance Beacon) — low confidence
   Steps:
   1. Fast‑travel to Abbey Entrance Beacon.
   2. Progress to the tower scaffolding area referenced by the existing page.
   3. Search beneath/around the tower scaffolding for a chest that contains a Blackmarrow Key (landmark‑level lead only).
   Reward: One Blackmarrow Key (per the existing page).
   Evidence note: Abbey Entrance is a confirmed Beacon name; the chest‑beneath‑scaffolding detail is page‑level only and not independently verified here.

5) Child's Feeding Grounds (Sanguine Shoreline Beacon → Child's Feeding Grounds Beacon) — medium-to-high confidence
   Steps:
   1. Fast‑travel to Sanguine Shoreline Beacon and complete The Lost Child boss route.
   2. Continue through the Child's Feeding Grounds progression and activate the Child's Feeding Grounds Beacon.
   3. Return to the dungeon’s innermost chamber / the former Ova location (the route requires this return step after the boss).
   4. Collect the Blackmarrow Key found behind the former Ova area.
   Reward: One Blackmarrow Key.
   Evidence note: The Lost Child page independently documents the Beacon/Ova progression and the necessary return trip to collect the key.

Prerequisites and general guidance (short, actionable)
1. Night Mode: Required for at least the Abandoned Outpost (Collector) and Shrine routes — enable via the Gloombound Flame / Thestus sequence when ready. Night Mode is reversible per the Gloombound Flame notes.
2. Slayer Seal: Must be in inventory (not equipped) to satisfy the Desolate Keep prerequisite in the local page.
3. Gold: The Collector purchase is listed at 1,500 Gold on the existing key page.
4. Shells: Unlock a permanent Shell (Tiel, Proxima, Gragu, Eredrim, Smert, Lazlo, Sariel, Genessa/Sester Genessa per the shells research) before opening the matching Shade chest.
5. Tracking: Keep separate counts of Blackmarrow Keys collected vs. Shade chests opened at Marrow Keep to spot missing routes without assuming a chest‑to‑Shell mapping.

Checklist to track while collecting (use these in order)
1. Count Blackmarrow Keys in inventory after each route.
2. Count Shade chests opened at Marrow Keep elevator room after each spend.
3. If a chest interaction fails, verify the corresponding permanent Shell is unlocked.
4. Mark which of the five route leads you have checked.
5. Keep one slot for “unconfirmed” locations — do not fill it with speculative coordinates.

Common Mistakes
- Equipping the Slayer Seal to test access (equipping carries an achievement consequence; keep it in inventory only unless you accept the achievement lock).
- Spending a Blackmarrow Key on a chest before confirming the matching Shell is unlocked — the key is consumed.
- Treating every locked chest/object as a Shade chest — Chapel, Crypt, Damp, Light, and dungeon keys are separate systems.
- Assuming the Collector purchase restocks or that keys duplicate in New Game Plus — no restock / duplication evidence is documented here.
- Presenting the current eight‑key model as a guaranteed complete list — local launch coverage included an eight‑vs‑nine conflict; three locations remain unconfirmed.

What happens next (after collecting keys)
1. Bring your collected Blackmarrow Keys to Marrow Keep.
2. At the Marrow Keep elevator‑room area, choose an unopened Shade chest to spend a key on (the chest must match a Shell you already own).
3. After opening, go to any Beacon and use Change Shades to select the new cosmetic Shade.
4. Track keys spent vs. chests opened; if counts mismatch, consult the checklist and the five known route leads. The three remaining locations are unresolved in this evidence set.

Version, coverage, and unresolved items (short)
- Current local working model: eight Blackmarrow Keys → eight Shade chests (supported by the existing Blackmarrow page and the Shells research that treats Harros as prologue‑only and eight permanent Shells).
- Unresolved: Three exact key locations are not identified in this checkout. An earlier launch conflict recorded eight vs. nine keys; this guide preserves that uncertainty and does not claim a verified exhaustive list.
