Short answer: there is no single fixed boss order. Use a three-part progression backbone — Prologue/early fights, the six Corrupted Gates (three Fainweald + three Mammon) cleared in any accessible order, then the Unfound Path guardians (three tendril branches) which must all be cleared to unlock the final boss Zmey. Below is a recommended, source-qualified route backbone with precise anchors, followed by an indexed list of optional / shell / weapon-linked encounters, common mistakes, and what to do after the final fight.

Progression backbone (follow these steps in order of campaign dependency)
1. Prologue / early scripted encounters (classification conflict)
   1. Gloombound Ritualist — approach from Village Outskirts Beacon; AllThingsHow routes it as an early prologue fight (AllThingsHow). Gamer Guides also lists a second Gloombound Ritualist entry elsewhere; do not merge those labels without verification.
   2. Tar Golem — Disciple’s Grotto Beacon; this is a scripted prologue encounter and is treated as the prologue boss by GameSpot (GameSpot). Achievement: it’s possible to deplete the health bar during the cutscene for an achievement, per AllThingsHow (AllThingsHow).
   - Note: sources disagree whether both of the above are counted as “major bosses” (GameSpot explicitly excludes the scripted Tar Golem from its ten-boss count). Keep prologue fights separate from the major-boss backbone.

2. Fainweald Corrupted Gates — clear any of these three once their regions are open (flexible order)
   1. Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods — Glutted Mire / approach from Sunken Village route (AllThingsHow). GameSpot suggests Magdalena is commonly the earliest gate encountered but does not mark her as a mandatory first boss (GameSpot).
   2. The Lost Child — Sanguine Caverns; approach from Sanguine Shoreline through the Tarblighted Stoner approach (AllThingsHow). Note: GamesRadar’s Balance Patch 1 directly nerfed The Lost Child (GamesRadar).
   3. The Nameless Captive — Captive’s End inside Prisoner’s Domain. The site’s Peter’s Perfect Parry attempt must happen before the kill (or be deferred to NG+); see the Nameless Captive and Peter’s Perfect Parry pages for details.
   - Internal links: Magdalena, Lost Child, Nameless Captive, Peter’s Perfect Parry.

3. Mammon Corrupted Gates — clear any of these three once accessible (flexible order)
   1. Droeg the Conqueror — Conquered Temple; current guide starts at Ovate’s Chamber Beacon and uses the traversal gate (AllThingsHow / internal guides).
   2. Hexapod — Withered Shoals; current guide uses Nameless Pass Beacon and a route below Ruk (sources).
   3. Isaac, the Scholar Prince — Faded Citadel / Ashen Sanctum; current route uses Citadel Atrium Beacon after the Gate of Infinity approach. Isaac’s alias varies in sources (Isaac / Sir Isaac / Scholar Prince); use site canonical page name.
   - Internal links: Droeg, Hexapod, Isaac the Scholar Prince.

4. Unfound Path guardian branches (group dependency; internal order flexible)
   1. Orrem, the Discarded Golem → Orrem, the Reclaimed — Bridge of Procession Beacon. This is a two-form fight (Discarded → Reclaimed) for the hub; some checklists split Orrem’s subtitles into separate labels (Nerd’s Chalk). Present Orrem as a single two-form guardian for progression and note the checklist conflict.
   2. The Monolith — Obsidian Vault Beacon. GamesRadar’s Balance Patch 1 nerfed and retimed some Monolith attacks (GamesRadar).
   3. Malborn Offspring — Faithful’s Shelter Beacon. Sources report a two-phase fight.
   4. After all three tendril branches are cleared, the centre route opens; proceed to Zmey.
   - Internal links: Orrem (Discarded Golem), Monolith, Malborn Offspring.

5. Final / endgame
   1. Zmey, the Unbidden — centre platform of the Unfound Path; current guide uses Hidden Nave Beacon and the updraft to reach the arena. Zmey is gated behind clearing the three Unfound Path guardian branches (GameSpot / AllThingsHow).
   2. Ova / post-boss Beacon reward: sources report a post-major-boss Beacon with Ova rewards and note conflicting thresholds for an Ova unlock (reports vary; some sources cite 167/168). Do not hard-code a universal Ova threshold — link to the Ova guide and follow the in-game prompt (`/mortal-shell-ii/ova/`).
   - Internal links: Zmey, Ova guide.

Key route rules and source qualifications (do not skip these)
- Do not publish a single fixed 1→46 route. Gamer Guides’ 46-entry list is a locations checklist, not a mandatory campaign order.
- The six Corrupted Gates (three Fainweald, three Mammon) are route-flexible; clear any accessible gate branch first (GameSpot, AllThingsHow).
- The Unfound Path is group-ordered: you must clear all three tendril guardian branches before the centre platform and Zmey unlock — but internal branch order is not proven mandatory (GameSpot, AllThingsHow).
- Prologue classification is conflicted: GameSpot excludes the scripted Tar Golem from its ten major-boss count, while AllThingsHow lists both prologue fights. Present prologue encounters separately and do not fold them silently into the major-boss count.
- Orrem should be shown as a two-form encounter for progression; note that some third-party checklists split Orrem’s subtitles into separate checklist lines (Nerd’s Chalk).
- Ova thresholds are source-conflicted (reports of 167/168); do not hard-code a threshold. Link the Ova guide and instruct players to follow the in-game prompt.

Common mistakes players make (and how to avoid them)
1. Treating the six Corrupted Gates as a single forced order — they are flexible. Pick whichever gate branch you can access and clear it (GameSpot, AllThingsHow).
2. Counting the Tar Golem the same as the ten major bosses — sources conflict on whether the scripted prologue boss is a major boss. Keep prologue fights separate (GameSpot).
3. Assuming Orrem is multiple independent bosses — treat Orrem as one two-form guardian for progression; mention checklist variants as a separate point (Nerd’s Chalk).
4. Hard-coding an Ova entry number — sources disagree on the exact Ova threshold. Use the in-game Ova prompt and the Ova guide rather than a fixed number.
5. Merging duplicate boss labels from checklist sources — Gamer Guides lists many locations that may reuse names or represent variants (Gamer Guides). Don’t merge the two Gloombound Ritualist entries without in-game confirmation.
6. Ignoring patch notes — Balance Patch 1 nerfed Lost Child and Monolith and retimed Monolith attacks; assume other boss stats may be changed in future patches (GamesRadar).
