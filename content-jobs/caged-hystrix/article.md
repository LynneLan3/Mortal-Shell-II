Can I fire Caged Hystrix without meeting a Resolve threshold?
Yes — according to Cold Symmetry’s Balance Patch 1 notes (2026-08-20), Caged Hystrix no longer has a minimum Resolve requirement to fire on the Balance Patch 1 PC build described in those official notes.

How to get and use Caged Hystrix (step-by-step)
1. Reach the Chamber of Becoming by following the Sariel route (/mortal-shell-ii/sariel/).
2. Complete Sariel’s revival-marker sequence and the reward / Memory sequence.
3. Instead of leaving immediately, continue up the rear exit stairs toward the final exit area.
4. Collect the separate Caged Hystrix manual Sidearm pickup located near that final exit area. [IMAGE: final exit stairs and Caged Hystrix pickup area]
5. You can fire Caged Hystrix immediately on the Balance Patch 1 PC build described in the official notes — there is no longer a minimum Resolve threshold you must meet to fire it. Do not wait to reach an old Resolve threshold referenced in pre-patch guides.

Key location and route notes
- Caged Hystrix is a separate manual Sidearm pickup near the Chamber of Becoming exit after completing the Sariel route from Silent Steps. It is not the same object as the Sariel reward or the Clockwork Scythe (/mortal-shell-ii/clockwork-scythe/).
- The patch change removed the minimum Resolve requirement to fire; it did not change where the Sidearm appears. Collect the Sidearm before exiting so you don’t have to replay the dungeon.

Common mistakes
- Treating Caged Hystrix as the Sariel or Clockwork Scythe reward. It is a separate pickup that must be picked up near the final exit area.
- Assuming you still need to hit the old minimum-Resolve threshold before firing. Balance Patch 1’s official note explicitly removes that requirement for the PC/live build described in the patch notes.
- Exiting the dungeon immediately after the Sariel sequence and missing the pickup, then having to replay the route because you didn’t check the rear exit area.

What happens next
- After picking up Caged Hystrix you can fire it immediately on the Balance Patch 1 PC build described in the official notes.
- The patch change affects firing eligibility only. The notes do not publish a full stat sheet or the rest of the weapon’s behavior, so do not assume the patch changed damage, range, upgrade values, or Tarstone compatibility.
- The official Balance Patch 1 note used here is the PC/Steam patch announcement; the notes do not document a platform-by-platform rollout in full. Keep that qualification in mind and check /mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/ for any updates.
