Short answer
Follow these steps: cleanse Sester's Gate, move through the temple using the bridges/stairs/altars for landmarks, clear (optional) Winterglass Lake if you want Mornefrost Grave, defeat Urrig the Executioner, use the Jump Pad/elevator to activate Ovate's Chamber, cross the active traversal gate/elevator into the frozen arena, defeat Droeg the Conqueror (see Droeg page for full mechanics), cleanse the Conqueror's Hoard Beacon, pick up the Ova, and — if desired — push on through the cold valley to the Frostshard Stone.

Route (step‑by‑step)
1. At Sester's Gate Beacon (Sester's Refuge): cleanse it if the Corrupted Gate is blocking access to Conquered Temple. This is the Mammon-side access check that clears the Corrupted Gate (GameSpot / AllThingsHow).
   [IMAGE: Sester's Gate Beacon and cleared Corrupted Gate entrance]

2. Enter the snowed‑in Conquered Temple and move inward, using broad environmental landmarks rather than compass directions: bridges, stair flights, multi‑level altar rooms, and statue/altar areas. Do not rely on a single turn-by-turn map during this section.

3. Reach and cleanse Winterglass Lake Beacon at the bottom of the large stair descent after the altar-room / multi-level structure section. Cleansing Winterglass Lake opens the optional Mornefrost Grave dungeon. If you want the optional dungeon:
   1. Use the wall plate to open the gate, proceed through a hand‑shaped alcove, handle an obstruction that vanishes after a first sweep, and pass a giant‑hand doorway into Mornefrost Grave.
   2. Fight Caged Grisha; after that fight interact with the Ovum at the back to cleanse and leave (Gamer Guides).
   [IMAGE: Winterglass Lake Beacon at bottom of stair descent / multi‑level structure]

4. Continue the main temple route toward Urrig, the Executioner (mid‑dungeon fight). Useful landmarks reported by guides: a repaired bridge, a bat area, a fork, a narrow bridge on the left, a path around, then a drop into the arena. Use these landmarks as checkpoints rather than absolute turn instructions (Gamer Guides / Gaming ProMax).
   [IMAGE: Repaired bridge and narrow bridge landmarks en route to Urrig]

5. After defeating Urrig, take the Jump Pad and elevator sequence described in multiple sources to reach the area near Ovate's Chamber. Activate the Beacon there — listed in sources as Ovate's Chamber (follow the in‑game Beacon label). Cleansing Ovate's Chamber opens the optional Pilfered Crypt dungeon. Gamer Guides reports Pilfered Crypt contains:
   - A small first room, a top‑left chest with a Winterglass Gem, and an altar with a corrupted Ovum on the far side.  
   Activate Ovate's Chamber before taking the arena/boss route.
   [IMAGE: Jump Pad / elevator route up to Ovate's Chamber Beacon]

6. From Ovate's Chamber, proceed using the active traversal gate or elevator you see in your run to cross the gap into Droeg’s frozen arena. Sources describe this same final checkpoint three ways (traversal gate / elevator / continue to a frozen lake) — follow the active route visible on your run (Gaming ProMax / AllThingsHow / Beacon lists). A green summon sign before the arena can be spent (one Glimpse) to unlock the optional Gragu summon; this is optional, not required.
   [IMAGE: Traversal gate / elevator entrance into frozen boss arena; pre‑arena green summon sign]

7. Fight Droeg the Conqueror. Key route warnings here (mechanics detail belongs on the Droeg boss page):
   - Phase one: watch for the long‑range bone‑club sequences (wide swings and multi‑hit slams); let the full chain finish before attempting to punish (AllThingsHow / Gaming ProMax).
   - Phase two: when Droeg kneels or appears staggered and a red warning appears, that red warning is the Blazing Frostfire/homing projectile tell. It is not a safe free‑hit window. Sprint or move sideways until the volley ends, then re‑engage. (See the dedicated Droeg page for full coverage.)
   [IMAGE: Droeg arena, showing open space and bone‑club reach]

8. After defeating Droeg:
   1. Activate and cleanse the post‑boss triangular Conqueror's Hoard Beacon in the arena (local Beacon lists use the name Conqueror's Hoard; some sources spell a variant).
   2. Collect the Ova in the pod chamber behind/near the arena. For Ova handoff and wider progression rules, consult the Ova and corruption page.
   [IMAGE: Conqueror's Hoard Beacon in the boss arena and nearby pod chamber]

9. Optional — Frostshard Stone route (post‑boss, beyond the Conqueror's Hoard area): leave the pod chamber, pass ruined architecture and praying statues, pray at the large snow‑covered statue, cross a valley of giant stone hands and campfires, cross a narrow bridge, drop at the end, and open the stone chest to get the Frostshard Stone. The area has extreme cold that inflicts periodic damage; move continuously and bring healing. Do not assume any specific cold‑damage number or timer (AllThingsHow / GameSpot).
   [IMAGE: Large snow‑covered statue and valley of giant stone hands en route to Frostshard Stone]
   
Common mistakes
- Treating Droeg’s red‑warning stagger as a free‑hit. It signals the incoming homing/blazing volley — sprint or sidestep until it finishes (AllThingsHow / Gaming ProMax).
- Skipping Sester's Gate cleanse and wondering why Conquered Temple is blocked. Cleanse Sester's Gate to clear the Corrupted Gate.
- Confusing optional Beacon/dungeon cleanses with mandatory progress. Winterglass Lake (Mornefrost Grave) and Ovate's Chamber (Pilfered Crypt) are optional.
- Assuming Gragu is required. The green summon sign consumes one Glimpse and is optional.
- Missing the Ova in the pod chamber after the boss or failing to cleanse the Conqueror's Hoard Beacon.
- Relying on a single "map coordinate" or a single turn list: sources use different landmark orders, so rely on in‑game Beacon labels and visible traversal gates/elevators.

Conflicts and important notes (preserved from sources)
- Beacon naming: some sources show “Ovate Chamber” while others show “Ovate's Chamber.” Follow the game’s in‑game label (this guide uses Ovate's Chamber to match the local Beacon list).
- Ovate → Droeg approach: sources describe a traversal gate, an elevator, or “continue to the frozen lake” for the same final checkpoint. Use the active traversal gate/elevator visible in your current run (Gaming ProMax / AllThingsHow / various Beacon lists).
- Winterglass Lake order: guides disagree on whether Winterglass Lake appears before or after Ovate's Chamber visually; the reliable fact is Winterglass Lake is the large‑structure/altar stair checkpoint and Ovate's Chamber is the Urrig‑side boss checkpoint.
- Conqueror's Hoard spelling: some pages use “Conqueror's,” some “Conquerer's.” This guide uses Conqueror's Hoard to match the local Beacon list.
- Reward totals: sources differ on reported Gloom reward totals for Droeg. This route does not publish a fixed Gloom amount.
