Answer-first route

1. Start at Sester's Gate Beacon (Sester's Refuge). Cleanse it if the Corrupted Gate is blocked — cleansing Sester's Gate clears the Corrupted Gate and opens access into Conquered Temple.
2. Enter the snowed-in temple. Establish the interior route by using the temple's bridges, stairs, multi-level structure, and altar-room landmarks rather than relying on a single compass heading.
3. Find and check Winterglass Lake Beacon at the bottom of the large stair descent after the altar-room / multi-level-structure section. Cleansing this Beacon also opens the optional Mornefrost Grave dungeon.
4. Continue the main route to Urrig, the Executioner. Useful landmark checks reported across sources: a repaired bridge, a bat area, a forward/right fork, a narrow bridge on the left, and a drop into Urrig's arena — treat these as landmarks, not a rigid turn-by-turn map.
5. After defeating Urrig, use the Jump Pad and elevator sequence to reach Ovate's Chamber Beacon. Activate Ovate's Chamber before attempting the arena route; cleansing this Beacon opens the optional Pilfered Crypt dungeon.
6. From the Ovate's Chamber area follow the active traversal gate / elevator route across the gap into the frozen arena and defeat Droeg the Conqueror. A green summon sign before the arena can spend one Glimpse to unlock Gragu as an optional summon.
7. After Droeg falls, activate and cleanse the arena's Conqueror's Hoard Beacon, then collect the Ova in the pod chamber. For Ova handoff and progression rules, see /mortal-shell-ii/ova/.
8. Optional Frostshard Stone: leave the pod chamber, pass the praying statues, pray at the large snow-covered statue, cross the valley with giant stone hands and campfires, cross the narrow bridge, drop at the end and open the stone chest. The route beyond the arena is extremely cold — move continuously and bring healing.

Beacons & optional dungeons (summary)

- Sester's Gate Beacon: Mammon-side access; cleanse to clear the Corrupted Gate into Conquered Temple.
- Winterglass Lake Beacon: interior stair / large multi-level-structure bottom — cleansing opens Mornefrost Grave (optional).
- Ovate's Chamber Beacon: the Urrig-side respawn point — cleansing opens Pilfered Crypt (optional).
- Conqueror's Hoard Beacon: appears in the arena after Droeg; cleanse it and loot the pod room Ova.

Route landmarks and navigation tips

- Do not rely on a single universal turn sequence — sources use slightly different ordering. Use the documented landmarks (altar room, multi-level stairs, repaired bridge, bat area, forks, narrow bridges, Jump Pad/elevator) to orient yourself.
- If Winterglass Lake and Ovate's Chamber appear in a different visual order for you, prioritize the in-game Beacon labels and the path that visibly leads toward Urrig and the Jump Pad sequence.

Droeg — encounter-ending warning (short)

- Droeg's phase-one reach weapon chains and long-range bone-club sequences must finish before attempting to punish. In phase two Droeg may kneel or appear staggered while a red warning appears — that red-warning volley is the Blazing Frostfire homing projectile tell and is not a safe free-hit. Sprint or move sideways until the volley ends, then re-engage. Full mechanics and detailed phase coverage live on the Droeg boss page: /mortal-shell-ii/droeg-the-conqueror/.

Post-boss and rewards

- The boss reward for defeating Droeg is the Conqueror's Reward Tarstone for the Black Needle. Sources differ on Gloom totals; this route guide omits any fixed Gloom amount.
- After the fight, cleanse the Conqueror's Hoard Beacon and collect the Ova in the pod chamber. For how Ova feed into Marrow Keep progression, see /mortal-shell-ii/ova/.
- For information about the Black Needle weapon compatibility, link to /mortal-shell-ii/black-needle/.

Frostshard Stone (optional)

- The Frostshard Stone sits beyond the post-boss area after the Conqueror's Hoard Beacon. The short landmark route: leave the pod chamber, pass ruins and praying statues, pray at the large snow-covered statue, cross the valley with giant stone hands and campfires, cross a narrow bridge, drop at the end and open the stone chest. Expect extreme cold damage in that area — move continuously and bring healing; do not assume a fixed timer or damage value.

Conflicts and naming notes (preserve uncertainty)

- Ovate's Chamber naming: sources use both “Ovate Chamber” and “Ovate's Chamber.” Follow the in-game label; this guide uses the local Beacon label Ovate's Chamber.
- Ovate-to-Droeg approach: sources describe the final checkpoint as a traversal-gate, an elevator, or "continue to the frozen lake" — these refer to the same final checkpoint area but not one universal sequence. Use whatever traversal gate/elevator is active in your run.
- Winterglass Lake order: some lists show Winterglass Lake before Ovate's Chamber visually, others show the opposite. The reliable identification is the large-structure / altar-room stair Beacon.
- Conqueror's Hoard spelling: sources vary; this guide uses Conqueror's Hoard to match local Beacon lists.
- Reward amounts: source pages disagree on exact Gloom totals (do not rely on a specific number here).
- No coordinates: no map coordinates are supplied — navigate by Beacons, bridges, altars, statues, and frozen-valley landmarks.

Common mistakes

- Mistaking Droeg's red-warning homing projectile for a safe stagger window and getting hit.
- Trying to force a single turn-by-turn map instead of using in-game Beacon labels and the temple's landmarks.
- Confusing Beacon cleanse interactions with mandatory progression — Winterglass Lake and Ovate's Chamber both open optional dungeons.
- Forgetting to activate Ovate's Chamber Beacon as the last respawn before Droeg.
- Not preparing for extreme cold when going after the Frostshard Stone; the area deals periodic cold damage and requires continuous movement and healing.
