## Evidence boundary

The stable route facts are the Sester's Gate access check, the three Conquered Temple Beacon names, Urrig as the mid-dungeon fight, the Ovate's Chamber checkpoint before Droeg, the post-fight Conqueror's Hoard Beacon, and the cold landmark route to the Frostshard Stone. Current sources do not share one room-by-room map or one calibrated coordinate system, so the article must navigate by Beacon names, elevators, bridges, altar rooms, statues, and the frozen valley rather than coordinates.

The fight is deliberately handed off to the existing [Droeg the Conqueror](/mortal-shell-ii/droeg-the-conqueror/) page, but the route page should still warn about the false-stagger / red-warning projectile tell because it is the main route-ending failure point.

## Answer-first route

1. Start at **Sester's Gate Beacon** in Sester's Refuge and cleanse it if the Corrupted Gate remains blocked. Current coverage says this cleanse clears the entrance to **Conquered Temple**.
2. Enter the snowed-in temple and establish the interior route through its bridges, stairs, multi-level structure, and altar-room landmarks. Do not force a compass direction from one guide.
3. Check **Winterglass Lake Beacon** at the bottom of the large stair descent after the altar-room / multi-level-structure section. Its cleanse opens the optional **Mornefrost Grave** dungeon.
4. Continue through the main temple route to **Urrig, the Executioner**. The stable navigation checks are the repaired bridge, bat area, fork, narrow bridge, and drop into the arena; exact left/right wording varies.
5. After Urrig, use the Jump Pad and elevator route to reach **Ovate's Chamber Beacon**. Activate it before taking the arena route. Its cleanse opens the optional **Pilfered Crypt** dungeon.
6. From the Ovate's Chamber area, follow the active traversal gate / elevator route across the gap into the frozen arena and defeat **Droeg the Conqueror**. Summoning **Gragu** is optional.
7. After the boss, activate and cleanse the arena's **Conqueror's Hoard Beacon**, then collect the Ova in the pod chamber.
8. For the optional Frostshard Stone, leave the pod chamber, pass the praying statues, pray at the large snow-covered statue, cross the giant-stone-hands valley, cross the narrow bridge, drop at the end, and open the stone chest.

## Source-backed facts

### Entry and gate access

- Sester's Gate is the Mammon-side access Beacon. GameSpot and AllThingsHow both say that cleansing it clears the Corrupted Gate leading to Conquered Temple; the current local Beacon guide also places Sester's Gate in Sester's Refuge.
- The wider Mammon order is flexible. The article should not say that Conquered Temple must be the first Mammon gate, only that it is one of the region's major Corrupted Gate routes.
- No exact map coordinate is carried into the article. Coordinates shown by interactive-map sites are not an audited repository coordinate contract and are outside this item.

### Interior Beacon and optional dungeon branches

- The current Beacon checklists identify **Winterglass Lake**, **Ovate's Chamber**, and post-boss **Conqueror's Hoard** as the three Conquered Temple Beacons.
- Gamer Guides places Winterglass Lake at the bottom of the large stair set after leaving the altar room. Probonk describes the same Beacon at the bottom of a large multi-level structure, reached by exiting and descending three flights of stairs.
- Cleansing Winterglass Lake opens **Mornefrost Grave**. The checked dungeon route uses a wall plate to open the gate, a hand-shaped alcove, an obstruction that disappears after the first sweep, and a final giant-hand doorway leading to **Caged Grisha**. After the fight, interact with the Ovum at the back to cleanse and leave.
- After Urrig, multiple sources converge on a Jump Pad / elevator sequence leading to **Ovate's Chamber**. Cleansing that Beacon opens **Pilfered Crypt**. Gamer Guides describes a small first room, a top-left chest containing a Winterglass Gem, and an altar with the corrupted Ovum on the opposite side.
- The optional dungeon branches should be marked as optional so a player does not confuse a Beacon cleanse interaction with the main route to Droeg.

### Route to Urrig and Droeg

- Gaming ProMax places Urrig in the middle of Conquered Temple and says that the route after Urrig leads to Ovate's Chamber, the last respawn point before Droeg.
- Gamer Guides' Urrig route summary describes repairing a bridge, crossing past bats, choosing the forward/right fork, taking a narrow bridge on the left, following the path around, and dropping into the arena. This is useful as a landmark chain, but not as a universal turn-by-turn map.
- From Ovate's Chamber, Gaming ProMax says to take a traversal gate across a gap into the boss encounter. AllThingsHow describes continuing from the Beacon to a frozen-lake arena, while another Beacon listing describes the Beacon as being at the top of an elevator leading to Droeg's arena. The page should state the conflict and instruct the player to use the active traversal gate / elevator route visible in the current run.
- A green summon sign before the arena can spend one Glimpse to unlock the **Gragu** summon. This is an optional distraction tool, not a progression requirement.

### Droeg and post-boss completion

- Droeg's phase-one threat is his long-range bone-club sequence. AllThingsHow lists two wide swings, a triple vertical slam, and a wide-wide-downward variation; Gaming ProMax also warns that the full chain must finish before punishing.
- In the later phase, Droeg kneels or appears staggered while a red warning appears. This is the Blazing Frostfire / homing projectile tell, not a safe free-hit window. The safe route-page advice is to sprint or move sideways until the volley ends, then re-engage. Full attack coverage belongs on the [Droeg page](/mortal-shell-ii/droeg-the-conqueror/).
- The boss reward is the **Conqueror's Reward** Tarstone for the **Black Needle**. Source pages differ in exact explanatory wording and Gloom totals; the route page should not publish a fixed Gloom amount.
- The **Conqueror's Hoard Beacon** appears in the arena after Droeg. Cleanse the triangular Beacon and separately collect the Ova in the pod chamber. For the wider Ova handoff and progression rules, link [Ova and corruption progression](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/).

### Frostshard Stone landmark route

- AllThingsHow describes the optional route after the post-boss Beacon: leave the cleansed chamber, pass the ruins and praying statues, pray at the large snow-covered statue, cross a valley with giant stone hands and campfires, cross a narrow bridge, drop at the end, and open a stone chest.
- GameSpot independently confirms that the area beyond the Beacon has extreme cold that causes periodic damage and that the Frostshard Stone is at the end. The article should advise moving continuously and bringing healing, without inventing a timer or damage value.

## Conflicts and uncertainty to preserve

1. **Ovate's Chamber naming:** sources use both “Ovate Chamber” and “Ovate's Chamber.” Use the local page / Beacon label **Ovate's Chamber** and tell the reader to follow the in-game label.
2. **Ovate-to-Droeg approach:** traversal-gate, elevator, and “continue to the frozen lake” descriptions refer to the same final checkpoint area but do not establish one universal sequence. Keep all three cues and avoid a precise turn claim.
3. **Winterglass Lake order:** checklist pages do not present the same visual order for Winterglass Lake and Ovate's Chamber. The reliable fact is that Winterglass Lake is the large-structure / altar-room stair checkpoint and Ovate's Chamber is the Urrig-side boss checkpoint.
4. **Conqueror's Hoard spelling:** source pages vary between “Conqueror's” and “Conquerer's.” Use **Conqueror's Hoard** to match the local Beacon list, while recognizing the variant in the uncertainty section if needed.
5. **Reward quantities:** AllThingsHow reports 38,753 Gloom, while other checked pages omit or do not cross-check the amount. Omit the number from the answer-first guide.
6. **No coordinates:** do not copy map coordinates or create a new marker. This worker is explicitly not changing `src/data/map-markers.ts`.
