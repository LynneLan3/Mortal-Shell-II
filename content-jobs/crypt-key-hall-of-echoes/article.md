Short answer
- Where to get the Crypt Key: In a ruined structure at Desolate Peak (the building near Baghead and the entrance to Illusionist's Cache). Inspect the table/shelf/counter around that dungeon entrance — sources differ on the exact surface. (GameSpot; AllThings How; Gamer Guides.)
- Where it opens: The Crypt Key unlocks the Hall of Echoes door in the eastern/northeastern Fainweald landmark cluster around Grisha Hunting Grounds and Stones of Remembrance. Sources disagree on whether the door is north or south of the Grisha landmark. (GameSpot; AllThings How.)
- The key appears to be consumed by the door prompt on launch builds. (GameSpot; AllThings How.)

Getting the Crypt Key (exact pickup surface varies)
1. Fast-travel or walk to Desolate Peak (the ruined structure near the Baghead NPC and the Illusionist's Cache entrance). GameSpot and AllThings How both place the key here.
2. Inspect the ruined building that contains the Illusionist's Cache entrance. Look specifically at any table, shelf, or rear counter near/above the dungeon entrance — sources use different furniture words, so thoroughly search the immediate interior surfaces. (GameSpot: wooden table; AllThings How: shelf above entrance; MortalShell2Guide: rear table/right side.)
3. Pick up the Crypt Key from the surface you find it on.

Route to the Hall of Echoes entrance (practical approach reported in launch coverage)
1. Start at Blackridge Pass Beacon.
2. Travel toward Shattered Beacon.
3. From Shattered Beacon, proceed east and use the corruption-cleansed route toward the Stones of Remembrance / Grisha Hunting Grounds cluster. (AllThings How reports this as a usable approach; other routes may exist.)
4. When you reach the Stones of Remembrance / Grisha Hunting Grounds area, look for the locked Hall of Echoes door nearby. Note that guide sources disagree on whether it sits north or south of the Grisha landmark; search the landmark cluster rather than relying on a single direction. (GameSpot places it due north of Grisha; AllThings How places it south of Grisha.)
5. Use the Crypt Key on the door. On launch build coverage the door prompt consumes the Crypt Key.

Minimum Hall of Echoes puzzle route (entering the Hall with the Crypt Key)
1. Use the lift and descend into the Hall; clear the first enemies that spawn.
2. At the end of the first passage, strike the left wall to reveal a hidden path. (Hidden-wall strike is reported by multiple guides.)
3. In the next area, strike the wall left of the floor switch to open a side room near a Gloom Tree; clear that side room and pick up the Laterite that is reported there (optional resource). (Gamer Guides.)
4. Stand on the floor panel. In the opened room, shoot the bell to create the bridge/path you need to progress.
5. Reach the stone statue/stone block, push it onto the pressure panel.
6. With the block on the panel, enter the final reward room.

Confirmed Hall rewards (launch-guide consensus)
- Winterglass Gem (reported by GameSpot, Gamer Guides, AllThings How)
- Dorsalite (reported by GameSpot, Gamer Guides, AllThings How)

Disputed/version-sensitive reward
- Sheephead Totem: GameSpot and AllThings How report a Sheephead Totem obtained by killing a sheep in the Hall room. Gamer Guides does not list the Totem in the Hall and instead places it in the Alchemist's Workshop. Treat the Totem placement as source-disputed/version-sensitive; it should not be relied on as a guaranteed Hall reward without further verification.

Common mistakes
- Searching the wrong building: the key is at Desolate Peak in the ruined structure next to Baghead and the Illusionist's Cache entrance, not every ruined house in Fainweald.
- Skimming the room: because sources disagree on the pickup surface (table, shelf, rear counter), players often miss the key by only checking one surface type.
- Relying on a single direction from Grisha: sources conflict on whether the Hall door is north or south of the Grisha Hunting Grounds; search the Stones of Remembrance / Grisha cluster rather than assuming one direction.
- Not cleansing corruption or taking an indirect route: the practical approach reported by AllThings How goes through a corruption-cleansed route from Shattered Beacon; other approaches may be blocked or hazardous.
- Expecting the key to be reusable: launch guides report the Crypt Key is consumed by the door prompt.
- Missing puzzle triggers: failing to strike the hidden walls or to push the stone block onto the panel will block progression.

What happens next
- Opening the Hall with the Crypt Key grants access to the Hall of Echoes puzzle and its final reward room (Winterglass Gem and Dorsalite on launch guides). According to launch coverage the key is consumed at the door prompt, so you cannot reuse it to relock/reopen the same Hall without obtaining another key (no official dev documentation was found during review).
- If you are hunting the Sheephead Totem, be aware its placement is disputed across launch guides; you may need to check alternative locations such as the Alchemist's Workshop if it is absent from the Hall in your play session.

Troubleshooting and uncertainty notes (summary of source limits)
- Pickup-surface wording varies between guides; inspect all nearby interior surfaces (table/shelf/counter) around the Illusionist's Cache entrance at Desolate Peak. (GameSpot; AllThings How; MortalShell2Guide.)
- The Hall door’s exact north/south relation to the Grisha Hunting Grounds is inconsistent across launch coverage. Search the Stones of Remembrance / Grisha landmark cluster. (GameSpot vs. AllThings How.)
- Sheephead Totem placement is disputed between launch sources; treat it as version-sensitive until confirmed in your build. (GameSpot and AllThings How vs. Gamer Guides.)
- No official developer or Steam page documenting the Crypt Key location or Hall walkthrough was found during the bounded review; claims above reflect multiple independent launch guides but not first-party confirmation.
