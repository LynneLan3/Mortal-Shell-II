# Item 15 research: Crypt Key + Hall of Echoes

Research date: 2026-08-24

## Scope

## Evidence boundary and working answer

The strongest convergent launch-window evidence places the key in the ruined structure at **Desolate Peak**, near **Baghead** and the entrance to **Illusionist's Cache**. The pickup is described as being on a table, shelf, or rear counter above/near that dungeon entrance. The exact surface wording differs by source, so the guide should use the stable landmark and warn readers to inspect the structure rather than overstate one furniture label.

The key opens the **Hall of Echoes** entrance in eastern/northeastern Fainweald, in the landmark cluster around the **Grisha Hunting Grounds** and **Stones of Remembrance**. The sources disagree on the door's exact north/south relation to Grisha: GameSpot describes it due north of Grisha, while AllThings How describes it south of Grisha. The most actionable route in current coverage starts at **Blackridge Pass Beacon**, goes to **Shattered Beacon**, then east through the corruption-cleansed route toward Stones of Remembrance and the locked door. The key is reported as consumed by the door prompt; retain that as launch-guide evidence rather than official documentation.

Inside Hall of Echoes, the evidence supports this short puzzle sequence:

1. Descend the lift and clear the first enemies.
2. Strike the left wall at the end of the first passage to reveal a path.
3. In the next area, strike the wall left of the floor switch and clear the side room near the Gloom Tree; current coverage reports a Laterite there.
4. Stand on the floor panel, shoot the bell in the opened room, and use the created bridge/path to reach the stone statue/block.
5. Push the stone object onto the panel, then enter the final reward room.

Two sources agree on **Winterglass Gem** and **Dorsalite** as Hall rewards. GameSpot and AllThings How additionally report a **Sheephead Totem** obtained by killing a sheep in the room. Gamer Guides does not list that totem in its Hall of Echoes section and instead places a Sheephead Totem in Alchemist's Workshop. The guide must therefore call the Hall Sheephead Totem placement **source-disputed / version-sensitive** and should not make it the lead answer.
