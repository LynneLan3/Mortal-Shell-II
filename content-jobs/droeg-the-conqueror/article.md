Short answer — where and how to start:
Droeg the Conqueror sits at the end of the snowed-in Conquered Temple in the Mammon region (Ruins of Mammon). Reach Ovate’s Chamber Beacon (the last documented checkpoint before the arena), activate it, then use the traversal gate across the gap to trigger the encounter.

Route (do these steps in order)
1. Enter the Conquered Temple from its Corrupted Gate in the Mammon / Sester’s Refuge branch. Clear the dungeon to reach Urrig, the Executioner. (All guide sources agree on this dungeon and route.)
2. Defeat Urrig and follow the post-Urrig path: use the jumping pad, go upstairs, take the elevator to Ovate’s Chamber. (Probonk documents the elevator placement.)
3. Activate Ovate’s Chamber Beacon. From the Beacon, step onto the traversal gate across the gap to trigger Droeg’s arena and start the fight. (Probonk and GamingProMax agree the traversal gate triggers the encounter.)

Prerequisites and optional help
- You must be able to access and progress through the Conquered Temple to Urrig. Sources describe Droeg as one of the Corrupted Gate bosses on the Mammon branch but do not establish a single mandatory boss order for the whole game.  
- Before entering the arena a green summon sign can be used to unlock Gragu for 1 Glimpse. This is optional assistance, not required to enter or finish the fight. (Multiple sources mention Gragu; the summon sign is optional.)

Fight outline and step-by-step handling
1. Phase 1 — melee spacing and punish windows
   1. Droeg’s Phase 1 is primarily melee. Watch for the club/bone-club tells: club resting across the shoulders or arms thrown back, long lift tells before heavy slams, and an overhead finish. (GamingProMax; AllThingsHow.)
   2. Typical attacks documented: two wide swings, a triple vertical slam (with arms thrown back), wide-horizontal bone sweeps, close stomp, and occasional chained slams. (AllThingsHow; GamingProMax.)
   3. How to approach: wait for a full heavy chain to finish and use the recovery window for a short punish. GamingProMax recommends only two or three hits before backing off and resetting—do not assume long combos are safe.
   4. Important spacing note: Droeg has long reach. Don’t attack on the first apparent pause—follow-up slams can still be queued.

2. Phase 2 — red-glow projectile bait (around half health)
   1. Around half health Droeg will add the homing Blazing Frostfire projectile barrage. Tell: Droeg may kneel, stumble, or look briefly stunned and a red glow / warning appears. This is a projectile summon and not a free stagger punish. (AllThingsHow; GamingProMax.)
   2. Reliable response: start moving immediately and sprint or dodge sideways continuously until the volley has passed. Projectiles home and can apply Burn / ongoing fire damage as reported by guides.
   3. Re-engage only after the barrage ends; reserve stamina for movement during this phase rather than spending every opening on long combos.
   4. Optional tech: community reports (and GamingProMax notes) that Gragu’s special punch or weapon specials with invulnerability frames can sometimes interrupt or bypass the barrage. Treat this as player-tech and not a guaranteed mechanic.

3. Positioning and dodge-direction conflict (preserve source disagreement)
   - Sources disagree on the ideal dodge direction. AllThingsHow advises dodging backward for the two wide swings and sideways for the vertical slam; GamingProMax suggests dodging forward or forward+side through swings to reach Droeg’s flank.  
   - Practical compromise: pick the dodge direction based on the named attack and your spacing. Use the documented sideways solution for vertical slams, give room for wide arcs, and confirm the full chain before punishing. (Do not follow a universal “always forward” or “always backward” rule.)

Shells, weapons, and consumables (recommendations from guides)
- Gragu: optional summon from the green sign before the arena (costs 1 Glimpse). Useful as a distraction and to make safer healing/punish windows while learning the fight. (Multiple guides.)
- Axatana: recommended by AllThingsHow as a fast weapon for quick strikes during openings. Not mandatory.
- Tiel: recommended by GamingProMax for its perfect-dodge invisibility / Shadow Strike tech to turn well-timed dodges into damage opportunities. Community observations only—useful but not required.
- Burnt Effigy: recommended (AllThingsHow, GamingProMax) if you want to reduce Burn buildup or soften projectile mistakes. Useful if available.
- Black Needle: noted as compatible with Droeg’s Conqueror’s Reward Tarstone but not required to obtain the reward.

Rewards (what Droeg drops and immediate post-fight progression)
- Conqueror’s Reward Tarstone: a Tarstone that works with the Black Needle; it costs 100 Resolve to activate and triggers a fast piercing-thrust series. AllThingsHow provides additional effect numbers (single-source and possibly patch-sensitive). Present these numerical bonuses as source-reported if you need them.  
- Gloom: Droeg grants Gloom. AllThingsHow’s reward table lists 38,753 Gloom for their run—treat that number as run/source-specific, not a guaranteed universal payout.
- Conqueror’s Hoard Beacon and Ova: a Beacon appears in the arena after the fight. Cleanse the Conqueror’s Hoard Beacon and collect the Ova from the pod chamber before leaving. (AllThingsHow; Probonk.)

Frostshard Stone optional cold detour (follow after cleansing the beacon)
1. From the cleared arena, continue the cold route past a series of praying statues.
2. Pray at the large snow-covered statue.
3. Cross the valley of giant stone hands.
4. Cross the narrow bridge and drop at the end.
5. Open the stone chest to obtain the Frostshard Stone. The cold area deals periodic damage—prepare accordingly. (AllThingsHow.)
- AllThingsHow identifies the Frostshard Stone as a Level 1 Common Summon/Infusion Tarstone for Solocru and lists exact Resolve/capacity numbers; treat those exact numbers as single-source and potentially version-sensitive.

Common mistakes
- Attacking too early in Phase 1: striking after the first apparent pause can be punished by a queued follow-up slam. Wait for chain completion recovery.
- Standing still when the red glow appears: the Phase 2 stagger is bait for the projectile barrage—start moving immediately.
- Burning stamina on long combos instead of saving it for projectile movement or dodges.
- Ignoring the optional Gragu summon sign when you could use a distraction while learning timings.
- Assuming the numerical Gloom reward or Tarstone stats in one guide are universal—treat guide numbers as run- or patch-sensitive.

What happens next (immediate steps after Droeg’s defeat)
1. Enter and cleanse the newly spawned Conqueror’s Hoard Beacon.
2. Collect the Ova from the pod chamber inside the Beacon area.
3. Optional: take the cold detour to get the Frostshard Stone (praying statues → large snow-covered statue → stone-hand valley → narrow bridge/drop → stone chest). Be aware of periodic cold damage.
4. Return or fast-travel after the detour if desired, then continue through the remaining Mammon branch. Guides say other Mammon gate bosses are route-flexible; there’s no single mandatory next boss established by the sources.

Trophy / achievement
- Defeating Droeg unlocks the “It burnssssssssss” achievement/trophy (listed by XboxAchievements, Exophase, Gamer Guides, AllThingsHow). No source in this set identifies the trophy as missable or requiring a special pre-kill action—meeting the normal defeat condition is the documented unlock.

Patch notes and version cautions
- Balance Patch 1 (developer summary reported via GamesRadar and local patch files) lists adjustments to other bosses and economy/weapon/Tarstone changes but does not list a direct Droeg health/damage/timing change.  
- Hotfix 2.0 Steam notes focus on crashes/stability/UI and do not list Droeg-specific changes.  
- Safe wording: Balance Patch 1 and Hotfix 2.0 do not list direct Droeg adjustments in the checked notes; future updates may change the encounter or reward numbers.
