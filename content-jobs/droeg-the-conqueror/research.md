# Droeg the Conqueror — research notes

Research date: 2026-08-24
Scope: Item 26 only. No map x/y coordinates. The notes below are the factual boundary for the GPT writer; claims without direct support are qualified rather than filled from memory.

## Answer-first fact set

- **Boss location:** Droeg the Conqueror is at the end of the snowed-in **Conquered Temple** in the Mammon region / Ruins of Mammon. One source describes the dungeon as the southeastern Corrupted Gate of the region; treat the dungeon name and Mammon location as the stable fact, not an invented global order.
- **Nearest practical Beacon:** **Ovate’s Chamber Beacon** is the last documented checkpoint before the arena. Probonk places it at the top of the elevator leading to Droeg’s arena; after Urrig, the Executioner, use the jumping pad, go upstairs, and take the elevator. From the Beacon, use the traversal gate across the gap to trigger the encounter.
- **Route:** Enter Conquered Temple from its Corrupted Gate in Sester’s Refuge / Mammon, clear the dungeon, defeat Urrig, follow the post-Urrig route to Ovate’s Chamber, activate the Beacon, then take the traversal gate. Sources agree on this route. Do not add coordinate directions.
- **Prerequisites:** The player must have access to the Conquered Temple route and be able to progress through the dungeon to Urrig. Sources commonly describe Droeg as one of the six Corrupted Gate bosses and often place the Mammon branch after early Fainweald progress, but they do not establish a universal fixed number or mandatory boss order. Do not claim that exactly three Fainweald bosses are required.
- **Summon prerequisite:** A green summon sign before the arena can unlock **Gragu** for 1 Glimpse; this is optional help, not a requirement to enter or defeat Droeg.

## Fight mechanics, phases, tells, and punish windows

### Phase 1: melee spacing

- GamingProMax describes Phase 1 as melee-only and lists a ground / bone-club slam with a long lift tell, a possible chain of follow-up slams, wide horizontal bone sweeps, and a close stomp.
- AllThingsHow gives a compact attack table: two wide swings, a triple vertical slam, and a wide-wide-downward variation. Its tells are the club resting across the shoulders, arms thrown back for the vertical slams, and the later overhead finish.
- Stable strategy: wait for the full sequence to end, then take a short punish. The safest repeatable window described by both guide sources is the recovery after a completed heavy chain / slam. GamingProMax recommends two or three hits and a reset; do not assume a long combo is safe.
- Long reach is repeatedly emphasized. Attacking after only the first apparent pause is unsafe because a follow-up may still be queued.

### Phase 2: red-glow projectile bait

- Around half health, sources say Droeg adds the dangerous **Blazing Frostfire** / homing flame-and-frost projectile barrage. The exact health percentage is not independently verified; write “around half health” rather than an exact threshold.
- Tell: Droeg appears to kneel, stumble, or look briefly stunned; a red glow / red warning indicator appears. This is a projectile summon, not a free stagger punish.
- Response: start moving immediately and sprint or dodge sideways continuously until the volley has passed. Re-engage only after the barrage is over. Projectiles home toward the player and can build Burn / deal ongoing fire damage according to the guide sources.
- AllThingsHow also reports extra spins before some melee swings in Phase 2; wait for the actual pause / strike rather than dodging the spin animation automatically.
- GamingProMax mentions a community-reported possibility that Gragu’s special punch or a weapon special with invulnerability frames can interrupt / bypass the barrage. Treat this as optional player-tech, not a guaranteed mechanic; the article should lead with movement as the reliable solution.

### Conflicting movement advice

- AllThingsHow says to dodge backward for the two wide swings and sideways for the vertical slam; GamingProMax generally recommends dodging forward and to the side through swings to reach Droeg’s flank or back. This is a real presentation conflict, not a reason to guess.
- Article treatment: avoid a universal “always forward” or “always backward” rule. Explain that the direction depends on the named attack and current spacing: use the documented sideways answer for vertical slams, preserve room for wide arcs, and confirm the whole chain before punishing. Attribute the forward-through advice as a positioning option rather than a law.

## Rewards

- **Conqueror’s Reward Tarstone:** works with the **Black Needle** and costs 100 Resolve to activate a fast series of piercing thrusts. AllThingsHow gives additional effect numbers (200% damage, four additional strikes, increased Resolve gain); because those numbers are single-source details and may be patch-sensitive, the page should either attribute them as source-reported or omit the exact numbers.
- **Gloom:** AllThingsHow reports 38,753 in its reward table. This is a run/source-specific number and should not be presented as a universal guaranteed payout without qualification; the stable claim is that Droeg grants Gloom.
- **Conqueror’s Hoard Beacon and Ova:** a Beacon appears in the arena after the fight. Cleanse it and collect the Ova from the pod chamber before leaving; the beacon / Ova progression is confirmed by AllThingsHow and Probonk’s Beacon list.
- **Frostshard Stone:** after cleansing the beacon, continue through the cold route: pass the praying statues, pray at the large snow-covered statue, cross the valley of giant stone hands, cross the narrow bridge and drop at the end, then open the stone chest. The cold area causes periodic damage. AllThingsHow identifies the item as a Level 1 Common Summon/Infusion Tarstone for Solocru; exact 20 Resolve / 4% details should be qualified as source-reported if included.

## Shell / weapon suggestions

- **Gragu:** documented as the optional spirit summon associated with Droeg. Spend 1 Glimpse at the sign before the arena to unlock / call him. Use him for distraction and safer healing or punish windows; do not call him mandatory.
- **Axatana:** AllThingsHow suggests fast weapons such as Axatana for quick strikes during openings. Present this as a practical recommendation from that guide, not the only viable weapon.
- **Tiel:** GamingProMax calls Tiel a strong choice because perfect-dodge invisibility / Shadow Strike can turn well-timed dodges into damage opportunities. This is a recommendation, not a source-proven best Shell.
- **Burnt Effigy:** AllThingsHow and GamingProMax recommend it for reducing Burn buildup / softening projectile mistakes. Keep the wording as “useful if available,” not a prerequisite.
- **Black Needle:** the confirmed relevance is the reward’s compatible weapon; do not claim the player must already own it.

## Trophy / missable status

- **It burnssssssssss:** achievement / trophy for defeating Droeg the Conquerer. This is independently listed by XboxAchievements, Exophase, Gamer Guides, and AllThingsHow.
- No source found in this pass identifies Droeg’s defeat trophy as missable or requires a special pre-kill action. State that the normal defeat condition is the documented unlock, and do not invent a missable warning.

## What happens after the fight

1. Enter and cleanse the newly spawned **Conqueror’s Hoard Beacon**.
2. Collect the Ova from the pod chamber.
3. Take the optional cold detour for Frostshard Stone: praying statues → large snow-covered statue → stone-hand valley → narrow bridge / drop → stone chest.
4. Return / fast travel after the detour if desired, then continue through the remaining Mammon branch. Sources say the other Mammon gate bosses are route-flexible; do not claim a single mandatory next boss.

They do **not** list a direct Droeg health, damage, or attack-timing change.
- Safe article wording: “Balance Patch 1 does not list a direct Droeg adjustment in the currently checked notes; future updates may change the encounter.” Do not infer that Droeg is unchanged forever or that the page’s source-specific numbers are permanently current.
- Hotfix 2.0’s Steam release notes are about crashes, stability, performance, UI, and weapon-stealing during ripostes; no Droeg-specific change is listed there.

## Practical player insights

- Treat the apparent Phase 2 stagger as bait and keep moving when the red warning appears; this is the most consistently repeated survival lesson across the independent guides.
- Keep the arena readable and reserve stamina / healing for projectile movement rather than spending every opening on a full combo.
- Summoning Gragu trades 1 Glimpse for distraction; it is most useful while learning the timing, but solo is not ruled out.
- Community discussion reports that the projectile attack can feel difficult to read and that some players use Tiel or other mobility / iframe options. These are anecdotal and should be labeled as community observations, not universal facts.
