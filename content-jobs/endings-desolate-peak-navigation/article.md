## How to Reach Desolate Peak

Short answer: Desolate Peak is in western Fainweald and is reached by walking through a nearby Bone Gate — there is no clearly documented Beacon sitting directly beside Baghead, the Illusionist’s Cache entrance, or the Crypt Key pickup. The most useful nearby Beacons are Widow’s Overlook and Outskirts of Nochte; from one of those you must travel to a Bone Gate (commonly the Bone Gate south of the Citadel of Penance) and pass through it to arrive at Desolate Peak. You cannot fast-travel directly to Baghead.

### Nearest useful Beacons

- Widow’s Overlook Beacon and Outskirts of Nochte Beacon are the nearest practical fast-travel anchors. See the Beacon locations and fast travel guide for details and fast-travel mechanics: /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/.

### From Widow's Overlook

1. Start at the Widow’s Overlook Beacon (a pre-cleansed early Fainweald anchor).
2. Travel north from the Beacon.
3. Curve west toward the Citadel of Penance side.
4. Find the Bone Gate located south of the Citadel of Penance.
5. Use that Bone Gate to enter the Desolate Peak area.
6. When the area name changes to Desolate Peak, look for Baghead and the nearby ruined structure as confirmation landmarks.

### From Outskirts of Nochte

1. Start at the Outskirts of Nochte Beacon (western Fainweald swamp edge).
2. Move north toward the Citadel / western Fainweald Bone Gate approach.
3. Use the Bone Gate you find there to arrive in Desolate Peak.

Note: both routes are supported by guides; neither should be presented as the sole possible entrance.

### Confirm you are in Desolate Peak

When you arrive, verify the location by these stable landmarks:
- Baghead seated on his throne/stone seat.
- A large ruined building directly opposite or near Baghead.
- The Illusionist’s Cache entrance inside that ruined structure and the Crypt Key pickup area in the same building cluster.

If you can see Baghead and the ruined building containing the Illusionist’s Cache entrance, you are in the correct Desolate Peak area. For the Crypt Key pickup and where to use it, see the Crypt Key guide: /mortal-shell-ii/crypt-key-hall-of-echoes/.

Common Mistakes

- Expecting a dedicated “Desolate Peak Beacon” or a fast-travel point directly beside Baghead. No reliable guide documents such a Beacon.
- Trying to fast-travel to Baghead itself — you must reach a nearby Beacon and then walk through a Bone Gate.
- Assuming only one Bone Gate works; the Bone Gate south of the Citadel of Penance is the commonly cited entrance, but other nearby Bone Gates (for example from Outskirts of Nochte) are also used by players.
- Skipping confirmation landmarks — enter the area first, then follow the Baghead secret-ending steps on this page.

What Happens Next

Once you confirm the landmarks (Baghead + ruined structure with the Illusionist’s Cache), continue with the Baghead secret-ending steps below on this page. If you need the Crypt Key or the Illusionist’s Cache entrance location, follow the Crypt Key guide linked above.

## FAQ additions (merge into existing FAQ section)

### What is the nearest Beacon to Desolate Peak?

There is no Beacon directly beside Desolate Peak. The nearest useful Beacons are Widow’s Overlook and Outskirts of Nochte; from either you must travel to a nearby Bone Gate and pass through it to reach Desolate Peak. See /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/ for Beacon locations.

### How do you reach Desolate Peak in Mortal Shell 2?

Reach a nearby Beacon (Widow’s Overlook or Outskirts of Nochte), travel toward the Citadel of Penance approach, find the Bone Gate (commonly the Bone Gate south of the Citadel), and use it to enter Desolate Peak.

### Can you fast travel directly to Desolate Peak?

No — current reliable guides do not document a Desolate Peak Beacon or a fast-travel point directly to Baghead. Fast-travel to a nearby Beacon, then walk to a Bone Gate and use it.

### Where is Baghead in Desolate Peak?

Baghead is in western Fainweald’s Desolate Peak, seated near a large ruined building that contains the Illusionist’s Cache entrance and the Crypt Key pickup area. If you see Baghead and that ruined structure, you are in the correct spot. For the Crypt Key details, see /mortal-shell-ii/crypt-key-hall-of-echoes/.
