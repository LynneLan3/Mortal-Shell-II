## Critical beacon-intent answer

**There is no clearly documented Beacon directly beside the main Desolate Peak landmarks (Baghead, Illusionist's Cache entrance, Crypt Key area).** Current reliable guides do **not** support a standalone "Desolate Peak Beacon" for fast-travel directly to Baghead.

Correct framing: players reach Desolate Peak from a **nearby Beacon**, then walk to a **Bone Gate** and use it to enter the area. Do **not** invent a Desolate Peak Beacon, direct fast-travel to Baghead, or a teleport unlock.

## Evidence-backed location facts

- **Desolate Peak** is in **western Fainweald**.
- The area contains **Baghead**, a **ruined building opposite/near him** with the **Illusionist's Cache** entrance inside, and the **Crypt Key** pickup area in that structure.
- **Illusionist's Cache** is in Desolate Peak (PowerPyx; GameSpot; cross-source).
- **Baghead secret-ending / Bag Holder trophy route** is in Desolate Peak (PowerPyx; NerdsChalk; AllThings How; local endings page).
- Desolate Peak is reached through a **Bone Gate**, not by fast-travelling directly to Baghead.

## Verified routes (do not present either as the only entrance)

### Route A — from Widow's Overlook (strongest cross-source support)

1. Start at **Widow's Overlook Beacon** (pre-cleansed early Fainweald anchor).
2. Travel **north**.
3. **Curve west** toward the **Citadel of Penance** side.
4. Find the **Bone Gate south of the Citadel of Penance**.
5. Use the Bone Gate to reach **Desolate Peak**.
6. When the area name changes to **Desolate Peak**, look for **Baghead** and the **large ruined structure** as confirmation landmarks.

Sources: cross-guide consensus (Widow's Overlook → north/west → Bone Gate south of Citadel → Desolate Peak). GameSpot Crypt Key guide also describes Citadel of Penance lower cliffside approach to Desolate Peak / Baghead side.

### Route B — from Outskirts of Nochte (supported but keep shorter)

1. Start at **Outskirts of Nochte Beacon** (western Fainweald swamp edge; listed in launch Beacon checklist).
2. Move **north / toward the relevant Bone Gate** in the Citadel / western Fainweald approach.
3. Use the **Bone Gate** to arrive at **Desolate Peak**.

Sources: cross-guide reports that Outskirts of Nochte can also reach Desolate Peak via Bone Gate. Keep this route brief — fewer step-level details than Route A.
