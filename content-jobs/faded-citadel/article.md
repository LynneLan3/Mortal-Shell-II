Short answer — how to run Faded Citadel:
1) Clean (or confirm) access at the Gate of Infinity Beacon in Mammon, then enter the Faded Citadel. 2) Push through Ashen Sanctum to the Citadel Annex Beacon; optionally Cleanse the Annex to run the Hushed Sanctum and grab the Stillblade's Stone. 3) Continue the main path to Citadel Atrium, follow the route past the blue-flame zones and Servant encounters, decide whether to spend 1 Glimpse on the Lazlo summon, and drop to fight Isaac. 4) After the boss, activate/cleanse the Scholar‑Prince’s Chamber Beacon and collect the Ova.

Detailed route (follow these steps in order)
1. Reach Gate of Infinity Beacon in Mammon and confirm it is usable. If the Faded Citadel entrance is blocked, cleanse the Gate of Infinity Beacon first (use the Beacon label and the visible Corrupted Gate to confirm you’re at the right spot; overworld approach descriptions vary between sources).
2. Enter the Faded Citadel. Move through Ashen Sanctum, passing the egg‑filled cave and the staircase to a balcony. Pick up the Laterite near that balcony and follow the stone platform to the opening that contains the Citadel Annex Beacon.
3. Activate Citadel Annex Beacon.
   - If you want the Beacon reward, choose Cleanse Beacon to enter Hushed Sanctum. To complete that cleanse:
     1. Cross the first narrow bridge (expect a ranged caster).
     2. Proceed through the tunnel with melee enemies and spiders.
     3. Ascend the stairs with the teleporting lightning caster.
     4. Clear the large pillared spider room.
     5. Use the platforming/drop section to reach the circular arena and red rift.
     6. Take the red rift up to the high ledge with a mage and a chest; take the Stillblade’s Stone (a Level 1 Tarstone with a Light Combo Finisher effect).
     7. Touch the corrupted crystal core. A “Beacon Cleansed” message confirms the Hushed Sanctum completion — merely taking a chest and leaving is not enough.
4. Continue on the main Faded Citadel path from Citadel Annex. Use the chest beside a tree and the stairs down on its left as a route check; continue down the main descent to reach the Citadel Atrium Beacon. Expect blue‑flame zones and Servants of the Scholar‑Prince on the approach; follow the next labeled Beacon you encounter rather than relying on any single guide’s room ordering.
5. From Citadel Atrium, continue deeper along the only available route. Near the boss approach you will encounter Ruk and a Lazlo summon sign; you can spend 1 Glimpse to activate Lazlo before dropping into Isaac’s arena. For Isaac combat mechanics, phase notes, rewards, and achievement details, follow the dedicated Isaac guide at /mortal-shell-ii/isaac-the-scholar-prince/.
6. After defeating Isaac, activate or cleanse the Scholar‑Prince’s Chamber Beacon and pick up the Ova there. Use the in‑game prompt to collect Ova; do not rely on a fixed post‑boss Ova count in this guide.

Optional: Faded Gland (annex area)
- From Citadel Annex (rest at the Beacon), turn away from the normal ascent and drop into the lower courtyard. Clear the dual‑sword enemies and inspect the candle‑ringed stone dish in the center to collect the Faded Gland for the Seedbearer’s Scripture. This is an optional side objective, not required to access the Citadel or defeat Isaac.

Common mistakes
- Assuming a single overworld route: guides disagree on the exact approach to Gate of Infinity. Use the Gate of Infinity Beacon label and the visible Corrupted Gate rather than compass directions or a fixed chain of landmarks.
- Skipping the Hushed Sanctum core interaction: taking the chest in Hushed Sanctum and leaving does not register the Beacon as cleansed — you must touch the corrupted crystal core and see the “Beacon Cleansed” message.
- Treating Hushed Sanctum and Ashen Sanctum as the same space: Ashen Sanctum is the main Faded Citadel/boss area; Hushed Sanctum is the separate cleanse dungeon you enter from Citadel Annex.
- Expecting a guaranteed Ova quantity: sources list post‑boss Beacons as major Ova locations, but exact counts can vary by checklist; collect whatever Ova the Beacon gives rather than relying on a fixed number.
- Repeating a locked combat table: Isaac’s finer combat timing (Riposte, shield removal, etc.) is patch‑sensitive and covered on the dedicated Isaac page — don’t rely on this walkthrough for detailed boss mechanics.

Conflicts and uncertainties you should know
- Overworld approach variance: GameSpot and AllThingsHow describe different landmark sequences to Gate of Infinity. Use the Gate of Infinity Beacon and the visible Corrupted Gate to confirm location.
- Slight narration differences around Servants/Atrium: sources place blue‑flame zones and the Servant encounters at marginally different points in the main path; follow the next Beacon label you find.
- Naming distinction: Ashen Sanctum = main approach/boss area; Hushed Sanctum = the optional cleanse dungeon reached from Citadel Annex.
- Post‑boss Ova quantity: guide checklists commonly list 26 Ova for major post‑boss Beacons but counts can vary; collect the Ova shown in‑game.
- Isaac combat details are version‑sensitive; consult the Isaac guide rather than relying on this route for mechanical specifics.
- No coordinates or single calibrated room map are published here — use Beacon labels and the described environmental landmarks.
