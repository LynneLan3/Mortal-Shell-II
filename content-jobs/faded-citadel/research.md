## Evidence boundary

The stable route is **Gate of Infinity access → Citadel Annex → Citadel Atrium → Isaac, the Scholar Prince → Scholar-Prince's Chamber**. The Faded Citadel is the southern Mammon Corrupted Gate, and current guide coverage agrees that its three named interior Beacons are on the main route. The Citadel Annex has a separate Hushed Sanctum cleanse, while the post-boss Scholar-Prince's Chamber Beacon is the Ova handoff.

The checked sources do not provide one audited, calibrated room map. Overworld approaches to Gate of Infinity use different nearby landmarks, and guide prose differs slightly on where the blue-flame/Servant encounter sits relative to the Atrium checkpoint. The article must navigate by Beacon labels, Ashen Sanctum, the egg-filled cave, Laterite, the tree-and-chest descent, Ruk, and the Lazlo summon sign. It must not publish map coordinates or imply a single mandatory Mammon-gate order.

No image is proposed. The route is answerable with named Beacons and environmental landmarks; no verified local screenshot is necessary for this bounded item.

## Answer-first route

1. Reach **Gate of Infinity Beacon** in Mammon and cleanse it if the Faded Citadel entrance is still blocked. The exact overworld approach varies in current coverage; use the Beacon label and the visible Corrupted Gate rather than a compass direction.
2. Enter Faded Citadel and climb through **Ashen Sanctum**. Pass the egg-filled cave and staircase to the balcony, collect the nearby Laterite, and follow the stone platform to the opening containing **Citadel Annex Beacon**.
3. Activate Citadel Annex. If you want its Beacon reward, choose **Cleanse Beacon** and clear **Hushed Sanctum**: cross the first bridge, clear the caster/spider rooms, use the red rift to reach the high ledge, take the **Stillblade's Stone**, and touch the corrupted crystal core. The Beacon Cleansed message confirms completion.
4. Continue on the main path from Citadel Annex. Use the chest near the tree and the stairs down on its left as the route check for **Citadel Atrium Beacon**. The blue-flame zones and Servants of the Scholar-Prince are part of this approach; follow the next labeled Beacon rather than forcing a guide's exact room order.
5. From Citadel Atrium, continue deeper along the only available route. When **Ruk** and the **Lazlo** summon sign appear near the boss descent, decide whether to spend 1 Glimpse, then drop into Isaac's arena. Hand detailed combat mechanics to the existing [Isaac, the Scholar Prince guide](/mortal-shell-ii/isaac-the-scholar-prince/).
6. After defeating Isaac, activate/cleanse the post-boss **Scholar-Prince's Chamber Beacon** and collect its Ova before leaving. Use the existing [Ova guide](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/) for the wider progression handoff and the [Beacons and fast travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/) for Beacon state differences.

## Source-backed facts

### Gate access and region context

- GameSpot's Beacon coverage places Gate of Infinity directly south of Castigator's Keep: ride the lift, pass the Starved Harridan encounter, move through the pylon area, and clear the blue-flame lantern protection around the Beacon. It explicitly says Gate of Infinity must be cleansed to access Faded Citadel.
- AllThingsHow describes Faded Citadel as the southern Mammon Corrupted Gate reached through Gate of Infinity in the Vestige of Infinity section. Its location wording is compatible with the Beacon identity but does not provide the same overworld turn sequence as GameSpot.
- The wider route is flexible. GameSpot and the local Ova page list three Mammon gates—Conquered Temple, Withered Shoals, and Faded Citadel—but do not establish that Faded Citadel must be the final gate for every save.

### Citadel Annex and Hushed Sanctum

- AllThingsHow places Citadel Annex in Ashen Sanctum after an egg-filled cave, a staircase to a balcony, a nearby Laterite pickup, and a stone-platform opening. This is the strongest landmark chain for the first interior Beacon.
- Choosing Cleanse Beacon at Citadel Annex enters Hushed Sanctum. The checked route is: first narrow bridge with a ranged caster; tunnel with melee enemies and spiders; stairs with a teleporting lightning caster; a large pillared spider room; a platforming/drop section; a circular arena; red rift; high ledge with a mage and chest; then the corrupted crystal core.
- The Hushed Sanctum reward is **Stillblade's Stone**, described as a Level 1 Tarstone with a Light Combo Finisher effect. The article should present the cleanse as the Annex completion step, not as a new Faded Citadel route or a required Isaac prerequisite.
- A missing Beacon Cleansed notification means the core interaction was skipped. The source specifically warns that taking the chest reward and leaving is not enough.

### Citadel Atrium and main route

- AllThingsHow identifies Citadel Atrium as the second Faded Citadel Beacon. From Annex, open the chest near the tree, take the stairs down to the left, and continue down the main path; the Beacon is described as visible at the end of that descent.
- Beacon checklists from AllThingsHow and GamingProMax agree on the three-Beacon inventory: Citadel Annex, Citadel Atrium, and post-boss Scholar-Prince's Chamber.
- GamingProMax and the local Isaac page place the blue-flame zones and Servants of the Scholar-Prince on the route before the boss approach, followed by the Atrium checkpoint and the final descent. A separate Isaac route summary describes the Servant encounter and then the Atrium. The stable practical instruction is to follow the main path and activate each labeled Beacon when encountered.

### Isaac handoff and post-boss Beacon

- Isaac is the Faded Citadel boss in Ashen Sanctum. Current coverage agrees that Ruk appears near the final approach and that a Lazlo spirit summon can be activated for 1 Glimpse before entering the arena.
- The dedicated local Isaac page owns the shield/three-sword phase notes, the patch-sensitive Riposte skip, Scholar's Wormstone, the Nightmare Fuel achievement, and the post-boss Beacon. The walkthrough should not duplicate its disputed combat timing table.
- The post-boss Beacon is named **Scholar-Prince's Chamber**. GamingProMax and GameSpot-style Beacon coverage report Ova there, with current checklists repeatedly using 26 Ova for major post-boss Beacons. The route page should say to collect the Ova and avoid making a fixed quantity the success condition.

### Optional Faded Gland landmark

- AllThingsHow's Seedbearer's Scripture guide places the **Faded Gland** below Citadel Annex: rest at Annex, turn away from the normal ascent, drop into the lower courtyard, clear the dual-sword enemies, and inspect the candle-ringed stone dish in the center.
- The Faded Gland is an optional side objective for the Seedbearer's Scripture. It is not evidence of a required step for entering Faded Citadel or defeating Isaac. Include it as an optional Annex-area stop only, with no coordinates.

## Conflicts and uncertainty to preserve

1. **Overworld approach to Gate of Infinity:** GameSpot uses Castigator's Keep, the lift, Starved Harridan, pylons, Ruk, and the blue-flame lantern; AllThingsHow frames the route as the Vestige of Infinity entrance. These are not safe grounds for a universal compass route. Use **Gate of Infinity Beacon** and the visible Corrupted Gate as the confirmation.
2. **Annex/Atrium/Servant narration:** Sources agree on Annex first and Atrium second, but their prose places the blue-flame zones and Servants at slightly different points in the same main-path sequence. Do not publish a room count or claim that a player must see the Servants before or after a specific unmarked turn; follow the next Beacon label in the current run.
3. **Ashen Sanctum versus Hushed Sanctum:** Ashen Sanctum is the main Faded Citadel approach and boss-area name in current guide coverage. Hushed Sanctum is the separate cleanse dungeon entered from Citadel Annex. They should not be presented as alternate names for one space.
4. **Post-boss Ova quantity:** Major-guide checklists repeatedly report 26 Ova in post-boss Beacons, but exact reward counts are not necessary to complete this route and may be framed differently by sources or saves. Tell readers to collect the Ova and use the in-game prompt.
5. **Isaac combat behavior:** Shield removal, Riposte timing, and Phase 2 details are version-sensitive and already documented with uncertainty on the local Isaac page. This route guide should link out rather than restate them as universal facts.
6. **No coordinates:** External map pages expose coordinates, but there is no repository coordinate contract or calibrated map asset for this item. Do not add coordinates or modify `src/data/map-markers.ts`.
