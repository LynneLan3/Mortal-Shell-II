Short answer — what to do immediately after the Prologue
1) Play the Prologue once (recommended). 2) On first arrival at Marrow Keep: rest at the Beacon, inspect the Tarforge, talk to the Shellkeeper and vendors, then take the upper travel lift into Fainweald and head for Widow’s Overlook. In practical terms: treat Marrow Keep as your hub, learn one defensive Seal and the Break → riposte loop, activate any nearby Beacon you find, and prioritize spending Gloom/Glimpse/Ova on one clear upgrade (weapon, healing improvement, or a single Shell Bond) before chasing optional detours.

Step-by-step first-hours route (recommended spine)
Follow these numbered steps in order as a practical, spoiler-light route rather than a hard speedrun:

1. Finish the Prologue deliberately
   1. Complete the Prologue to learn the opening combat loop and arrive at Marrow Keep. The Prologue also contains two achievement/trophy opportunities (see /mortal-shell-ii/trophies/ and /mortal-shell-ii/skip-prologue/). If you’re eligible for the Prologue skip (Open Beta carryover), it remains an option later but don’t skip by default on your first playthrough.
2. Settle Marrow Keep
   1. Rest/activate the Marrow Keep Beacon.
   2. Inspect the Tarforge UI and available Tarforge functions before spending materials.
   3. Speak with the Shellkeeper and vendors (no fixed dialogue order is required by the UI).
   4. Note: after Balance Patch 1, Shell-map reveal costs use Gloom (previous Glimpse spending was refunded — Balance Patch 1).
   5. Use the upper travel mechanism once the hub opens to leave for the first region.  
   [IMAGE: Marrow Keep upper travel mechanism]
3. Enter Fainweald and secure Widow’s Overlook
   1. Use the upper travel lift to go to Fainweald (the safe opening region recommended by multiple launch guides).
   2. Travel to Widow’s Overlook and activate/rest at its Beacon.
   3. Pick up any nearby Glimpse or Ruk map fragment you encounter (see /mortal-shell-ii/map-fragments/).
   4. Use Widow’s Overlook as your early anchor; it gives a natural path to Tiel and the Great Arbiter / Martyr’s Tomb branches (GameSpot route coverage).
   [IMAGE: Widow’s Overlook Beacon / Tiel grave]
4. Take one early Shell and one stable weapon path
   1. Consider Tiel (near Widow’s Overlook) for evasive/mobility play, or Proxima (via Blackridge Pass and the Shattered Beacon) for utility — these are clear early Shell targets referenced in multiple guides. Harros is the Prologue Shell and is not a permanent post-Prologue choice (GamesRadar / local Shell research).
   2. Keep weapon choices route-focused:
      - Prologue weapon: fine baseline for learning.
      - Axe & Dagger: obtainable early in Fainweald via the Chapel Key/Shrine route.
      - Great Martyr’s Blade: heavier option tied to Widow’s Overlook / Holding Cells routes.
      - Axatana: an optional, riskier Mammon detour (not a recommended default).
      - Troubadour’s Lute: an early sidearm with status utility; note Patch 1 buff to its damage.
   3. See /mortal-shell-ii/shells/ and /mortal-shell-ii/weapons/ for route details.
   [IMAGE: a launch-build Fainweald route image]
5. Build a Beacon loop and bring home progress
   1. Activate/rest at every new Beacon you reach — this creates a checkpoint and a travel destination.
   2. After exploring a branch, return to Marrow Keep and deposit meaningful progress (Gloom, Ova, Glimpses, Tarstones, weapon materials, healing upgrades).
   3. Spend on one focused improvement (one weapon upgrade OR one healing upgrade OR a single Shell Bond) rather than scattering resources.
   4. Only cleanse a Beacon’s corruption-gated dungeon when you are prepared to complete it; tagging/activating is not the same as fully cleansing.
   [IMAGE: Beacon menu showing tag/cleanse or the Mether’s Breath prompt]
6. Open Mether’s Breath and follow in-game prompts
   1. Collect Ova/Ovum on your runs and return them to the Marrow Keep Gloom Siphon / Ova Nursery when prompted.
   2. Different sources disagree on the exact Ova thresholds required for Mether’s Breath unlock; follow the in-game unlock prompt rather than a fixed number (see /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ and the Beacon research notes). GameSpot’s route suggests cleansing Mushroom Village Gate contributes significant Ova on the early loop.
7. Repeat the loop
   1. Use this repeatable pattern: rest/tag Beacon → explore one branch → bring home Gloom/Ova/Tarstones/materials → spend on a single meaningful upgrade → return to hub → repeat a new branch.

Combat fundamentals you must understand before exploring deeply
Use these as a short training checklist — practice each in sequence.

1. Pick and learn one defensive Seal, then stick to it for a while
   1. Untarnished Seal (block/guard), Vatra’s Seal (Harden), Infinite Seal (parry), and Slayer Seal (difficulty modifier) are all valid. Learn one timing system first instead of switching between different defenses under pressure (/mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/).
   2. Note: equipping Slayer Seal disables achievements on that save (launch coverage) and the Beacon-menu Slayer Seal UI lock was fixed in Hotfix 2.0.
2. Master Break → riposte
   1. Fill an enemy’s Break meter (attacks + defensive mechanic contributions) and then take the critical/riposte opening rather than pressing further.
   2. Practice on single enemies before crowds.
3. Understand Shell separation and death penalties
   1. If a Shell’s health is depleted, you separate to the Harbinger — you can re-enter by damaging the Shell meter back. Dying as Harbinger sends you to the last tagged Beacon and creates a Gloom recovery run (see local Shell and Beacon research).
4. Learn Resolve, sidearms, and melee interplay
   1. Sidearm use and some actions consume/scale with Resolve; melee builds fill and spend Resolve differently. Don’t assume Resolve is an infinite ammo pool.
5. Basic exploration combat habits
   1. Pull enemies into clear space, test new timings on one enemy, use the Beacon before long detours, and don’t carry large unspent Gloom into an unmarked high-risk area.

What to activate, spend, or carry back to Marrow Keep (priorities)
Follow these numbered guidelines when deciding what to keep or spend on a first loop.

1. Activate/rest at Beacons — they’re your checkpoint and travel anchors (see /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).
2. Tarforge: inspect before spending materials — Tarforge smelting costs were reduced by 75% in Balance Patch 1; check the Tarforge UI in Marrow Keep (/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/).
3. Gloom: spend a modest amount at the hub rather than hoarding everything. Balance Patch 1 changed Shell-map costs to Gloom (previous Glimpse refunds were issued).
4. Glimpse: reserve for one or two Shell Bonds after you’ve tested playstyles (Shell bonding uses Glimpses; after Patch 1 the Shell-map reveal cost now uses Gloom in many cases — GamesRadar/local Shell research).
5. Ova/Ovum: return meaningful Ova to the Marrow Keep Gloom Siphon when prompted — do not rely on a single claimed threshold; sources conflict (see /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ and Beacon research).
6. Tarstones and Tarforge materials: pick up relevant Tarstones and keep them until you’ve read their slot/compatibility; Patch 1 changed several Tarstones and made some stronger.
7. Healing: invest in a healing upgrade (Revered Glands, Synaptic Vessels, Mether’s Pulse improvements) if survivability is your limiting factor.

Early Shells, weapons, and healing upgrades that make sense as optional first targets
Do not treat these as mandatory — they are practical, route-friendly targets.

- Shells
  - Tiel — early, near Widow’s Overlook; suits evasive/mobility play. See /mortal-shell-ii/tiel/.
  - Proxima — reachable via Blackridge Pass / Shattered Beacon routes for utility options. See /mortal-shell-ii/proxima/.
  - Harros — Prologue-only Shell; not part of the permanent roster.
- Weapons / Sidearms
  - Prologue weapon — keep it to learn mechanics.
  - Axe & Dagger — accessible early in Fainweald (Chapel Key/Shrine route).
  - Great Martyr’s Blade — heavier option on the Widow’s Overlook route.
  - Troubadour’s Lute — sidearm with crowd/status utility (damage buffed in Patch 1).
  - Axatana — long Mammon detour; optional and higher risk.
  - See /mortal-shell-ii/weapons/ and region-specific pages for pickup routes.
- Healing / support
  - Revered Glands — increase Mether’s Pulse charges.
  - Synaptic Vessels — increase Mether’s Pulse amount; prioritize if raw healing amount is failing you.
  - Spend on one concrete healing improvement early rather than small purchases across many categories.

Checklist before skipping the Prologue, equipping Slayer Seal, or leaving a vendor/region
Follow this short numbered checklist before taking those potentially irreversible actions.

1. Before skipping the Prologue:
   1. Confirm you don’t need the two Prologue achievements/trophies (local skip guide and /mortal-shell-ii/trophies/). The skip is allowed for some Open Beta carryovers, but playing the Prologue once is the safer first-playthrough recommendation.
2. Before equipping the Slayer Seal:
   1. Know that equipping it acts as a difficulty modifier and will disable achievements on that save (launch coverage). The Beacon-menu Slayer Seal UI lock was fixed in Hotfix 2.0; but do not equip it casually.
3. Before leaving a vendor/region for good:
   1. Buy time-sensitive items if a vendor quest/event might remove them later (GameSpot reports vendor removal can happen). The exact vendor-death schedule is not universally documented; use caution.

Patch-sensitive and uncertain launch-build details to watch
Preserve these as living caveats — they can change with patches.

1. Balance Patch 1 (Aug 20, 2026) — confirmed changes:
   - Shell-map costs now use Gloom; earlier Glimpse spending was refunded.
   - Gold (coin) drops increased (Mammon drops doubled).
   - Tarforge smelting is much cheaper (75% less Gloom).
   - Several weapon and Tarstone values changed (Great Martyr’s Blade +20% damage; Troubadour’s Lute +100% damage; tracking and guard changes).
   - These changes materially affect early spending priorities compared with Open Beta-era guides.
2. Hotfix 2.0 — reliability fixes only:
   - Stability/performance fixes, Beacon-menu Slayer Seal UI lock fix, and a fix for stealing enemy weapons during ripostes.
3. Ova thresholds and Beacon counts:
   - Official and independent sources disagree on exact Ova numbers needed to unlock Mether’s Breath. Follow the in-game unlock prompt and check /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/ for the live Beacon checklist.
4. Tarstone/weapon balance and compatibility:
   - Patch 1 buffed or changed several Tarstones and weapon behaviors; don’t rely on beta-era lists or “universal” Tarstone tiers.
5. Vendor timing and event-dependent stock:
   - Some vendor items are reportedly time-sensitive or gated behind events; the universal schedule isn’t confirmed here.

Common mistakes new players make
1. Skipping the Prologue as a default — you may miss mechanics practice and two achievements/trophies. See /mortal-shell-ii/skip-prologue/.
2. Equipping the Slayer Seal immediately — it disables achievements for that save and is a deliberate difficulty modifier.
3. Confusing “tag/activate” with “cleanse” for Beacons — activating grants a travel point; cleansing completes the associated dungeon/route and yields progression rewards (see /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).
4. Hoarding all Gloom/Glimpse without spending anything — early focused spending (one weapon or one healing upgrade or one Shell Bond) is often more productive than hoarding.
5. Carrying a large unspent Gloom balance into a risky, unmarked gate — you can lose Gloom on death; only carry as much as you’re willing to risk on a recovery run.
6. Relying on a single written Ova number — sources disagree; follow the in-game prompt rather than a fixed external number.
7. Treating beta-era routes and priorities as current — Balance Patch 1 changed many early economics and some combat values.

What happens next (short expected progression after the first loop)
1. Establish the loop: repeat leaving and returning to Marrow Keep while expanding the number of Beacons you can reliably clear and the Shells/weapons you can use comfortably.
2. Unlock Mether’s Breath when the game prompts you to do so after returning sufficient Ova; use it to open faster corruption clearing and travel once available (do not hard-code a numerical threshold — sources disagree).
3. Use the Tarforge at Marrow Keep to convert materials into weapon upgrades and Tarstones once you understand compatibility (Tarforge costs were reduced in Patch 1).
4. After you have a comfortable Shell/weapon and a healing upgrade, tackle a corrupt dungeon or a Mammon route as a milestone before moving into more difficult regions.
5. If you want higher challenge, consult /mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/ — but first learn the standard defensive mechanics and Break timing.
