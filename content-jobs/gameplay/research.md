## First-hours route supported by the evidence

This is a recommended route spine, not a mandatory speedrun order:

### 1. Finish the Prologue deliberately on a first completion run

- The Prologue teaches the game's opening combat loop and leads to Marrow Keep.
- If the player is eligible for the launch Prologue skip from Open Beta progress, the skip is an option rather than a requirement.
- The local skip guide identifies two Prologue-bound achievement/trophy opportunities: **Mid Summer?** and **No, You Still Can't Win**. The safest first-playthrough advice is to play the Prologue once before using the skip.
- The exact skipped spawn/UI and whether every opening collectible is bypassed are not fully documented by official sources; do not state an exhaustive missable list.

### 2. Settle Marrow Keep before travelling

Marrow Keep is the service hub. On the first visit, the player should:

- activate/rest at the Beacon;
- inspect the Tarforge and its currently available functions;
- speak with the Shellkeeper and hub vendors;
- understand that Shell Bonding uses Glimpses while launch Patch 1 uses Gloom for Shell-map reveals;
- improve the current weapon or healing setup when the required materials are available;
- use the upper travel mechanism after the hub sequence opens the first region.

Do not prescribe a fixed NPC dialogue order beyond what the current UI requires. The sources agree on the hub role, not every conversation trigger.

### 3. Enter Fainweald through the clear route

The opening route from the upper Keep points toward **Fainweald**. The route toward the **Ruins of Mammon** is blocked by corrupted growths early on and is also a poor first destination for an unprepared character. The first practical Fainweald anchor is **Widow's Overlook**.

At Widow's Overlook:

- activate/rest at the Beacon;
- collect the nearby Glimpse if it is still available;
- use the area as the anchor for the early Tiel and Great Arbiter/Martyr's Tomb routes;
- collect a map from a nearby Ruk when encountered rather than treating every unexplored path as a required detour.

### 4. Take one early Shell and one stable weapon path

The clearest early Shell targets are:

- **Tiel**, near Widow's Overlook, for a mobile/evasive playstyle. The local route uses the torch-marked grave and Rash Memory landmarks.
- **Proxima**, after the Prologue through Blackridge Pass and the Shattered Beacon, for an early utility option. The local route records the Unknown Shell memory sequence.

Harros is the Prologue Shell and is not a permanent post-Prologue choice. The repository's current roster model is nine total Shells, eight permanent; GamesRadar's “eight Shells” wording counts the equipable permanent roster and separately names Harros.

For weapons, keep the advice route-based rather than tier-based:

- the Prologue weapon is a valid learning baseline;
- Axe & Dagger is an early Fainweald option via the Chapel Key/Shrine of Trials route;
- Great Martyr's Blade is a heavier Widow's Overlook/Holding Cells/Martyr's Prison route;
- Axatana is an optional, substantially riskier Mammon detour, not a literal starting reward;
- Troubadour's Lute is an early sidearm option with confusion utility, but status resistance and patch behavior still matter.

The complete weapon page currently identifies eight primary weapons while GameSpot counts 16 total melee weapons and sidearms. Those are different scopes and should not be collapsed into a single “16 weapons” primary roster claim.

### 5. Build a Beacon loop before a deep dungeon push

Every new Beacon should be activated/rested at even if the player is not ready to cleanse its dungeon. The sources and local Beacon research distinguish:

- **tagging/activating/resting:** creates a checkpoint and travel destination;
- **cleansing:** completing the attached route, claiming its Ovum/Ova reward, and potentially opening nearby corruption-gated content.

Resting restores Health and Resolve but respawns ordinary enemies. The exact travel menu and cleanse rewards can vary by Beacon. Use the [Beacons + Fast Travel] page for the full checklist rather than duplicating a 49-name list here.

### 6. Open corruption clearing and direct travel when the game says it is ready

The early Fainweald loop commonly continues through Blackridge Pass, Mushroom Village, and Mushroom Village Gate. GameSpot reports that cleansing the Mushroom Village Gate yields at least five extra Ova in that route and enables the return to Marrow Keep's Ova chamber for Mether's Breath.

The repository's Beacon research records conflicting launch reports: four returned Ova, five additional Ova, roughly seven to eight total including starting stock, and other lower totals. Therefore the page should say:

- collect and return meaningful Ova progress to the Marrow Keep Gloom Siphon/Ova Nursery;
- follow the live in-game unlock prompt for Mether's Breath;
- do not hard-code one universal threshold;
- use Mether's Breath for corruption clearing and faster travel after it unlocks.

### 7. Use the first ten hours to establish a repeatable loop

A defensible first-hours loop is:

1. Rest/tag the nearest Beacon.
2. Explore one branch and mark the map/Ruk landmarks.
3. Bring home Gloom, Glimpses, Ova, Tarstones, weapon materials, and healing upgrades.
4. Spend on one weapon, one Shell Bond, or a clear healing improvement instead of scattering every resource.
5. Return to the hub after a major dungeon, new Shell, or difficult route.
6. Re-enter a Corrupted Gate only after the player can consistently handle its local enemy patterns.

The “ten hours” label is editorial convenience. No checked source gives a universal first-ten-hours completion time or proves that a particular boss/dungeon order is mandatory.

## Combat fundamentals to teach

### Defensive Seal choice

The current launch guide sources identify four relevant Seal patterns:

- **Untarnished Seal:** block/guard; a well-timed perfect guard can contribute Break damage.
- **Vatra's Seal:** Harden; the next incoming hit is nullified, with perfect timing contributing Break damage.
- **Infinite Seal:** parry; higher-risk timing for stronger Break pressure.
- **Slayer Seal:** optional accessibility/difficulty modifier with stronger stagger/riposte tools; equipping it disables achievements on that save according to the current launch coverage.

The page should tell new players to learn one defense first, watch attack tells, and avoid alternating unfamiliar timing systems under pressure. It should not claim a universal best Seal.

### Break, Riposte, Resolve, and Shell separation

- Fill an enemy's Break meter with attacks and the equipped defensive mechanic.
- When the enemy is broken, take the critical/riposte opening rather than continuing to mash.
- Melee play and sidearms interact with Resolve; GameSpot's launch guide describes sidearm use as Resolve-dependent and melee as a way to fill it. Do not treat Resolve as a passive infinite ammunition pool.
- If the Shell's health is depleted, the Harbinger can be separated from it. The player can attack to refill the Shell meter and re-enter at low health; dying as the Harbinger sends the player to the last tagged Beacon and creates a Gloom recovery run.
- Combat is presented officially as stamina-unrestricted, but that does not remove enemy commitment, recovery, Break, or positioning risks. Avoid promising that stamina-free combat makes aggression safe.

### Exploration habits that reduce deaths

- Pull enemies into clear space before committing to a group.
- Test a new attack/defense timing on one enemy before entering a crowd.
- Shoot bells, ropes, and suspicious targets; inspect glyph-marked or illusory walls when a route seems to dead-end.
- Use the Beacon before a long optional detour.
- Treat a boss arena, corrupted dungeon, or Mammon route as a decision point; do not carry a large unspent Gloom balance into an unmarked risk unless the player accepts the corpse run.

## Pitfalls, missables, and uncertainty to preserve

1. **Do not skip the Prologue by default.** Eligible beta players can use the full-game skip, but the local launch guide identifies two Prologue-bound achievement/trophy opportunities and does not claim every opening collectible is recoverable later.
2. **Do not equip the Slayer Seal casually.** Picking it up and equipping it are separate actions; the current launch coverage associates the achievement lock with equipping/switching. Link the dedicated difficulty page.
3. **Do not confuse a tagged Beacon with a cleansed Beacon.** The first is enough for a checkpoint/travel destination; the second is a completed route and resource/progression action.
4. **Do not hard-code the Mether's Breath Ova threshold.** Current launch sources disagree on whether the relevant count is four returned, five additional, or a total including starting stock. Defer to the in-game prompt.
5. **Do not treat open-beta routes as current automatically.** Balance Patch 1 altered Shell-map costs, economy, combat values, weapon damage/tracking, Guard behavior, and Tarstones. The launch Axe & Dagger route also differs from some beta-era location claims on the dedicated local page.
6. **Buy from time-sensitive vendors before leaving an NPC quest branch.** GameSpot reports that quests/events can remove vendors; the exact universal vendor-death schedule is not established here.
7. **Night Mode is optional.** The Gloombound Flame → Marrow Keep lantern → Thestus chain is a harder world state, not the normal first-hours route. The dedicated guide should own the exact activation sequence.
8. **No universal best Shell, weapon, or first boss order is confirmed.** Tiel/Eredrim and specific item recommendations are playstyle or outlet opinions; the page should use route and risk language.
9. **Do not promise a literal ten-hour endpoint.** The first-hours label is a reader-friendly planning frame, not a sourced completion time.

## Patch/version notes

### Balance Patch 1 — official Steam developer post, August 20, 2026

Relevant to this guide:

- Shell-map locations now cost Gloom; prior Glimpse spending was refunded in the Shellkeeper's alcove in Blackmarrow.
- Gold drops increased, with Mammon drops specifically doubled.
- Tarforge weapon smelting costs 75% less Gloom.
- Selected enemies/bosses were rebalanced; Great Martyr's Blade damage rose 20%, Troubadour's Lute damage rose 100%, and Caged Hystrix/Triarch Repeater lost minimum Resolve-to-fire requirements.
- Axatana, Black Needle, and Clockwork Scythe light-attack tracking improved.
- Guard can be used while walking; several Tarstones were buffed.

These changes are enough to invalidate beta-era resource priorities, but they do not document a changed first-region route, Beacon total, or Mether's Breath threshold.

### Hotfix 2.0 — official Steam developer post

The hotfix addresses stability/performance, the Beacon-menu Slayer Seal UI lock, and enemy-weapon stealing during ripostes. It is a reliability note, not evidence for a new first-hours progression rule.
