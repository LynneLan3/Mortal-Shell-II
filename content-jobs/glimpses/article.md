Short answer — what they are and how to treat them:
- Gloom = the Harbinger-leveling resource you spend at Beacons. Treat it like your level-up bank and a death-recovery risk. (GamesRadar; Balance Patch 1 post.)
- Glimpses = scarce Shell-specific Bond currency used at the Shellkeeper/Bonding interaction to open higher Shell ability tiers and memories. Not the same as Gloom and not a confirmed infinite farm. (GamesRadar; AllThingsHow.)
- Shell Points = ability/perk points awarded at some Harbinger level milestones; they buy abilities inside Bond-opened rows and, per current guides, can be refunded. (NerdsChalk; AllThingsHow.)
- Resolve = the active combat meter you build during fights and spend on weapon/sidearm/Shell actions; exact per-action rules are unresolved. (GamesRadar; ShowU.)

Gloom — how to use it, safely
- What it is: the resource you earn from enemies, bosses, and some world sources and spend at Beacons to level the Harbinger. (GamesRadar)
- Key safety note: carried Gloom is dropped on death and can be recovered at the drop point unless you die again before recovery. Treat unspent Gloom as a risk. (GamesRadar)
- Patch change to know: Balance Patch 1 moved Shell-map reveals to cost Gloom instead of Glimpses; the developer post says refunded map Glimpses are claimable in the Shell Keeper’s alcove in Blackmarrow and that the patch was live on PC with console rollouts following. Don’t assume every platform has the same rollout timing. (Cold Symmetry patch note; GamesRadar)
- Practical steps
  1. At a Beacon, open the Beacon/level menu to view the next Gloom cost and the reward preview. [IMAGE: Beacon level-up menu]
  2. Spend only the amount you intend to lock in—use the preview to confirm the stat changes (do not rely on memorized cost numbers; patch prices change).
  3. If you die, go back to the death site to recover dropped Gloom before attempting risky runs that could cause a second death and permanent loss.
- Additional pointers: respawning enemies and world routes can be used for Gloom farming (see the dedicated gloom-farm guide for repeatable routes and returns). Do not mix Gloom with Glimpses — their uses are distinct. (GamesRadar; local gloom-farm handoff)

Glimpses — how to get and spend them
- What they are: a Shell-specific progression resource used only at the Shellkeeper/Bonding interaction to deepen a chosen Shell’s Bond, opening additional ability rows and exposing memories. They are scarce and persistent when spent. (AllThingsHow; GamesRadar)
- Common sources reported by current guides: Cleansed Beacons, exploration past Cleansed Gates, occasional rare world finds, and a limited Merrick purchase. Coverage lists these as sources but does not show a universal infinite farm. (GamesRadar)
- How to spend them
  1. Travel to the Shellkeeper/Bonding interaction for the Shell you want to advance. [IMAGE: Shell bonding menu]
  2. Choose the Shell and spend Glimpses to increase its Bond level; each Bond step can unlock a new row/tier of abilities or memories.
  3. Confirm before spending — current coverage treats Glimpses spent on Bond as persistent decisions (except for the narrow Patch 1 refund case noted below).
- Important limits and patch note:
  - Glimpses do not appear to drop on death, but they are not freely refundable in the general case.
  - Balance Patch 1 refunded Glimpses specifically spent on Shell-map reveals; that refund is limited to that use and does not imply Bond spending is refundable. The patch refund location is the Blackmarrow Shell Keeper’s alcove per the developer post. (Cold Symmetry)
- Numbers and scarcity: guide estimates conflict (examples: Neonsect suggests 27 per Shell / 216 total; GamesRadar reports “more than 20 per Shell” and a playthrough total just over 90). These are guide calculations, not developer-confirmed totals — do not treat them as fixed. Preserve the scarcity: prioritize bonding the Shell you intend to use. (Neonsect; GamesRadar)

Shell Points — how they work and how to spend them
- What they are: milestone rewards tied to Harbinger levels (earned via Gloom-funded leveling). They are spent on individual abilities within rows that a Shell’s Bond has already unlocked. (NerdsChalk; AllThingsHow)
- How to acquire and use them
  1. Level the Harbinger at Beacons (spend Gloom) and watch for Harbinger level milestones that grant Shell Points.
  2. Open the Shell’s ability tree for the Shell you intend to use; confirm the relevant Bond tier is unlocked before attempting to spend a Shell Point on that row.
  3. Spend Shell Points on individual abilities. Current guides report an in-game refund option for reassigning Shell Points; use the UI prompt to refund/redistribute as needed. (NerdsChalk)
- Pool/accounting note: guides differ on whether Shell Points draw from a single shared pool or are tracked per Shell. The reliable player action is to inspect the current UI for the chosen Shell’s unlocked rows and remaining capacity, and confirm prompts before spending. (NerdsChalk; other guides)
- Distinction to keep in mind: Shell Points are NOT the same as Glimpses — Glimpses unlock rows; Shell Points buy the individual abilities inside those rows.

Resolve — how to build and spend it in combat
- What it is: an active combat meter on the HUD that you build through normal combat and spend to trigger weapon skills, sidearm uses, Shell effects, and other combat actions. It is not a world pickup or vendor currency. (GamesRadar; ShowU)
- How to use it safely
  1. Watch the Resolve meter on your combat HUD during exchanges. [IMAGE: Combat HUD showing Resolve meter]
  2. Build Resolve by engaging in combat — GamesRadar’s review describes successful melee strikes as a refill loop; other current guides report hits, kills, and successful Guard/Parry/Harden as gains, but those defensive triggers and exact per-action values are not fully corroborated and should be treated as unresolved. (GamesRadar; ShowU)
  3. Spend Resolve on the special attack, sidearm, or Shell action the UI indicates when enough Resolve is available.
- Caution: do not assume exact per-hit values, that every defensive action fills the meter, or that original Mortal Shell parry/Harden rules transfer unchanged. The safest policy is to observe the meter in your current build and test before relying on a fixed technique. (ShowU)

Practical decision model (quick reference)
- If you want to level: prioritize Gloom, spend at Beacons, avoid risky unspent balances.
- If you want to open Shell progression rows: spend Glimpses at the Shellkeeper/Bonding interaction for the Shell you will use.
- If you want to buy a Shell ability: use Shell Points earned from Harbinger milestones and confirm the Bond row is unlocked first.
- If you want to fire a special attack or sidearm effect: build and spend Resolve in combat; watch the meter.

Common mistakes
1. Treating Glimpses as a general currency you can farm endlessly — current coverage calls them scarce and reports no confirmed universal infinite farm. (GamesRadar)
2. Confusing Gloom and Glimpses — Gloom levels the Harbinger at Beacons and can be dropped on death; Glimpses are used only for Shell Bond progression. (GamesRadar; AllThingsHow)
3. Assuming Shell Points bypass Bond gates — a Bond tier must be open before its ability row can be spent into. (AllThingsHow; NerdsChalk)
4. Expecting precise Resolve rules from earlier Mortal Shell titles — current evidence warns against importing old parry/resolve rules; test the meter in your build. (ShowU)
5. Relying on guide totals or fixed prices — numerical totals and price tables vary between guides and are patch-sensitive; use the in-game preview or the dedicated gloom-farm page for current numbers. (Neonsect; local gloom-farm handoff)

What happens next (player-facing outcomes)
1. Spending Gloom at a Beacon immediately increases Harbinger level/stats as shown in the Beacon menu; unspent Gloom remains at risk until banked. (GamesRadar)
2. Spending Glimpses at the Shellkeeper permanently deepens that Shell’s Bond (except for the narrow Shell-map refund case created by Patch 1); this opens new rows in that Shell’s ability tree. (AllThingsHow; Cold Symmetry patch note)
3. Spending Shell Points unlocks abilities within an already-open row; current guides report you can refund redistributed Shell Points via the UI prompts if you change your mind. (NerdsChalk)
4. Building Resolve in combat immediately enables the Resolve-costed actions the UI offers; how much per hit or per defensive action you gain should be verified in your current build rather than assumed. (GamesRadar; ShowU)

Conflicts and omissions to keep in mind
- Glimpse totals: guide estimates conflict (example figures: 27 per Shell / 216 total vs. a one-playthrough total just over 90). These are not developer-published totals. (Neonsect; GamesRadar)
- Shell Point pool: sources disagree on shared vs. Shell-specific accounting; inspect the UI in-game. (NerdsChalk vs. other summaries)
- Resolve refill triggers: successful melee strikes are supported in review coverage, but defensive-action triggers and per-action values are not fully corroborated — do not publish fixed refill tables. (GamesRadar; ShowU)
- Patch rollout: Balance Patch 1 details were published by the developer and were live on PC at check time, with consoles following; do not assume identical platform timing. (Cold Symmetry)
