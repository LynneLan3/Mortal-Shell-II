## Patch: Night Mode activation & Gloombound Flame (compact)

SEO title: Mortal Shell 2 Gloombound Flame — Fainweald & Night Mode

Meta description: Find the Flame in Fainweald's Great Arbiter arena, light Marrow Keep's LIGHT EXTINGUISHED LANTERN, summon Thestus, and enable Night Mode.

---

### How to Unlock Night Mode

1. Where the Flame is
   - The Gloombound Flame sits in Fainweald inside the Great Arbiter of Flesh arena, beside a ritual stone / stone marker near the Widow's Overlook Beacon. [IMAGE: Great Arbiter arena marker area]

2. What the LIGHT EXTINGUISHED LANTERN is
   - At the top of Marrow Keep you'll find an interactable prompt labeled exactly LIGHT EXTINGUISHED LANTERN. It must be lit with the Gloombound Flame — interacting alone without the Flame will not summon Thestus.
   [IMAGE: Marrow Keep lantern spot]

3. How to find Thestus
   - After you light the LIGHT EXTINGUISHED LANTERN with the Gloombound Flame, Thestus appears at the top of Marrow Keep. He is not present until the lantern has been lit.

4. Choosing CALL FORTH THE NIGHT
   - Talk to Thestus and select CALL FORTH THE NIGHT. The prompt reads "Enable increased difficulty." That triggers Night Mode.

Notes:
- The activation chain must be: pick up Gloombound Flame → use Flame to light LIGHT EXTINGUISHED LANTERN → Thestus appears → choose CALL FORTH THE NIGHT.
- Launch gameplay footage shows the Flame can be picked up while the Great Arbiter is still alive; this is gameplay-observed/video-supported, not developer-confirmed.

---

### What Changes in Night Mode

Confirmed / source-supported changes

- Night Mode increases overall difficulty and introduces extra encounters and hazards.
- Night Mode can yield more rewards / Gloom and adds night-only content such as a night-only merchant and night-related quest/dungeon content (for example, the Shrine of Sorrows has Night Mode-only access). These effects and reward increases are supported by launch-era coverage; exact multipliers or currency figures are not published here.
- Seedbearer's Scripture is a separate Night Mode-only objective that requires Depleted, Thawed, and Faded Glands at the Forgotten Tower (see the existing Seedbearer's Scripture page for details).

Community-reported discoveries (anecdotal)

- Players have reported additional dungeons, new vendors, extra Glimpses near Shell locations, and generally tougher encounters with altered enemy behavior and rewards. Treat these as community reports rather than confirmed mechanics.

Evidence and continued development

- Official Balance Patch 1 (developer post) says Night Mode will be extended in future updates but does not change the activation chain: https://steamcommunity.com/app/2584270/discussions/0/582805931178489108/
- Community reports (examples): https://www.reddit.com/r/MortalShell/comments/1vwwutf/just_beat_the_game_for_the_first_time_have_some/ and https://www.reddit.com/r/MortalShell/comments/1vwwu9t/how_much_better_is_the_loot_in_night_mode/

### How to Return to Day Mode

- Night Mode is reversible via Thestus. The option to revert (often described as "Seek Daylight" in secondary launch coverage) is supported by secondary sources but was not locally video-verified for this page; label as secondary-source supported.

---

### Common mistakes

- Trying to summon Thestus by interacting with the lantern without using the Gloombound Flame — the Flame must be used on the LIGHT EXTINGUISHED LANTERN first.
- Assuming exact reward multipliers or fixed Gloom numbers — community reports vary and exact figures were not published in the official patch notes.
