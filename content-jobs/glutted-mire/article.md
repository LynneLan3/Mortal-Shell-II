Short answer
Start at Mushroom Village Gate Beacon (clear the Hall of Illusions first — see /mortal-shell-ii/hall-of-illusions/). Go through the Corrupted Gate into the Glutted Mire, cleanse the Mire Beacons on the way, complete the shepherd encounter by hitting the red-glowing sheep to fill its Break meter, collect the Etching Needles from the post‑shepherd chest (return them to Franz at the Marrow Keep Tarforge to unlock Temper Tarstones), defeat Magdalena, then activate Magdalena's Glade Beacon and collect the Ova before returning to Marrow Keep.

Step-by-step route (do these in order)
1. Prerequisite — Hall of Illusions
   1.1. Start at Mushroom Village Gate Beacon and clear the Hall of Illusions dungeon first. Sources agree clearing that dungeon opens the Corrupted Gate into Glutted Mire (see /mortal-shell-ii/hall-of-illusions/).  
   [IMAGE: Mushroom Village Gate Beacon entrance]

2. Enter the Glutted Mire and watch for early traps
   2.1. Enter the Mire through the Corrupted Gate. Several guides report tripwire/arrow traps shortly after entry — move slowly and look for tripwires. (Into Indie Games)  
   [IMAGE: tripwire arrow trap area]

3. Activate and cleanse the first Mire Beacon
   3.1. Proceed to the first Mire Beacon (listed in some guides as Seeping Mire; one walkthrough calls it Sleeping Mire). Use the Beacon’s in‑game label. (Beacon lists: Seeping Mire / one walkthrough)  
   [IMAGE: Seeping/“Seeping Mire” Beacon location sign]

4. Move through the Sunken Village section
   4.1. Continue a poison-heavy route toward Sunken Village. Inspect the three-candle offering chest reported by one source (contains a Siegebreaker’s Stone Tarstone per that walkthrough). Activate and cleanse the Sunken Village Beacon when you reach it. (Into Indie Games; Neoseeker; AllThingsHow Beacon lists)  
   [IMAGE: Sunken Village offering chest]

5. Cross to the shepherd encounter
   5.1. Cross the bridge toward the shepherd arena. The fight is commonly called either Tarblighted Shepherd or The Wandering Shepherd; sources differ on the name. The shared, actionable mechanic is: several sheep become hostile/black and some will glow red — hit the red-glowing sheep to fill the shepherd’s Break meter, then use Riposte when the Break meter is the objective. Directly attacking the shepherd is reported to cause teleport/transform behavior, so focus on the red-glowing sheep where possible. (Into Indie Games; AllThingsHow)  
   [IMAGE: shepherd encounter red-glowing sheep]

6. Reach the Etching Needles chest (post-shepherd)
   6.1. After the shepherd arena, use the gap in the boards/crates and follow the wooden walkway/long bridge. Look for Ruk on an arch as the landmark indicating the pre‑Magdalena section. Sources disagree on whether the chest sits at the far end of the bridge or below/aside the obvious route; inspect the side alcove/drop below the path before committing to the boss drop. The chest here contains the Etching Needles. (AllThingsHow; Games.GG; 2UpSkill)  
   6.2. Return the Etching Needles to Franz at the Marrow Keep Tarforge to unlock Temper Tarstones. See /mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/ and /mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/.  
   [IMAGE: Ruk arch landmark and chest approach]

7. Magdalena, Lady of the Woods — arena and fight
   7.1. From the Etching Needles chest area, reach the ledge beyond the chest and drop into Magdalena’s flooded arena. Magdalena is at the end of this Mire route. (AllThingsHow; Into Indie Games)  
   7.2. Conservative fight tips reported by sources: dodge the charge and spinning fire attack, expect fire attacks and summoned small enemies, and use Riposte when the Break meter fills. One source also reports a vehicle-like charge and a stationary/malfunctioning vehicle period around half health. After the kill, a Beacon (Magdalena’s Glade) spawns; cleanse it and collect the Ova. (AllThingsHow; Into Indie Games; Neoseeker)  
   [IMAGE: Magdalena flooded arena]

Common mistakes
- Attacking the shepherd directly: multiple sources warn that directly attacking the shepherd can cause teleport/transform behavior. Instead, focus on the red-glowing sheep to fill the Break meter. (Into Indie Games; AllThingsHow)  
- Missing the Etching Needles chest: sources disagree on the chest’s exact placement (bridge end vs. below/side alcove). If you follow the bridge past Ruk, check the side/below route before dropping into Magdalena. (AllThingsHow; Games.GG; 2UpSkill)  
- Running past tripwires: early tripwire/arrow traps are repeatedly reported — proceed cautiously on first entry. (Into Indie Games)  
- Assuming Beacon names or rewards are identical across guides: follow the Beacon’s in‑game label and do not rely on one walkthrough’s naming. See /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/ for Beacon behavior.

Uncertainties and source notes (preserve conflicting reports)
- Seeping Mire vs Sleeping Mire: the local Beacon list and most guides call the first Mire Beacon Seeping Mire; one walkthrough calls it Sleeping Mire. Follow the in‑game Beacon label. (local Beacon guide; one walkthrough)  
- Shepherd name: sources use both Tarblighted Shepherd and The Wandering Shepherd for the same encounter. The confirmed shared mechanic is the red‑glowing sheep/Break meter approach; avoid treating a single name as definitive. (AllThingsHow; Into Indie Games; local legacy notes)  
- Etching Needles chest approach: sources disagree whether the chest is at the far end of the bridge or below/nearby the obvious route. Use Ruk, the board gap, and the bridge as landmarks and inspect side alcoves. (AllThingsHow; Games.GG; 2UpSkill)  
- Magdalena page Open Beta scope: an existing magdalena.md is labeled Open Beta-scoped. That legacy page should be treated as related coverage, not definitive launch-era confirmation. See /mortal-shell-ii/magdalena/ for the legacy page and its scope.  
- Rewards and counts: Etching Needles and the named Tarstones (e.g., Siegebreaker’s Stone per one source) are supported by sources; exact Ova counts and full unverified loot totals are omitted because sources differ or do not specify. (Into Indie Games; AllThingsHow)
