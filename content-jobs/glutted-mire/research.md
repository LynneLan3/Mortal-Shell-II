# Item 38 research: Glutted Mire Walkthrough

Research date: 2026-08-24
Scope: Item 38 only — `mortal-shell-ii/glutted-mire`
Status: evidence-bounded draft research; no writer CLI or provider call used

## Answer-first route

The supported route begins at **Mushroom Village Gate Beacon** in eastern/southeastern Fainweald. Clear the Beacon's **Hall of Illusions** dungeon first. Completing that dungeon opens the route through the Corrupted Gate into **Glutted Mire**. The practical area sequence is:

1. Enter the Mire and avoid the early tripwire/arrow traps.
2. Activate and cleanse the first Mire Beacon. The local site calls it **Seeping Mire**; one walkthrough calls it **Sleeping Mire**.
3. Continue through the poisonous village route to **Sunken Village Beacon**. Check the nearby offering chest and the lower route before moving on.
4. Cross the bridge toward the shepherd encounter. Defeat the shepherd by attacking the sheep that glow red when the Break meter is the relevant objective; directly attacking the shepherd is reported to cause teleport/sheep-transformation behavior.
5. Use the gap in the boards/crates after the shepherd arena, follow the wooden walkway/bridge, and use **Ruk** as the landmark for the pre-Magdalena section.
6. Collect the **Etching Needles** from the chest in this section before dropping into **Magdalena, Lady of the Woods**.
7. Reach the ledge beyond the chest and drop into Magdalena's flooded arena. After the fight, activate and cleanse **Magdalena's Glade Beacon**, collect the Ova, and return to Marrow Keep for the area's progression handoff.

No map coordinates should be published. The sources use different map presentations and do not provide a shared coordinate system.

## Source-backed facts

### Entry and Hall of Illusions

- The local `hall-of-illusions.md` identifies Mushroom Village Gate Beacon as the entry anchor and says clearing Hall of Illusions opens the route toward Glutted Mire.
- AllThingsHow describes the same Beacon as the entrance to Hall of Illusions and says the route opens after defeating Vrannic and cleansing the Beacon/corrupted gate.
- Into Indie Games places the Glutted Mire gate beyond Mushroom Village Gate and says the Hall of Illusions must be cleared first.

### Mire route and Beacons

- Into Indie Games reports tripwire arrow traps shortly after entering, then a poison-heavy route to Sunken Village.
- The same walkthrough describes a three-candle offering chest in the village with a source-reported **Siegebreaker's Stone** Tarstone, then a first Mire Beacon and a bridge onward to Sunken Village Beacon.
- The local Beacon guide lists the Glutted Mire dungeon-region Beacons as **Seeping Mire**, **Sunken Village**, and **Magdalena's Glade**.
- Gamer Guides' Beacon checklist also lists Seeping Mire, Sunken Village, and Magdalena's Glade, and says Magdalena's Glade unlocks after defeating Magdalena.
- 100% Guides confirms that Glutted Mire has three Beacons on the main route through the eastern gate from Mushroom Village, but does not name all three in the text extract.

### Shepherd encounter

- Into Indie Games calls the encounter **The Wandering Shepherd**. It says several sheep become black/hostile and the player should hit the red-glowing sheep to fill the Shepherd's Break meter before the riposte.
- AllThingsHow and the local Tarstones/Tarforge pages call the same pre-Magdalena encounter **Tarblighted Shepherd** and describe sheep that transform into demons.
- Because the name is not reconciled across sources, the article should use **the shepherd encounter** in the answer-first route and explain both labels in an uncertainty note. The red-glowing-sheep mechanic is the usable common instruction.

### Etching Needles and the chest approach

- AllThingsHow places the Etching Needles in an unguarded chest after the shepherd and before Magdalena. Its route uses the board gap, a second wooden bridge, and Ruk on an arch as landmarks.
- Games.GG says to break the crate near the shepherd, squeeze through, cross the long bridge, and open the chest at the far end.
- 2UpSkill describes the chest as below the obvious route near Ruk, with a drop and a side alcove.
- The safe article instruction is therefore: after the shepherd, pass the board gap, follow the bridge/walkways, use Ruk as the landmark, and inspect the side/below route before committing to Magdalena. Do not claim an exact chest coordinate or a single universally confirmed “right/left” turn.
- The local Tarstones guide says the Etching Needles unlock **Temper Tarstones** after returning them to Franz at the Marrow Keep Tarforge. The local Tarforge guide owns the component/function explanation.

### Magdalena and completion

- AllThingsHow places Magdalena at the end of the Mire after the Sunken Village, shepherd, and Etching Needles section. Its conservative fight advice is to dodge the charge and fire spin, take one or two hits during recovery, and use Riposte when the Break meter fills.
- Into Indie Games reports fire attacks, an unblockable vehicle charge, a spinning fire attack, a stationary/malfunctioning vehicle period around half health, summoned small enemies, the **Magdalena's Memento** Tarstone, and a Beacon spawning after the kill.
- Neoseeker's search result places Magdalena's Glade Beacon after the fight and says the cleansed Beacon provides Ova; exact Ova quantity is not needed for this guide.
- The local legacy `magdalena.md` is Open Beta-scoped and uses the Wandering Shepherd name. The new full-game route page should link to it only as existing related coverage, while clearly labeling the route page's launch-window evidence as needs-verification.

## Explicit uncertainty to retain in article

1. **Seeping vs Sleeping Mire:** the local guide and checklist sources use Seeping Mire; one walkthrough uses Sleeping Mire. Tell readers to follow the Beacon's in-game label.
2. **Tarblighted vs Wandering Shepherd:** sources use both names for the same route encounter. Explain the shared red-glowing-sheep Break mechanic without presenting one label as definitively official.
3. **Etching Needles chest approach:** sources differ between a chest past Ruk/at the end of the bridge and a chest below the obvious route. Use Ruk, the board gap, and the bridge as landmarks; do not invent coordinates.
4. **Legacy Open Beta wording:** the existing Magdalena page is explicitly Open Beta-scoped. Do not copy its beta-only completion claims into the launch walkthrough as settled full-game facts.
5. **Rewards/counts:** name Etching Needles and the source-reported Tarstones, but omit exact Ova quantities and any unverified complete loot count.
