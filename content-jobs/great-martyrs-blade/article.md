Where is the Great Martyr’s Blade and how do I reach it — short answer
- The Great Martyr’s Blade is a direct pickup in Martyr’s Prison: it appears in the gated treasure room that opens after you clear the final chamber. Follow the established Widow’s Overlook → Mushroom Village / Blackridge Hamlet → Holding Cells (upper route) → Gloomshade Grove → Martyr’s Prison route to reach it. Do not look for it as a boss drop or NPC reward.

Route — step-by-step (follow in order)
1. Finish the Prologue and reach the open-world at Widow’s Overlook.
2. From Widow’s Overlook, continue the supported route through Mushroom Village / Blackridge Hamlet toward the Holding Cells.
3. Enter the Holding Cells and take the upper-route shortcut that leads toward Martyr’s Prison (the guide’s existing Holding Cells upper-route directions remain the supported path).
4. Proceed through Gloomshade Grove and follow the path into Martyr’s Prison.
5. Clear the final chamber in Martyr’s Prison. After the area trigger, wait for the gated / treasure room to open.
6. Enter the gated treasure room and pick up the Great Martyr’s Blade (it is a straight pickup — not a drop or quest reward).

Patch change and what changed for the weapon
- Cold Symmetry’s official Balance Patch 1 notes list: Great Martyr’s Blade Attack Damage +20% (Cold Symmetry, Balance Patch 1 - Patch Notes, 2026-08-20). The patch note does not include a new moveset, scaling table, acquisition change, or a universal upgrade-level change.
- Practical consequence: the +20% attack-damage buff meaningfully increases the blade’s damage-per-opening and makes committing to its slower swings payoff more often. This changes the weapon’s practical value compared to older wording that emphasized it being “weak” or too risky. It does not automatically make it the best weapon in all builds or remove the route’s combat risks.

Practical player insights (how to use this post-patch)
- Expect better damage when you successfully land its slower hits: the weapon’s payoff for committing to openings is improved.
- The route still carries the same combat and gating risk as before; the patch did not change the pickup location or the route requirements.
- The Slayer Seal is an optional difficulty/build choice and is not required to obtain or use the Great Martyr’s Blade.
- One run of existing route research observed a +4 pickup on a single playthrough. Treat that as an isolated observation, not a guaranteed or universal state.

Conflicting or uncertain facts you should be aware of
- No official post-patch damage sheet, DPS comparison, or universal tier ranking for the blade was released with the patch notes.
- Community opinions about whether +20% is “enough” are not mechanical proof; they should not replace testing on your own build.
- Route timing around Ova / Mether’s Breath and similar area triggers can vary by save progression; the established route remains the evidence-backed path, but timing differences may affect how quickly you get to Martyr’s Prison.

Common mistakes
- Mistaking the blade for a boss drop or NPC reward. It is a direct pickup in the gated treasure room after the final chamber.
- Assuming Balance Patch 1 changed the pickup location, moveset, or scaling. The patch note only lists +20% Attack Damage; acquisition is unchanged.
- Re-running the entire route solely because older write-ups called the blade “weak.” The +20% buff alters the weapon’s damage expectations; retest it in your build instead of discarding it based on pre-patch statements.
- Treating the single observed +4 pickup as a rule. That was seen once and is not documented as universal.
- Equating community debate with mechanical evidence — run personal tests (damage on your build, timing of openings) to evaluate the blade for your playstyle.
