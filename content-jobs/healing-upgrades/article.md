Short answer
- Current launch coverage reports 21 total healing upgrades: 11 Revered Glands and 10 Synaptic Vessels. Two are bought from Merrick in Marrow Keep (one Gland, one Vessel) for 1,500 coin each; the other 19 are found in the world at the anchors listed below. This count and list follow current guide coverage (see Evidence boundary).

Gated pickups — how to reach the ones that require actions
- Nocthean Gate / Thieves Retreat (location 4)
  1. From Nocthean Gate Beacon, head north and drop one level.  
  2. Use the switch to open the dungeon route.  
  3. Follow the invisible walkways; use the suspended bodies as visual landmarks to navigate to the end of the Thieves Retreat.

- Mushroom Village / Holding Cells (location 5)
  1. From Mushroom Village Beacon, go northwest to the well that enters Holding Cells.  
  2. Use Mether's Breath on the corruption blocking the jump pad.  
  3. Enter via the well and find the button behind boxes to open the Revered Gland room.

- One Legged Wolf / Pale Grisha reward (location 8)
  1. Reach Grisha Hunting Grounds (northwest past the ruins).  
  2. Defeat the Pale Grisha boss to receive the Revered Gland.

- Citadel of Penance (locations 10 and 11)
  1. For the gland behind a breakable wooden wall (10): go southeast of the Beacon to the southwestern outer-castle corner and break the wooden wall.  
  2. For the vessel behind a gate (11): find and press the button on the wall opposite Eredrim's former position to open the gate.

- Abbey Entrance (location 16)
  1. Enter the cave east of the town.  
  2. Destroy all three glowing tombstones to open the door to the upgrade.

- Sunken Village (location 19)
  1. Cleanse the Beacon at Sunken Village.  
  2. Return and go down the hill behind you to find the container with the upgrade.

- Prisoners' Domain (location 20)
  1. Topple the giant statue by destroying the pots at its base.  
  2. Proceed past the iron-maiden room to reach the upgrade beyond the toppled statue.

Merrick purchases (Marrow Keep)
1. Speak to Merrick in Marrow Keep.  
2. He sells one Revered Gland and one Synaptic Vessel for 1,500 coin each.

How to verify you received an upgrade
1. Return to the reported location: if the pickup is gone, it was collected.  
2. Check your healing item stats in-game: a Revered Gland increases the number of healing items you can carry; a Synaptic Vessel increases the potency (how much each use restores).  
3. There is no in-game collection counter; the practical verification is (a) pickup removed from world and (b) your healing-item count or heal amount changed.

Common mistakes
- Expecting a collection counter — none is reported.  
- Missing gating prerequisites: not cleansing the Sunken Village Beacon; not destroying the three glowing tombstones at Abbey Entrance; not using Mether's Breath in Holding Cells; not using the switch or following invisible walkways in Thieves Retreat; not toppling the Prisoners' Domain statue; not breaking the wooden wall or pressing the Citadel button.  
- Assuming LootMap's “15 Chalice” layer or the “Devout's Chalice” artifacts are the same as Revered Glands/Synaptic Vessels — current coverage treats those separately (see Evidence boundary).

What happens next
- Current guide coverage reports that none of the 21 upgrades are missable: uncollected upgrades remain in place after the final boss during free roam. This is a community guide report, not an official developer guarantee and may change with patches.

Evidence boundary and conflicting reports
- The 21-location list and the 11/10 split come from current launch coverage (AllThingsHow, updated 2026-08-18).  
- LootMap reports a different set labeled as 15 map-layer “Healing Upgrades” chalices and 12 “Devout's Chalice” artifacts; do not merge those artifact/chalice items into this page.  
- The ordered list above follows one current guide's Beacon sequence; it is not a required collection order. I have not added map coordinates or invented locations beyond the anchors listed in the cited coverage.
