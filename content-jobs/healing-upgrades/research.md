# Research: Healing Upgrades (CREATE)

Scope: create `/mortal-shell-ii/healing-upgrades/`. Cover the two upgrade types, the complete current 21-location list, gated pickups, the Merrick purchases, and how to verify upgrades. Keep source/version limits explicit.

## Confirmed facts

- Current launch coverage reports 21 healing upgrades: 11 Revered Glands and 10 Synaptic Vessels.
- Revered Glands increase the number of healing items carried. Synaptic Vessels increase the potency of each healing use.
- Two upgrades are sold by Merrick in Marrow Keep for 1,500 coin each: one Revered Gland and one Synaptic Vessel.
- Current coverage reports the following 21 anchors and locations:
  1. Marrow Keep — Merrick, Revered Gland, 1,500 coin.
  2. Marrow Keep — Merrick, Synaptic Vessel, 1,500 coin.
  3. Stonebled Gate — south of Beacon, beside camp fire and boxes.
  4. Nocthean Gate — end of Thieves Retreat dungeon, north of Beacon and one level down.
  5. Mushroom Village — Holding Cells dungeon, entered through a well northwest of Beacon.
  6. Mushroom Village — north of Deserted Mine, east edge of the stone circle around the Trial Obelisk.
  7. Outskirts of Nochte — south of Beacon in the upper castle area, inside the north interior.
  8. One Legged Wolf — Pale Grisha reward at the end of Grisha Hunting Grounds, northwest past the ruins.
  9. Widow's Overlook — south of Beacon, beside a body near the western cliff edge.
  10. Citadel of Penance — southeast of Beacon, behind a breakable wooden wall in the southwestern outer-castle corner.
  11. Citadel of Penance — behind a gate opened by the button on the wall opposite Eredrim's former position.
  12. One Legged Wolf — through the eastern jump into Temple of Vatra, right before the large staircase.
  13. Sester's Gate — far back wall near boxes, past the long bridge with the bell-shooting route.
  14. Gate of Infinity — first bookshelf climb point, left on entry.
  15. Castigator's Keep — south of Beacon, central courtyard.
  16. Abbey Entrance — cave east of town, behind a door opened by destroying three glowing tombstones.
  17. Abandoned Outpost — Slums, among tombstones north of the western church building.
  18. High Lord's Courtyard — central long building on the south side of the outer courtyard, in front of a statue.
  19. Sunken Village — container down the hill behind the player, after cleansing the Beacon.
  20. Prisoners' Domain — past the toppled statue, beyond the iron maidens.
  21. Sanguine Shoreline — cave along the main path after the first open area.
- None of the 21 is reported as missable; current coverage says uncollected upgrades remain after the final boss in free roam.
- There is no collection counter. The practical check is whether the pickup remains at its location and whether the healing item has more charges or restores more per use.

## Gated route details

- Thieves Retreat: from Nocthean Gate, head north and drop one level; use the switch to open the dungeon route, then follow the invisible walkways using suspended bodies as visual landmarks.
- Holding Cells: go northwest from Mushroom Village, use Mether's Breath on the corruption blocking the jump pad, interact with the well, then find the button behind boxes to open the Gland room.
- Pale Grisha: reach Grisha Hunting Grounds northwest of One Legged Wolf and defeat the boss for the Gland.
- Citadel of Penance: use the breakable wooden wall for the Gland and the wall button opposite Eredrim's former position for the Vessel.
- Abbey Entrance: destroy all three glowing tombstones to open the cave door.
- Sunken Village: cleanse the Beacon first, then return down the hill to the container.
- Prisoners' Domain: topple the giant statue by destroying the pots at its base, then pass the iron-maiden room.
