Short answer
- Enter Withered Shoals from the Gate of Mammon, use Nameless Pass as your anchor, drop below Ruk to optionally pick up the Depleted Gland, optionally summon Proxima for 1 Glimpse, then jump into the void to trigger the encounter. Fight by watching the tentacles and shell telegraphs, wait for clear recovery windows, land short 2–3 hit punishes, and handle the later burrow/eruption or dive with lateral dodges.

Route to Hexapod (step-by-step)
1. Reach the Gate of Mammon in the Ruins of Mammon and enter Withered Shoals.  
2. Activate Ossinite Falls Beacon and then Nameless Pass Beacon if they are inactive; guides use Nameless Pass as the last approach anchor (GamingProMax, GameSpot).  
3. From Nameless Pass, follow the nearby path until you reach the area with Ruk. Drop below Ruk and return through the bone tunnel to collect the Depleted Gland if you want it — GamingProMax lists the gland as useful but not required.  
4. Move to the void edge where a green summon sign may appear. If you want a spirit aid, use the sign to summon Proxima for 1 Glimpse (optional; GamingProMax).  
5. Jump to the void. A short intro plays and the arena encounter begins immediately.

Preparation checklist (evidence-bounded)
- Heal and replenish at the last Beacon (Nameless Pass) before the jump.  
- Keep the arena center readable and clear space for lateral movement.  
- Reserve stamina (for lateral dodges and short recovery-window punishes).  
- Bring a weapon/Shell you already know and control; do not rely on a single “required” weapon.  
- The Depleted Gland is optional (GamingProMax).  
- If you want help, plan to spend 1 Glimpse to summon Proxima (GamingProMax).

Optional summon
- Proxima: a green summon sign before the void can summon Proxima for 1 Glimpse. Sources report she draws aggro and uses the Triarch Repeater, creating room for you to apply status or punish (GamingProMax).

How the fight loops (general)
- Core loop: watch the tentacles and the shell, wait for a clear attack or for the shell opening/recovery, land a short two- or three-hit punish, then reset. Both checked guides describe highly visible telegraphs.  
- Positioning advice differs by source: GamingProMax recommends attacking from the front during recovery windows rather than chasing sides/back; PatchCrazy describes moving around the boss while its shell is closed. Treat both as viable approaches rather than a single “correct” angle.

Phase notes (conflicting source summaries preserved)
- GamingProMax frames the encounter as two phases: an early tentacle-melee phase and a later phase that adds an underground burrow/eruption after an unblockable red-warning slash.  
- PatchCrazy frames three stages: two early shell/melee stages and a third stage that adds a ranged attack and an underwater/dive re-emergence; it also notes that critical-hit staggers can advance stages.  
- Because sources disagree, expect “early melee phase(s)” with tentacle telegraphs, then a “later phase” with burrow/eruption and possible ranged/dive behaviors (source-attributed differences remain).

Attack tells and counters (practical)
- Lateral tentacle slash  
  - Tell: tentacles sweep horizontally; motion is slow and wide (both guides).  
  - Counter: dodge backward for room or dodge through on iframes; re-engage only after the swing finishes.  
- Triple tentacle stab  
  - Tell: three tentacles line up and thrust forward in a straight line.  
  - Counter: dodge sideways out of the line; a short follow-up window is reported beside the body.  
- Overhead slash  
  - Tell: outer tentacles lift then come down in a slow arc.  
  - Counter: easiest to parry according to GamingProMax; otherwise dodge backward and use a quick two-hit punish.  
- Tentacle flurry / running slash  
  - Tell: many tentacles strike rapidly while Hexapod moves toward/away.  
  - Counter: create distance and wait for the sequence to finish; do not try to roll through every hit.  
- Squid Surge charge (red-icon)  
  - Tell: Hexapod jumps back, a red indicator flashes, then it charges straight.  
  - Counter: dodge sideways (not backward). A failed charge reportedly leaves a reliable 1–2 second punish window (GamingProMax).  
- Phase-transition red-warning slash (GamingProMax)  
  - Tell: a red warning accompanies an unblockable/unparryable slash used to create space.  
  - Counter: back off and give it room; do not attempt to parry. This commonly leads into the underground sequence.  
- Underground burrow / eruption (later-phase behavior)  
  - Tell: Hexapod digs underground and a red indicator appears on/near the arena floor.  
  - Counter: dodge laterally as the indicator reaches you. A missed eruption briefly exposes the boss for a few hits; keep the arena center available (GamingProMax).  
- Later ranged attack (PatchCrazy single-source)  
  - Tell: PatchCrazy reports a ranged projectile attack in its third stage (no settled pattern provided).  
  - Counter: do not parry; move laterally and dodge repeatedly until the attack ends. Avoid creating excessive distance, which the source warns may provoke a rapid underwater/dive attack.  
- Underwater/dive re-emergence (PatchCrazy single-source)  
  - Tell: the boss may dive and re-emerge quickly from another direction.  
  - Counter: unlock the camera, run while dodging facing away, then relock after re-emergence (PatchCrazy).  
- Front grab after stagger (GamingProMax reported)  
  - Tell: an unblockable grab can occur directly in front during recovery from a stagger.  
  - Counter: reposition to a flank before the boss wakes up; avoid standing directly in front during the wake-up (GamingProMax).

Shells, weapons, and status options (guide-sourced suggestions)
- Proxima (summon): pulls aggro and uses the Triarch Repeater — useful to create space/status opportunities (GamingProMax).  
- Tiel: recommended by PatchCrazy (perfect dodge → temporary invisibility) and mentioned by GamingProMax for pairing Shadow Strike with Squid Surge punishes. Recommendation, not a requirement.  
- Sester Genessa / Black Needle: PatchCrazy suggests a ranged-stagger/critical build with duplicates and Moonshine. Guide suggestion only.  
- Forgotten Crossbow + Shuddering Stone: recommended by Gamer Guides for knockback when Hexapod lunges; GamingProMax also notes sidearm/crossbow as optional ranged chip. Do not treat ranged knockback as guaranteed.  
- Status effects: GamingProMax suggests Stasis, Burn, or Lightning while Proxima holds aggro; Stasis is called especially useful for slowing Hexapod.

Rewards (conflicts preserved)
- Hexapod Core: drops from Hexapod and works with the Axatana according to GamingProMax and Nerdschalk. Those sources report a 60 Resolve cost and a rapid succession of slashing/striking attacks; PatchCrazy confirms the core drop but does not list Resolve or Axatana specifics. Treat the Axatana/60 Resolve data as source-reported and possibly patch-sensitive (GamingProMax, Nerdschalk, PatchCrazy).  
- Gloom payout: PatchCrazy and Nerdschalk list 38,753 Gloom; GamingProMax lists 54,250 Gloom. All agree the boss grants a large Gloom payout; the exact number is source- and run-sensitive.  
- Post-fight Beacon and Ova/Ovum: sources say a Beacon appears after the fight; GamingProMax calls it Hexapod’s Burrow Beacon and notes mist can make it hard to spot. GameSpot explains the last Beacon in a major dungeon appears after the main boss and contains Ova/Ovum for campaign progression. Cleanse/activate the post-fight Beacon and collect the Ova/Ovum (GameSpot, GamingProMax).  
- Achievement / trophy: “Something in the deep…” is awarded for defeating Hexapod and is documented by XboxAchievements, TrueAchievements, Gamer Guides, and PatchCrazy. No checked source identified a special pre-kill action or missable condition for the achievement in this pass.

Common mistakes (practical, evidence-backed)
- Retreating straight backward at the Squid Surge — this can leave you in the charge lane. Move laterally instead (GamingProMax).  
- Chasing the boss around its flanks while it turns — both guides recommend waiting for clear recovery windows rather than continuously circling. GamingProMax favors front-facing punishes; PatchCrazy favors movement around the closed shell.  
- Overcommitting after burrow/eruption or dive — the later phase(s) are the most likely point to die on a first attempt; keep the arena center available and watch floor indicators.  
- Missing the post-fight Beacon in the mist — GamingProMax warns the Hexapod’s Burrow Beacon can be hard to spot.

What happens next (immediate post-kill steps)
1. Locate the post-fight Beacon (referred to as Hexapod’s Burrow Beacon by GamingProMax) in the misty arena area and cleanse/activate it.  
2. Collect the Ova/Ovum from the cleared dungeon Beacon (GameSpot, GamingProMax).  
3. Continue exploring Mammon or fast travel from the newly activated Beacon. The six major Corrupted Gate encounters are route-flexible; do not assume a single required next boss unless your save state requires it.  
4. If you obtained a Hexapod Core and own the Axatana, refer to the Axatana guide and Tarstone/Tarforge pages for how to use the Core (Nerdschalk, GamingProMax).
