## After Hotfix 3 — Current PC Status

Hotfix 3 (Steam PC hotfix, Sept 1, 2026) is the latest PC hotfix released after the Week 1 Update; crashes, freezes, and performance issues may still persist for some players. The developer confirmed a set of targeted fixes but did not claim to resolve all crash or performance reports.

### What Hotfix 3 fixed (official)

- Devout / Pre-Order Shell Shades not appearing  
- Opening world map while using FSR  
- Citadel Annex giant-spider traversal issue  
- Some enemies not reacting to the player  
- Several collision issues  
- Minibeacon lens flare / visual issues

### What Hotfix 3 did not confirm

- The developer did not state that Hotfix 3 fixed all crashes, freezes, or performance regressions.  
- Community reports after Hotfix 3 still include stutter, 1% low drops, general performance degradation, and severe frame drops on some Steam Deck units.

### If the problem started after Week 1 or Hotfix 3

Follow these safe troubleshooting steps in order:

1. Confirm Steam installed the Week 1 Update and Hotfix 3 (no pending download); restart Steam to ensure updates applied.  
2. Follow the official Week 1 precautions before/after updating: remove or disable mods, and ensure engine.ini is not read-only (the official note says a read-only engine.ini can affect frame generation).  
3. Retest the exact trigger that caused the crash, freeze, or stutter.  
4. Match your symptom to the relevant section on the page (for example: Keyboard Bindings, loading crashes, etc.).  
5. If the issue persists, file a bug report that includes your GPU and driver version and whether Frame Generation was enabled when the problem occurred.

See the Week 1 Update and the main Updates hub for the official posts and further guidance: /mortal-shell-ii/updates/week-1-update/ and /mortal-shell-ii/updates/.

### FAQ

Q: Did Hotfix 3 fix Mortal Shell II crashes?  
A: Hotfix 3 fixed the specific issues listed in the official notes (see above) but the developer did not claim it fixed all crashes; community reports of crashes and performance issues continue.

Q: Why is Mortal Shell II freezing after the latest patch?  
A: The developer did not confirm a universal freeze fix. If freezing began after the Week 1 Update or Hotfix 3, follow the troubleshooting steps above (remove/disable mods; ensure engine.ini is not read-only; restart Steam; retest and file a bug report with system details).

Q: Why won't Mortal Shell II start after updating?  
A: Start failures can be caused by mods or a read-only engine.ini among other factors. Follow the official pre-update precautions: remove/disable mods and ensure engine.ini is writable, restart Steam to confirm the update applied, then retest.

Q: Is the Week 1 Update live on PS5 and Xbox?  
A: PS5 is available Sept 1; Xbox was delayed (status checked Sept 2, 2026).
