Short answer
LootMap’s Hidden Paths layer (build 24841087) reports 13 Hidden Paths in Mortal Shell 2: six literal hidden walls, two hidden-wall switches, two plain Hidden Path markers, two secret pipes, and one breakable ceiling. They cluster around Marrow Keep / Fainweald (8 markers at or north of Marrow Keep) and the Ruins of Mammon to the south (5 markers). LootMap is an unofficial map layer; use it as an inventory, not developer documentation.

Where the 13 markers are (use these landmark anchors in-game)
Numbered list below enumerates the 13 LootMap markers and the concrete landmarks you can use to find each. I do not give coordinates.

1. Hidden-wall anchor at the northern map edge, beside a Glimpse.
2. Hidden-wall anchor in the Wormfish Exchange pocket, beside a Healing Power Chalice.
3. Hidden-wall anchor inside Marrow Keep, near the Marrow Keep map marker and the Locked Shells note.
4. Hidden-wall anchor above Genessa’s Shell site, beside a chest.
5. Hidden-wall anchor north of the deep-south City Map Station, beside a chest.
6. Hidden-wall anchor west of that same City Map Station, near a Lore Book and a bone gate.
7. Hidden-wall switch a few steps west of the Crypt Key Door, near a chest and the Tarstones marker.
8. Hidden-wall switch near a coffin and a Lift Call (route landmark: Wormfish Exchange).
9. Hidden Path marker southwest of the Forgotten Crossbow and north of the Forest Map Fragment/map station.
10. Hidden Path marker northwest of the Chapel Key, near Tiel’s Shell site and a Door marker.
11. Secret pipe (far south) just north of Sariel’s Shell pickup.
12. Secret pipe (far south) northeast of Sariel among Galvanic Shard nodes and Stone Statue enemies; the Healing Charge Chalice at Tar Root Gate is nearby.
13. Breakable ceiling in the northwestern forest clearing, beside Eredrim’s Shell pickup and a Healing Power Chalice.

How to discover and open them — step-by-step
1. Enable the in-game Hidden Paths map layer (or use LootMap’s Hidden Paths layer for reference). The map layer is the reliable source for the 13-marker count.  
2. Travel to the anchor landmark listed above for the marker you want to check (named pickups, chests, map stations, Shell sites, doors, switches, pipes, and ceilings are the useful local cues).  
3. Identify the marker type from context before you act:
   - If the marker is a literal hidden wall anchor, test a suspicious surface with an attack only when the route context supports a wall (e.g., a dead-end passage, wall directly adjacent to the anchor, or chest/Shell site behind it).  
   - If the marker is a hidden-wall switch, look for an interactable switch or a small architectural detail a few steps away; use interaction inputs rather than repeatedly attacking solid surfaces.  
   - If the marker is a secret pipe, look for a pipe model or pipe-access hole and use the appropriate interaction.  
   - If the marker is a breakable ceiling, look upward in the forest clearing or chamber and use a ceiling-breaking attack or interaction when available.
4. Check immediately for the nearby named rewards or landmarks after opening: chests, Shell pickups (Genessa, Eredrim, Sariel), Healing Power/Healing Charge Chalices, map stations, or lore items mentioned at each anchor.
5. Do not publish or rely on world coordinates—this repository and LootMap intentionally lack a calibratable world-map coordinate system.

Mechanism notes and terminology
- “Illusory wall” is the common player-facing umbrella term; the map layer and player reports mix names (“hidden path,” “secret door,” “breakable ceiling,” etc.). Preserve the distinction: not every Hidden Path marker is a literal, attackable illusory wall.  
- Use the interaction type suggested by the landmark (switch, pipe, ceiling) rather than assuming a single universal detection/attack mechanic.

Common mistakes
- Attacking every textured wall. Many markers are switches, pipes, or ceilings—attacking won’t help and will waste time.  
- Ignoring the Hidden Paths map layer and searching by wall texture alone. The layer is the practical discovery tool.  
- Missing nearby map stations, Shell sites, or chests that confirm you triggered the right route. Always scan for the named pickups listed at each anchor.  
- Relying on other community lists that claim different counts or hidden bosses/rewards; a separate “secret areas” article reports 22 areas and unsupported reward/mechanic claims and is not used for the 13-marker count here.  
- Publishing or depending on explicit coordinates—this guide follows the repository’s no-coordinate practice.

What happens next
- After you open a Hidden Path, expect to find nearby chests, Shell pickups (Genessa, Eredrim, Sariel are specifically tied to anchors above), map stations, lore books, and healing chalices at several anchors. LootMap is the current inventory of these markers but is unofficial and may change with new builds.  
- If you want to locate map fragments, fast-travel beacons, or upgrades mentioned at anchors, consult the related pages: /mortal-shell-ii/map-fragments/, /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/, /mortal-shell-ii/healing-upgrades/, and /mortal-shell-ii/crypt-key-hall-of-echoes/. For an example of a related in-game pickup note, see /mortal-shell-ii/axe-dagger/.
