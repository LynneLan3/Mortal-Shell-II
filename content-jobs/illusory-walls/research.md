# Research: Illusory Walls (CREATE)

Scope: create `/mortal-shell-ii/illusory-walls/`. Treat “illusory walls” as the player term for the game's broader `Hidden Paths` map layer. Explain the 13 current map markers, the different mechanism types, and the most useful landmarks without inventing exact coordinates.

## Confirmed facts

- LootMap's current build 24841087 map layer reports 13 Hidden Paths in Mortal Shell 2. The 13 consist of six hidden walls, two hidden-wall switches, two markers typed Hidden Path, two secret pipes, and one breakable ceiling.
- Eight markers are at or north of Marrow Keep on the Fainweald side; five are south through the Ruins of Mammon.
- Six hidden-wall anchors: near the northern map edge beside a Glimpse; in the Wormfish Exchange pocket beside a Healing Power Chalice; inside Marrow Keep near the Marrow Keep marker and Locked Shells; above Genessa's shell site beside a chest; north of the deep-south City Map Station beside a chest; and west of that City Map Station near a Lore Book and bone gate.
- Two hidden-wall switches: one a few steps west of the Crypt Key Door near a chest and the Tarstones marker; the other near a coffin and Lift Call, using Wormfish Exchange as the route landmark.
- Two plain Hidden Path markers: one southwest of the Forgotten Crossbow and north of the Forest Map Fragment/map station; the other northwest of the Chapel Key, near Tiel's shell site and a Door marker.
- Two secret pipes are in the far south: one just north of Sariel's shell pickup; the other northeast of Sariel among Galvanic Shard nodes and Stone Statue enemies, with the Healing Charge Chalice at Tar Root Gate nearby.
- The single breakable ceiling is in the northwestern forest clearing, beside Eredrim's shell pickup and a Healing Power Chalice.
- The map layer is the reliable count source for this page. “Illusory wall” is a player-facing umbrella term; not every marker is a literal wall that opens by a normal attack.

## Practical discovery guidance

- Use the in-game/map `Hidden Paths` layer rather than relying on a universal wall texture rule.
- Check for named pickups, chests, map stations, Shell sites, doors, switches, pipes, and ceilings at the anchors above.
- For a literal hidden wall, test the suspicious surface with an attack only when the route context supports it. For a switch, pipe, or breakable ceiling, use the nearby landmark and the interaction type rather than repeatedly attacking a solid wall.
- Do not publish coordinates because the repository intentionally lacks a calibratable world-map coordinate system.

## Evidence boundary and conflicts

- A separate “secret areas” article reports 22 secret areas across five differently named zones and includes unsupported claims about unique weapons, hidden bosses, and exact detection mechanics. It is not used for the 13-marker count or reward claims.
- LootMap itself labels the page unofficial and unaffiliated. Use it as a current map-layer inventory, not as official developer documentation.
- Player reports and source pages use “hidden path,” “secret door,” “illusory wall,” “hidden wall,” and “breakable ceiling” inconsistently. Preserve the distinction in the article and do not call all 13 literal illusory walls.
