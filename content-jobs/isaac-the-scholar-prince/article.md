Short answer — where Isaac is and the quick tactic
- Location: Isaac, the Scholar Prince is the major boss at the end of the Faded Citadel Corrupted Gate in the Mammon / Ruins of Mammon branch (arena named Ashen Sanctum). Sources: GamingProMax, GameSpot, AllThingsHow.
- Best pre-fight checkpoint: Citadel Atrium Beacon (GamingProMax).
- Core tactic: let Isaac commit to full attack strings, dodge or parry when the string finishes, then take a short punish. Avoid chasing and avoid mid-range heals because his Leaping Lunge covers surprising distance (GamingProMax).

Route to Isaac (step-by-step)
1. Reach the Mammon region and enter the Faded Citadel / Gate of Infinity route (access to Mammon required; sources do not prescribe a fixed global boss order).
2. Progress the Faded Citadel route: cross the first hallway, take the stairs into the Ashen Sanctum area, and clear the two blue-flame zones noted by GamingProMax.
3. Defeat the Servants of the Scholar-Prince encounter (occuring before the final descent as reported by GamingProMax).
4. Activate Citadel Atrium Beacon (the last convenient pre-fight checkpoint listed by GamingProMax).
5. Follow the only available path to the arena entrance; Ruk is reported near the entrance and provides backstory (Ruk detail comes from guide reporting).
6. Optionally summon Lazlo at the arena entrance for 1 Glimpse (Lazlo summon confirmed by GameSpot, AllThingsHow, and GamingProMax).
7. Drop into the Ashen Sanctum arena to start the fight.

Fight progression and how to approach it
1. Prepare: upgrade your chosen Shell, weapon, and healing. GamingProMax recommends Tiel for Shadow Dodge / Shadow Strike as an option, but that is a recommendation, not a requirement.
2. Phase 1 (sword + shield):
   - Isaac uses a large shield that blocks sidearm shots; save ranged pressure for Phase 2 if you rely on a sidearm (GamingProMax).
   - Standard loop: let Isaac approach, read his entire attack sequence, dodge or parry the string (parry is a practical option in Phase 1 per GamingProMax), take a short punish during his recovery pause, then reset. Do not chase when he backs away.
   - At roughly half health Isaac will fall to his knees (approximate threshold reported by sources). The kneel triggers a Riposte prompt in many cases; whether you take that Riposte affects Phase 2 (see Optional Phase 2 skip).
3. Phase 2 (three swords) — conditional:
   - If the Riposte transition is triggered (source-reported), Isaac loses his shield and gains two extra swords (three total). He becomes faster and less predictable; his shield no longer blocks sidearm shots but his movement makes clean ranged shots harder (GamingProMax).
   - Parrying the first hit becomes unreliable in many Phase 2 strings (GamingProMax). Prioritize dodging full strings and punishing the recovery windows.

Attacks, tells, and counters (descriptive names from the guides)
- Leaping Lunge — closes a large gap with a forward jump/pounce.
  - Response: dodge the landing or move out of line; do not heal in front of him at mid-range.
  - Punish: use the landing pause for a short punish once recovery is confirmed (GamingProMax).
- Aerial Spin Slash — rises, spins, then drives the sword downward.
  - Response: dodge the aerial attack and wait for him to land.
  - Punish: short combo while he stands briefly on landing (source-reported).
- Ground Slash Combo — two- or three-hit ground string.
  - Response: parry in Phase 1 if comfortable (per GamingProMax), otherwise dodge the full string.
  - Punish: attack after the final swing / visible pause.
- Triple Sword Combo (Phase 2) — rapid three-blade sequence.
  - Response: do not assume parrying the first strike covers follow-ups; dodge the full string.
  - Punish: hit during the recovery, not the first blade.
- Unparryable Rush Stab — Phase 2 red-warning forward stab.
  - Response: sidestep as Isaac closes; treat the red warning as a dodge instruction.
  - Punish: re-engage after it passes.
- Pin Cushion — repeated downward stabs that track the player.
  - Response: maintain your rhythm and delay the final dodge; the sixth strike reportedly has an extra pause.
  - Punish: attack only after the delayed sixth hit and recovery.
- Final Spin Attack — low-health Phase 2 diagonal spins followed by a fast spin finish.
  - Response: GamingProMax recommends dodging left with each swing and staying close rather than running away (guide recommendation, not frame-tested proof).
  - Punish: re-engage after the full sequence finishes.

Optional Phase 2 skip (source-reported, conditional)
- GamingProMax reports that if you avoid the Riposte when Isaac kneels at roughly 50% health—using heavies or back attacks rather than the normal Riposte prompt—you can keep Isaac in the more predictable Phase 1 pattern.
- Preserve as a conditional technique: this is documented by a single detailed guide and may depend on how Riposte rules behave in your patch. A day-one press report mentioned light/ heavy attack riposte interactions changing; re-test this locally rather than assuming it always works.

Rewards, Ova, and trophy
- Main boss reward: Scholar's Wormstone — a Tarstone associated with the Clockwork Scythe. GamingProMax describes the Wormstone as an ability tarstone that drains Resolve over time to power the saw and augment attacks while active; GameSpot and AllThingsHow cross-check the association of Isaac → Scholar's Wormstone → Clockwork Scythe.
- Note: Isaac grants the Wormstone Tarstone, not the Clockwork Scythe weapon itself. The Clockwork Scythe acquisition is tied to Sariel’s Chamber of Becoming per the local Clockwork Scythe route (/mortal-shell-ii/clockwork-scythe/).
- Post-boss Beacon: Scholar-Prince's Chamber (GamingProMax). GameSpot reports the Beacon contains 26 Ova; verify the actual pickup locally if needed.
- Isaac also grants Gloom; checked sources do not give a stable exact amount.
- Trophy: defeating Isaac unlocks the bronze Nightmare Fuel achievement (cross-checked by XboxAchievements and current trophy lists). No checked source lists an Isaac-specific missable pre-kill action.

Shells, summons, and summons vs. Shell unlocks
- Lazlo is the Isaac-area spirit summon and costs 1 Glimpse to activate (confirmed by GameSpot, AllThingsHow, and GamingProMax). Present Lazlo as optional aggro support.
- GamingProMax recommends Tiel for Shadow Dodge / Shadow Strike as a useful Shell choice; this is a recommendation only.
- Do not assume Lazlo summon equals a playable Shell unlock. Lazlo’s summon is separate from the playable Shell acquisition routes (local references tie playable Shell unlocks to other content).

Practical in-fight tips (concise)
- Patience > chasing: wait for full strings, survive them, then punish briefly.
- Avoid mid-range healing because Leaping Lunge covers distance quickly.
- Phase 1 parrying is viable with practice, but Phase 2 follow-ups make “parry the first hit” risky.
- Conserve stamina for Pin Cushion rhythm and red-warning Rush Stab windows.
- If Phase 2 is blocking progress, test the kneel / no-Riposte option carefully rather than assuming it will work every patch.

Common Mistakes
- Chasing Isaac after he backs away and getting hit by follow-ups.
- Healing at mid-range instead of after a confirmed recovery (Leaping Lunge punishes this).
- Trying to parry the first strike in Phase 2 strings—parry becomes unreliable there.
- Wasting sidearm shots in Phase 1 while his shield blocks them (GamingProMax).
- Treating the Phase 2 skip as guaranteed—it is a source-reported conditional technique and may depend on current riposte behavior.

What happens next (post-boss steps)
1. Activate / cleanse the Scholar-Prince's Chamber Beacon and collect the Ova.
2. Use Ova toward the Unfound Path when your campaign threshold is met; Isaac is route-accessible but not established as a mandatory final Corrupted Gate boss in the checked sources.
3. If you want the Clockwork Scythe weapon itself, follow the separate Sariel route (/mortal-shell-ii/clockwork-scythe/)—Isaac provides the Wormstone Tarstone only.
4. For a rematch, use Citadel Atrium Beacon as the route anchor to return; the Scholar-Prince's Chamber Beacon only exists after victory (GamingProMax).
