# Item 28 research — Isaac, the Scholar Prince

Checked: 2026-08-24. Scope: Item 28 only, `mortal-shell-ii/isaac-the-scholar-prince`, action `create`.

## Location, nearest Beacon, and route

- Isaac is the major boss at the end of the **Faded Citadel** Corrupted Gate in the southern Mammon / Ruins of Mammon branch. The boss arena is identified as **Ashen Sanctum** inside Faded Citadel by GamingProMax. GameSpot and AllThingsHow independently place Isaac in Faded Citadel in Mammon.
- The most useful pre-fight checkpoint is **Citadel Atrium Beacon**, reached after the Servants of the Scholar-Prince encounter and before the final descent. GamingProMax gives this as the last documented Beacon before the arena.
- Route reported by GamingProMax: enter **Gate of Infinity** at Faded Citadel; cross the first hallway and take the stairs into Ashen Sanctum; progress through the area; extinguish blue flames in two zones; defeat **Servants of the Scholar-Prince**; activate **Citadel Atrium Beacon**; follow the only available path to the boss entrance; activate Lazlo's summon; drop into the arena.
- Ruk is reported near the arena entrance and provides Isaac backstory. This NPC/lore detail is guide-reported and should not be expanded beyond that source.
- After victory, the post-boss Beacon is named **Scholar-Prince's Chamber** in GamingProMax's Beacon checklist. It is the post-boss Ova checkpoint, not the pre-fight route anchor.

## Prerequisites and preparation

- Access to the Mammon region and the Gate of Infinity / Faded Citadel route is required. The checked sources do not establish a single mandatory order for all six Corrupted Gate bosses; describe them as route-flexible once accessible. Do not require a fixed number of earlier bosses.
- Clear the Servants of the Scholar-Prince encounter and the two blue-flame areas before the Citadel Atrium Beacon, according to GamingProMax. The exact arena-clearing interaction is not independently cross-checked, so keep the attribution.
- Lazlo can be summoned near the arena for **1 Glimpse**. It is optional assistance, not a prerequisite. Current sources spell the Shell **Lazlo**; avoid copying alternate spellings from unrelated pages.
- Upgrade the chosen Shell, weapon, and healing before entering. GamingProMax recommends Tiel for Shadow Dodge / Shadow Strike, but this is a recommendation rather than a required or universally best Shell. The site already has Tiel and general [Weapons](/mortal-shell-ii/weapons/) context.
- Isaac's shield blocks sidearm shots in Phase 1 according to GamingProMax; save ranged pressure for Phase 2 if relying on a sidearm. This is a source-backed practical recommendation, not a claim that every sidearm interaction has been exhaustively tested.

## Fight mechanics and phases

### Phase 1: sword and shield

- Isaac begins with one sword and a large shield. His primary threat is long melee reach, especially when he closes distance with **Leaping Lunge**.
- The stable loop is: let Isaac approach, read the full attack sequence, dodge or parry it, take a short punish during the recovery pause, then reset. Do not chase him when he backs away.
- GamingProMax reports that Isaac's ground attacks can be parried in Phase 1 and that the patterns become readable after practice. Because the source does not provide frame data, write this as a practical option rather than a promise that every animation is equally safe.
- At approximately half health, Isaac drops to his knees. The exact threshold is approximate, not a verified percentage. The source-reported Riposte interaction determines whether the shield is removed and Phase 2 begins.

### Phase 2: three swords

- If the Riposte transition is triggered, Isaac loses the shield and takes up two additional swords, for three swords total. He becomes faster, more aggressive, and less predictable.
- His shield no longer blocks sidearm shots in this phase according to GamingProMax, but his movement makes clean shots harder. Prefer short, confirmed openings over standing still.
- Phase 2 is not just Phase 1 with more damage: the three-sword strings and delayed Pin Cushion finisher make early parries and early dodges unreliable.

## Optional Phase 2 skip

- GamingProMax reports that Isaac remains in the more predictable Phase 1 pattern if the player avoids the Riposte when he kneels at roughly 50% health. That guide recommends using only heavy attacks or attacks from behind during the kneel rather than taking the normal Riposte.
- Preserve this as a source-reported option, not a guaranteed universal exploit. The day-one patch report says heavy attacks no longer open a Riposte and only light attacks do, which may explain or affect the timing. No primary developer note was found that documents the Isaac-specific skip.

## Practical insights

- The fight punishes chasing more than passive play: wait for Isaac to commit, survive the entire string, then take a short punish.
- Mid-range healing is unsafe because Leaping Lunge covers more distance than the visual gap suggests. Heal only after a confirmed recovery or when Lazlo has clear aggro.
- Phase 1 parry practice is viable, but Phase 2's three-sword follow-ups make “parry the first hit” a trap unless the whole sequence is understood.
- Save stamina for the Pin Cushion rhythm and Phase 2 red-warning Rush Stab. A short punish is safer than a full combo.
- If Phase 2 is the blocker, the source-reported kneel / no-Riposte route is worth testing, but it should remain labeled as a conditional technique rather than a guaranteed skip.
