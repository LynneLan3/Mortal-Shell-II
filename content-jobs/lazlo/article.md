Short answer
- Lazlo the Justiciar is a permanent Shell found in Mammon. Fast-travel to the High Lord's Courtyard Beacon, take the keep/crypt route, defeat Vellen (the boss gate), then continue into the chamber beyond the arena and interact with Lazlo’s remains to trigger the first Memory sequence — that Memory sequence is the permanent-unlock step, not merely killing Vellen (sources: GameSpot; Destructoid; Gaming ProMax; AltChar; local Shells guide).

Where to go (route)
1. Fast-travel to the High Lord's Courtyard Beacon. (Multiple guides reference this beacon as the practical start point.)
2. Proceed along the keep route toward the Royal Crypt / dark crypt beneath the keep. (Guides vary in exact turns/jump/air-current wording; use the beacon → keep → crypt sequence.)
3. Defeat Vellen, Lord of Mammon, who blocks the path forward.
4. After the fight, enter the room/crypt beyond the boss arena and interact with Lazlo’s corpse/inhabit prompt to begin the first Memory sequence. If no immediate inhabit prompt appears, manually inspect the body in the last chamber (GameSpot; Destructoid; Gaming ProMax; AltChar).

Recommended starting build (practical template)
- Core idea: Heat-and-Overheat bruiser. Keep Lazlo’s armor/Heat meter active, build Resolve through melee, trigger Overheat for a Burn/shockwave burst, then use the short armor-off window to convert with critical hits. (AllThings.How; Gaming ProMax)
- 1) Primary weapon: Axatana is the common starting recommendation. See /mortal-shell-ii/axatana/ for route and basics. (AllThings.How; Gaming ProMax)
- 2) Sidearm: choose one based on your playstyle:
  - Caged Hystrix — safer ranged pressure during armor-off windows (recommended by AllThings.How; see /mortal-shell-ii/caged-hystrix/).
  - Ballistazooka — higher burst potential for critical-hit follow-ups when the armor is down (recommended by Gaming ProMax).
  - See /mortal-shell-ii/sidearms/ for context; sources disagree on a single “best” sidearm.
- 3) Seal: Vatra’s Seal is recommended by AllThings.How as a good fit for this loop; sources did not provide an independent, controlled comparison. See /mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/ for Seal context.
- 4) Tarstones and secondary choices: favor crit-oriented Tarstones to exploit the armor-off window (AllThings.How). See /mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/ for effects and collection info.
- 5) Perks (source-reported recommendations — avoid treating these as fixed numbers): Worthy Armor, Warmed Up, Incandescence, Temperament, Furnace, Inflamed, Rush. These are repeated in current community guides but exact values/costs are patch-sensitive (AllThings.How; Gaming ProMax).
- 6) Play the loop: maintain Fortified Plate armor while building Heat (Retribution heats armor creating smaller shocks), then trigger Overheat for the bigger shockwave and armor removal, and press advantage with critical hits and burst sidearm use (AllThings.How; Gaming ProMax).

Why this setup
- Fortified Plate provides the armor-based protection while the Heat meter is active; Retribution and Overheat are the heating/burst mechanics that form the build’s core loop (AllThings.How; Gaming ProMax).
- The Axatana is commonly recommended as a comfortable primary for the melee/Resolve build loop; sidearm choice is situational and contested across sources.

Alternatives and disagreements to note
- Sidearm: Caged Hystrix vs. Ballistazooka — both are recommended by different guides for different reasons. Pick based on whether you want safer ranged pressure (Hystrix) or higher single-window burst (Ballistazooka).
- Seal: Vatra’s Seal is a guide recommendation, not an objectively verified best choice.
- No single guide establishes an undisputed “best build.” Presented loadouts are defensible starting templates rather than absolute meta rules.

Common mistakes
- Treating Vellen’s death alone as the complete Shell unlock. Multiple guides recommend continuing into the chamber and interacting with Lazlo’s remains to trigger the Memory sequence (GameSpot; Destructoid; Gaming ProMax; AltChar).
- Following compass/turn-by-turn directions copied from different guides that disagree on bearings (west/south/stairs/air-current). Use the High Lord’s Courtyard Beacon → keep → crypt → Vellen sequence instead.
- Treating one sidearm recommendation as universally superior; sources conflict on Hystrix vs. Ballistazooka.
- Quoting or relying on exact perk percentages, costs, HP, or damage numbers from current guides — those values are patch-sensitive. Refer to the site’s Balance Patch 1 page for patch boundaries (/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/).

What happens next
- Interacting with Lazlo’s remains should begin the first Memory sequence; that sequence is the step that makes the Shell permanent according to GameSpot and the site’s Shells model. After the Memory sequence completes, consult the Shells Guide (/mortal-shell-ii/shells/) for bonding/progression and see the Glimpses page (/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/) for resource context if you need to bond or advance the Shell.

What is not confirmed / uncertainty summary
- Exact area naming varies across sources (High Lord’s Courtyard / High Lord’s Keep / Royal Crypt / Mammon crypt); anchor on the in-game High Lord’s Courtyard Beacon and the Vellen encounter (see cited sources).
- Guide bearings (which stairs, air currents, or jump routes) differ between write-ups; the beacon → keep → crypt → Vellen flow is the stable landmark-level instruction.
- Some guides report an immediate inhabit prompt after Vellen; others instruct inspecting a corpse in the last chamber. The safe action is to go into the room beyond the boss and interact with the body to complete the Memory.
- Sidearm and Seal recommendations are not universally agreed; they are presented as source-backed options rather than definitive choices.
- Perk/weapon numeric values are patch-sensitive and were not independently reconciled here. See /mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/ for patch context.
