## Confirmed launch-window evidence

- The current Glutted Mire guide and its existing research place Magdalena, Lady of the Woods at the end of the Glutted Mire route.
- Reach her from Mushroom Village Gate after clearing Hall of Illusions and opening the Corrupted Gate; follow Seeping Mire / source-reported Sleeping Mire, Sunken Village, the shepherd encounter, and the post-shepherd board gap / bridge route.
- The Etching Needles chest is after the shepherd and before Magdalena, near Ruk. Sources disagree whether it is at the bridge end or below/near the obvious route. Preserve the landmark-based instruction and do not invent coordinates or a universal left/right turn.
- The current route drops from a ledge into Magdalena's flooded arena.
- Use the current evidence terms: charge / vehicle sequence, spinning fire attack, fire pressure, Break meter and Riposte openings, summoned small enemies, and fly cloud / burning-ground hazards. Do not invent attack names, HP, damage values, official phase names, or a universal best build.
- Current conservative approach: hold mid range, let the charge come to you, dodge the charge / fire-spin sequence, take one or two quick recovery hits, build Break with safe melee or ranged hits, use Riposte when the opening appears, keep moving when summons appear, and pull her away from fly cloud or burning ground.
- After the fight, activate and cleanse Magdalena's Glade Beacon and collect the Ova the game provides. The exact Ova quantity is intentionally omitted because the checked sources disagree.
- The route reward context includes Magdalena's Memento as source-reported Tarstone evidence. Do not expand this into an unverified complete loot table.
- The achievement guide maps Magdalena to `Ultimate Karen` / `Wheelie Good Time` platform-name variants. Preserve the variants only if useful; do not claim one universal platform title beyond the evidence.
- A practical loadout should be framed conservatively: a familiar weapon or a weapon with manageable recovery, and any Shell the player already understands. Do not declare Tiel or another Shell mandatory.
