Answer — three-point summary
1. Find the Makeshift Projectile at the southern edge of the Outskirts of Mammon starting island (below the far end of the large stone bridge). A note sits beside the pickup; Grisha Hunters may guard the area.
2. Take the projectile to the catapult southwest of the Outskirts of Mammon beacon — the grassy area beside the statue where Ruk gives the Map of Mammon fragment. Interact with the catapult’s loading basket and confirm “Place Ammo?” (it will show Makeshift Projectile: 1).
3. The shot hits a mountainside near a construction site in the island chain west of Castigator’s Keep (east of the Gate of Mammon beacon). The resulting crater opens the Alchemist’s Workshop dungeon entrance.

1) Find the Makeshift Projectile (Outskirts of Mammon)
1. From the Outskirts of Mammon beacon, go to the southern edge of the starting island.
2. Look under the far end of the large stone bridge that runs south of the beacon. The Makeshift Projectile pickup sits on the cliff edge; a note is beside it.
3. Alternate route: you can also reach the pickup via the southeast rope bridges, past Vlas, into the lowland and along the southern cliff edge.
4. Be prepared: sources report Grisha Hunters may be guarding the pickup.

[IMAGE: Makeshift Projectile pickup near the southern cliff edge and bridge]

2) Return to the catapult and load the ammo
1. From the Outskirts of Mammon beacon, head southwest to the grassy area beside the statue where Ruk gives the Map of Mammon fragment. Soldiers watch this area.
2. Locate the catapult/loading basket beside the statue.
3. With the Makeshift Projectile in your inventory, interact with the loading basket.
4. At the “Place Ammo?” prompt choose “Yes.” The prompt will show “Makeshift Projectile: 1.” The catapult will fire after you confirm.

3) Follow the crater to the Alchemist’s Workshop
1. After the catapult fires, travel to the island chain west of Castigator’s Keep and east of the Gate of Mammon beacon.
2. Look for a fresh crater on a mountainside near a construction site west of Castigator’s Keep. That crater opens the entrance to the Alchemist’s Workshop dungeon.
3. Note: one recent source reports the false wall at that construction site can also be broken with a regular attack; this is reported as an alternative access method, but the catapult is the intended route in the confirmed sources.

[IMAGE: Crater near the construction site west of Castigator’s Keep that leads to the Alchemist’s Workshop]

4) What’s inside the Alchemist’s Workshop (reported contents)
- Near the entrance: an alchemical brew on a desk. Interacting with that desk reportedly tilts the camera until you use the desk prompt again.
- Inner chamber: a Synaptic Vessel and five Glimpse (reported).
- Bottom of the elevator shaft: a Clockwork Shardstone Tarstone guarded by a Shell Snatcher (reported).

Sources agree on these workshop rewards and the elevator-shaft tarstone placement; wording and exact camera behavior are reported in guide sources.

Common mistakes
- Searching the catapult for the Makeshift Projectile. The pickup is on the southern edge of the Outskirts starting island (bridge/rope-bridge routes), not on the catapult itself.
- Going to the wrong construction site. The crater opens in the island chain west of Castigator’s Keep (east of the Gate of Mammon beacon), not immediately adjacent to the catapult launcher.
- Assuming the projectile lands near the launcher. The projectile travels to the distant mountainside/construction site described above.

What happens next
- Enter the newly opened Alchemist’s Workshop to collect the reported Synaptic Vessel, five Glimpse, and to reach the Clockwork Shardstone Tarstone at the bottom of the elevator shaft (guarded by a Shell Snatcher).
- If you prefer, one recent report indicates the false wall at the construction site can also be broken by a regular attack; that source presents the catapult as the intended route but not an absolute requirement.
