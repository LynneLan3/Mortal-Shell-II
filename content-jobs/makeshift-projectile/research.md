# Research: Makeshift Projectile (CREATE)

Scope: create `/mortal-shell-ii/makeshift-projectile/`. Answer where to find the item, how to load the catapult, where the shot lands, and what the Alchemist's Workshop contains. Do not create a broader Mammon map or sidearm index.

## Confirmed facts

- The Makeshift Projectile is siege ammunition in the Mammon region and is used with a catapult.
- The pickup is at the southern edge of the Outskirts of Mammon starting island, below the far end of the large stone bridge south of the Outskirts of Mammon beacon. A second route reaches it through the southeast rope bridges, past Vlas, to the lowland and southern cliff edge. A note is beside the item; Grisha Hunters may guard it.
- The catapult is southwest of the Outskirts of Mammon beacon in a grassy area beside the statue where Ruk gives the Map of Mammon fragment. Soldiers watch the area.
- Return with the item, interact with the loading basket, choose “Yes” at the “Place Ammo?” prompt showing `Makeshift Projectile: 1`, and the catapult fires.
- The projectile strikes a mountainside near a construction site west of Castigator's Keep, in the island chain west of Castigator's Keep and east of the Gate of Mammon beacon. The crater opens the entrance to the Alchemist's Workshop dungeon.
- Reported workshop contents: an alchemical brew on a desk that tilts the camera until the desk prompt is used again; a Synaptic Vessel and five Glimpse in an inner chamber; and a Clockwork Shardstone Tarstone at the bottom of the elevator shaft guarded by a Shell Snatcher.
- One recent source reports the false wall can also be broken with a regular attack, meaning the catapult is the intended route but not necessarily the only access method. Phrase this as source-reported and do not make it the main route.

## Evidence boundary and conflicts

- GameSpot and AllThingsHow agree on the Mammon route, item, catapult, and workshop. Exact cardinal wording varies slightly; use named Beacons and landmarks instead of coordinates.
