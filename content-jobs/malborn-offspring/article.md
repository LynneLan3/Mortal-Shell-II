Short answer — How to beat Malborn Offspring:
Survive each full delayed cleaving string, build the boss’s Stagger gauge, deal heavy damage on the brief break, then reset; expect a slow resurrection after the first bar (use that as a limited damage window), and watch for a lightning‑infused second phase with faster swings and ranged bolts. Approach from Faithful’s Shelter Beacon and remember to interact with the arena tendril/ribcage after the kill to extract the gland.

Route (from the nearest Beacon)
1. Fast travel to Faithful’s Shelter Beacon (cleanse it first if possible so the checkpoint is available).
2. Go up the nearby stairs and take the right-hand route.
3. Cross the narrow stone bridge over the gap.
4. Climb the long stone staircase to the circular boss arena where Malborn Offspring waits.

Notes on unlocking the area
- The Unfound Path is gated by Ova obtained from the Corrupted Gate dungeons; sources agree an Ova requirement opens the area but disagree on the exact threshold (some write five gates are enough; others report a higher total). (AllThingsHow / Mortal Shell II Wiki / Gamer Guides)
- The Unfound Path contains three tendril branches: Faithful’s Shelter (Malborn Offspring), Bridge of Procession (Orrem), and Obsidian Vault (The Monolith). All three branches must be cleared to open the central platform toward Zmey the Unbidden. (AllThingsHow)

Fight overview — order of operations
1. Phase 1: Read full cleaving strings → build Stagger → punish briefly → disengage.
2. Transition: After the first health bar, the boss performs a slow resurrection; continue attacking but preserve enough stamina and space to react to phase two.
3. Phase 2: Boss gains lightning on weapon and attacks, adds ranged electric bolts, and swings faster; repeat the Stagger plan but expect shorter punish windows.

Detailed fight steps (do these in order)
1. Stand at mid‑range and watch the boss begin each chain. Malborn Offspring uses long cleaving strings (commonly five or six chained hits). Do not rely on a fixed rhythm: swings have deliberately uneven delays—watch the actual motion.
2. For each string, either dodge the full string or block where appropriate; do not attempt to attack after the first apparent pause (that is a common trap).
3. Be aware some strings contain a red‑circle unblockable attack. AllThingsHow reports one appearing on every third swing in some sequences; other sources confirm red‑circle attacks exist but do not commit to a universal cadence. If you see the red circle, dodge—do not attempt a normal parry or standard block.
4. While surviving strings, apply safe, measured damage to fill the Stagger gauge. When the gauge breaks, perform a short, reliable combo (one heavy or a few quick hits) to maximize damage, then disengage before the next string. Do not try to trade through the next chained sequence.
5. When the first health bar depletes, keep attacking during the boss’s slow resurrection animation to use that documented damage window—but conserve enough stamina and positioning to react when the second phase begins.
6. In phase two, expect:
   - Weapon swings with electrical effects and faster/aggressive timing.
   - Occasional ranged electric bolts/projectiles; avoid standing in-line with their path and dodge laterally.
   - Red‑circle unblockables remain a major threat; previous Stagger strategy still applies but punish windows are shorter—limit follow-ups to one or two safe hits before resetting.

Attacks, tells, and punish guidance (descriptive names)
- Delayed cleaving chain: watch the swing animation; dodge or guard the whole string. Punish after the final delayed hit and visible recovery.
- Red‑circle strike: dodge; refrain from conventional parries/blocks. Re-engage only after the red attack resolves.
- Stagger break: maintain measured pressure until the break, then heavy damage during the short stun, then disengage.
- Slow resurrection (transition): continue attacking during the rise but leave stamina/healing to handle phase two.
- Lightning phase strings / electric bolts: preserve stamina and avoid forcing long combos; return after the projectile sequence.

Shells, summons, and equipment notes
- Proxima is recommended by AllThingsHow and GameSpot for the second phase when her Ampereon Bond upgrades provide electricity immunity (this is conditional on having that Bond upgrade, not required). (/mortal-shell-ii/proxima/)
- Sester Genessa’s summons are useful for creating Stagger and healing windows, per checked guides. Use /mortal-shell-ii/sester-genessa/ for details.
- The checked guides list no spectral Shell summon available specifically for this arena; do not expect a Glimpse ally.
- No single weapon or upgrade level is required by the sources. Use a weapon and Shell you control consistently, preserve stamina, and consult the Tarstones and weapons pages for broader preparation (/mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/, /mortal-shell-ii/weapons/).

Rewards and related uncertainties
- Offspring Trophy: confirmed reward. GameSpot and the Mortal Shell II Wiki report the trophy is tradeable for 5,000 coins.
- Gloom: Gamer Guides reports 63,333 Gloom for this kill; AllThingsHow only describes “a large batch.” Treat the 63,333 figure as source‑reported and potentially version/run‑sensitive.
- Achievement: AllThingsHow reports an achievement named “Giga Chad” for the kill; this is source‑reported and should be verified against platform achievement lists before treating it as definitive.
- No checked source lists a Malborn‑specific pre‑kill trophy interaction or unique missable action inside this boss fight.

Post‑kill steps (must do this to complete the branch)
1. Move to the back of the arena and interact with the large fleshy ribcage/tendril structure to extract the gland. AllThingsHow says this triggers a short cutscene and a “Gloom Retrieved” prompt—defeating the boss alone does not complete the branch.
[IMAGE: Phase transition — Malborn rising during the slow resurrection]
2. Confirm the tendril is removed. Clear the Orrem and Monolith branches (if still uncleared). The central platform toward Zmey the Unbidden opens only after all three tendrils are gone. (AllThingsHow)
3. The post‑fight return Beacon name is spelled “Chamof of the Forsaken” in one AllThingsHow passage; the spelling and exact post‑fight return point differ across sources, so treat that name as uncertain.

Common mistakes
- Counting beats or parry timing instead of watching the actual swing animation; Malborn’s strings deliberately vary timing.
- Trying to parry or block a red‑circle unblockable (dodge instead).
- Chasing a full combo after a Stagger break in phase two—punish windows are shorter there.
- Spending all stamina or all healing during the resurrection window; you need resources at the phase two start.
- Skipping the post‑fight tendril interaction—defeating the boss alone does not complete the branch.

What happens next
1. Extract the gland from the arena tendril/ribcage to complete the local branch (AllThingsHow).
2. Clear the remaining Unfound Path tendrils (Orrem, The Monolith) to open the central platform.
3. Once the central platform opens, the route leads to Zmey the Unbidden (endgame progression).
4. Patch notes checked (Balance Patch 1 and Hotfix 2.0) do not list direct Malborn Offspring adjustments; say only that no direct Malborn adjustment was found in those checked patch notes—future patches may differ. (Cold Symmetry post / Steam Community hotfix notes / GamesRadar+ summary)
