## Scope and confidence

This is an evidence pass for the Faithful's Shelter boss in The Unfound Path. The stable, cross-checked facts are the dungeon segment, the nearby Beacon route, the two-phase structure, delayed cleaving strings, lightning in the second phase, the Offspring Trophy, and the post-fight tendril progression. Exact Ova thresholds, exact Gloom payout, attack cadence, and the precise name of the post-fight return Beacon are not stable across the checked sources and are kept as uncertain below.

## Location and route

- **Dungeon:** The Unfound Path, in the Faithful's Shelter segment.
- **Nearest named Beacon:** Faithful's Shelter Beacon.
- **Route from the Beacon:** Go up the nearby stairs, take the right-hand route, cross the narrow stone bridge over the gap, then climb the long stone staircase to the circular boss arena. AllThingsHow gives this sequence; Gamer Guides independently places Malborn Offspring at Faithful's Shelter and describes the same initial stair/right-hand route.
- **Route context:** The Unfound Path has three tendril branches: Faithful's Shelter (Malborn Offspring), Bridge of Procession (Orrem), and Obsidian Vault (The Monolith). All three guardians must be defeated to open the central platform leading to Zmey the Unbidden.
- No map x/y coordinates are used. The repository does not have an audited coordinate dataset for this route.

## Prerequisites and entry uncertainty

- The Unfound Path is a late-campaign area unlocked through Ova obtained from the six Corrupted Gate dungeons in Fainweald and Mammon.
- The checked sources agree that Corrupted Gate progression and Ova are required, but conflict on the exact threshold and whether five or all six gate clearances are sufficient. AllThingsHow's final-dungeon guide describes each major boss Beacon as providing 26 Ova and says the route opens after the required Ova are gathered; the Mortal Shell II Wiki says five gates provide enough and another AllThingsHow route page reports a threshold above 168. These numbers do not reconcile cleanly, so the article should say “after the Ova requirement opens The Unfound Path,” not assert one exact total.
- The Faithful's Shelter Beacon should be cleansed before the boss attempt if possible so the checkpoint is available. A specific “all bosses must be defeated first” order is not established; the three Unfound Path branches are described as separate guardian routes.

## Fight mechanics

### Phase 1

- Malborn Offspring uses long cleaving strings, commonly described as five or six chained hits.
- The timing within a string is deliberately uneven: animation speeds and delays change, so counting a fixed rhythm or attacking after the first apparent pause is unsafe.
- Some strings contain a red-circle unblockable. AllThingsHow describes one on every third swing in some sequences; other sources confirm the red-circle threat but do not establish a universal cadence. Treat the “every third swing” detail as source-reported, not a guaranteed rule.
- Safe damage builds the boss's Stagger gauge. When the gauge breaks, the boss is vulnerable for a short punish window. Use a short, reliable combo, then reset rather than trading through the next string.

### Transition

- Depleting the first health bar starts a slow resurrection/revival animation rather than ending the encounter.
- This is a documented damage window: continue attacking while the boss rises, but leave enough stamina and space to react when the second phase starts.

### Phase 2

- The weapon and attacks gain lightning/electrical effects.
- Swings become faster and more aggressive, and the boss adds ranged electric bolts/projectiles.
- Red-circle unblockables remain a major threat. The Stagger-gauge plan still applies, but safe punishes are shorter and greedier follow-ups are less reliable.

## Attacks, tells, and punish windows

| Attack / tell | Evidence-backed response | Punish window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delayed cleaving chain | Watch the actual swing; do not count beats; dodge or guard the complete string | After the final delayed hit and visible recovery |
| Red-circle strike | Dodge; do not attempt a normal parry/block | Re-engage only after the red attack resolves |
| Stagger gauge break | Keep measured pressure until the gauge breaks | Heavy damage during the short stun, then disengage |
| Slow resurrection | Continue attacking during the rise | Free transition damage, ending before the new phase resumes |
| Lightning-infused phase-two string | Preserve stamina and use the same full-string read | One or two safe hits after recovery; do not force a full combo |
| Electric projectile / bolt | Dodge laterally or out of the line; avoid standing still at range | Return after the projectile sequence, not during it |

No checked source supplies reliable frame data, a complete attack list, or a universal phase-health percentage. The article should label attack names as descriptive where they are not in-game UI names.

## Shell and weapon recommendations

- **Proxima:** AllThingsHow and GameSpot both recommend Proxima for the second phase when her Ampereon Bond upgrades provide electricity immunity. This is conditional on the relevant Bond upgrades, not a claim that Proxima is mandatory.
- **Sester Genessa:** Both sources describe Genessa's summoned doubles as useful distractions that create Stagger and healing windows. Use the local page name `sester-genessa` for the internal link.
- **Summon:** The checked boss guides list no spectral Shell summon for this arena, so do not promise a Glimpse-based ally.
- **Weapon:** No source establishes a required weapon, exact upgrade level, or mandatory Tarstone. The practical recommendation is to use the weapon and Shell the player can control consistently, preserve stamina, and consider the local [Tarstones](/mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/) guide for defensive or electrical mitigation context. Do not invent an elemental weakness.

## Rewards, trophy, and missable status

- **Reward:** Offspring Trophy. GameSpot and the Mortal Shell II Wiki say it is tradeable for 5,000 coins.
- **Gloom:** Gamer Guides lists 63,333 Gloom; AllThingsHow only says a large batch. Keep 63,333 explicitly source-reported and potentially version/run-sensitive, or omit the number from the answer-first summary.
- **Achievement:** AllThingsHow reports “Giga Chad” as the unlock for the kill. This should be presented as source-reported unless independently checked against an achievement listing.
- **Missable:** No checked source identifies a Malborn-specific pre-kill trophy interaction. The article should not import the Nameless Captive's Peter's Perfect Parry warning into this fight. Completing this guardian contributes to the normal Unfound Path route; the checked final-dungeon guide says there is no side-dungeon missable content inside the Unfound Path, but this is not a guarantee about every future patch.

## What happens after the kill

1. Move to the back of the arena and interact with the large fleshy ribcage/tendril structure to extract the gland. AllThingsHow says this triggers a short cutscene and a “Gloom Retrieved” prompt; defeating the boss alone is not the complete branch-clear step.
2. Confirm the tendril is removed, then clear the Orrem and Monolith branches if they remain. The central platform opens only after all three Unfound Path tendrils are gone.
3. The opened platform leads to Zmey the Unbidden. This is endgame progression, not a claim that Malborn must be fought last.
4. The post-fight return Beacon is spelled “Chamof of the Forsaken” in one AllThingsHow passage and differs from other route naming. Keep that destination name uncertain or describe it as the post-fight return point rather than asserting the spelling.

## Trophy / missable and patch notes

- No Malborn-specific missable action was found in the checked sources.
- Balance Patch 1's developer-published notes list direct changes to The Lost Child and The Monolith, plus economy, weapon, Tarstone, seal, and general fixes. They do not list a Malborn Offspring health, damage, animation, reward, or lightning change.
- Hotfix 2.0 lists crash/stability/performance fixes, a Slayer Seal menu fix, and a riposte weapon-stealing fix. It does not list a Malborn-specific change.
- Therefore the page should say **no direct Malborn adjustment was found in the checked patch notes**, rather than claiming the boss has never changed. Future platform/build differences remain possible.

## Practical player insights

- Treat the fight as “survive the full string → build Stagger → cash out during the break → reset.” The changing delays are the core difficulty, not a fixed parry rhythm.
- Keep the camera and stamina available for the late hit in each chain. A punish after the first slow swing is the common trap.
- During the resurrection, damage is valuable, but do not spend all stamina or healing before phase two begins.
- If electricity immunity is not available, spacing and lateral movement matter more than chasing damage; the sources do not establish a universal lightning counter.
- If the boss feels under-upgraded, leave through the Beacon and improve the build elsewhere. The checked sources describe the branch as endgame content, not as a forced first encounter in The Unfound Path.

2. **Red-circle cadence:** “every third swing” appears in one guide's description of some strings; do not turn it into a universal pattern.
3. **Gloom:** 63,333 is a Gamer Guides listing, not cross-checked by the other guides.
4. **Post-fight destination spelling:** “Chamof of the Forsaken” appears in one source and should not be normalized by guessing.
