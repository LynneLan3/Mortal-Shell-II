Short answer
- Monolith is the guardian at the end of the Obsidian Vault branch of The Unfound Path. Beat it by avoiding its jump/slam openings, using short punish strings on the exposed fleshy center, and timing dodges during the beam phase (dodge to the beam timing rather than rolling early). After killing the construct you must sever the red gland in the arena to clear the Obsidian Vault tendril. Reward: a Monolith Fragment that reports list as tradeable for 5,000 (the in-game currency label may differ by build/UI). (Sources: GameSpot, All Things How, Nerd’s Chalk; see Patch note caveats below.)

Location, nearest Beacon, and route (follow these steps)
1. Reach The Unfound Path via your Ova progression (see the site’s [Ova guide](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/) for the campaign gate information).  
2. From the Unfound Path central area, enter the Obsidian Vault branch. (All Things How and GameSpot place Monolith on this branch.)
3. Activate/use the Obsidian Vault Beacon (the nearest named checkpoint reported). Nerd’s Chalk describes the arena as beyond that Beacon.
4. Proceed through the branch to the dedicated Monolith arena—Monolith waits at the end of this branch. There is no confirmed route to Monolith through other Unfound Path branches. (Sources: All Things How, GameSpot, Nerd’s Chalk.)

Preparation checklist (do these before attempting)
1. Activate the Obsidian Vault Beacon and refill healing/items. The fight is reported as long; avoid spending all healing in the opening half. (GameSpot / All Things How / Nerd’s Chalk.)
2. Bring a Shell, primary weapon, reliable healing, and a sidearm fallback for safe chip damage if desired.
3. Expect a solo fight—All Things How reports Monolith has no spectral Shell summon; do not budget a Glimpse for a summon ally.
4. Verify your platform’s installed patches (see Patch changes below for PC/console rollout notes).

Shell / weapon recommendations (source-backed suggestions, not requirements)
- Nerd’s Chalk: Proxima for durability; Black Needle for reach/poke at the exposed center; Burnt Effigy if you want mitigation against Burn during the laser phase.  
- All Things How: a mobile Shell such as Genessa, the Wayward paired with the Axatana to favor movement and avoidance.  
Link references: [Proxima](/mortal-shell-ii/proxima/), [Black Needle](/mortal-shell-ii/black-needle/), [Axatana](/mortal-shell-ii/axatana/), [weapons](/mortal-shell-ii/weapons/).

Fight procedure (do these steps in order)
1. Opening phase (above roughly half health):
   - Watch for the boss lifting/jumping—the principal early pressure is large-body jump/slam. Move out of the landing shadow/impact line as the boss rises; do not stand under the drop.
   - After the slam recovery, the boss’s two stone halves often open to expose a fleshy center. Move into that opening, land a short punish string (about two quick hits), then disengage. Nerd’s Chalk warns against chasing a third hit.
2. Manage heals and resets:
   - The reliable baseline strategy is movement + short punish strings and leaving the arena center/landing area before the next slam.
   - Refill and conserve enough healing for the beam phase; the fight is described as long.
3. Beam phase (starts at roughly half health):
   - Expect beams crossing the arena and rotating/sweeping beams. Keep the beam origin and boss in view; do not try to outrun a beam in a straight line.
   - Time lateral dodges to the beam’s arrival—dodge on the beam’s timing rather than rolling early. Maintain readable spacing; both close-timed dodging and mid-range spacing are reported viable (sources disagree on a single best distance).
4. Continue the short-punish rhythm where safe:
   - Only press when the center is open and the boss is recovering. If the boss repositions or the center closes, create space and wait for the next reliable tell.
5. Kill and post-kill interaction:
   - After the boss falls, walk to the red gland hanging in the arena and sever it with the interaction prompt. This step is necessary to clear the Obsidian Vault branch’s tendril contribution; killing the construct alone does not complete the progression interaction. (All Things How.)

Attack response quick-reference table
| Attack / tell | Response | Punish window |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Jump / body slam; boss rises and a landing area forms | Sidestep or leave the landing area; do not stand under the drop | Return after impact/recovery; use the open center for a short string |
| Center opens between the stone halves | Move in for the fleshy center | About two quick hits, then disengage; sources warn against greed |
| Center closes or side-roll / repositioning motion begins | Stop chasing damage and create space | None until a new opening |
| Beam fires across the arena | Keep a readable distance and dodge when the beam reaches you | Gap between beam sets, if the arena is clear |
| Rotating / sweeping beams | Circle with the arena and time the dodge to the sweep; do not roll early | Brief gap after the sweep if the boss is in recovery |
| Awkward or uncertain animation | Dodge rather than force an unpracticed parry | Re-engage after the motion ends |

Notes on phase counts and naming
- Sources agree on a major transition around roughly half health: an opening slam-heavy stretch and a beam-driven back half. Some sources frame the escalation as two phases; Nerd’s Chalk uses three stages phrasing. Use “opening phase” and “beam phase” for practical play; treat the “three stages” description as source framing rather than an additional required health threshold.

Rewards, currency, and missable status
- Reward: Monolith Fragment. Multiple reports list a 5,000 trade value for the Fragment; the UI may label the currency differently (coins, tar, etc.) depending on build—follow the in-game label. (GameSpot, All Things How.)  
- A separate checklist source lists an approximate 65,511 Gloom figure for the encounter but that exact Gloom number is not cross-verified in the reviewed sources and should be treated as uncertain.  
- Missable / trophy: No Monolith-specific missable trophy or pre-kill interaction is confirmed by the reviewed sources. The red-gland sever is mandatory for route progression but is not reported as a separate missable trophy. Do not treat the absence of confirmation as definitive for every platform/build.

Balance Patch 1 (what changed and platform notes)
- Reported Monolith changes in the developer announcement / repost (and corroborated by GamesRadar and other patch reporters): Base damage -19%, maximum health -10%, and several Monolith attacks were retimed to be fairer/readable.  
- Patch reporting indicates PC availability first; console rollout timing varies by platform and may lag—verify your installed patch version before attributing the nerfs to your platform. Exact per-attack timing deltas are not published in the reviewed sources. (Developer repost / GamesRadar.)

Practical player insights and execution tips
- Treat this as a spacing and patience fight: short repeatable bursts are safer than long combos. (Repeated across sources.)
- Keep the boss and beam origin in view; running around the boss’s back risks camera issues that trade a readable slam for confusion.
- If a beam catches you while sprinting straight, switch to a timed lateral dodge rather than trying to outrun it in-line.
- Pick one recovery plan for the beam phase and stick to it: practiced close-distance dodging or holding a mid-range position. Sources disagree on a single universal safest distance—make it a personal execution choice.
- After a death, change only one variable (spacing, punish length, or a single loadout tweak) instead of overhauling everything.

Common mistakes
- Standing in the slam landing zone when the boss rises.  
- Chasing a third hit on the fleshy center and getting caught by the next animation.  
- Rolling early during beam sweeps instead of timing the dodge to the beam’s arrival.  
- Spending all healing in the opening half of the fight; the encounter is described as long and requires reserve.  
- Forgetting to sever the red gland after the kill and leaving without confirming the tendril clear.  
- Assuming PC patch numbers automatically apply on consoles—verify installed version.

What happens next (progression steps)
1. Sever the red gland in the arena after the kill to clear the Obsidian Vault branch’s tendril contribution. Confirm the transition/progression state before leaving. (All Things How.)  
2. The other Unfound Path guardians reported by reviewed sources are Orrem (Bridge of Procession) and Malborn Offspring (Faithful’s Shelter). After clearing all three guardians’ tendril contributions, the route leads toward Zmey the Unbidden. Link: [Orrem](/mortal-shell-ii/orrem-discarded-golem/) and the Boss Hub when the Integrator card is available. (GameSpot, All Things How, Mortal Shell II Wiki.)

Where sources disagree or data is unverified (three-stage framing, exact currency label, a single “best” beam distance, exact Gloom amount, console patch timing), the guide preserves that uncertainty and qualifies those points rather than presenting them as definitive.
