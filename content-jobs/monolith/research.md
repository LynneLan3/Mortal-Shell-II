# Monolith — research dossier

## Evidence summary

The strongest cross-checked facts are:

- Monolith is the guardian at the end of the **Obsidian Vault** branch of **The Unfound Path**, the late-game dungeon.
- The fight begins with large-body jump/slam pressure. After roughly the halfway health point, beam attacks and a rotating beam pattern are introduced.
- The reliable baseline is movement, short punish strings, and leaving the arena center/landing area before the next slam. During beams, dodge on the beam's timing rather than rolling early.
- The reward is a **Monolith Fragment**. GameSpot and All Things How both report that it can be traded for 5,000 coins/tar; the exact currency label should follow the in-game UI if it differs by build.
- After the kill, the red gland in the arena must be severed. This clears the Obsidian Vault branch's tendril contribution; killing the boss alone is not the complete progression interaction.
- Balance Patch 1 reports a 19% base-damage reduction, 10% maximum-health reduction, and retimed Monolith attacks. The detailed patch post was surfaced through a developer announcement repost, while the current boss reports independently repeat the same values.

## Location, nearest Beacon, and route

**Confirmed location:** The Unfound Path → Obsidian Vault → Monolith arena. GameSpot identifies Monolith as one of the three Unfound Path guardian bosses; All Things How names Obsidian Vault as its branch and says the other guardians are Orrem in Bridge of Procession and Malborn Offspring in Faithful's Shelter.

**Nearest Beacon:** The **Obsidian Vault Beacon** is the nearest named checkpoint in the available reporting. Nerd's Chalk describes the arena as being past that Beacon. The accessible sources do not provide an auditable turn-by-turn interior map or a second checkpoint immediately before the arena, so do not invent one.

**Route, evidence-bounded:**

1. Reach the late-game **Unfound Path** after the Ova-gated campaign progression. The exact Ova threshold is not needed for this item and should link to the site's [Ova guide](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/) rather than be repeated as a hard number here.
2. Use the central Unfound Path area to enter the **Obsidian Vault** branch.
3. Activate or use the Obsidian Vault Beacon, then continue through that branch to its dedicated arena.
4. Monolith waits at the end of the branch. There is no evidence that the boss can be reached through the Bridge of Procession or Faithful's Shelter branches.

The final-dungeon branch order is flexible in the sources: each of the three guardians protects a different tendril. The full boss-order source calls Monolith a mandatory endgame guardian, but does not establish that it must be the first or second guardian killed.

## Prerequisites and preparation

- Unlock access to The Unfound Path through the main Ova progression. The exact threshold is handled by the site's [Ova guide](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/), and broad source reporting has not been consistent enough to restate a number here.
- Reach the Obsidian Vault branch and use its Beacon.
- Bring a Shell, primary weapon, healing, and sidearm resource you can execute reliably. All Things How says Monolith has no spectral Shell summon, so plan for a solo fight rather than budgeting a Glimpse for a boss ally.
- The fight is long according to GameSpot, All Things How, and Nerd's Chalk. Refill before the attempt and avoid spending all healing during the opening half.
- A sidearm can be a practical fallback for safe chip damage while keeping distance, but the available reports do not establish a single mandatory sidearm or a universal damage build.

## Shell and weapon recommendations (source-backed, not mandatory)

Nerd's Chalk specifically recommends **Proxima** for durability, **Black Needle** for quick reach-based pokes at the exposed center, and **Burnt Effigy** to mitigate Burn during the laser phase. All Things How offers a different practical pairing: a mobile Shell such as **Genessa, the Wayward** with the **Axatana**, because the encounter rewards movement over trading blows.

These are recommendations, not prerequisites. The sources do not prove that either build is required, nor do they establish that one is universally best. ## Fight mechanics, phases, tells, and punish windows

### Phase 1 / above roughly half health

Monolith is a large stone construct. Multiple reports describe jumping body slams as the principal early pressure. The tell is the boss lifting/jumping before the landing zone is established. Move out of the landing shadow/impact line, then return only after the landing and recovery have finished.

The most repeatable punish is short: attack the exposed fleshy center when it opens, land about two hits, then reset. Nerd's Chalk describes the boss's two halves opening around a fleshy middle and calls that the reliable damage target. Do not chase a third hit if the center closes or the next animation begins.

### Phase 2 / below roughly half health

GameSpot and All Things How agree that beam attacks begin after the boss passes approximately half health. All Things How describes beams crossing the arena and a rotating version that sweeps the space; the practical response is to keep moving and dodge as the beam reaches the player. Do not roll early and remain in the beam's path.

The reports use different phase counts. Some describe a simple first half / beam phase; Nerd's Chalk frames the escalation as three stages, with an opening beam-heavy stretch and a later laser-heavy closeout. This is a presentation difference, not a proven extra boss health threshold. The page should use “opening phase” and “beam phase,” and label the three-stage description as source terminology if mentioned.

### Attack response table

| Attack / tell | Response | Punish window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jump / body slam; boss rises and a landing area forms | Sidestep or leave the landing area; do not stand under the drop | Return after impact/recovery; use the open center for a short string |
| Center opens between the stone halves | Move in for the fleshy center | About two quick hits, then disengage; source guidance warns against greed |
| Center closes or side-roll / repositioning motion begins | Stop chasing damage and create space | None until a new opening |
| Beam fires across the arena | Keep a readable distance and dodge when the beam reaches you | Gap between beam sets, if the arena is clear |
| Rotating / sweeping beams | Circle with the arena and time the dodge to the sweep; do not roll early | Brief gap after the sweep, only if the boss is still in recovery |
| Awkward or uncertain animation | Dodge rather than force an unpracticed parry | Re-engage after the motion ends |

The exact names of individual attacks, frame timings, and whether every move is parryable are not consistently documented. The article should not turn the source's descriptive labels into official move names.

## Patch changes

Balance Patch 1 is reported as live on PC, with console rollout reported separately and subject to platform timing. The Monolith changes listed in the developer announcement/repost are:

- Base damage: **-19%**
- Maximum health: **-10%**
- Some attacks retimed to be fairer/readable

GameRadar's patch reporting, the developer-announcement repost, and the current boss reports agree on the damage/health reductions and retiming. Exact per-attack timing deltas are not published in the sources reviewed. Do not state that the reductions apply to every platform at the same time; advise console players to verify the installed patch.

## Practical player insights

- Treat this as a spacing and patience fight, not a damage race. Short repeatable bursts are safer than full combos.
- Keep the boss and the beam origin in view; chasing around the back can trade a readable slam for a camera problem.
- If the first beam catches you while sprinting straight away, switch to a timed lateral dodge rather than trying to outrun it in a dead line.
- Keep one recovery plan for the beam phase: either stay at a practiced close distance or use the middle of the arena at range. The sources disagree on which distance is universally safest, so present this as an execution choice.
- Change one variable after a death (spacing, punish length, or loadout) instead of changing the whole build; this is an editorial practical recommendation derived from the repeated source emphasis on repeatable timing.

## Conflicting or uncertain claims to preserve

- **Two phases vs. three stages:** sources agree on a major transition around half health but package the back half differently. Use two broad phases and label “three stages” as a source framing.
- **Exact reward currency/value:** 5,000 trade value is repeated; whether the UI calls it coins or tar and the separate 65,511 Gloom figure are not fully cross-verified. Keep the 5,000 value qualified and omit the Gloom number unless labeled uncertain.
- **Best distance during beams:** close dodging and ranged spacing are both reported as viable. Do not promise one universal answer.
- **Exact Beacon-to-arena turns and Ova threshold:** named branch and Beacon are cross-checked, but the reviewed sources do not provide enough auditable route detail for a precise turn count or a hard threshold in this page.
- **Trophy:** no Monolith-specific missable is confirmed by the reviewed sources; do not convert that absence of evidence into a definitive “no trophy” statement.
- **Console patch timing:** PC availability is reported; console rollout timing should be qualified by installed version.
