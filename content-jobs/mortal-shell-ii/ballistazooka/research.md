# Item 47 research: Ballistazooka Location

## Scope and evidence boundary

This research is for Item 47 only: `mortal-shell-ii/ballistazooka`. It covers the current launch location, the route into Sentry's Grave, the Subjugated Guardian encounter, the reward and practical use of the Ballistazooka, trophy / achievement implications, patch context, conflicts, useful internal links, and source URLs.

No map coordinates are asserted. The route is expressed through named Beacons, buildings, stairs, portals, and dungeon landmarks because current sources do not provide one audited coordinate system. No shared hub, config, plan, deployment, IndexNow, or unrelated item changes belong to this checkpoint.

## Direct answer supported by the sources

- The Ballistazooka is a **sidearm** awarded after defeating the **Subjugated Guardian** at the end / lowermost level of **Sentry's Grave**.
- Sentry's Grave is in the **Lonesome Spire** / fort area near the **Gate of Mammon** in Mammon. The practical route anchor is **Gate of Mammon Beacon**.
- Current route sources agree on the named sequence `Gate of Mammon area → Lonesome Spire / interior fort access → Sentry's Grave → descend to Subjugated Guardian`, but describe the dungeon transition as stairs, a doorway, or a portal. The article should preserve that naming difference instead of inventing a fixed turn count.
- The Ballistazooka is not the weapon needed to win its own unlock fight. Bring a melee weapon and Shell abilities, then collect the weapon reward after the Guardian dies.
- The Steam Global Achievements page confirms the individual achievement **Speared — Unlock the Ballistazooka** and the collection achievement **Guns. Lots of Guns — Unlock all Sidearms**.
- Current guide evidence describes the weapon as a heavy, deliberate ranged sidearm. It is useful for creating safe ranged openings, punishing an enemy after an attack, or finishing a weakened target, but it is not presented as a fast replacement for a primary melee weapon.
- Current guides describe sidearms as compatible with Tarstones and upgradable at the Tarforge after the relevant sidearm-enhancement function is available. Do not assert an exact damage number, cost, ammunition count, or controller binding without stronger evidence.

## Route and prerequisites

### Stable route facts

The stable player-facing access checks are:

1. Reach the **Gate of Mammon Beacon** in the Mammon area.
2. Use the nearby stairs / fort interior route toward **Lonesome Spire**.
3. Find the entrance transition into **Sentry's Grave**.
4. Descend through the dungeon to its deepest section.
5. Defeat the **Subjugated Guardian** and collect the Ballistazooka reward.

The checked sources do not establish a Ballistazooka-specific key, Ova threshold, Mether's Breath threshold, quest, or dialogue requirement. They do establish the Mammon / Gate of Mammon route and the boss-gated reward. If the current game build blocks the broader Mammon route behind a Corrupted Gate, follow the in-game Beacon cleanse or progression prompt rather than applying an unverified universal count.

### Current route detail

The sources describe the approach with slightly different landmarks:

- **AllThingsHow:** from Gate of Mammon Beacon, go northwest up the first stairs, continue around the building to an indoor area, then take the stairs down into Sentry's Grave inside Lonesome Spire.
- **Thumb Wars:** from the Gate of Mammon / Corrupted Gate-side spawn, take the stairs up twice, pass a heavy knight and archers, enter the room behind them, and use the doorway on the right to reach the dungeon.
- **Nerd's Chalk:** start at Corrupted Gate, travel toward Lonesome Spire, then use the portal there to enter Sentry's Grave.

These descriptions point to the same castle / Lonesome Spire interior but do not justify one exact compass route or a claim that every platform labels the transition identically. The article should use **Gate of Mammon**, **Lonesome Spire**, **stairs / doorway / portal**, and **Sentry's Grave** as the route anchors.

### Sentry's Grave navigation

- The dungeon is reported as a largely linear descent. Keep moving downward rather than searching the upper approach for a loose Ballistazooka pickup.
- Current guides report repeated ranged **Ballista Head / crossbowhead** enemies in the dungeon. Their bolts make the approach itself a larger risk than a simple item pickup.
- The arena is at the bottom. Two similar ranged enemies are reported on elevated ledges around the Subjugated Guardian; the sources recommend using the side stairs to reach them or using positioning so their shots can strike one another / the boss.
- No source provides a reliable room-number system or map coordinate. Do not add one.

## Subjugated Guardian fight and reward

The Subjugated Guardian is the boss / miniboss at the end of Sentry's Grave. The checked sources agree that defeating it awards the Ballistazooka automatically or as the post-fight pickup. Gamer Guides additionally lists **7,008 Gloom** as a reported reward; Thumb Wars reports Gloom, a Glimpse, and the Ballistazooka without a number. Because the reward table is not consistent across sources, the article may say that Gloom / Glimpse rewards are reported, but should not state 7,008 as a universal fixed reward unless clearly attributed.

Practical fight guidance supported by current reports:

1. Enter with a melee weapon and Shell abilities; the Ballistazooka cannot be assumed before this fight.
2. Treat the two elevated ranged enemies as immediate pressure. Use the stairs on either side of the arena to reach them, or reposition so their bolts are less likely to crossfire you.
3. Close distance on the Subjugated Guardian when possible. AllThingsHow reports that this limits its ranged pressure and leaves head-slam attacks that can be blocked or parried; Thumb Wars recommends dodging bolts and using close-range attacks, then hardening / parrying the closer attacks when the timing is comfortable.
4. Use short attack strings and reset rather than committing while multiple bolts are active.
5. After the Guardian dies, confirm the Ballistazooka has been added before leaving the arena. The trophy / achievement should be tied to the unlock, not to a separate Tarforge upgrade.

The reports differ on whether the boss bolts are best described as explosive on impact or simply heavy / damaging. Preserve the practical warning—avoid standing in the ranged line and close distance when safe—without publishing an unverified damage phase or attack count.

## Ballistazooka use and upgrades

- **Role:** heavy ranged sidearm / ballista. The most reliable public descriptions call it heavy and unwieldy, with deliberate ranged damage rather than rapid mobile fire.
- **Best use:** create distance, punish a committed enemy, open an encounter before melee, or finish a weakened target. It complements a melee weapon rather than replacing it.
- **Customization:** current launch guides identify Tarstones as part of sidearm customization. Exact compatible Tarstone lists, damage values, reload timing, ammunition behavior, and capacity are not sufficiently cross-validated for this item checkpoint.
- **Upgrade path:** the local Tarforge guide identifies the **Obsidian Lathe** as the component that unlocks sidearm enhancement at Franz's Tarforge in Marrow Keep. The local Sidearms guide owns the roster-level upgrade note. Link to those pages rather than duplicating the whole Tarforge system.
- **Practical caution:** because the weapon is deliberate and heavy, fire from a safe opening instead of trying to use it as a close-range panic button. The checked sources do not establish a universal best Shell or build for it.

## Conflicts and uncertainties to preserve

- **Entry label:** sources call the access point Gate of Mammon, Corrupted Gate, Lonesome Spire, or a fort interior. Use the stable named anchors and explain that the stairs / doorway / portal descriptions vary.
- **Location wording:** Sentry's Grave is described as inside Lonesome Spire and as a dungeon reached from the Gate of Mammon / Corrupted Gate area. Do not turn this into a map coordinate or claim that one named structure is a separate overworld region without direct evidence.
- **Reward table:** all checked sources agree on Ballistazooka after the Guardian, but reported Gloom / Glimpse rewards differ. Keep the weapon reward as certain and secondary currency as source-reported.
- **Boss attack description:** sources differ on explosive bolts versus heavy ranged bolts. Publish the shared tactical advice, not a precise attack-property claim.
- **Exact stats:** no reliable public stat sheet was found for damage, reload time, range, ammunition capacity, or Tarstone compatibility. Omit fixed numbers.
- **Patch scope:** the official Balance Patch 1 note is a dated snapshot and does not prove later builds or equal platform rollout.
