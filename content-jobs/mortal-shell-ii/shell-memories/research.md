# Item 45 research: Seeking the Past / Shell Memories / NG+ issue

## Scope and evidence boundary

This research is for Item 45 only: the `mortal-shell-ii/shell-memories` guide. It covers the **Seeking the Past** trophy/achievement, the all-Shell-Memories requirement, the Glimpse and Bond progression loop, the special Genessa interaction, and the current New Game Plus planning/issue reports. It does not expand into unrelated trophy routes, Shell combat builds, or a complete NG+ guide.

No reliable item-specific image was found for this topic. The guide can be complete without an image, so do not add an image placeholder or create `public/images/shell-memories/`.

## Direct answer supported by the sources

- **Seeking the Past** requires watching **all Shell Memories**. The Steam global achievements page and PlayStation trophy listings use that wording directly.
- The current guide evidence models the permanent roster as **eight playable Shells**, with **five Memories per Shell**, for **40 Memories total**. Harros is a temporary prologue Shell and is not part of the eight-Shell completion count in the checked trophy guide. The 40 total is supported by the current achievement checklist and 100% guide, but it is not stated on the Steam achievement entry itself.
- Shell Memories are opened through Shell Bond progression. The checked skills guide says Glimpses deepen a selected Shell's Bond, raise Bond Tier I–IV, reveal further Memories, and open more ability tiers. Glimpses are not refundable according to that guide.
- For seven Shells, the current 100% guide directs the player to Zhirelle in Marrowkeep. It identifies **Genessa as the exception**: use the Stray Sester in the training arena rather than Zhirelle for Genessa's Bond progression.
- A practical completion route is to finish ordinary first-run cleanup before `Send Ova`, then use NG+ cycles to collect more Glimpses and finish the remaining Bonds/Memories. The exact number of cycles is not a fixed developer-published requirement.

## Shell and Memory progression

### What the progression loop does

The current evidence supports this player-facing sequence:

1. Unlock a permanent Shell and complete its first Memory sequence.
2. Return to the Shellkeeper/Bonding interaction for that Shell. The local guide calls the Shellkeeper Zhirelle in Marrow Keep.
3. Spend Glimpses to raise that Shell's Bond Tier.
4. Select and watch each newly revealed Memory. A Memory is not counted merely because its tier is available; the completion guides say the player must watch the Memories.
5. Repeat across all eight permanent Shells, including the Genessa-specific Bond interaction.

The external skills guide describes each Bond tier as revealing more Memories and opening another band of abilities. The 100% guide presents the current level model as four Bond levels, with one Memory becoming available at each level and five Memories available by level 4. Preserve this as current launch-guide evidence, not as an official in-game data table.

### Resource cost and run-count estimates

- The current checklist reports **27 Glimpses per Shell** to reach maximum Bond and **216 Glimpses across eight Shells**. The 100% guide also states that the Glimpse supply is limited per playthrough and estimates NG+, followed by almost another full NG+2 run, for all 40 Memories.
- A separate current NG+ guide says the earliest practical completion can be NG++ because one playthrough does not provide enough Glimpses. Community reports range from “two NG+ cycles” to “part of NG+2,” depending on exploration, Night Mode, patch state, and spending choices.
- Therefore the article should say **plan for NG+ and possibly a partial third cycle**, while labeling the 27/216 totals and the run count as guide/community estimates. Do not state that every player must complete exactly three full campaigns.

### Current economy change relevant to this item

The developer's Balance Patch 1 notes say Shell-map reveals now cost Gloom and that Glimpses spent on Shell maps are refunded in the Shell Keeper's alcove in Blackmarrow. This changes the resource-planning advice: after that patch, avoid treating Shell-location reveals as a permanent Glimpse sink, and verify the refund in the current build. Do not claim the refund is available on every platform or older save unless the build shows it.

## New Game Plus: what is supported

The current NG+ guide reports that the following carry into NG+: character level and Shell Points, unlocked Shells and their Memories, weapons and sidearms with upgrades, Glimpses/Gloom/gold, and other listed character/inventory progress. It reports that dungeons, Gates, encounters, bosses, quests, and Ova count reset. For this Item 45 page, the stable actionable claim is narrower: **already unlocked Shell progress and Glimpses are reported to carry over, while the world activities that replenish the next run's Glimpse supply must be repeated**.

The guide reports that NG+ starts through **Send Ova** at the Gloom Siphon after the campaign completion route. Existing local Ova/New Game Plus pages already own the broader trigger and reset details; link to them instead of duplicating a full NG+ walkthrough.

Before selecting `Send Ova`, the current completion checklist recommends:

- watch every currently unlocked Memory;
- finish first-run optional cleanup that would be annoying to repeat;
- especially advance Sester Genessa's Bond in the first playthrough;
- keep a record of which Shells and Memories are complete.

The Genessa warning is version-sensitive. The current checklist reports that Genessa's Bond interaction can remain stuck on its tutorial state in NG+, while the public achievement/trophy lists and official patch notes do not confirm that behavior. Phrase it as a **reported current workaround/risk**, not a guaranteed bug.

## NG+ achievement-counting issue

There are current player reports that `Seeking the Past` did not count watched Memories during NG+ on PS5, and another player reported a save in NG++ that failed to count Shell Memories and required a new save slot. These are anecdotal reports, not an official diagnosis or proof that every platform/build is affected.

The official Steam developer bug thread confirms that the game had achievement-tracking problems at launch and that the developer was investigating one group of incorrect unlocks. That thread does not specifically confirm the Shell-Memory/NG+ report. The article should therefore give cautious troubleshooting only:

1. Confirm that the Memory is actually selectable and play it to completion; do not assume opening the menu is enough.
2. Track Shell and Memory completion outside the trophy UI.
3. Keep a manual save/backup or a separate checkpoint before entering another NG+ cycle where the platform allows it.
4. If the trophy still does not pop after all 40 Memories are visibly watched, treat it as a possible platform/build issue and check for a current patch or official support update rather than replaying blindly.

Do not promise that replaying a Memory, reinstalling, changing platform, or starting NG+ fixes the issue. No checked official source establishes a universal workaround.
