# Item 46 research: Troubadour's Lute + Heavy Metal Trophy

## Scope and evidence boundary

This research is for Item 46 only: `mortal-shell-ii/troubadours-lute`. It covers the current launch location, route, prerequisite, pickup and use details, the `Heavy Metal` trophy/achievement, relevant patch changes, practical combat and completion advice, conflicts, and useful links into the existing Mortal Shell II guide set.

No map coordinates are asserted. No shared hub, config, plan, deployment, IndexNow, or unrelated item changes belong to this checkpoint.

## Direct answer supported by the sources

- The current launch-build location is the **One-Legged Wolf Tavern** in eastern Fainweald, near the **One-Legged Wolf Beacon**. The Lute is on or immediately beside the stage, behind / to the left of the performer. Multiple launch guides agree on this location.
- The practical route anchor is the **Mushroom Village Gate / Mushroom Village Beacon**. From there, current route reporting crosses the wooden bridge, reaches the corruption barrier, uses the **Cleanse Corruption** prompt after Mether's Breath is available, then follows the opened path to the One-Legged Wolf Beacon and tavern.
- The route gate is **Mether's Breath**, which the current local Ova and Beacon pages describe as the Marrow Keep / Ova Nursery Gloom Siphon progression reward that clears corruption and enables direct Beacon travel. Published guides disagree on the exact Ova threshold, so the article must tell players to follow the in-game Siphon prompt rather than invent a universal number.
- The pickup itself is a free environmental interaction. The checked sources do not require a boss kill, puzzle, dialogue choice, or quest completion at the tavern. A separate prerequisite may be needed to reach the area because of the corruption wall.
- The Steam Global Achievements page defines `Heavy Metal` as **“Play all Lute Tracks in the Game.”** Current launch guide evidence reports five tracks. The safe article wording is: equip the Lute, play every track once, cycle through the track selector, and continue until all five reported melodies have been played; do not claim a hidden order or a combat-kill requirement.
- The Lute is useful outside the trophy: current guides describe it as a Resolve-using sidearm that applies **Confusion**, causing affected enemies to attack nearby allies for a short period. This is most useful against groups; it is less valuable as a one-target damage tool.
- The same tavern contains **Gragu**, so players can combine the Lute pickup with the opening step of the [Gragu guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gragu/). This is a practical same-location tip, not a requirement for the Lute.

## Route and prerequisites

### What is required

The source-supported gate is access to the corruption-clearing function of Mether's Breath. Current local pages identify the wider sequence as:

1. Reach Marrow Keep and enter Fainweald.
2. Cleanse eligible Beacons / Corrupted Gate routes and collect Ova.
3. Return to the Ova Nursery / Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep.
4. Follow the in-game prompt until Mether's Breath is unlocked.
5. Return to the Mushroom Village-side route and clear the corruption barrier.

Nerd's Chalk gives a more specific source-reported setup: defeat **Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods** in the Mushroom Village Corrupted Gate, collect the resulting Ova, then return to Marrow Keep and use the Gloom Siphon. This is useful route context, but the local [Ova guide](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/) intentionally does not turn one guide's early threshold into a fixed rule. The article should describe Magdalena as a reported route to the needed Ova, not claim that the Lute itself has a boss-lock unless the current in-game route requires it.

### Current route steps

The most repeatable route description across sources is:

1. Travel to the **Mushroom Village Gate / Mushroom Village Beacon** once it is available.
2. Leave the Beacon area and cross the wooden bridge. A large spider-like enemy is reported near the bridge; dodging past it is a practical time-saving option, not a mandatory mechanic.
3. Continue to the dark corruption barrier and use **Cleanse Corruption** with Mether's Breath.
4. On the opened side, deal with or pass the reported Cannibal Knight and bear right / continue uphill toward the **One-Legged Wolf Beacon**.
5. Revitalize or activate the Beacon if needed so it becomes a reliable route anchor.
6. Follow the dirt path from the One-Legged Wolf Beacon to the **One-Legged Wolf Tavern**.
7. Enter the non-hostile tavern, move to the stage, and interact with the Troubadour's Lute behind / to the left of the performer.
8. Equip the item in the sidearm slot before attempting the trophy or testing Confusion.

Destructoid describes the tavern as east of Mushroom Village and south of the Overrun Quarry. GameTyrant describes it as east of Mushroom Village, adjacent to the One-Legged Wolf Beacon, with a northeast bridge route from the Mushroom Village Corrupted Gate. Because guide landmarks are described at different levels of detail, do not add a coordinate or pretend one source establishes a precise compass path beyond these anchors.

## Pickup and use details

- **Pickup:** interact with the Lute on the stage / by the performer. No fight is reported inside the tavern for the pickup.
- **Equipment:** place it in the sidearm slot. The exact UI labels and controller bindings can vary by platform and current control scheme; use generic “aim / fire / cycle track” wording unless the article gives platform-specific examples as source-reported controls.
- **Combat effect:** the checked launch guides describe musical blasts that apply Confusion. Confused enemies attack nearby allies instead of prioritizing Harbinger. Nerd's Chalk identifies Resolve as the resource used by the sidearm; AltChar reports five casts held and roughly five seconds per Confusion application. Treat those capacity and duration figures as secondary guide observations, not universal unpatched data.
- **Practical use:** fire into groups, then use the confusion window to reposition, land safe melee hits, or rebuild Resolve. Do not oversell the Lute as a replacement for direct damage, and do not assert that every enemy can be confused.
- **Same-trip objective:** Gragu is in the same tavern. Link to the existing [Gragu guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gragu/) for his Heart of Vatra quest rather than duplicating that questline.

## Heavy Metal trophy / achievement

### Confirmed requirement

The official Steam achievement entry says `Heavy Metal` requires **Play all Lute Tracks in the Game**. PlayStationTrophies gives the same requirement as **Play all Lute Tracks in the Game** for the PS5 trophy list.

Current launch guide instructions say:

1. Equip the Troubadour's Lute as the active sidearm.
2. Aim it as a sidearm.
3. Play the currently loaded track.
4. Use the sidearm-cycle input to select the next track.
5. Repeat until all **five reported tracks / melodies** have been played once.

The evidence does not establish a required order, a kill condition, a specific location where the songs must be played, or a need to hit enemies. The article should recommend a safe empty-area cleanup attempt and say the trophy is expected on the last track, while preserving that the exact platform trigger behavior is not documented in the official achievement text.

## Patch and version notes

- Cold Symmetry's official **Balance Patch 1** Steam post dated August 20, 2026 lists **“Troubador’s Lute Attack Damage +100%”** under Weapons. The official post uses the “Troubador” spelling; the item and guide use “Troubadour.” This is a direct damage buff, not evidence that the Confusion behavior or trophy requirement changed.
- The same official post says Balance Patch 1 was live on PC and coming to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series shortly. Do not claim universal platform rollout without a current platform receipt. Link to the existing local [Balance Patch 1 guide](/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/) for the broader update status.
- Launch coverage reports that an open-beta / pre-launch Mushroom Village chest route involving three offering stations is no longer the current launch location. Nerd's Chalk says the Lute was moved to the One-Legged Wolf Tavern and the old chest instead held a Glimpse and an Auspicious Stone. This move is not listed in the checked official Balance Patch 1 note, so phrase it as a beta-versus-launch correction supported by current launch guides, not as an official patch-note claim.
- The developer Balance Patch 1 thread contains a player report that putting the Lute away and sprinting could cause a sit-down / softlock state, and the developer replied that the issue was on its list to investigate. This is a reported bug, not a confirmed current universal failure or a documented fix. Include a short caution to update to the current build and avoid presenting the report as an achievement blocker.

## Conflicts and uncertainties to preserve

- **Mether's Breath spelling:** local canonical pages and the strongest current route sources use `Mether's Breath`; one secondary sidearm page uses `Mother's Breath` and another uses `Meythr's Breath`. Follow the local canonical spelling and tell readers to trust the in-game item name.
- **Ova threshold:** published launch guides report different early thresholds / totals. Do not state “eight Ova,” “seven to eight,” or any other exact threshold as universal for this page.
- **Exact pickup wording:** sources say “on the stage,” “left of the musician,” “behind the performer,” or “leaning against the wall behind the performer.” These all point to the same stage-area pickup; preserve the small positional variation instead of creating an exact coordinate.
- **Track count:** the official achievement says all tracks but not a number; current guides report five. State five as current secondary evidence and retain the official wording in the Sources section.
- **Input bindings:** published trophy instructions use examples such as LT/L2, X/Square, and D-pad cycling. Do not treat those examples as universal PC/Xbox/PlayStation bindings.
- **Patch rollout and bug status:** official Balance Patch 1 platform availability and the Lute softlock report are time-sensitive. Report the developer post and player report without claiming that all platforms have the same build or that the bug is fixed.
