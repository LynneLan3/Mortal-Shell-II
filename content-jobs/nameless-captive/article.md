Location

Nameless Captive is at Captive's End inside the Prisoner's Domain in Fainweald.

[IMAGE: Captive's End arena overview]

How to reach / trigger

1. Cleanse the Nochtean Gate beacon.
2. Enter the Corrupted Gate on the western / northwest side used by the current route.
3. Pass Executioner's Pulpit, then move past Tishina's Confessor.
4. Continue onward to arrive at Captive's End and trigger the encounter.

How to beat (summary)

- Let the boss finish its full sword strings—these are multi-hit combos with long recovery.
- Only take short punish windows after the combo completes; the reliable rhythm is "survive the combo → wait for recovery → punish briefly → reset."
- Dodge any red-warning attacks instead of trying to parry them.
- Keep the fight in the central arena space; avoid being pinned to the edges.

[IMAGE: sword string telegraph / red-warning indicator]

Attacks and what to watch for

- Sword string: A long multi-hit combo. Do not try to interrupt mid-string; wait for the recovery window.
- Headspin / windmill: A spinning follow-up that can be dealt with by creating distance for a standard kill.
- Kick / red attack: Marked by a red warning—dodge these rather than parrying.
- Leap / slam: Gap closer with a ground impact; be ready to dodge and avoid cornering yourself.

Rewards

- Captive's Scabstone — used to progress the Great Martyr's Blade path.
- Headcase trophy — tied to defeating the Nameless Captive.

Pre-kill notes and special setups

- Peter's Perfect Parry is a separate method and requires the Untarnished Seal setup plus surviving the full seven-hit sequence before you finish the boss. Treat this as a pre-kill warning: do not kill the boss early if you're attempting that parry setup. See the Peter's Perfect Parry page for details: /mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/.
- Eredrim is optional support and can help if you need 1 Glimpse for related progression.
- New Game Plus considerations: this fight appears as usual on NG+ runs—see the New Game Plus page for broader guidance: /mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/.

Common mistakes

- Trying to punish mid-sword string—this usually gets you hit by follow-ups.
- Chasing the headspin instead of creating distance; the headspin is safer to avoid by stepping back.
- Attempting to parry red-warning attacks—these should be dodged.
- Killing the boss early when attempting Peter's Perfect Parry without meeting the Untarnished Seal and seven-hit sequence requirements.

Quick combat checklist

- Cleansed Nochtean Gate beacon? ✔
- Entered western/northwest Corrupted Gate route? ✔
- Keep center arena space and wait for full combos to finish? ✔
- Dodge red-warning attacks, punish briefly, reset? ✔
