Yes — Mortal Shell 2 has a New Game Plus transition. To begin it: finish the final route, return to the Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep, and select Send Ova. Selecting Send Ova is irreversible and (in the reported launch build) has no confirmation prompt.

[IMAGE: Gloom Siphon — Send Ova prompt (/images/ova/send-ova-siphon.webp)]

How to start NG+ (exact order)
1. Complete the final route and defeat the late bosses sequence (current route reporting places the late gate at 168 Ovum and the final underground bosses ending with Zmey). See the local route for details: /mortal-shell-ii/ova/.
2. Return to the Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep after the final-boss sequence.
3. Select Send Ova at the Gloom Siphon. This selection is the reported irreversible point of no return. Note: defeating the final boss and selecting Send Ova are separate events; the save remains available until you choose Send Ova.

What is carried over (reported launch-build behavior)
- Character progress: player level and Shell Points.
- Shells and unlocked Shell memories.
- Seals, materials, keys, and permanent pickups.
- Weapons and sidearms plus any applied upgrades.
- Tarstones and invested Tarforge upgrades.
- Glimpse, Gloom, and gold (currency).
- Mether's Pulse upgrades (the upgrades you’ve already bought remain).
- Revealed map areas.
- Tarforge itself and its invested upgrades.
- Custom map markers are reported to be cleared (revealed map stays, custom markers do not).

What resets (reported launch-build behavior)
- Ovum returns to zero.
- Most Beacons need cleansing again (the two starting unlocked Beacons are reported to remain unlocked).
- Dungeons and Corrupted Gates repopulate.
- Enemies and bosses return (they reappear in world and dungeons).
- NPC quests restart.
- Ovum-gated progress resets (see route at /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ for the current 168 Ovum gate).

What respawns — and what does not (reported launch-build behavior)
- Respawns: Revered Glands and Synaptic Vessels respawn in NG+ so you can collect further Mether's Pulse upgrades on the new run.
- Do NOT expect duplicate gear to respawn: weapons, sidearms, and Tarstones do not respawn as duplicates; their pickup locations are reported to hold upgrade materials instead.

Pre-commit checklist — what to finish before you select Send Ova
Complete anything you absolutely want preserved or done on this run. Suggested items (reported important or commonly missed):
1. Optional bosses you want to finish on the current run.
2. All NPC quests you want resolved before reset (quests restart in NG+).
3. Collect any weapons/sidearms/Tarstones you still need — these do not respawn as duplicates in NG+.
4. Finish Shell unlocks and Shell memory collection you want to keep.
5. Finish upgrades and invest Tarforge resources you don’t want to risk repeating.
6. Cleanse Beacons you want kept as currently active (most beacons must be re-cleansed in NG+; two starting beacons remain unlocked).
7. Pick up Revered Glands/Synaptic Vessels if you want them now or plan on collecting more in NG+ (they do respawn).
8. Attempt any missable trophies now. Specifically: attempt Peter’s Perfect Parry before killing the Nameless Captive if that trophy/achievement is still outstanding (/mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/).
9. Finish anything tied to your current Ovum total — current route reporting places the late gate at 168 Ovum; consult /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ for route specifics.

No-confirmation warning
- Reportedly there is no confirmation prompt when you select Send Ova and the choice is irreversible in the launch build. Double-check you want to start NG+ before selecting.

Common mistakes
- Mistaking final-boss defeat for the NG+ transition. Defeating Zmey (or the final boss) does not automatically start NG+ — Send Ova must be selected.
- Selecting Send Ova by accident (remember: no confirmation prompt reported).
- Assuming weapons/sidearms/Tarstones will respawn as duplicates — they reportedly do not; pickup spots hold upgrade materials instead.
- Expecting custom map markers to persist — revealed map areas carry over but custom markers are reported to be cleared.
- Skipping Peter’s Perfect Parry / other missable trophy attempts before advancing past the Nameless Captive or selecting Send Ova.

What happens next (after selecting Send Ova, reported launch-build behavior)
- Your current save transitions into the NG+ run: Ovum resets to zero and world-state resets as listed above.
- You keep character-level progression, Shells, invested upgrades (Tarforge, Mether’s Pulse purchases), currencies (Glimpse, Gloom, gold), revealed map areas, and permanent pickups.
- World content repopulates: Beacons (most need re-cleansing), dungeons, Corrupted Gates, enemies, bosses, and quests restart.
- Revered Glands and Synaptic Vessels will respawn, enabling additional Mether's Pulse upgrades during NG+ playthroughs.
- Weapons/sidearms/Tarstones do not duplicate at their pickup locations and are replaced by upgrade materials in those spots (reported behavior).
