Yes — but beware: selecting Send Ova is irreversible and has no confirmation prompt. Finish the final route, return to the Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep, and pick Send Ova to begin NG+.

How to start NG+

1. Finish the final route (the late gate is reported at 168 Ovum and the underground boss sequence ends with Zmey — see the local Ova route for complete steps).
2. Return to Marrow Keep and go to the Gloom Siphon.
3. Select Send Ova. This selection starts New Game Plus immediately and is irreversible; defeating the final boss and selecting Send Ova are separate events.

For the full route and the 168 Ovum gate, see /mortal-shell-ii/ova/.

What is carried over (reported launch-build behavior)

- Character: level and Shell Points.
- Shells & memories: unlocked Shells and collected memories.
- Gear & upgrades: weapons, sidearms, and their applied upgrades; Seals; Tarstones and invested Tarstone upgrades; Tarforge; permanent pickups and keys; materials used for upgrades remain in inventory as reported.
- Currencies & resources: Glimpse, Gloom, and gold.
- Progress systems: Mether's Pulse (and its current upgrades) is retained; revealed map areas are reported to remain revealed.

Note: these items are described as "carried over" in launch-build reports and may change with patches.

What resets (reported launch-build behavior)

- Beacons: most Beacons must be cleansed again. Two starting Beacons remain unlocked after Send Ova.
- World content: dungeons and Corrupted Gates repopulate; enemies and bosses return; quests restart.
- Ovum: returns to zero on NG+.
- Map markers: custom map markers are reported to be cleared.

What respawns vs what does not

- Respawns: Revered Glands and Synaptic Vessels respawn in NG+, letting you collect further Mether's Pulse upgrades.
- Does not respawn as duplicates: weapons, sidearms, and Tarstones do not respawn as duplicate gear. Their spawn spots are reported to hold upgrade materials instead.

Pre-commit checklist — what to finish before selecting Send Ova

- Optional bosses and dungeon content you want to complete on this run.
- NPC questlines you wish to finish (quests restart in NG+).
- Weapons, Shells and upgrades you want to pick up or fully upgrade (some drops do not duplicate in NG+).
- Cleanse Beacons you care about (most will need re-cleansing in NG+; two starting Beacons remain unlocked).
- Collect Tarstones you need (Tarstones don't duplicate in NG+ spawn spots).
- Missable trophy attempts — attempt any trophies that depend on current-world states before Send Ova.
- Peter's Perfect Parry: if you still need the perfect-parry trophy or step, attempt it before killing the Nameless Captive; see /mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/.

Common mistakes

- Treating final-boss defeat as the NG+ trigger. The irreversible trigger is selecting Send Ova at the Gloom Siphon, not the boss kill.
- Assuming every pickup will reappear. Weapons, sidearms and Tarstones are reported not to duplicate; their locations can instead spawn upgrade materials.
- Relying on a confirmation prompt. Send Ova reportedly has no confirmation prompt — your save remains available only until you select Send Ova.

After you Send Ova

- NG+ begins immediately after selection. Your carried-over progress (see above) should appear in the new run while world content and resources listed as resetting will be back to fresh states.
