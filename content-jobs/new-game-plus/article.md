Answer first: How to start the second run
- Finish the final route, return to the Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep after the final-boss sequence, and select "Send Ova." That selection triggers New Game Plus (NG+) for the current build; it is reported as irreversible and has no confirmation prompt.

How to start NG+ (step‑by‑step)
1. Complete the final-route sequence and defeat the final boss(es) in the route (the route owner is the local Ova page: /mortal-shell-ii/ova/).
2. Return to the Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep (the Send Ova interaction is located there).
3. Select Send Ova at the Gloom Siphon. The current save remains available after the final boss until you choose this; reports call Send Ova the irreversible point of no return and say there is no confirmation prompt.

What carries over (source-reported)
- Character progress
  - Level and Shell Points
  - Unlocked Shells and their unlocked memories
- Gear, upgrades, and permanent pickups
  - Weapons and sidearms (and applied upgrades)
  - Seals, materials, keys, permanent pickups
  - Tarstones and invested Tarstone upgrades
  - Tarforge
- Currencies and meters
  - Glimpse, Gloom, and gold
  - Mether's Pulse (and its invested upgrades)
- Map and navigation
  - Revealed map areas
  - Note: custom map markers are reported to be cleared on transition

What resets (source-reported)
- World state
  - Most Beacons will need cleansing again (two starting Beacons remain unlocked)
  - Dungeons and Corrupted Gates repopulate
  - Enemies and bosses return
  - Quests restart
- Progress counters
  - Ovum returns to zero
- Save / decision boundary
  - Defeating the final boss is not the same as selecting Send Ova; the save remains until you actually select Send Ova

What respawns and what does not (source-reported)
- Respawns in NG+
  - Revered Glands and Synaptic Vessels respawn so you can collect additional Mether's Pulse upgrades
- Does not respawn as duplicates
  - Weapons, sidearms, and Tarstones do not respawn as duplicate items; locations that previously held those items are reported to hold upgrade materials instead

Pre‑commit checklist — things to finish before selecting Send Ova
(Complete these in order of personal priority; numbered to follow as steps if you want a quick checklist)
1. Finish any NPC quests you care about (quests restart in NG+).
2. Kill optional bosses you want finished for rewards or trophies.
3. Obtain any weapons, sidearms, Tarstones, or Shells you want to keep in your original run (these will not reappear as duplicates).
4. Spend or collect upgrades you need (Mether's Pulse upgrades can be recollected later, but some item pickups do not duplicate).
5. Cleanse Beacons you want already active (most Beacons will need cleansing again; two starting Beacons remain unlocked).
6. Complete any missable trophy attempts now — Peter's Perfect Parry in particular should be attempted before killing the Nameless Captive if that trophy is still outstanding.
7. Confirm your Ovum total if you track late‑gate thresholds (current route reporting places the late gate at 168 Ovum, then four underground bosses ending with Zmey before returning to Marrow Keep and selecting Send Ova).

Warnings and limits (preserve source uncertainty)
- The lists above are source‑reported behavior from the launch build and may change with patches or on different platforms. Treat carry-over/reset behavior as reported rather than guaranteed for future updates.
- Reports state Send Ova is irreversible and lacks a confirmation prompt — plan accordingly. The ability to restore the pre-Send Ova state depends on your game saves and platform, not on an in-game confirmation.

Common mistakes
- Selecting Send Ova immediately after the final boss because you assumed the game prompts for confirmation. Reports say there is no confirmation prompt.
- Assuming weapons, sidearms, or Tarstones will duplicate on NG+; reported behavior is that these do not respawn as duplicates and their previous item spots hold upgrade materials instead.
- Confusing "final boss defeated" with "Send Ova selected." The save is still available after the final boss until you pick Send Ova.
- Expecting custom map markers to persist — reports say custom markers are cleared.

What happens next
- After you select Send Ova you begin the NG+ run under the reported carry-over/reset rules above. For the detailed post‑final route and exact routing (the late gate, underground bosses, Zmey, and the return to Marrow Keep), see the route owner at /mortal-shell-ii/ova/.
