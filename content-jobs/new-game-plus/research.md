# Research: New Game Plus (UPDATE)

Scope: update `/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/` only. Answer how to start the second run, what carries over, what resets, and what to finish before `Send Ova`. Do not turn this into a complete ending, trophy, or map guide.

## Confirmed facts

- Current launch coverage calls the transition New Game Plus and reports that it begins by selecting `Send Ova` at the Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep after the final-boss sequence.
- The current save remains available after defeating the final boss until `Send Ova` is selected. The selection is reported as the irreversible point of no return and has no confirmation prompt.
- Character-facing progress reported as carrying over: level and Shell Points; unlocked Shells and memories; Seals, materials, keys, permanent pickups, weapons, sidearms, and applied upgrades; Tarstones and invested upgrades; Glimpse, Gloom, and gold; Mether's Pulse; Tarforge; and revealed map areas. Custom map markers are reported to be cleared.
- World-state progress reported as resetting: most Beacons need cleansing again except the two starting unlocked Beacons; dungeons and Corrupted Gates repopulate; enemies and bosses return; quests restart; Ovum returns to zero.
- Revered Glands and Synaptic Vessels respawn in NG+ so further Mether's Pulse upgrades can be collected. Weapons, sidearms, and Tarstones do not respawn as duplicate gear; their spots are reported to hold upgrade materials instead.
- The practical pre-NG+ checklist is optional bosses, NPC quests, weapons, Shells, upgrades, Beacons, Tarstones, and missable trophy attempts. Peter's Perfect Parry should be attempted before killing the Nameless Captive if it is still outstanding.
- Current route reporting places the late gate at 168 Ovum, followed by four underground bosses ending with Zmey, then the return to Marrow Keep and the `Send Ova` interaction. Keep the local Ova page as the detailed route owner.

## Evidence boundary and conflicts

- Source wording alternates between Ova and Ovum and between “final boss” and the named Zmey sequence. Use the local guide's established terminology and link to `/mortal-shell-ii/ova/` rather than expanding the entire route here.
- Carry-over lists are source-reported launch-build behavior and may change with patches. Do not claim that every consumable, NPC state, or map marker behaves identically on every platform without an official save-system reference.
- The page should distinguish “final boss defeated” from “Send Ova selected”; they are not the same transition.
