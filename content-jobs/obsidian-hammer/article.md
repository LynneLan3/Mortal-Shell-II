Short answer
1. Go to the Obsidianite Mines in the Ruins of Mammon and defeat the Prophet of Profane Infinities in the lowest arena. Multiple route guides report the boss grants the Obsidian Hammer automatically after the kill (no chest to open).

Route — conservative step-by-step
1. If the Mammon area is tainted in your playthrough, verify whether Mother’s Breath is currently required to access the route (some guides report this as a possible gate; this appears to vary by progression). Do not assume a fixed Ova count or beacon requirement.
2. Fast travel to Outskirts of Mammon Beacon.
3. Head west through the small camp and cross the rope bridge; follow the descending path toward the mining complex.
4. When prompted, use the Bone Gate Traverse prompt to continue the western approach.
5. Continue across the rope-bridge section and past any bridge guards toward the Deserted Slums / mining complex.
6. Find the large circular pit / quarry area; the Obsidianite Mines entrance is reported to lie at or near this open quarry.
[IMAGE: large circular quarry / mine entrance area in Mammon]
7. Enter the mine and descend carefully — the shaft is vertical with collapsing-floor rooms, ledges, and dangerous drops. Drop one platform at a time; avoid fighting at ledge edges where a single hit can send you falling.
8. Use visible platforms/ledges to reach the lower chasm. Guides report a lower recovery route if you fall, but do not treat any fall-recovery as a guaranteed no-damage shortcut.
9. Proceed to the lowest arena and defeat the Prophet of Profane Infinities.
10. After the kill, check your weapon list; the Obsidian Hammer should be added automatically.

Optional shorter approach (Abandoned Outpost Beacon)
1. If you already unlocked Abandoned Outpost Beacon, it is reported as a shorter alternative: head downhill from that beacon, use the teleportation gate, route around the coffin area, cross the small bridge, and continue toward the same mine/quarry area.
2. Treat this as an optional shortcut—available in some progression states—rather than the required route.

Boss: Prophet of Profane Infinities — practical tactics
1. Refill resources before entering the mine. A death here can require repeating the vertical approach.
2. Read the wind-up on each hammer attack; sources consistently describe the boss’s swings as slow and telegraphed with visible recovery windows.
3. Dodge or parry during the full hammer wind-up, then punish in short bursts — roughly one to three hits depending on your weapon and spacing — and disengage. Do not assume long combos are safe after single punishes.
4. Use Break damage and Riposte opportunities if you build toward them; guides report these as effective tools but not mandatory.
5. Avoid fighting on narrow ledges or near collapseable floors where a hit can cause an avoidable fall.
6. Tiel’s Shadow Dodge is mentioned by some guides as a comfortable matchup; this is a recommendation, not a requirement.
7. Area-of-effect short-range bursts have been reported by at least one guide; remain alert for non-hammer telegraphs in the arena.

Weapon reward and identity
- The Obsidian Hammer is a slow, heavy blunt weapon with Heavy Stomps (strong impact/stagger identity and slow commitment). Sources agree on this broad identity; do not assume specific numerical stats or scaling.
- The weapon is reported to be granted automatically when the Prophet of Profane Infinities dies — check your weapon inventory rather than searching for a chest.
- An optional Merrick map fragment for the Obsidian Hammer location is reported by multiple guides; this fragment can appear for sale but reported price and spawn details vary between sources.

Upgrade guidance (qualified)
- Reviewed guides agree on a material transition for upgrades: Ventrium is used for early upgrade tiers and Laterite is used for later tiers. The exact breakpoint (which + level) differs between sources, so do not rely on a single tier table here.
- For the game’s upgrade system reference, see the Tarforge guide on this site: /mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/

Common mistakes
- Assuming the Obsidian Hammer is in a chest: sources consistently report it is awarded automatically after the Prophet is defeated.
- Rushing the vertical descent: the mine contains collapsing floors and big drops — dropping several platforms at once or fighting at edges commonly causes deaths and repeat approaches.
- Ignoring the possible Mother’s Breath gate: some playthroughs require Mother’s Breath to clear taint on the route; check your progression state before planning the run.
- Expecting a single universal shortcut: Abandoned Outpost Beacon can be shorter for some players, but it’s not a guaranteed safer or always-available route.

What happens next
- After the Prophet is defeated and the Obsidian Hammer appears in your inventory, you can equip it like any other weapon. Guides report no additional chest interaction is needed.
- If you plan to upgrade the hammer, begin with Ventrium materials at early tiers, then switch to Laterite at higher tiers per reviewed guidance; consult the Tarforge guide for how the upgrade system works in general.
- If you died during the descent, you will usually need to repeat the vertical approach from the chosen beacon; prepare consumables and checkpoints accordingly.

Notes and sources/uncertainty
