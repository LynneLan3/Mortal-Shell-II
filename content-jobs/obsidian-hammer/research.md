## Direct answer

Reach the **Obsidianite Mines** in the **Ruins of Mammon**, descend to the lowest section, and defeat the **Prophet of Profane Infinities**. The boss is reported to award the **Obsidian Hammer automatically**, so the weapon is not a normal chest pickup. The safest common route starts at **Outskirts of Mammon Beacon**, crosses the western Mammon route through the **Deserted Slums / mining complex**, and enters the mine at the large circular pit. **Abandoned Outpost Beacon** is reported as a shorter alternative when already unlocked.

## Route guidance for the page

Use the following conservative sequence, combining the repeated route landmarks without inventing a turn-by-turn map:

1. If the Mammon route is still tainted, check whether **Mother’s Breath** is required in the current progression state. Do not publish a hard count or Ova threshold.
2. Fast travel to **Outskirts of Mammon Beacon**.
3. Travel west through the small camp and across the rope bridge, then follow the descending path.
4. Use the **Bone Gate** when the **Traverse** prompt appears.
5. Continue across the rope-bridge section and past or through the bridge guards.
6. Reach the **Deserted Slums / mining complex** and look for the large circular pit/quarry area where the Obsidianite Mines entrance is located.
7. Inside the mine, expect a vertical route. Watch for the collapsing-floor room, take drops one at a time, and avoid fighting beside ledges when a hit could knock the player down.
8. Cross the lower chasm using the visible platforms/ledges where possible. If a fall happens, the reviewed guide reports a lower recovery route with enemies and a launch pad; do not present that recovery as a guaranteed no-damage shortcut.
9. Enter the lowest arena and defeat the Prophet of Profane Infinities.
10. Check the weapon list after the kill. The Obsidian Hammer should be granted automatically rather than requiring a chest interaction.

The **Abandoned Outpost Beacon** route should be a short optional subsection: head downhill, use the teleportation gate, route around the coffins, cross the bridge, and continue toward the same mine area. The source supports the shortcut at the area level but not a safer or universally available route.

## Boss guidance for the expansion

- Refill before entering because a death in the mine can require repeating the vertical approach; this is a practical warning from the route descriptions, not a claim about a missing dedicated checkpoint.
- Watch the full hammer wind-up, then dodge or parry and punish the recovery.
- Use a short punish—roughly one to three hits depending on the player's weapon and position—then reset. Do not promise that a full combo is safe.
- Break damage and a Riposte are supported as useful options by Gaming ProMax. Present them as optional tools, not prerequisites.
- Tiel's Shadow Dodge is reported as a comfortable matchup, but this is a recommendation rather than a requirement. Do not make Tiel or any other Shell mandatory.
- Keep the area-of-effect warning qualified: Nerd’s Chalk reports short-range bursts, while the other sources focus on hammer swings and do not provide a complete attack list.
