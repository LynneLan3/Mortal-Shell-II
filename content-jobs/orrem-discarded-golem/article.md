Short answer — where Orrem is and how to beat him
- Orrem appears as the Bridge of Procession tendril guardian inside The Unfound Path. Cleanse the Bridge of Procession Beacon, follow the Bridge path to the guardian arena, then fight Orrem in two phases: Orrem, the Discarded Golem (axes) and Orrem, the Reclaimed (tail/centipede spine). Conserve at least one healing flask for phase two and favor lateral dodges over backing away. Sources: AllThingsHow, GameSpot, GameTyrant.

Getting to Orrem (route and checkpoint)
1. Unlock The Unfound Path through the Marrow Keep opening. A dedicated Bridge of Procession route lists 150 Ova as the entry threshold; that number is source-reported and has conflicting search results elsewhere, so treat it as requiring verification. See /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ for Ova progression.
2. Enter The Unfound Path via the Marrow Keep hole.
3. From the entrance: pass the dark cavern and the NPC room with Vratko (presented as Sister Genessa). The foot-wash choice can reportedly be declined without penalty (AllThingsHow).
4. Descend the stairs and drop down the deep vertical shaft into the watery cave.
5. Read the stone tablets and use the first teleporter to reach the foggy exterior; you may clear enemies and grab Voidstone if desired (AllThingsHow).
6. Use the second teleporter to reach the stone bridge; follow the cliffside ledges to the next platform.
7. Use the third teleporter to the red-lit corridor; Lanolite is available here if you want it; clear the spider-like enemies in the circular arena room.
8. Climb the final staircase past a ghost and two scythe guards to the red structure that shows BEACON FOUND, BRIDGE OF PROCESSION and summons Sariel; you may CLEANSE BEACON to create a full rest/management point. Cleanse the Beacon before pressing on to the guardian arena (AllThingsHow, Gamer Guides).
- The nearest checkpoint is Bridge of Procession Beacon. For Beacon mechanics and fast travel options see /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/.

Preparation checklist (what sources recommend)
- Access: late-game Unfound Path access via the Marrow Keep opening; Bridge route source lists 150 Ova as the likely threshold (verification qualifier).
- Keep at least one healing flask reserved for phase two (AllThingsHow, GameTyrant).
- Optional summon: Sariel (1 Glimpse) is repeatedly identified as the summon available at the Bridge Beacon; helpful but not mandatory (/mortal-shell-ii/sariel/).
- Recommended items/approaches reported by sources:
  - Burnt Effigy: recommended source-backed mitigation for Orrem’s flaming-axe Burn (GameTyrant).
  - Tiel Shell (Shadow Dodge): a community-suggested option to create Shadow Strikes (GameTyrant; see /mortal-shell-ii/tiel/).
  - Ranged Tarstone attacks: useful for maintaining distance and possibly causing a phase-two stagger (GameTyrant; see /mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/).
- No checked source mandates a specific weapon, exact weapon level, or exact stat thresholds.

Fight plan — Phase 1: Orrem, the Discarded Golem
1. Keep lateral space: do not rely on backing straight away. Dodge sideways when Orrem bangs his weapons and advances (GameTyrant).
2. Watch for the flaming-axe melee combos. The axes can inflict Burn on contact (GameTyrant).
3. After Orrem’s melee combo, watch for Cascading Fire (delayed flame explosions) as a follow-up — treat it as a delayed follow-up rather than a guaranteed free punish window (GameSpot, AllThingsHow).
4. When Orrem jumps high and slams down, avoid the landing area and wait until the landing sequence completes before re-engaging (GameSpot, AllThingsHow).
5. After a safe window following his combo/explosion, take a short punish; preserve healing because phase two is more demanding.
6. Possible riposte: one source reports a likely Riposte opportunity around half health in phase one; list this as a single-source, timing-dependent possibility rather than a guaranteed, scripted opening (GameTyrant).

Fight plan — Phase 2: Orrem, the Reclaimed (tail/spine active)
1. After the first health bar you will fight the Reclaimed form: Orrem grows a centipede-like spine/tail and becomes faster (GameSpot, AllThingsHow).
2. Expect more scurrying, tail lashes, and flame explosions from range (GameSpot, AllThingsHow). Conserve at least one heal for this phase.
3. Dodge immediately for the reported unparryable rush — sources identify that rush as unparryable and advise dodging before the landing rather than trying to parry or block (GameTyrant).
4. When the centipede mouth glows red it may fire blood-bullet projectiles; move when you see the glow instead of standing in front of the projectile line (GameTyrant).
5. Watch both the main body and the tail/spine. The body and spine create separate pressure; keep the camera on both and avoid getting pinned between them (GameTyrant).
6. Sources report an arm slam and a pincer behavior where Orrem may run to one side and wait; if Orrem pulls away before you close, wait for the pincer or fight from range. Tarstone shots at safe distance can contribute to a reported phase-two stagger around mid-health (GameTyrant).
7. If the boss staggers, punish while remaining mindful of the tail’s independent attacks — treat the stagger timing and trigger as source-reported, not universal.

Tells, counters, and safety rules (summary)
- Lateral dodges beat many advancing/ground-bang attacks; avoid retreating straight back (GameTyrant).
- Conserve at least one healing flask for phase two (AllThingsHow, GameTyrant).
- Treat the Cascading Fire after melee as a delayed explosion that can punish naive re-engagement (GameSpot, AllThingsHow).
- The phase-two rush is reported as unparryable — dodge it rather than parry (GameTyrant).
- Identify the spine’s red-mouth glow as the blood-bullet tell; move when it glows (GameTyrant).
- Do not assume every attack is parryable; sources only explicitly note the unparryable rush and specific tells above.

Rewards and achievements (source-backed)
- Orrem’s Head: drops on defeat and can be traded for 5,000 coins at the Marrow Keep merchant context (AllThingsHow, GameSpot).
- Gloom: Gamer Guides lists 64,442 Gloom for the encounter; this figure is single-source and may be version/run-sensitive — treat it as source-reported if you cite it.
- Optional summon: Sariel (1 Glimpse) is the Beacon-summon identified at the Bridge (AllThingsHow, GameSpot, GameTyrant).
- Trophy/Achievement: “Lost Your Head” — condition listed as Defeated Orrem, the Reclaimed on XboxAchievements and TrueAchievements; multiple lists reflect this (XboxAchievements, TrueAchievements, Insider Gaming).
- No checked source ties a specific weapon drop to Orrem or a missable trophy step to the fight.

Common mistakes (what players commonly do wrong, per sources)
- Backing straight away from Orrem’s advancing attacks — GameTyrant reports this makes dodging harder. Dodge sideways instead.
- Using all healing flasks before phase two — both AllThingsHow and GameTyrant advise keeping at least one heal for the tail phase.
- Trying to parry the reported unparryable rush — GameTyrant advises dodging immediately before the landing.
- Ignoring the tail/spine: the tail fires projectiles and creates separate pressure; don’t focus only on the body.
- Assuming Orrem drops a primary weapon or unique Shell reward — no checked source supports that.
- Treating Riposte or mid-phase stagger as guaranteed — Riposte and stagger reports are single-source timing observations and may not always occur.

Patch and version cautions
- Balance Patch 1 and Hotfix 2.0 notes reviewed do not list a direct Orrem adjustment (GamesRadar+, Steam Hotfix 2.0 notes). Future patches may change behavior, rewards, or route requirements.
- The 150 Ova entry threshold and the encounter Gloom figure (64,442) are source-reported values with potential conflicts or version sensitivity; treat both as requiring verification if exact numbers are critical.

What happens next (after defeating Orrem)
1. Defeating the Bridge of Procession guardian clears that branch’s tendril and contributes toward opening the central Unfound Path route (AllThingsHow).
2. Continue to clear the other Unfound Path guardians — The Monolith and Malborn Offspring — in whatever order your route dictates; the sources do not establish a mandatory order among the three.
3. After all three branches are cleared the central/final Beacon route toward Zmey the Unbidden becomes available according to the reviewed route source (AllThingsHow).
4. Use the Bridge of Procession Beacon to return to Marrow Keep if you want to leave; see /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/ for Beacon mechanics and /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ for progression context.
