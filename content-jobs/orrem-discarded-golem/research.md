## Location, access, nearest Beacon, and route

- Orrem is the guardian at the end of the **Bridge of Procession** segment inside **The Unfound Path**, the late/final dungeon. The encounter has the two names **Orrem, the Discarded Golem** and **Orrem, the Reclaimed**.
- A dedicated Bridge of Procession route guide says The Unfound Path opens through a hole in Marrow Keep after collecting **150 Ova**. It describes 150 as the entry threshold; another broad boss-index result has reported a different Ova threshold, so do not publish a second number as settled fact without further verification.
- The dungeon has a central circular structure and three branching story paths. Bridge of Procession is one of those paths, and its tendril guardian must be defeated before that branch can be cleared.
- Route reported by AllThingsHow:
  1. Enter The Unfound Path from the hole in Marrow Keep.
  2. From the entrance, pass the dark cavern and the NPC room where Vratko speaks with you as Sister Genessa; the guide says the foot-wash choice can be declined without penalty.
  3. Go down the stairs and drop through the deep vertical shaft into the watery cave.
  4. Read the nearby stone tablets and use the first teleporter to reach the foggy exterior; pass the enemies and collect Voidstone if desired.
  5. Use the second teleporter to reach the stone bridge and follow the narrow cliffside ledges to the next platform.
  6. Use the third teleporter to reach the red-lit corridor; collect Lanolite if desired, then clear the spider-like enemies in the circular arena room.
  7. Climb the final staircase past a ghost and two scythe-wielding guards to the red structure. Interacting with it shows **BEACON FOUND**, **BRIDGE OF PROCESSION**, summons Sariel, and allows **CLEANSE BEACON**.
- The closest named checkpoint is the **Bridge of Procession Beacon**. Cleansing it creates a full rest/management point with level-up, Shell/equipment, shades, Mother’s Pulse, Mother’s Breath, Return to Marrow Keep, and Leave options.
- Route wording differs slightly across sources: AllThingsHow presents the Bridge Beacon at the top of the final staircase before discussing Orrem as the branch guardian; Gamer Guides describes Orrem at the end of the pathway opposite the Bridge of Procession Beacon. Keep the practical advice: cleanse the Beacon when reached, then follow the Bridge of Procession path to the guardian arena; do not claim the Beacon is literally inside the boss arena.

## Prerequisites and preparation

- The supported prerequisite is late-game access to The Unfound Path through the Marrow Keep opening and enough Ova to trigger it. The dedicated route source reports 150 Ova.
- Orrem is one of three tendril guardians blocking the central Unfound Path route. The other two are the Monolith and Malborn Offspring; the sources reviewed describe the branches as separate paths, not a fixed order.
- The encounter has two phases. AllThingsHow explicitly advises keeping at least one healing flask for the tail phase; GameTyrant likewise recommends saving healing for phase two.
- **Sariel** is an optional Shell/spirit summon for **1 Glimpse**. GameSpot, AllThingsHow, and the boss index all identify Sariel as the summon. Sariel is assistance, not a stated entry requirement.
- **Burnt Effigy** is a source-backed recommendation because GameTyrant reports that Orrem’s flaming axes can inflict Burn and that the item helps with Burn mitigation. It is not established as mandatory.
- **Tiel** is a source-backed option: GameTyrant recommends using Tiel’s Shadow Dodge to create Shadow Strikes. This is a build suggestion, not proof that Tiel is the best Shell.
- Ranged Tarstone attacks are reported by GameTyrant as useful for maintaining distance and creating a possible phase-two stagger. The specific Tarstone is not established by the checked sources; do not name one as required.
- No checked source establishes a mandatory weapon, exact recommended weapon level, exact Shell stats, or a universal damage breakpoint.

## Mechanics, phases, attacks, tells, and counters

### Phase 1: Orrem, the Discarded Golem

- GameTyrant describes two flaming axes that can inflict Burn when they connect.
- GameSpot and AllThingsHow describe **Cascading Fire** flame explosions after Orrem’s melee combo. Treat the post-combo explosion as a delayed follow-up rather than a guaranteed melee punish window.
- Orrem periodically jumps high and crashes down / slams into the arena. The safe response supported by the sources is to avoid the landing area and re-engage only after the landing sequence is complete.
- GameTyrant reports that Orrem is difficult to dodge while moving backward. When Orrem bangs the weapons on the ground and advances, dodge sideways rather than retreating straight back.
- Around half health, GameTyrant reports a likely Riposte opportunity. This is a single-source, timing-dependent observation; present it as a possible opening, not a guaranteed scripted event.
- Practical phase-one loop: keep lateral space, read the axe advance, survive the complete melee/explosion sequence, then take a short punish. Preserve healing where possible because phase two is the more demanding section in the checked guides.

### Phase 2: Orrem, the Reclaimed

- After the first health bar is depleted, Orrem grows a tail / centipede-like spine and becomes faster. GameSpot and AllThingsHow describe more frequent scurrying, tail lashes, and flame explosions from range.
- GameTyrant reports an obvious unparryable rush. Dodge immediately before the landing rather than trying to block or parry it.
- GameTyrant also reports an arm slam that can be dodged. Do not turn this into an exact timing claim because no frame data is supplied.
- The centipede-like spine can fire blood bullets. GameTyrant identifies the tell as the centipede mouth glowing red; move when that glow appears instead of staying in front of the projectile line.
- Around half health in the Reclaimed phase, GameTyrant reports that Orrem may stagger, especially when the player uses safe-distance ranged Tarstone attacks. Treat the timing and trigger as reported, not universal.
- GameTyrant reports that Orrem may run to one side and wait. Before rushing, wait for the centipede to attempt a pincer; if the player gets too close, Orrem can push itself away. Fighting from range or waiting for Orrem to leap is the safer source-backed approach.
- Keep the camera on both Orrem and the spine. The sources establish that the body and the tail/spine create separate pressure, but do not establish a complete attack list or exact hitboxes.

## Rewards, Shell/weapon evidence, and trophy status

- Defeating Orrem awards **Orrem’s Head**. GameSpot and AllThingsHow say it can be traded for **5,000 coins** at the relevant merchant / Marrow’s Keep merchant context. Gamer Guides also lists it as a Trade Item.
- Gamer Guides lists **64,442 Gloom** for the encounter. This number is not independently confirmed by the other checked sources, so it must be labeled source-reported and potentially version/run-sensitive or omitted from the final page if a conservative reward list is preferred.
- The checked sources identify **Sariel** as the optional 1-Glimpse summon. They do not identify a weapon drop from Orrem. Do not claim Clockwork Scythe as an Orrem reward; the checked Sariel route associates Clockwork Scythe with Sariel’s own encounter, not this boss.
- The achievement/trophy is **Lost Your Head**, whose listed condition is **Defeated Orrem, the Reclaimed**. XboxAchievements and TrueAchievements both expose that condition; Insider Gaming also lists the same trophy condition.
- No checked source reports a special pre-kill interaction, a separate phase-specific trophy requirement, or a missable Orrem trophy condition. A trophy guide labels it story-related/unmissable, but the safest page wording is that current listings define it as the normal defeat and no special missable step is documented.

## What happens next

- Defeating the Bridge of Procession guardian allows that branch’s tendril to be cleared and contributes to opening the central route. The reviewed sources say the other Unfound Path branches still need to be completed before the final central beacon / route to Zmey is available.
- Continue to the remaining Unfound Path guardians, **The Monolith** and **Malborn Offspring**, in the order supported by the player’s route; the reviewed sources do not establish a mandatory order among the three.
- After all three story paths are cleared, the central/final Beacon route opens toward **Zmey the Unbidden** according to AllThingsHow.
- If the player wants to leave, the Bridge of Procession Beacon can return them to Marrow Keep; use the existing [Beacons and fast travel] guide for broader Beacon mechanics and the [Ova] guide for the progression loop.

## Practical insights and uncertainty/conflicts to preserve

- The strongest practical rule is lateral movement: both the route/boss coverage and GameTyrant’s specific advice warn against treating backward movement as the universal answer to Orrem’s advancing attacks.
- Reserve at least one heal for phase two, but avoid publishing an exact flask count beyond the source’s “at least one” advice.
- Treat Riposte timing, phase-two stagger timing, exact health thresholds, and the 64,442 Gloom figure as source-reported rather than guaranteed universal behavior.
- Do not state that every attack is parryable or that all red effects have the same meaning. The checked sources only explicitly call the phase-two rush unparryable and identify the spine’s red mouth glow as the blood-bullet tell.
- The 150-Ova entry threshold comes from the dedicated Bridge of Procession guide; a broad search result reports a different threshold. Preserve the dedicated source’s value with a verification qualifier or avoid an exact number in the page quick answer.
