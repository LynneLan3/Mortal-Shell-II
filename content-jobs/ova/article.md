Quick answer
- To unlock Mether's Breath: collect Ova by cleansing eligible Beacons, return to the Ova Nursery in Marrow Keep, and interact with the Gloom Siphon to siphon/deposit the collected Ova. The siphon interaction is the unlock action — carrying Ova in the field is not enough. If the siphon shows the Mether's Breath reward or a cleanse option, the item is granted and you can use it to clear black corruption growths and to fast-travel between discovered/rested Beacons.

Early route (practical steps to reach Mether's Breath)
Follow these numbered steps in order.

1. Finish the prologue and return to Marrow Keep (the game’s early hub after the prologue; GameSpot).
2. From Marrow Keep, enter Fainweald and activate Widow’s Overlook Beacon as your first Fainweald node (GameSpot).
3. Continue along the Fainweald route toward Blackridge Pass and the Mushroom Village branch; rest/discover Beacons as you go (GameSpot).
4. When the Beacon menu offers a cleanse route for a Beacon, complete the cleanse dungeon for that Beacon. GameSpot lists these early useful Ova targets and reported rewards:
   - Blackridge Pass Beacon — reported as 1 Ovum after cleansing its dungeon.
   - Mushroom Village Beacon — reported as 1 Ovum after cleansing its dungeon.
   - Mushroom Village Gate Beacon — reported as 3 Ova after cleansing (GameSpot).
   (Treat these numbers as launch-guide examples rather than a complete in-game table.)
5. Return to Marrow Keep and go to the Ova Nursery. Interact with the Gloom Siphon and deposit/siphon the collected Ova — this is the unlock action for Mether’s Breath. If you think you have enough Ova but see no reward, return to the Ova Nursery and interact with the Siphon again (AllThingsHow; GameSpot; NerdsChalk).
6. After the siphon grants Mether’s Breath, revisit routes blocked by black corruption. Use the Breath to clear growths and then rest at any newly discovered Beacons.

What “cleansing a Beacon” means
- Cleansing an eligible Beacon is different from merely discovering or resting at it: the Beacon menu must offer a cleanse route, and you must complete that cleanse route to claim the Ova reward. Discovery/resting matters for travel; cleansing matters for Ova progression (GameSpot; Beacons page).

Mether’s Breath: uses and how to clear corruption
- Primary uses reported at launch: 1) clear black corruption growths that block overworld routes, and 2) enable direct travel between discovered/rested Beacons (AllThingsHow; GameSpot).
- To clear a corruption growth after you have Mether’s Breath:
  1. Fast travel or walk back to the corruption blockage.
  2. Approach the black growth.
  3. Hold the interact prompt (platform-specific button) until the growth dissolves (AllThingsHow; AltChar).
  4. Walk through the opened route and rest at any newly discovered Beacon.

Thresholds and uncertainty (important)
- Multiple launch-build sources disagree on the early Ova number required to unlock Mether’s Breath. Reported values include:
  - GameSpot: the prologue’s Ova plus at least five additional Ova from its listed Beacons (interpreted as that route’s practical result).
  - AllThingsHow: 7–8 Ova and notes that the game does not show a hard number in the corruption message.
  - NerdsChalk: an 8-Ova threshold.
  - AltChar: 10 Ova from firsthand experience, while noting route-order dependence may matter.
- Do not treat any single number above as a guaranteed universal threshold. The defensible player rule is: cleanse nearby eligible Beacons, return to the Ova Nursery and siphon when you’ve made meaningful progress, and accept the in-game siphon prompt / Mether’s Breath appearance as the authority (AllThingsHow; GameSpot; NerdsChalk; AltChar).
- Note: the late-game 168 Ovum requirement is a separate milestone and is not the same as the early Mether’s Breath threshold.

Late-game 168 Ovum, the post-boss sequence, and Send Ova → New Game Plus
1. Bank 168 Ovum (current launch guides consistently report this as the late-game requirement at the Marrow Keep Siphon; AllThingsHow).
2. Return to the Gloom Siphon room in Marrow Keep and speak to the NPC there to open the underground route beneath Marrow Keep (AllThingsHow).
3. Clear the four bosses in the underground route. Guides report Zmey, the Unbidden, as the final boss in that sequence (AllThingsHow; GameSpot).
4. After defeating the final boss, return to Marrow Keep. Current launch reporting says the Gloom Siphon will present Send Ova as a menu option.
5. Selecting Send Ova is the reported irreversible action that starts New Game Plus (GameSpot; NerdsChalk). Guides indicate:
   - Defeating the final boss does not automatically force the reset; you can return to the world to finish cleanup.
   - Choosing Send Ova is the point of no return. Current reports say there is no confirmation prompt.
6. Before selecting Send Ova, finish any optional current-run cleanup you do not want to repeat (Beacons, weapons/Shell pickups, quests, missable trophies). See the New Game Plus page for carry-over vs reset details (/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/).

Rewards and concrete post-boss examples
- Some launch guides report a post-boss Beacon in a major Corrupted Gate granting 26 Ova; treat this as guided reporting not a universal rule for every Gate (GameSpot; AllThingsHow).
- Mether’s Breath’s reward is the corruption-clearing and Beacon-travel capability (not a Shell or weapon).
- After obtaining Mether’s Breath, use it to reach new routes and Beacons; see the Gloom Farm guide for a practical revisit example north of Blackridge Pass (/mortal-shell-ii/gloom-farm/).

Troubleshooting
- If you think you have enough Ova but Mether’s Breath does not appear: return to the Ova Nursery and interact with the Gloom Siphon again (AllThingsHow; GameSpot).
- If a Beacon still shows a corrupted state after you believe you cleansed it, treat that as a possible launch-build/UI bug; community reports mention stale Beacon labels and fast-travel-list issues without a confirmed workaround.
- Do not assume resting at a discovered Beacon is sufficient for travel availability — resting/discovery status affects the travel menu; cleansing affects Ova rewards (GameSpot).

Common mistakes
- Attacking or searching for hidden keys when the UI explicitly says a route is blocked by corruption — the correct action is Ova progression, not a key.
- Confusing carrying Ova with siphoning it. The unlock action is at the Gloom Siphon in the Ova Nursery; you must deposit/siphon there.
- Treating the 168 Ovum late-game milestone as the same as the early Mether’s Breath threshold — they are separate progression gates.
- Assuming a single reported early-Ova number (4/5/8/10) is universal — sources disagree; follow the in-game siphon prompt.
- Skipping rest at Beacons you want available for future travel — an unrested / undiscovered node may not appear in the travel list as expected.

What happens next (after Mether’s Breath or after the 168/boss sequence)
- After Mether’s Breath: you can clear corruption walls, reach previously blocked routes, discover and rest at new Beacons, and use the direct Beacon travel network to speed future routing. Example post-Mether’s-Breath revisits are covered by the Gloom Farm guide (/mortal-shell-ii/gloom-farm/).
- After the 168 Ova → boss sequence: return to the Gloom Siphon. You can either finish remaining current-world cleanup or select Send Ova to start New Game Plus. Selecting Send Ova is reported to be irreversible and the actual NG+ trigger (GameSpot; NerdsChalk).

Conflicting claims preserved
- Early Mether’s Breath threshold: reported values vary (five additional Ova on one route; 7–8; 8; 10). Use the game’s siphon prompt as the final arbiter (GameSpot; AllThingsHow; NerdsChalk; AltChar).
- Ova/Ovum wording: sources use both terms for the same resource; this guide uses “Ova” and “Ovum” interchangeably per source reporting.
- Corrupted Gate reward specifics (26 Ova for some post-boss Beacons) are launch-guide reports and not presented here as a complete database (GameSpot; AllThingsHow).
- Post-patch UI/Beacon behavior: community reports of stale Beacon states exist but are not established as alternate progression rules (community reports; patch notes reviewed).
