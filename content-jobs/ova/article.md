Quick answer
- To clear early-world corruption: collect and deposit Ova at the Ova Nursery’s Gloom Siphon in Marrow Keep until Mether’s Breath unlocks (the game shows the unlock/prompt). Mether’s Breath lets you fast-travel between rested Beacons and, once equipped, hold the interact prompt at a black corruption growth to dissolve it.
- To trigger New Game Plus: current launch reporting says bank 168 Ovum, clear the reported four-boss underground route, return to the Gloom Siphon and choose Send Ova. Multiple guides identify selecting Send Ova as the irreversible NG+ action (reported to have no confirmation prompt).

Early prerequisites and a practical early route
Follow these steps after finishing the prologue and reaching Marrow Keep (the hub):

1. Inspect the Ova Nursery in Marrow Keep and note the Gloom Siphon interact; you must deposit/siphon collected Ova there to trigger Mether's Breath — carrying Ova in the field is not enough.
2. Enter Fainweald (prologue route) and activate/rest at early Beacons. GameSpot’s early-route coverage names Widow’s Overlook as the first Fainweald Beacon to use.
3. Move toward Blackridge Pass and the Mushroom Village branch and activate/rest at the named Beacons along that path. GameSpot lists Blackridge Pass, Mushroom Village, and Mushroom Village Gate as early cleanse targets.
4. When a Beacon’s menu offers a “cleanse” route (this is different from discovery/rest), complete the cleanse to make that Beacon eligible for Ova rewards.
5. Return to Marrow Keep and interact with the Gloom Siphon in the Ova Nursery to deposit/siphon the collected Ova. If the siphon gives Mether’s Breath, you can then travel directly between rested Beacons and clear corruption walls.

Ova rewards reported in launch guides (examples; attributed)
- GameSpot reports: Blackridge Pass Beacon — 1 Ovum after cleansing; Mushroom Village — 1 Ovum; Mushroom Village Gate — 3 Ova after cleansing its dungeon.
- AllThingsHow reports: 3 Ova from finishing the tutorial, 1 from a regular Beacon, 3 from a Beacon next to a Corrupted Gate, and 26 Ova from clearing a full Corrupted Gate and its final boss.
These numbers are launch-guide evidence for route planning; treat them as attributed reports, not a complete official reward table.

Mether’s Breath, Gloom Siphon usage, and troubleshooting
- What Mether’s Breath does (launch reporting): unlocks direct travel between discovered/rested Beacons and allows you to clear black corruption growths that block overworld routes.
- To unlock it: deposit/siphon Ova at the Gloom Siphon in the Ova Nursery and watch the in-game prompt; the game’s prompt is the authoritative indicator you have unlocked Mether’s Breath.

Troubleshooting if the unlock doesn’t appear:
1. Return to the Ova Nursery, interact with the Gloom Siphon again and confirm you used the siphon (do not assume carrying Ova in the field counted).
2. If the Siphon still shows no change, rely on the in-game Beacon menu and the Siphon prompt as your progress authority — see “Thresholds and uncertainty” below for why exact counts vary across guides.

How to clear an individual corruption growth after Mether’s Breath
1. Fast travel or walk back to the black corruption growth that previously blocked the route.
2. Approach the growth and hold the interact prompt until the growth dissolves (sources report “hold-to-interact”; wording avoids hard-coding platform buttons).
3. Walk through the opened route and rest at any newly discovered Beacon to make it available for future fast travel.

Thresholds and uncertainty — what the guides disagree on
- Do not rely on a single posted number for the early Mether’s Breath unlock. Launch reporting conflicts:
  - GameSpot’s early route implies the unlock can occur after the tutorial Ova plus at least five additional Ova from listed Beacons.
  - AllThingsHow recommends 7–8 Ova and explicitly notes the game does not show a hard number.
  - NerdsChalk reports an 8-Ova threshold.
  - AltChar reports 10 Ova from firsthand experience but allows that a lower total may suffice depending on route order.
- Defensible player instruction: cleanse eligible nearby Beacons, return to the Ova Nursery, siphon your collected Ova, and treat the in-game siphon prompt / Mether’s Breath grant as the final authority.

The six major Corrupted Gates (direct answer + recommended order)
Current cross-checked launch reporting identifies six major Corrupted Gate dungeons (three in Fainweald, three in Mammon). Recommended practical order and named approach Beacons follow the cited guides; this is a suggested, low-backtracking route, not a mandatory lock order.

1. Glutted Mire (Fainweald) — boss: Magdalena, Lady of the Woods — approach: Mushroom Village Gate. (GameSpot / Gamer Guides)
2. Sanguine Caverns (Fainweald) — boss: The Lost Child — approach: Stonebled Gate. (GameSpot / Gamer Guides)
3. Prisoners' Domain (Fainweald) — boss: The Nameless Captive — approach: Nochtean Gate. (GameSpot / Into Indie Games)
4. Withered Shoals (Mammon) — boss: Hexapod — approach: Gate of Mammon. (GameSpot / Gamer Guides)
5. Conquered Temple (Mammon) — boss: Droeg the Conqueror — approach: Sester’s Gate. (GameSpot / Gamer Guides)
6. Faded Citadel (Mammon) — boss: Isaac, the Scholar Prince — approach: Gate of Infinity. (GameSpot / AllThingsHow)

Notes on gate order and conflicts
- Reasoning: the Fainweald three are recommended southern-first (Glutted Mire → Sanguine Caverns → Prisoners’ Domain) because of access and route efficiency; the Mammon three form a practical loop (Withered Shoals → Conquered Temple → Faded Citadel). Sources treat the Mammon order as flexible.
- Conflict to preserve: AltChar reports Mammon has only two Gates; multiple other sources (GameSpot, AllThingsHow, Gamer Guides) support three. The page uses the six-Gate model while noting that specific guides conflict on Mammon’s gate count.

Post-boss Ova rewards (attributed)
- AllThingsHow and GameSpot both report that the post-boss Beacon in a major Corrupted Gate can contain 26 Ova. Treat the 26-Ova figure as launch-guide reporting for that post-boss Beacon rather than an exhaustive universal rule for all Beacons.

Send Ova, the 168 Ovum milestone, and New Game Plus
- Late-game milestone (attributed): multiple guides consistently report 168 Ovum as the amount to bank before a major late-game route unlocks at the Gloom Siphon. AllThingsHow outlines the sequence: bank 168 Ovum → return to the Gloom Siphon → a route opens allowing a four-boss underground sequence (sources report Zmey, the Unbidden as the last) → after clearing that sequence return to Marrow Keep and the Siphon.
- Starting NG+ (current launch reporting): sources (GameSpot and NerdsChalk) identify choosing Send Ova at the Gloom Siphon as the action that starts New Game Plus. This corrects older wording that said Send Ova does not start NG+. Reports say:
  - Defeating the final boss does not automatically force the reset; you can return to Marrow Keep and continue cleaning up the current world.
  - Selecting Send Ova is the irreversible commitment/point of no return and is reportedly not gated by a confirmation prompt.
- Before selecting Send Ova: finish any optional current-run cleanup you want to carry over or that is missable (weapons, Shell pickups, Beacons, quests, trophies). For details on carry-over vs reset behavior, see /mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/.

Common mistakes and practical tips
- Do not treat the corruption-block message as a missing key or hidden item: it is a progression gate tied to Mether’s Breath / Ova siphoning, not a regular breakable object.
- Do not confuse holding Ova in your inventory with siphoning them at the Gloom Siphon — the siphon deposit is the unlock action.
- Rest at Beacons you want available for travel; discovered-but-unrested nodes can behave differently in the travel menu.
- If a Beacon still shows a corrupted status after you believe you cleansed it, treat that as a potential launch-build/UI bug (community reports of stale Beacon labels and fast-travel-list issues exist). No stable workaround is established here.
- Keep a checklist of corruption-blocked routes you encounter, then revisit them after Mether’s Breath to avoid repeated aimless backtracking.
- Do not treat the early Mether’s Breath threshold as the same as the late 168 Ovum milestone; they are separate progression gates.

What happens next (after unlocking Mether’s Breath; and after Send Ova)
- After Mether’s Breath unlocks and you cleanse a corruption growth: you gain route access, can discover/rest at newly revealed Beacons, and can use the direct Beacon travel network to reduce future backtracking. See /mortal-shell-ii/gloom-farm/ for example revisit routes north of Blackridge Pass.
- After you bank 168 Ovum and finish the reported four-boss sequence: return to the Gloom Siphon to decide whether to Send Ova. According to launch reports, selecting Send Ova starts NG+ and resets world content (cleansed Beacons, dungeons, Corrupted Gates, Ova progression), while character-facing progress carries into NG+. For specifics on what carries over, see /mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/.
- The final-boss kill is not reported to force the reset; Send Ova is the player-controlled decision point.

Sources and final caveats
- This guide reflects launch-build reporting from GameSpot, AllThingsHow, NerdsChalk, AltChar, Gamer Guides, and other cited guides (see the repository research ledger). Where sources disagree (early Mether’s Breath threshold, Mammon gate count, exact Ova payouts) the guide preserves those conflicts and directs you to use the in-game prompts (Gloom Siphon messages, Beacon menus) as the true progress authority. Patch notes and hotfixes reviewed for the launch window did not list Ova-system changes, but platform/version differences and post-launch fixes can alter behavior; rely on in-game prompts if your save behaves differently.
