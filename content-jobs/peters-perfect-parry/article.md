Short answer
- Equip the Untarnished Seal and perform Perfect Guards on the Nameless Captive’s qualifying 7‑spin headspin in Prisoners’ Domain (western Corrupted Gate in Fainweald, beside the Nochtean Gate beacon). The trophy (“Peter’s Perfect Parry”) should pop immediately after the seventh successful Perfect Guard. (Reported in AllThingsHow; corroborated by community reports on XboxAchievements.)

Route and preparation
1. Travel to Prisoners’ Domain — the encounter is at the western Corrupted Gate in Fainweald, beside the Nochtean Gate beacon. [IMAGE: Nochtean Gate approach and beacon]
2. Confirm you have the Untarnished Seal equipped. The Untarnished Seal’s Perfect Guard is the registered input for this trophy; do not attempt this with the Infinite Seal (source: AllThingsHow).
3. Bring whatever loadout you normally use for the Nameless Captive fight. Be aware some guides recommend managing Break and health so the boss can reach the required phase and perform the qualifying pattern; this cue comes primarily from a recent guide and should be treated as a practical report rather than a frame-perfect rule.

How to identify the correct headspin (7 hits) vs other spins
- The Nameless Captive has multiple headspin patterns. Current coverage identifies three distinct patterns:
  - A 4‑hit headspin (does not count).
  - A 10‑hit opening pattern (does not count).
  - A qualifying 7‑hit pattern (the one that counts for the trophy).
- The qualifying 7‑hit pattern is reported to appear in phase two after the boss reaches roughly half health and after the scripted Riposte. The visual tell reported by players/guides: the Captive leans over for a wind‑up, does six faster spins, pauses briefly, then executes a seventh spin. Only the full sequence of seven Perfect Guards registers (source: AllThingsHow; community reports).

Seven‑parry attempt — step‑by‑step
1. Enter the fight with the Untarnished Seal equipped.
2. Let the Nameless Captive enter the phase where the qualifying pattern can appear — guides report this happens around half health after the scripted Riposte; treat “half health” as a practical cue from reports, not a guaranteed frame rule.
3. Watch for the lean‑over wind‑up (visual tell). Do not start parrying earlier.
4. When the spin sequence begins, Perfect Guard the first six faster spins in succession.
5. There will be a short, noticeable pause after the sixth spin — do not relax. Maintain your Perfect Guard timing through that pause and then Perfect Guard the seventh spin.
6. If you successfully Perfect Guard all seven spins without being hit, the trophy notification should appear immediately. If it does not, the attempt did not register.

Rhythm warning
- The critical point is the brief pause between spin six and spin seven. Parry rhythm must continue through that pause. The pause is where many attempts fail.

How to reset safely after a failed attempt (without killing the boss)
1. If you fail to secure the seven Perfect Guards, avoid finishing off the Nameless Captive on that run — killing it removes the current-run opportunity.
2. Repair the situation by keeping the boss alive and avoiding chipping its health too low. Several guides recommend managing Break so that a forced Riposte or another scripted move does not interrupt your attempts; this advice comes from player reports and a recent guide and should be used as practical guidance rather than a guaranteed mechanic.
3. Wait for the boss to cycle back to the qualifying pattern and try again.

Confirmation and edge cases
- Trophy pop: the notification is reported to appear immediately after the seventh successful Perfect Guard. If you do not see the notification, the attempt did not register and you must try again (while keeping the boss alive).
- Seal requirement: the Untarnished Seal’s Perfect Guard is the registered input for this trophy. Do not substitute the Infinite Seal for the attempt (source: AllThingsHow).

Common mistakes
- Using the wrong seal (Infinite Seal) instead of the Untarnished Seal.
- Misidentifying the spin pattern — parrying a 4‑hit or 10‑hit pattern does not count.
- Stopping parry timing during the short pause before the seventh spin.
- Getting hit on one of the spins (the sequence must be completed without being hit).
- Allowing the boss to die before completing the sequence; that run’s opportunity is lost.

What happens next (failure and run consequences)
