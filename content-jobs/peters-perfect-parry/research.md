# Research: Peter's Perfect Parry (UPDATE)

Scope: update `/mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/` only. Cover the Nameless Captive location, the Untarnished Seal requirement, the qualifying headspin, retry safety, and NG+ consequence. Do not invent frame timings or a complete boss build.

## Confirmed facts

- Peter's Perfect Parry is a hidden trophy/achievement tied to the Nameless Captive.
- The encounter is in Prisoners' Domain, the western Corrupted Gate in Fainweald, beside the Nochtean Gate beacon.
- Equip the Untarnished Seal. Current coverage says its Perfect Guard is the parry that registers; do not substitute the Infinite Seal for this attempt.
- The Nameless Captive has multiple headspin patterns. Current coverage identifies a 4-hit pattern, a 10-hit opening pattern, and a qualifying 7-hit pattern. Only the 7-hit pattern counts.
- The qualifying pattern appears in phase two after the boss reaches roughly half health and the scripted Riposte. The reported visual tell is a lean-over wind-up followed by six faster spins, a short pause, and a seventh spin. The practical instruction is to Perfect Guard all seven without being hit.
- If the attempt fails, keep the boss alive, avoid chipping the health too low, and wait for another qualifying pattern. Current coverage recommends managing Break so a forced Riposte does not interrupt the sequence.
- The trophy notification should appear immediately after the seventh successful Perfect Guard. If it does not, the attempt did not register.
- Killing the Nameless Captive before completing the trophy removes the current-run opportunity; current coverage identifies New Game Plus as the next reliable retry.
