Quick answer
- Route summary: From the Nochtean Gate Beacon, open the Corrupted Gate into the Prisoner’s Domain, follow the Prisoner’s Way lever/bridge/elevator sequence through Hangman’s Scaffold and Executioner’s Pulpit toward the Bone Gates and the pit arena. Fight the scythe mini‑boss Tishina’s Confessor, then the Nameless Captive at the bottom of the pit; after the Captive dies, cleanse/activate the final boss-area Beacon when it appears and collect the post‑fight Ova (do not assume an exact Ova count). First checklist (navigation anchors)
1. Slumwater Drain — hidden Beacon (optional; reached through a false wall and a drop into a drain).
2. Hangman’s Scaffold — main-route Beacon near a giant statue and shootable jars/barrels.
3. Executioner’s Pulpit — main-route Beacon with the elevator/bridge and the Ova behind a small puzzle gate.
4. Captive’s End — final boss‑area Beacon (activate/cleanse when it appears after/around the Nameless Captive fight).

Step‑by‑step route (follow in order)
1. Cleanse/activate Nochtean Gate Beacon to open the Corrupted Gate into Prisoner’s Domain.
2. Enter the Prisoner’s Way route and use the gate lever reported on the main path to progress into the Domain.
3. Early on, watch for a false wall and a drop/hole that lead to Slumwater Drain. This Beacon is optional and easy to miss if you follow only the linear route.
   [IMAGE: False wall and hole leading to Slumwater Drain entrance]
4. Continue the main route until you reach the area with a large statue and nearby jars/barrels. Shooting the jars/barrels is reported to collapse the statue and create a climbable route; this area corresponds to Hangman’s Scaffold Beacon.
   [IMAGE: Hangman’s Scaffold statue and shootable jars]
5. Proceed from Hangman’s Scaffold toward the Executioner’s Pulpit. Landmarks reported for Executioner’s Pulpit include stairs, a small bridge, a shootable lever that opens a wooden bridge, more stairs, and an elevator leading deeper.
6. At Executioner’s Pulpit, solve the cleanse puzzle (reported sequence):
   1) Unlock the first cell (use the indicated switch).
   2) Clear the hall beyond that switch.
   3) Shoot the small plate by the locked gate.
   4) Cross to the opposite chamber and step on the pressure plate — doing this should open the gate to reach the Ova behind it.
7. Continue through the Bone Gates toward the large tower hole/pit that leads to the final arena area (Captive’s End Beacon marks the boss area). The exact moment Captive’s End appears is reported inconsistently; activate/cleanse it when the in‑game prompt is available.
8. Before entering the pit arena, expect Tishina’s Confessor (scythe mini‑boss) on the approach. Defeating Tishina’s Confessor opens the practical route to the Nameless Captive arena in most reports.
9. Enter the pit and fight the Nameless Captive at the bottom. The Captive drops the Captive’s Scabstone (associated with the Great Martyr’s Blade reward in sources). Common Mistakes
- Missing Slumwater Drain: it’s a hidden Beacon reachable through a false wall and a drop — easy to miss if you only follow the linear path.
- Skipping the jars near the statue: the collapse created by shooting nearby jars/barrels is the reported way to create the climb at Hangman’s Scaffold.
- Doing the Executioner’s Pulpit steps out of order: the reported puzzle requires the switch/cell unlock first, then clearing the hall, then shooting the small plate by the locked gate, then using the opposite pressure plate to reach the Ova.
- Assuming Captive’s End is always present before the boss: sources conflict on the timing; wait for the in‑game prompt to activate/cleanse it.
- Proceeding to the Nameless Captive without consulting the Peter’s Perfect Parry guidance if you’re aiming for the current‑run parry trophy — that page must be checked first to preserve trophy options.

Conflicts and uncertainties to note (preserve source limits)
- Gate direction: one source calls the access gate northwest in Fainweald while another places the approach from a different region side. Use Nochtean Gate Beacon and the Corrupted Gate as the stable access markers rather than a hard compass bearing.
- Three vs four Beacons: some walkthroughs emphasize the linear path (fewer Beacons); multiple sources list four Beacons for the Prisoner’s Domain. Treat Slumwater Drain as a hidden/optional Beacon and Captive’s End as the final boss‑area anchor.
- Captive’s End timing: one source describes it as present before the final fights; another reports it emerging only after the Nameless Captive dies. Activate/cleanse when the in‑game prompt appears.
- Exact turn directions: several sources give left/right turn instructions inconsistently. Rely on the physical landmarks (false wall, statue/jars, bridge/lever/elevator, Bone Gates, tower pit) instead of precise turn orders.
