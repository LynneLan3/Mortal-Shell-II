## Source ledger

### Primary route walkthrough

[Into Indie Games — Mortal Shell 2: Walkthrough Part 4: Prisoner's Domain](https://intoindiegames.com/walkthroughs/mortal-shell-2-walkthrough-part-4-prisoners-domain/) (22–23 August 2026)

- Places the approach at the Nochtean Gate Beacon after the Citadel of Penance / Desolate Peak / Bone Portal route.
- Describes the Prisoner's Way gate lever, the giant statue that can be collapsed by shooting nearby jars, and the Hangman's Scaffold Beacon.
- Describes Executioner's Pulpit as the next Beacon and records an optional white-marked illusory wall shortcut from the statue area toward Tishina's Confessor.
- Gives the Executioner's Pulpit cleanse puzzle: unlock the first cell, use the switch, clear the hall, shoot the small plate by the locked gate, then use the pressure plate in the opposite chamber to reach the Ova.
- Places Tishina's Confessor before the Nameless Captive and says the Confessor opens the Bone Portal route to the arena.
- Records the Nameless Captive's Captive's Scabstone reward and a Beacon/Ova recovery step after the boss.

## Evidence synthesis

### Safe facts for the article

1. Cleanse the **Nochtean Gate Beacon** to open the Corrupted Gate route into Prisoner's Domain.
2. Use the four named Prisoners' Domain Beacons as navigation anchors: **Slumwater Drain**, **Hangman's Scaffold**, **Executioner's Pulpit**, and **Captive's End**.
3. Slumwater Drain is a hidden/optional Beacon reached by a false wall and a drop into the drain; it is easy to miss if the player follows only the linear route.
4. The main route uses the Prisoner's Way gate lever, the collapsed giant statue, Hangman's Scaffold, the Executioner's Pulpit elevator/bridge sequence, and Bone Gates toward Captive's End.
5. Executioner's Pulpit has a cleanse puzzle involving cell locks, a switch, a shootable plate, and a pressure plate; the Ova is behind the resulting gate.
6. Tishina's Confessor is the scythe-wielding mini-boss before the Nameless Captive. The existing [Nameless Captive page](/mortal-shell-ii/nameless-captive) owns the boss fight details.
7. The existing [Peter's Perfect Parry page](/mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry) must be consulted before killing the Nameless Captive if the player wants the current-run trophy attempt.
8. After the boss, activate/cleanse the post-fight Beacon when it is available and collect the Ova. Do not publish an exact Ova count from these sources.

### Conflicts and uncertainty to publish explicitly

- **Gate direction:** Gaming ProMax calls the access gate northwest in Fainweald; Into Indie Games describes the approach from the southwest part of the northern region. Use the stable check—Nochtean Gate Beacon and the Corrupted Gate—and avoid a hard compass claim.
- **Three versus four Beacons:** Into Indie Games focuses on the linear route and names Hangman's Scaffold and Executioner's Pulpit, while 100% Guides, AllThingsHow, and QM Games list the hidden Slumwater Drain and Captive's End for a total of four. Present Slumwater Drain as the hidden Beacon and Captive's End as the final-area Beacon rather than implying every walkthrough exposes all four in sequence.
- **Captive's End timing:** QM Games describes Captive's End as the Beacon reached through Bone Gates and the tower hole before the final fights; Into Indie Games describes a Beacon emerging after the Nameless Captive dies. Treat Captive's End as the final boss-area landmark, and tell readers to activate/cleanse it when the in-game prompt appears. Do not promise that it is available before the fight in every state.
- **Exact turns:** QM Games provides left/right turn instructions, but the other sources summarize the route by landmarks. Publish the physical checks—false wall, statue, bridge, lever, elevator, Bone Gates, tower pit—and state that exact turn descriptions vary. No coordinates.
- **Boss attack labels:** The local Nameless Captive and Peter's Perfect Parry pages already contain the site's bounded combat/trophy guidance. The route page should link to them instead of reconciling conflicting external attack labels.
