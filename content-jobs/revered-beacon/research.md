## Evidence-backed facts

### Location and region

- Revered Beacon is a dungeon in **Mortal Shell II** located in the **Sester's Abbey / Sester's Gate** area of Mammon.
- The clearest documented entrance route starts from **Sester's Gate Beacon**.

### Entrance route (Sester's Gate → Revered Beacon)

Consensus across PowerPyx dungeon/sidearm guides, GameSpot weapon locations, Neoseeker walkthrough, and NerdsChalk Cursed Child coverage:

1. From **Sester's Gate Beacon**, go up the **northwestern stairs**.
2. Enter the courtyard with the large **rolling / Rollerball enemy**.
3. Move onto the **western platform**.
4. Drop to the **lower ledge near the Bone Gate**.
5. Follow the path **north**.
6. Pass the **hammer-wielding enemy**.
7. The **Revered Beacon dungeon entrance** is on the **right**.

An alternate route from **Abbey Entrance** can reach the same general area, but this page should prioritize the Sester's Gate route only. Do not write multiple uncertain complex alternate routes for word count.

### Golden light hazard (inside dungeon)

After entering Revered Beacon:

- A **rotating / tracking golden light** sits in the central / upper area.
- Remaining **exposed in the beam drains health continuously**.
- The light **tracks the player**.
- **Solid walls and terrain cover break line of sight** and provide safe cover.
- The beam **can also damage enemies**, but not quickly enough to rely on as a primary strategy.
- Recommended movement logic: **wait behind cover → watch the beam sweep → move during a safe window → reach the next wall → continue uphill**.

PowerPyx and other walkthroughs describe the documented interior route as working **generally counterclockwise and uphill toward the tower**. Do **not** present counterclockwise as the only valid path. Safe wording: "the clearest documented route works generally counterclockwise and uphill."

Do **not** claim: dodging gives immunity, specific resistance negates the beam, or any unverified mitigation mechanic.

### Interior walkthrough shape

Organize traversal as:

- entrance
- first safe cover
- move between walls
- uphill progression
- upper tower / light source

Player primary question inside: "where do I go next?" Keep steps landmark-based, not coordinate-based.

### Completion (top of dungeon)

- At the upper area, **attack the central structure / pillar / light source** until the golden light is disabled.
- Do **not** invent HP values, required damage types, mandatory weapon types, or hidden interaction prompts.

### Reward

- Clearing Revered Beacon and shutting off the light makes **Cursed Child Sidearm** collectible in the upper area.
- This page mentions the reward briefly and links to `/mortal-shell-ii/cursed-child/` for weapon stats, Tarstones, build, and full sidearm analysis.
