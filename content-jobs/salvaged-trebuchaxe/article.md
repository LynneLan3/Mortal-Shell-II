Short answer
- The Salvaged Trebuchaxe is in the Ravaged Hideout (Fainweald), reachable from Gloomshade Grove Beacon after you defeat the Bloodcursed Lithopod. Kill the Lithopod, loot the Citadel Gate Lever it drops, enter the Ravaged Hideout through the newly opened dungeon veil, follow the dungeon route to the final room, and pick up the Trebuchaxe from a slumped/corpse at the end. Doing so should unlock the Chop Chop achievement.

Required progression (what must be done first)
1. Activate or access Gloomshade Grove Beacon. (/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/)
2. Travel from that Beacon toward the water/ravine below the Citadel of Penance hills (sources variously describe the Hideout as south, southeast, or directly below the Citadel-side hills).
3. Defeat the Bloodcursed Lithopod (the named world‑boss/miniboss that gates the Hideout).
4. Loot the Citadel Gate Lever from the dead Lithopod. Sources agree this is the Lithopod’s drop and that it opens access to the Ravaged Hideout.
5. Enter the Ravaged Hideout and clear the short linear route to the final room.

Step-by-step route to the Salvaged Trebuchaxe
1. From Gloomshade Grove Beacon, head toward the visible water/ravine at the base of the hills near the Citadel of Penance. (Guide descriptions vary between “south” and “southeast”; use the Beacon, the water, the Citadel hills, and the Lithopod as landmarks.)
2. Find and fight the Bloodcursed Lithopod. Loot the Citadel Gate Lever from its corpse when it dies.
3. With the Lithopod dead, locate the Ravaged Hideout entrance nearby (a dungeon veil/cave that becomes accessible after the Lithopod is cleared).
4. Enter the Hideout; take the lift down when available to descend into the lair.
5. Follow the main linear path through corrupted rock corridors and gaps. Proceed toward the open areas and the final chamber.
   - You may encounter a Gloom-lit Beacon inside; pass or activate it.
   - Some sources describe a clearing with a central corrupter pillar and a chest (Gamer Guides names a Tooth of Nochte in a chest; Thumb Wars describes a chest beside a pillar). Treat any such chest as optional loot—its exact placement is not universally consistent across sources.
   - Sources disagree on small internal wording (gaps, right-side squeeze, upper side route). Follow visible paths and the dungeon’s natural progression.
6. Near the final pickup you will need to deal with three enemies that appear there. After handling or bypassing them, interact with the slumped soldier/corpse in the final room to collect the Salvaged Trebuchaxe.
7. Be prepared for enemies on the exit; at least one secondary source reports additional foes (wraiths) appearing after pickup. That report is not universally confirmed, so remain cautious while leaving.

Notes about the Citadel Gate Lever
- The Citadel Gate Lever is the Lithopod’s drop and is the item that opens the Ravaged Hideout entrance.
- You may either enter the Hideout immediately after killing the Lithopod or take the Lever back to the Citadel and install it in the main gate mechanism; installing it will keep the Citadel’s main gate open permanently for the run. The Lever is a progression/route item, not the Trebuchaxe itself.
- A separate hidden teleport near One-Legged Wolf is mentioned by some Citadel guides as an alternative route into the Citadel, but it is not required to obtain the Trebuchaxe.

What the Salvaged Trebuchaxe does (practical summary)
- Category: Sidearm (Fainweald).
- Attack style: Launches axes as projectiles rather than firing bolts or performing a melee swing.
- Firing behavior: Has a chargeable shot—holding the fire button increases power. Charging trades shot speed/range for higher single-target burst.
- Role: Mid-range, heavy single-target burst sidearm. Charging can defeat some standard enemies in one hit according to at least one guide; treat that as a practical report, not a universal guarantee.
- Comparison: Slower charge and shorter reach than dedicated long-range sidearms; positioned as a heavier, high-burst alternative (not a sniper-style tool).

Upgrade and Tarstone context (what to expect; no numbers)
- The Trebuchaxe can be upgraded through the Tarforge once the Obsidian Lathe unlocks the sidearm-enhancement path. See /mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/ for the Obsidian Lathe context.
- Named Tarstones and stones that guides list for Trebuchaxe builds include Tarred Fragment (listed as Trebuchaxe-specific), Squall Stone, Charged Stone, Devout Stone, and others. AllThingsHow highlights Devout Stone (Resolve restore on kill) and Charged Stone (faster charge) as practical options. These are reported build ideas; do not assume specific percentages or values without cross‑checked numbers.
- Balance Patch 1 notes do not list a direct Trebuchaxe tweak in the official patch list checked; do not claim a direct buff/nerf from those notes.

Common mistakes and things to avoid
- Mistaking the Citadel Gate Lever for the weapon itself. The Lever opens the Hideout/main gate; the Trebuchaxe is inside the Ravaged Hideout at the end of the dungeon.
- Expecting precise map coordinates or a single cardinal bearing from Gloomshade Grove; sources disagree (south vs. southeast vs. “below the hills”), so use visible landmarks (Beacon, water/ravine, Citadel hills, Lithopod).
- Treating the Lithopod as a normal minor enemy or mixing up spellings; the boss is the named Bloodcursed Lithopod (some sources misspell it “Lichopod”).
- Assuming any single source’s interior details (a specific chest, guaranteed Laterite, or a fixed wraith spawn on exit) are universal. Some interior loot/spawn reports come from individual guides and are not cross-checked as guaranteed.

What happens next (after pickup)
- The Salvaged Trebuchaxe appears as a sidearm pickup from the slumped soldier/corpse at the end of the Hideout. Confirm the pickup in your equipment/inventory to get the Chop Chop achievement.
- If you installed the Citadel Gate Lever at the Citadel main gate instead of entering the Hideout first, that main gate will remain open permanently for the current playthrough.
- To upgrade and customize the Trebuchaxe, use the Tarforge once you unlock the Obsidian Lathe sidearm path. See /mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/ and /mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/ for upgrade options and stone compatibility.
