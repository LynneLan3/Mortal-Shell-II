## Answer-first facts

- The **Salvaged Trebuchaxe** is a Fainweald sidearm found inside the **Ravaged Hideout**, near the lake / ravine at the base of the hills outside the **Citadel of Penance**.
- The practical route anchor is **Gloomshade Grove Beacon**. Sources describe the Hideout as south, southeast, or directly below the Citadel-side hills; the stable landmarks are the Beacon, the water, the Citadel of Penance, and the nearby Bloodcursed Lithopod.
- The **Bloodcursed Lithopod** is the nearby world-boss/miniboss gate. Defeating it drops the **Citadel Gate Lever** and opens access to the Ravaged Hideout. The same Lever can be installed at the Citadel’s main gate to keep that entrance open permanently.
- After the Lithopod is dead, enter the Hideout and follow its linear route to the final reward area. The Trebuchaxe is picked up from a slumped body / corpse at the end, not awarded by a separate NPC or a second boss.
- The sidearm launches **axes as projectiles**. Current guide coverage describes it as slower than many sidearms, with a chargeable shot that trades speed/range for high single-target burst. Do not publish fixed damage, Resolve cost, charge time, or capacity values because the checked sources do not provide a stable cross-checked stat sheet.

## Prerequisites and progression context

### Reliable requirements

1. Reach Fainweald and activate or access **Gloomshade Grove Beacon**.
2. Travel to the nearby water / ravine at the base of the Citadel of Penance hills.
3. Defeat the **Bloodcursed Lithopod**.
4. Loot the **Citadel Gate Lever** from the defeated creature. Sources agree that this is the item dropped by the Lithopod and that killing it opens the nearby Hideout.
5. Enter **Ravaged Hideout** and clear its short dungeon route to the final room.

No checked source requires a named key, quest hand-in, NPC dialogue, exact character level, particular Shell, or a prior Citadel boss kill for the Trebuchaxe pickup. Recommending a melee weapon, healing, and enough Resolve for the approach is practical preparation, not a documented item gate.

### Citadel Gate Lever relationship

The Lever is a useful route reward, but it is not the Trebuchaxe. After the Lithopod fight, the player can either enter the nearby Hideout first or return to the Citadel’s sealed main gate and place the Lever in the wall mechanism. The Citadel route sources agree that installing it opens the main entrance permanently for the current playthrough.

Some Citadel guides also report a separate hidden teleport near One-Legged Wolf that can bypass the main gate and drop the player into the Citadel’s prison cells. That alternative route is context only and should not be presented as a requirement for the Trebuchaxe; the Lithopod → Hideout route is the clear item-specific path.

## Route evidence

### Overworld approach

- **AllThingsHow — All 8 Sidearms:** places the Hideout south of Gloomshade Grove, at the base of the hills below the Citadel, and says the Lithopod must be killed before the lair opens.
- **AllThingsHow — All 16 Weapon and Sidearm Locations:** describes the Hideout at the base of the hilly area outside the Citadel and confirms the Lithopod also drops the item that opens the Citadel gates.
- **AllThingsHow — Best Sidearms to Get First:** describes the practical route as from Gloomshade Grove southeast to the Hideout and identifies the Lithopod as the world-boss gate.
- **Thumb Wars:** describes heading directly south from Gloomshade Grove toward a lake, then reaching the Hideout through winding paths and wooden boardwalks; it spells the boss name as “Lichopod,” which conflicts with the stable in-game/source spelling **Lithopod**.
- **Gamer Guides:** lists Ravaged Hideout as inaccessible until the Bloodcursed Lithopod is defeated and places the dungeon in the same local area.

Guide wording about “south” versus “southeast,” and whether the water is described as a lake, pool, or waterfall approach, should be treated as local route-description variance. The guide should say “head south/southeast from Gloomshade Grove toward the water below the Citadel hills” only as a qualified summary, then rely on visible landmarks.

### Dungeon route

The stable sequence is:

1. Enter through the dungeon veil / cave after the Lithopod gate is cleared.
2. Take the lift down into the Ravaged Hideout.
3. Follow the main path through the corrupted rock corridors and gaps.
4. Activate or pass the Gloom-lit Beacon landmark if encountered.
5. Check the open area and nearby chest before continuing. Gamer Guides reports a **Tooth of Nochte** in a chest; Thumb Wars reports a chest beside a central corrupter pillar. The exact chest landmark is not cross-checked as universal, so label this as an optional nearby loot check rather than a required weapon step.
6. Continue through the gap / upper side of the route toward the final room.
7. Deal with the three enemies that appear near the final pickup, then interact with the slouched soldier / body to collect the Salvaged Trebuchaxe.

Thumb Wars gives the most detailed interior sequence: descend by lift, follow the corrupted chasm, pass a Gloom Beacon, reach a clearing with a corrupter pillar and chest, go up the other side, squeeze through a gap on the right, then fight or bypass three enemies before looting the slouched soldier. Gamer Guides instead emphasizes boxes, a gap, an open area, a Gloom Tree with **Laterite**, and the same three-enemy encounter before the pickup. Preserve both as source-reported landmarks; do not turn every one into a mandatory room order.

One Portuguese guide reports wraiths appearing after the pickup on the way out. This is a practical warning from a single secondary source, not a stable universal encounter claim. The article may say “be ready for enemies on the exit” without asserting a fixed wraith spawn.

## Weapon details and practical use

### Stable description

- It is a **Sidearm** that fires thrown axes rather than arrows, bullets, or a melee swing.
- Holding the fire input is reported to charge the shot for a stronger hit.
- Current launch coverage positions it as a mid-range, high single-target-burst option. One guide says a charged throw can one-shot many standard enemies; preserve that as a practical report, not a universal damage guarantee.
- The slower charge and shorter effective reach than a dedicated long-range sidearm are the tradeoff. The Forgotten Crossbow is the better comparison for distant precision; the Trebuchaxe is the heavier single-target choice.

### Upgrade and Tarstone context

The sidearm can be upgraded through the Tarforge after the **Obsidian Lathe** unlocks the sidearm-enhancement path. Existing database coverage lists **Tarred Fragment** as a Trebuchaxe-specific ability stone and also lists **Squall Stone**, **Charged Stone**, **Devout Stone**, and other compatible stones. AllThingsHow specifically highlights **Devout Stone** for restoring Resolve on a kill and **Charged Stone** for faster charge; these are source-reported build ideas, not mandatory prerequisites.

The official Balance Patch 1 notes say **Squall Stone was buffed**, but do not document a direct Salvaged Trebuchaxe damage, firing, or charge change. The patch’s direct weapon list names other weapons and does not mention this sidearm. The guide should therefore say “no direct Trebuchaxe change is listed in the checked Balance Patch 1 notes,” not claim that the weapon was unchanged in every later build.

Do not copy exact Tarstone percentages or damage numbers from isolated guide/database entries into the page unless they are independently verified. The purpose of this page is location-first acquisition with a useful role summary.

## Trophy and achievement evidence

The official Steam Global Achievements page lists:

- **Chop Chop** — “Unlock the Salvaged Trebuchaxe.”
- **Guns. Lots of Guns** — “Unlock all Sidearms.”

The item pickup is the supported completion event. No checked source adds a kill, upgrade, Tarstone, or boss-specific condition beyond obtaining the sidearm. The guide should tell the reader to confirm the inventory pickup and watch for **Chop Chop**, then link to the eight-sidearm checklist and the wider trophies page.
