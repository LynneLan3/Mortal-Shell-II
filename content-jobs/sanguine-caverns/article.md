Short answer
Follow this sequence: Stonebled Gate → Pulsing Caves → Sanguine Shoreline → Tarblighted Stoner → The Lost Child → Child's Feeding Grounds. Cleanse the named Beacons as you go, collect the listed Ova and items, defeat Tarblighted Stoner and The Lost Child, then cleanse the Feeding Grounds and check the innermost chamber for the Blackmarrow Key.

Step-by-step route (do these in order)
1. Cleanse Stonebled Gate Beacon to open access to the Sanguine Caverns Corrupted Gate.
   - Into Indie Games and AllThingsHow both present Stonebled Gate as the required access checkpoint.
   - If you haven’t cleared the Stonebled Gate branch that includes the Hidden Keep / Lucian or the Thirsting Knight mini-boss, see /mortal-shell-ii/lucian-thirsting-knight/ before proceeding.

2. Enter Sanguine Caverns and cross the initial collapsing wooden-bridge / cliff approach.
   - Watch for explosive cocoons and flying enemies that can knock you off narrow bridges; avoid contact and keep lateral movement.

3. Activate and cleanse Pulsing Caves Beacon.
   - Landmarks reported by AllThingsHow: a succession of wooden bridges, a ranged-ambush wall, a drop through a gap, a red‑stained cliffside, then a grey sandy stretch ending at a dark organic Beacon structure.
   - Cleansing the Beacon establishes a respawn and travel checkpoint and reveals local Ova / blocked loot.

4. From Pulsing Caves, descend through the nearby hole/crater toward the lower caverns.
   - Into Indie Games describes this drop-down: avoid spike-filled holes, and do not approach the three cocoons blocking the lower path—shoot them from range to clear the path.
   - This descent leads to the Sanguine Shoreline Beacon area.

5. If Sanguine Shoreline is corrupted, run the Hermit’s Pass cleanse dungeon (short, practical checklist from AllThingsHow):
   1. Cross the wooden bridge.
   2. Defeat the spider-like skeletons in the area.
   3. Climb the narrow crevice on the right-side to collect the Revered Gland.
   4. Return to cleanse the corrupted tree and collect the Ova.
   - Sanguine Shoreline is the last stable Beacon before the Tarblighted Stoner / Lost Child approach in the checked guides.

6. Return to the surface and use the nearby bone teleporter (beacon/fast-travel context: see /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).
   - Approach and defeat Tarblighted Stoner (a rock-throwing miniboss).
   - Practical tips from Into Indie Games: stay mobile, treat most attacks as unsafe to parry unless the tell is very clear, and wait out the late rock-throw sequence before committing to punishment.

7. After Tarblighted Stoner dies, step onto the white spot that appears to transition into the Corrupted Gate boss arena.
   - In the approach cave leading to the arena, summon Tiel if the spirit-ally option is available (summon noted in Gaming ProMax).
   - Enter the arena and defeat The Lost Child. Concise, cross-source fight advice:
     - Position to the side rather than directly in front of its mouth.
     - Move laterally or diagonally to handle its projectile barrage.
     - Avoid the front-of-mouth Digest & Excrete grab; do not stand in front of the creature’s maw.
   - Reward: Lost Clotstone.

8. After the kill: cleanse the newly spawned Child's Feeding Grounds Beacon, collect the Ova, then re-enter the dungeon.
   - Check the innermost/former Ova chamber for the newly accessible Blackmarrow Key room (post-boss follow-up noted by the Lost Child and Blackmarrow Key sources).
   - XboxPlay and the local Lost Child page agree on this follow-up; see /mortal-shell-ii/blackmarrow-keys/ for key context.

Conflicts and source limits you should know (preserved exactly as reported)
- Where the route starts: sources tie the broader access sequence to Stonebled Gate, but many boss guides start the approach from Sanguine Shoreline because it is the practical last checkpoint before Tarblighted Stoner and The Lost Child.
- Area names vary between sources: Sanguine Caverns, Sanguine Caverns Gate, and Blood Caves are used; some sources distinguish the overworld area from the Corrupted Gate dungeon. This page uses Sanguine Caverns as the primary name.
- Beacon order wording: checked guides agree Pulsing Caves comes before Sanguine Shoreline but do not all present the same room-by-room map; this guide uses named Beacons and the described descent/cocoon sequence rather than asserting a full map.
- Child's Feeding Grounds label: sources alternate between “Child's Feeding Ground” (singular) and “Child's Feeding Grounds” (plural). The local Beacon guide uses Child's Feeding Grounds; follow the label your game shows.
- Post-boss key timing: sources agree you must cleanse the final Beacon before returning, but they disagree about whether the key room unlocks immediately after the kill or only after the cleanse. Safe instruction: cleanse Child's Feeding Grounds, then enter the dungeon and check the innermost chamber.
- Exact Ova totals and thresholds: reported Ova counts and global thresholds vary. Consult /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ and your in-game prompt rather than relying on a fixed number.

Common mistakes
- Skipping Stonebled Gate cleansing and getting stuck at the Corrupted Gate — Stonebled Gate is the named access checkpoint in the checked guides.
- Trying to melee the three cocoons instead of shooting them from range; approaching them is unsafe.
- Standing in front of The Lost Child’s mouth instead of staying to the side.
- Attempting to parry Tarblighted Stoner attacks without a clear tell and getting punished during the late rock-throw sequence.
- Missing the Revered Gland in Hermit’s Pass by failing to climb the narrow right-side crevice.
- Assuming the post-boss Blackmarrow Key room is unlocked before you cleanse Child’s Feeding Grounds; timing is inconsistent across sources—cleanse first, then check.
