## Answer-first route

The reliable route spine is **Stonebled Gate → Pulsing Caves → Sanguine Shoreline → Tarblighted Stoner → The Lost Child → Child's Feeding Grounds**.

1. Cleanse **Stonebled Gate Beacon** to open the Sanguine Caverns Corrupted Gate. The Stonebled Gate route includes the Hidden Keep / Lucian branch; see the existing Lucian page if that checkpoint is not cleared.
2. Enter Sanguine Caverns and cross the collapsing wooden-bridge and cliff route. Avoid explosive cocoons and flying enemies that can push the player from narrow bridges.
3. Activate and cleanse **Pulsing Caves Beacon**. The landmark sequence is wooden bridges, a drop past ranged/crawling enemies, a red-stained cliffside, and a grey sandy stretch ending at a dark organic Beacon structure.
4. From Pulsing Caves, descend through the nearby hole/crater. Continue downward, avoid spike-filled holes, and shoot the three cocoons blocking the lower path rather than approaching them. This route reaches **Sanguine Shoreline Beacon**.
5. If Sanguine Shoreline is still corrupted, enter its cleanse dungeon, **Hermit's Pass**. Cross the wooden bridge, defeat the spider-like skeletons, climb the narrow crevice for the **Revered Gland**, then cleanse the corrupted tree and collect the Ova.
6. Return to the surface and use the nearby bone teleporter. Defeat **Tarblighted Stoner**, then jump on the white spot to enter the Corrupted Gate boss arena.
7. In the approach cave, summon **Tiel** if the spirit-ally option is available. Enter the arena and defeat **The Lost Child**. Stay to the side, avoid the front-of-mouth grab, and move laterally against its projectile pressure.
8. Claim **Lost Clotstone**. Activate and cleanse the newly spawned **Child's Feeding Grounds** Beacon, collect the Ova, then re-enter the dungeon and check the innermost chamber for the newly opened Blackmarrow Key room before leaving.

No map coordinates are used. The checked sources describe the same route with different map labels and visual presentations, but do not provide a shared, auditable coordinate system.

## Source-backed facts

### Entry and gate access

- **Stonebled Gate Beacon** is the named access checkpoint for Sanguine Caverns in the local Ova and Beacon pages.
- Into Indie Games states that Stonebled Gate must be cleansed before the corruption outside the Sanguine Caverns gate can be cleared. Its route includes the Lucian, the Thirsting Knight mini-boss before the gate opens.
- AllThingsHow's Pulsing Caves guide also makes the Stonebled Gate cleanse the first gate-opening step and ties the required overworld corruption removal to the Ova / Mether's Breath progression.
- The exact broader Fainweald order is not mandatory. The local Ova page recommends Sanguine Caverns as the second major Fainweald gate, but this article should present that as a practical route recommendation, not a quest lock.

### Pulsing Caves

- Pulsing Caves is the first named Beacon inside the Sanguine Caverns Corrupted Gate in the checked AllThingsHow guide.
- The stable landmarks are wooden bridges, a wall/ranged ambush, a drop through a gap, a red-stained cliffside, and a grey sandy area before the dark organic Beacon structure.
- Cleansing the Beacon reveals the Ova / blocked loot and establishes a respawn and travel checkpoint. Exact reward counts are omitted because they are not needed for the walkthrough.

### Lower cavern route and Sanguine Shoreline

- Into Indie Games describes returning to the surface after Pulsing Caves, dropping through a nearby hole, descending through the cavern, avoiding spiked holes, and shooting three cocoons before reaching Sanguine Shoreline.
- AllThingsHow describes Sanguine Shoreline as a corrupted Beacon with a short Hermit's Pass cleanse dungeon. The practical sub-route is bridge, spider-like skeletons, narrow right-side crevice, Revered Gland, then the corrupted tree.
- Sanguine Shoreline is the last stable Beacon landmark before the Tarblighted Stoner and Lost Child approach. The local Lost Child page also uses Sanguine Shoreline as the route anchor.

### Tarblighted Stoner and the arena transition

- After cleansing or reaching Sanguine Shoreline, the checked walkthroughs place a bone teleporter near the miniboss approach.
- Tarblighted Stoner is described as a stronger rock-throwing enemy. The source-backed practical advice is to stay mobile, treat most attacks as unsafe to parry unless the tell is clear, and wait out the late rock-throw sequence before punishing.
- Into Indie Games says the white spot appears after the Stoner is defeated and is the transition into the Gate's boss arena. No coordinate or compass instruction is needed.

### The Lost Child and post-boss cleanup

- The Lost Child is the major boss at the end of Sanguine Caverns. The existing local [Lost Child page](/mortal-shell-ii/lost-child/) owns the detailed boss mechanics and Balance Patch 1 note; this route page should not duplicate that page's full attack guide.
- The consistent fight guidance is to stay to the side rather than in front of the mouth, move laterally or diagonally against the ranged barrage, and avoid the Digest & Excrete grab. The checked sources differ on attack names and phase timing, so the route page keeps this advice concise.
- The named reward is **Lost Clotstone**.
- After the kill, a new Child's Feeding Grounds Beacon appears in the arena. Cleanse it and collect the Ova before leaving.
- The local Lost Child page and the checked Blackmarrow Key route agree on the follow-up: re-enter the Child's Feeding Grounds dungeon after cleansing, return to the former Ova / innermost chamber area, and collect the newly accessible Blackmarrow Key.
- For Ova deposit, corruption, and gate progression context, link the existing [Ova guide](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/) instead of asserting a fixed universal threshold.

## Conflicting route descriptions to preserve explicitly

1. **Where the route starts:** the broader access sequence starts at Stonebled Gate, but boss guides often start at Sanguine Shoreline because it is the practical last checkpoint before Tarblighted Stoner and The Lost Child. The article should use both: Stonebled Gate for unlocking the dungeon, Sanguine Shoreline for the final approach.
2. **Area names:** sources alternate between **Sanguine Caverns**, **Sanguine Caverns Gate**, and **Blood Caves**, and sometimes distinguish the overworld area from the Corrupted Gate dungeon. Use Sanguine Caverns as the page's primary name and mention the variants once.
3. **Beacon order wording:** the checked guides agree on Pulsing Caves before Sanguine Shoreline, but not every guide exposes the same Beacon list or describes the same surface return. Use the named Beacon landmarks and the descent / cocoon sequence rather than claiming a room-by-room map.
4. **Child's Feeding Grounds label:** sources use both singular “Child's Feeding Ground” and plural “Child's Feeding Grounds.” The local Beacon guide uses **Child's Feeding Grounds**; readers should follow the label shown in their game.
5. **Post-boss key timing:** sources agree on cleansing the final Beacon before the return trip, but vary between saying the key room unlocks immediately after the kill and after the cleanse. The safe instruction is to cleanse Child's Feeding Grounds, return through **Enter Dungeon**, and check the former Ova / innermost chamber.
6. **Exact Ova totals:** source-reported Ova values and global thresholds vary. This page names the Ova handoff without publishing a disputed count; use the [Ova guide](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/) and the current in-game prompt.
