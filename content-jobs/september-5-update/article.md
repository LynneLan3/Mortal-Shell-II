The Sep. 5 update (official Steam announcement: “New Update Out Now”) introduces an Experimental Adaptive Difficulty option plus balance changes, bug fixes, and stability work described as “Several crash fixes and optimisations.” This page explains what that means for players, how to toggle the new system, and where to go next for the Adaptive Difficulty guide or current PC crash guidance.

What Adaptive Difficulty does (official)

- Adaptive Difficulty is optional and defaults to OFF.
- Toggle path: Game > Game Settings > Adaptive Difficulty.
- During sustained winning, enemy damage, health, and break resistance increase.
- During sustained deaths, those values reduce; the system also limits the maximum share of player health a single attack can remove.
- Difficulty changes gradually return toward normal balance over time.
- The system can push enemies beyond the game's original highest standard difficulty after sustained success. (The update notes do not call this infinite scaling.)

Why players are asking questions (community reports)

Community threads show active questions about how Adaptive Difficulty interacts with Night Mode, boss damage, and break resistance. Some players report increased break resistance has practical effects on parry- or break-oriented builds. Those reports explain why players are discussing interactions, but they do not replace the official descriptions above.

How this behaves in practice — quick editorial summary

- Think of Adaptive Difficulty as a two-way dynamic challenge system: it can make encounters harder after repeated successes and ease some pressure after repeated deaths.
- Changes are gradual and can drift back toward baseline over time.

(EDITORIAL ADVICE) When to use Adaptive Difficulty

- Use it if you want a dynamic challenge that responds to win/loss streaks.
- Keep it OFF if you want fixed, repeatable difficulty for build testing, speedrunning practice, or controlled comparisons — those activities rely on consistent enemy stats.

Where to toggle it (numbered steps)

1. Open the pause menu.
2. Go to Game > Game Settings.
3. Find "Adaptive Difficulty" and flip the toggle. It defaults to OFF.

Balance, combat, and bug-fix notes

- The update contains balance changes and combat-related fixes. The official brief did not list a new numeric stat table; do not assume specific multipliers or values.
- The Adaptive Difficulty adjustments explicitly mention damage, health, and break resistance but do not specify exact amounts.
- Bug fixes and optimisations are included; the official phrasing for stability work is “Several crash fixes and optimisations.”

Common mistakes to avoid

- Assuming Adaptive Difficulty is mandatory. It is optional and off by default.
- Treating community reports of specific effects (for example, on parry/break playstyles) as official mechanics. Use them as context for player discussion, not as definitive rules.
- Expecting all crashes to be resolved. The update notes only reference several fixes and optimisations.
- Turning on Adaptive Difficulty when you need repeatable, measurable enemy behaviour for testing builds or comparisons.

Practical tips for players

- If you want a steady challenge that adapts to your performance, try Adaptive Difficulty ON for several sessions and watch how enemy damage and break resistance respond.
- If you’re benchmarking a build or testing an exploit/technique, keep Adaptive Difficulty OFF to ensure consistent enemy behaviour.
- If you notice changes in boss behaviour or break resistance after the update, check whether Adaptive Difficulty was enabled while you were playing.
