## OFFICIAL CONFIRMED

- Official Steam announcement: “New Update Out Now”.
- The update adds **Adaptive Difficulty (Experimental)**.
- Adaptive Difficulty is optional and defaults to **OFF**.
- Toggle path: `Game > Game Settings > Adaptive Difficulty`.
- During sustained winning, enemy damage, health, and break resistance increase.
- During sustained deaths, those values reduce; the system also limits the maximum share of player health that one attack can remove.
- Difficulty changes gradually return toward normal balance over time.
- Adaptive Difficulty can push enemies beyond the game's original highest standard difficulty after sustained success. Do not call this infinite scaling.
- Night Mode does not become easier because Adaptive Difficulty scales downward.
- The update also contains balance changes and bug fixes.
- Official crash/stability wording: “Several crash fixes and optimisations.”

## COMMUNITY REPORTED

- Community discussion shows active player questions about Adaptive Difficulty with Night Mode, boss damage, and break resistance.
- Some players report that increased break resistance affects parry/break-oriented playstyles. Do not state a multiplier or universal result.

## EDITORIAL / INFERENCE

- Recommend the new guide for players who want a dynamic challenge, while preserving fixed, repeatable difficulty for build testing or controlled comparisons.
