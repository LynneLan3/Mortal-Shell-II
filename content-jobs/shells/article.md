Short answer — which Shells exist and which to pick first
- Mortal Shell II launch coverage lists nine Shells total: Harros (prologue-only) plus eight permanent Shells you can keep and Bond. The eight permanent Shells are: Tiel, Proxima, Gragu, Eredrim, Smert, Lazlo, Sariel, and Genessa (a.k.a. Sester Genessa). Harros is given in the prologue and is lost irreversibly at the prologue’s end.
- Permanence rule: locating a Shell’s body is usually not sufficient — you must complete that Shell’s first Memory or its route-specific Memory sequence before it becomes permanently unlockable.
- Bonding and perks: Shell Bonding uses Glimpses at the Shellkeeper; Balance Patch 1 moved Shell-map reveals from Glimpses to Gloom and refunds eligible pre‑patch Glimpse spending at the Shellkeeper alcove in Blackmarrow. See /mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/ and /mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/ for details and platform-timing caveats.

Roster and how to make a Shell permanent
Below are the nine roster entries with the exact route anchors and the concrete steps supported by the collected sources. For each permanent Shell follow the numbered steps in order.

1) Harros the Vassal (prologue-only)
1. Harros is the Shell you inhabit during the prologue.
2. Harros is not recoverable after the prologue ends — current guides report the loss is irreversible.

2) Tiel the Acolyte (Fainweald — near Widow’s Overlook)
1. Travel to the Fainweald route and find the large tree/wooden enclosure landmark near Widow’s Overlook.
2. Interact with the body/grave (two torches and a shallow grave are the local landmarks).
3. Complete the Rash Memory / side-window + flask sequence noted on the local route page.
4. After the Memory completes, Tiel is treated as a permanent Shell.

See the Tiel route page: /mortal-shell-ii/tiel/

3) Proxima the Broodseeker (Shattered Beacon — Blackridge Pass area)
1. Go to the Shattered Beacon near Blackridge Pass.
2. Perform the local unlock sequence (the local transcript records four interactions culminating in a UI message).
3. Confirm the UI message NEW SHELL DISCOVERED — PROXIMA and then complete the Shell’s first Memory to make Proxima permanent.

See the Proxima route page: /mortal-shell-ii/proxima/

[IMAGE: Proxima’s Shattered Beacon or the NEW SHELL DISCOVERED UI capture]

4) Gragu the Insatiable (One-Legged Wolf Tavern / Temple of Vatra)
1. Obtain the Heart of Vatra at the Temple of Vatra (the Heart is the quest objective for this route).
2. Return the Heart of Vatra to Gragu at the One-Legged Wolf Tavern.
3. The handoff triggers an event that makes Gragu’s body inhabitable.
4. Complete Gragu’s first Memory to make the Shell permanent.

Notes: wider travel/corruption access may be required to reach these areas; local guides avoid a fixed Ova count. See /mortal-shell-ii/gragu/ for the owner route.

5) Eredrim the Venerable (Citadel of Penance)
1. Reach the Citadel of Penance and defeat The Warden boss.
2. Interact with Eredrim’s remains after the Warden encounter.
3. Complete Eredrim’s first Memory to treat the Shell as permanent.

See /mortal-shell-ii/eredrim/

6) Smert the Apostate (Prophet’s Rest — Outskirts of Nochte)
1. Go to Prophet’s Rest and ensure all three blood pools/ritual prerequisites are active (the local route warns to check pool states before starting).
2. Begin the route sequence that starts The Convert.
3. Defeat the Hierarch and complete Smert’s associated Memory to make the Shell permanent.

See /mortal-shell-ii/smert/

[IMAGE: Smert’s three blood pools at Prophet’s Rest]

7) Lazlo the Justiciar (Mammon — High Lord’s Courtyard / keep)
1. Enter the Mammon High Lord’s Courtyard / keep and defeat Vellen, Lord of Mammon.
2. Proceed to the crypt/final chamber and interact with Lazlo’s remains.
3. Complete the required Memory to make Lazlo permanent.

Notes: this route is corroborated by independent guides but a local Lazlo page was not present in this checkout; avoid assuming room coordinates or extra steps.

8) Sariel the Endless (Mammon — Silent Steps → Chamber of Becoming)
1. Travel through Silent Steps toward the Chamber of Becoming.
2. Destroy the four revival markers/tablets described in the local route.
3. Defeat the encounter in the Chamber of Becoming. After the fight, interact with the nearby Unknown Shell and finish its Memory sequence to make Sariel permanent.

See /mortal-shell-ii/sariel/

9) Genessa / Sester Genessa the Wayward (Marrow Keep → Mammon Sester Secundus route)
1. On the Marrow Keep / Mammon route, defeat Sester Secundus (route owner: Sester Genessa page).
2. Retrieve the route-required item and return it as the handoff step. Guides conflict on the item’s name (Censer vs Chalice); preserve both names and follow the in‑game prompt.
3. Complete Genessa’s Memory and use the Shell prompt. Genessa is also noted as a dialogue-based Bonding exception in some guides.

Bonding, Shellkeeper, and resource changes (what to do next)
1. To open Shell perk tiers, interact with the Shellkeeper (Zhirelle) in Marrow Keep and spend Glimpses; higher Bond levels unlock higher perk tiers. See /mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/ for details.
2. Balance Patch 1 moved Shell‑map reveals from Glimpses to Gloom and refunded eligible pre‑patch Glimpse spending at the Shellkeeper’s Blackmarrow alcove. Local rollout timing was first observed on PC; PS5 receipt was reported; Xbox timing remained unconfirmed in the reviewed evidence. See /mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/ for the patch context.
3. If you opened a Shell body but didn’t finish that Shell’s first Memory, return to the site and complete the Memory — the permanent unlock only occurs after the Memory/route-specific sequence completes.

Progression timing guidance (early → late)
- Prologue: Harros is temporary and lost at the end.
- Immediately after prologue / early Fainweald: Tiel and Proxima are the clearest early permanent options.
- Fainweald mid-route: Gragu (Heart of Vatra), Eredrim (Citadel/Warden), and Smert (Prophet’s Rest ritual) are mid-route targets requiring wider travel/ritual access.
- Later Mammon: Lazlo, Sariel, and Genessa are Mammon-side route content, often gated by boss encounters or multi-stage sequences.

Practical use cases supported by the evidence
- Tiel — recommended as an early mobile / evasive / Shadow-style Shell in current launch guides and local route notes. Phrase: “good early choice for a mobile playstyle.”
- Proxima — the local transcript/UI and independent guides support a lightning-lance pull utility and observed damage‑mitigation behavior in a single walkthrough; useful for ranged/pull setups, but the mitigation claim is not a universal stat.
- Smert — local pages support a Miracle / time-control identity; use Smert when you want Miracle‑flavored options.
- Eredrim — independent coverage positions Eredrim as a mid→late recommendation; the route timing is the supported claim, not a full build table.
- Genessa — route- and dialogue-specific Bonding behavior is supported; treat Genessa as a unique interaction rather than a standard corpse pickup.

What we do not have reliable evidence for
- No universal S/A/B tier list is supported by the current evidence set.
- No complete HP/damage/stat tables, full ability trees, or absolute “best Shell” rankings are supported without controlled tests.
- No fixed Ova counts or guaranteed progression gates beyond the route descriptions above — local guides intentionally avoid rigid Ova numbers.

NPC questlines that affect Shells and stateful NPC rules
Begin with the reset rule: exhaust a character’s dialogue, rest once at a Beacon, return and recheck the NPC to confirm state changes. Below are the bounded NPC chains that interact with Shell and progression content.

1. Gorf, Hilga, and Grom (Fainweald)
- Shape: stateful chain moving from Weltcaps/Moonshine exchanges to the Poisoned Dagger handoff and a final cure/young-toad scene.
- Rule: use Beacon rests to advance and verify. Do not rely on a single fixed item-count from current guides.

2. Grisha Pup and Hunter trader (Mushroom Village area)
1. Buy the Grisha Remnant (and any desired stock) from the Hunter trader before opening the cage.
2. Open the cage and free the Grisha Pup. The Pup then gives the Sheephead Totem.
3. A later revisit after resting can award two Glimpses in current launch coverage.
- Warning: the trader may disappear after the event — buy what you want first.

3. Gragu (Shell-linked delivery)
- The Heart of Vatra from the Temple of Vatra is the Shell objective; return it to Gragu at the One-Legged Wolf Tavern to trigger Gragu’s body event.

4. Baghead (Desolate Peak secret ending)
1. Find the Special Moonshine and related note at the Desolate Peak stone throne / Illusionist’s Cache.
2. Return the item, then return empty-handed and confirm the “nothing” choice to trigger the secret ending.
- Safety: make a checkpoint/save first — this sequence is an ending trigger.

5. Egon (Gloom donations in Castigator’s Keep)
- Sources disagree on numeric thresholds (one reports 10,000; others report 20,000 total with a 40,000 return). Label the conflict and donate incrementally; wait for the in‑game completion prompt rather than assuming a specific number.

6. Vlas (broken-cart opening)
- The initial broken-cart / missing-cat opening is confirmed. Later route steps (cage/key/letter) are not consistently corroborated in the bounded sources — stop at the opening and verify in game.

7. Sester Genessa (Sester Secundus route)
- The route requires defeating Sester Secundus, returning the item (Censer vs Chalice — both names appear in current guides), and completing Genessa’s Memory. Genessa’s Bonding has a reported dialogue exception.

8. Zhirelle and Thestus
- Zhirelle acts as the Shellkeeper and mediates Bonding/Shell reveals. Thestus controls Gloombound Flame → lantern → Night Mode state in the existing local pages; consult the Gloombound Flame and Slayer Seal pages for those mechanics (/mortal-shell-ii/gloombound-flame.md and /mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/).

Common mistakes and traps
- Assuming Harros is one of the permanent Shells — Harros is prologue-only and permanently lost after the prologue.
- Expecting a boss kill to automatically make a Shell permanent — several Shell unlocks require a post-fight Memory or a return handoff (Sariel, Smert, Genessa, Gragu).
- Spending Glimpses on Shell map reveals after Balance Patch 1 — those reveals moved to Gloom and Glimpses spent pre-patch should be refundable at the Shellkeeper’s Blackmarrow alcove; verify platform timing.
- Buying from the Hunter trader after opening the Grisha Pup cage — buy desired stock before freeing the Pup.
- Treating Ova counts or fixed progression numbers as canonical — local pages and independent guides conflict on Ova needs; follow in‑game access prompts or /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ for progression context.
- Treating Egon’s donation threshold as settled — sources conflict; use the in‑game completion scene as the authority.
- Normalizing Genessa’s item name — both “Censer” and “Chalice” appear in guides; follow the in‑game prompt.

What happens next (after unlocking or engaging these routes)
- After you complete a Shell’s first Memory, the Shell becomes available in your permanent roster; you can equip it and, at the Shellkeeper, spend Glimpses to raise Bond tiers and unlock Shell-specific perks (see /mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/).
- Balance Patch 1: check /mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/ for the Gloom move and Glimpse refund steps; if you spent Glimpses on map reveals pre-patch, visit the Shellkeeper’s Blackmarrow alcove to confirm any refund.
- NPC chains: after each dialogue/handoff, rest at a Beacon and return to verify the NPC’s new state. Some sequences trigger endings (Baghead) or remove traders (Grisha Hunter), so save/checkpoints matter.
- If an NPC route or Shell step in these notes conflicts with what you see in-game, prefer the in-game prompt; several claims (Egon totals, Vlas later steps, platform patch timing) remain unresolved in the reviewed sources.
