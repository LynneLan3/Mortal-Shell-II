Short answer — Which Shells are “best”?
- There is no evidence-supported universal S/A/B tiering for all playstyles; existing launch sources and local route pages do not provide a controlled, current combat ranking. Instead: there are nine launch Shell entries (one prologue-only), eight permanent Shells you can collect, and several practical use-case recommendations below based on route and local evidence.

Roster (what you will actually collect)
- Harros the Vassal — prologue-only Shell. Given in the prologue and irreversibly lost at its end (not one of the eight permanent Shells). (GameSpot / AllThings.How / GamesRadar+)
- Eight permanent Shells you can unlock during the main game:
  - Tiel the Acolyte — Fainweald (Widow’s Overlook area). Route specifics: local route page is primary. (/mortal-shell-ii/tiel/)
  - Proxima the Broodseeker — Shattered Beacon near Blackridge Pass. Local transcript/UI shows “NEW SHELL DISCOVERED — PROXIMA.” (/mortal-shell-ii/proxima/)
  - Gragu the Insatiable — One-Legged Wolf Tavern (requires getting the Heart of Vatra from the Temple of Vatra and returning it). (/mortal-shell-ii/gragu/)
  - Eredrim the Venerable — Citadel of Penance; defeat The Warden then interact. (/mortal-shell-ii/eredrim/)
  - Smert the Apostate — Prophet’s Rest (Outskirts of Nochte); ritual with three blood pools and The Convert/Hierarch sequence. (/mortal-shell-ii/smert/)
  - Lazlo the Justiciar — Mammon (High Lord’s Courtyard / Vellen boss → crypt interaction). No local Lazlo route page in this checkout; use Mammon-area route descriptions from launch guides.
  - Sariel the Endless — Mammon (Silent Steps → Chamber of Becoming). Destroy four revival markers/tablets, finish the post-fight Unknown Shell/Memory sequence. (/mortal-shell-ii/sariel/)
  - Genessa / Sester Genessa the Wayward — Marrow Keep → Sester Secundus (Mammon side route); defeat Sester Secundus, return the required item to the proper NPC/spot, complete the Memory and accept the Shell prompt. Note the item name varies across guides (Censer vs Chalice). (/mortal-shell-ii/sester-genessa/)

How unlocking actually works (general model — preserved caveats)
1. Finding a Shell body (or killing the associated boss) is rarely the entire unlock on its own.
2. Interact with the remains or the route-specific Shell prompt when it appears.
3. Complete the route-specific first Memory (or the Memory sequence tied to that Shell). Independent guides and local pages consistently note that completing the first Memory is the step that makes a Shell permanent.
4. After the Memory completes, the Shell becomes permanently available on your Shell roster.
5. Bonding (upgrading Shell perks) is done via Glimpses at the Shellkeeper (Marrow Keep) in normal cases; Genessa’s bonding/dialogue is a reported exception handled at the Genessa encounter itself.
6. Balance Patch 1 moved Shell-map reveals from Glimpses to Gloom and refunded eligible Glimpse spending at the Shellkeeper alcove in Blackmarrow; platform rollout timing was uncertain in the evidence (PC first, PS5 reported, Xbox unconfirmed). (/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/, /mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/)

Unlock steps — quick numbered routes (use the linked local pages for full route detail)
- Tiel (Fainweald / Widow’s Overlook)
  1. Reach Widow’s Overlook area in Fainweald.
  2. Locate the grave/tree enclosure marked by two torches (local page has specifics).
  3. Interact with the body and follow the flask/side-window sequence and early Rash Memory to complete the Shell’s first Memory.
  4. Shell becomes permanent after Memory completion. (/mortal-shell-ii/tiel/)

- Proxima (Shattered Beacon / Blackridge Pass)
  1. Progress post-prologue toward Blackridge Pass.
  2. Find the Shattered Beacon location used in the Proxima route.
  3. Follow the four-interaction sequence until the UI shows “NEW SHELL DISCOVERED — PROXIMA.”
  4. Complete Proxima’s first Memory to make the Shell permanent. (/mortal-shell-ii/proxima/)  
  [IMAGE: Proxima’s Shattered Beacon unlock notification]

- Gragu (One-Legged Wolf Tavern / Temple of Vatra)
  1. Access eastern Fainweald routes toward Temple of Vatra (progression/corruption access required; local guide avoids a fixed Ova count).
  2. Acquire the Heart of Vatra inside the Temple of Vatra.
  3. Return the Heart to Gragu at the One-Legged Wolf Tavern and interact with the remains to trigger the event that makes the body inhabitable.
  4. Complete the Gragu Memory to secure the permanent Shell. (/mortal-shell-ii/gragu/)  
  [IMAGE: Gragu’s Heart of Vatra handoff at One-Legged Wolf Tavern]

- Eredrim (Citadel of Penance)
  1. Reach the Citadel of Penance and defeat The Warden boss.
  2. Interact with Eredrim’s body after the fight and do the first Memory sequence.
  3. Shell becomes permanent after Memory completion. (/mortal-shell-ii/eredrim/)

- Smert (Prophet’s Rest / Outskirts of Nochte)
  1. Reach Prophet’s Rest and confirm all three blood pools are active (the ritual prerequisite).
  2. Start the “The Convert” sequence and defeat the Hierarch at the end.
  3. Interact with Smert’s remains and finish the Memory to make the Shell permanent. (/mortal-shell-ii/smert/)  
  [IMAGE: Smert’s three blood pools at Prophet’s Rest]

- Lazlo (Mammon / High Lord’s Courtyard → crypt)
  1. In Mammon, defeat Vellen, Lord of Mammon, in the High Lord’s Courtyard.
  2. Continue into the crypt/final chamber and interact with Lazlo’s remains to trigger the route Memory/sequence.
  3. Complete the Memory to make Lazlo permanent. (Route evidence comes from launch guides; no local Lazlo page in this checkout.)

- Sariel (Mammon / Chamber of Becoming)
  1. In Mammon, reach Silent Steps and push onward to the Chamber of Becoming.
  2. Destroy all four revival markers/tablets in the area (local page details marker locations).
  3. Complete the post-fight Unknown Shell interaction and finish the Memory to secure Sariel permanently. (/mortal-shell-ii/sariel/)  
  [IMAGE: Sariel’s revival markers in the Chamber of Becoming]

- Genessa / Sester Genessa (Marrow Keep → Sester Secundus)
  1. In the Marrow Keep / Mammon route, defeat Sester Secundus.
  2. Collect the required item (called Censer in some sources, Chalice in others) and return it to the specified NPC/spot as prompted.
  3. Complete the route Memory and accept the Shell prompt. The in-game prompt will name the item/action required; guides report no differing Shell-unlock outcome for the choice at the end of Genessa’s Memory. (/mortal-shell-ii/sester-genessa/)

Practical use-case recommendations (evidence-backed, not a universal ranking)
- Tiel — recommended as an early mobile, evasive, Shadow-focused option; GamesRadar+ and the local Tiel page identify Tiel as a strong early pick for agile play. (/mortal-shell-ii/tiel/)
- Proxima — local transcript/UI indicates a lightning-lance-style pull utility and a single-walkthrough mitigation observation; treat this as practical for ranged/engage utility but not a verified numeric stat. (/mortal-shell-ii/proxima/)
- Smert — local page supports a Miracle/time-control / miracle-oriented identity; useful for playstyles favoring that toolkit. (/mortal-shell-ii/smert/)
- Eredrim — cited as a mid-late game option by GamesRadar+; route timing makes Eredrim a later-percentage choice in many playthroughs. (/mortal-shell-ii/eredrim/)
- Genessa — bond/dialogue exception: Genessa’s bonding/unlock has a dialogue-based element at the site, per independent guides and the local page. (/mortal-shell-ii/sester-genessa/)

What the evidence does NOT support (important limits)
- No launch evidence supports a definitive, game-wide S/A/B tier list. Do not treat pre-patch or single-run impressions as definitive.
- No complete per-Shell stat/ability/perk trees are verified by the available sources.
- No fixed Ova counts or exact map coordinates for routes — route descriptions use named landmarks and local pages (which were the primary route owners in this evidence set).
- Do not assume every boss kill automatically finalizes a Shell unlock — several Shells require an extra Memory/return/handoff step after the fight.

Common mistakes and how to avoid them
- Mistake: Treating Harros as a permanent collectable. Fix: Harros is described as prologue-only and lost at the prologue’s end.
- Mistake: Assuming defeating a boss always gives a permanent Shell. Fix: Check the route’s post-fight Memory or return/handoff requirement (Genessa, Sariel, Smert, Gragu examples).
- Mistake: Spending Glimpses on Shell-map reveals pre-patch and missing refunds. Fix: Balance Patch 1 moved map reveals to Gloom and refunded eligible Glimpse spending at the Shellkeeper (Blackmarrow alcove). Confirm refund availability on your platform if uncertain. (/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/, /mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/)
- Mistake: Relying on a single guide’s compass bearings for Tiel/Proxima. Fix: Use named landmarks (Widow’s Overlook, Shattered Beacon, Blackridge Pass) and the local route pages for exact steps.
- Mistake: Treating “Censer” vs “Chalice” as different items. Fix: The item name differs across launch guides; follow the in-game prompt where Genessa’s route asks you to return the item.

What happens next (after unlocking)
1. The unlocked Shell appears in your Shell roster and can be equipped like other permanent Shells (after the first Memory completes).
2. Bonding upgrades are available via Glimpses at the Shellkeeper (Marrow Keep) to unlock higher perk tiers; Genessa’s bonding prompt is a route/dialogue exception. (/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/)
3. Balance Patch 1 changes affecting Shell-map reveals and refunds may affect your progression planning; check the patch notes page for platform rollout status. (/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/)
4. Shells persist through New Game+ in most launch guides’ coverage; see the New Game+ page for retention specifics. (/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/)
