## Research conclusion

The existing page had the correct conservative boundary for the Tarstone system, but it stopped short of being a useful database. Current launch coverage supports a broader source-reported index, the four Tarstone categories, the Tarforge unlock path, the XP/Tarcore loop, several defensible early priorities, and a Temple of Vatra missable warning.

The exact collection total is not stable enough to state as a confirmed number. GamesRadar reports 75 Tarstones, while AllThings.How reports 73 Tarstones/fragments/mementos and says the inventory has two empty slots. The AllThings list and GamesRadar list also differ in names, locations, and completeness. The page therefore labels the index as a launch-window source-reported database and tells readers to verify the in-game inventory rather than claiming a final 73/75 count.

## Confirmed or strongly cross-checked mechanics

### Categories and slots

The current launch guides group Tarstones into four categories:

- **Support:** passive support effects. GamesRadar reports four Support slots.
- **Combat:** combat-triggered effects, generally tied to attacks or firing. GamesRadar reports one main-weapon and one sidearm slot for this category.
- **Infusion:** activated weapon/sidearm status effects that consume Resolve. Examples include Emberseed Stone and Arbiter's Prize.
- **Ability:** activated Tarstone abilities that consume Resolve. Examples include Unwieldy Stone and Hand of Rock.

GamesRadar reports a total of ten equipped Tarstones at once: four Support and two each of Combat, Infusion, and Ability. GameSpot independently confirms that Combat, Infusion, and Ability stones can be restricted to particular weapons or sidearms. The page should tell readers to inspect compatibility before building around a stone.

### Unlocking tempering

The Tarforge is in Marrow Keep and is managed by Franz. Etching Needles are found in the Glutted Mire after the Tarblighted Shepherd and before the Magdalena encounter, in the chest near Ruk. Returning the item to Franz adds the **Temper Tarstones** function.

The existing local Tarforge page owns the five-part Tarforge overview. The Tarstones page should explain only the Etching Needles/Tarstone handoff and link to `/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/` for the broader station checklist.

### XP, coins, and Tarcores

GameSpot and AllThings.How agree on the useful progression loop:

1. Slot the Tarstone in an equipped compatible weapon or sidearm.
2. Kill enemies while it is equipped so it gains XP.
3. At 10,000 XP, use the Tarforge and pay 100 coins to reach the next level and receive one Tarcore.
4. At the next 20,000-XP threshold, spend six Tarcores for the higher/max step.

The current guide should avoid saying that an inventory Tarstone passively gains XP. It should also avoid claiming that every Tarstone has identical effect text, compatibility, or upgrade behavior. The Tarcore economy is the reason “level every stone to level 2, then spend six Tarcores on favorites” is a useful strategy, but it remains a recommendation rather than a mandatory route.

### Missable and special-mode warnings

- **Berserker's Stone:** AllThings.How reports that its Temple of Vatra offering chest becomes permanently inaccessible after taking the Heart of Vatra and leaving. This is the most important collection warning, but it is a third-party report and should remain labeled as such until directly verified in-game.
- **Night-only merchant records:** AllThings.How reports Weeping, Infused, Fulminant, and Infested Stones at The Collector during the Night cycle in Abandoned Outpost.
- **Puzzle records:** Clockwork Shardstone requires the trebuchet setup; Curseblood Stone requires the Grimorium Obscurum setup; Frostshard Stone requires freezing the water; Inflamed Clawstone requires the Feeding Room Key setup. Candle offering chests include Auspicious, Siegebreaker, and Berserker.

## Conflicts and uncertainty labels to preserve

1. **Total count:** publish “source reports conflict: 73 vs 75,” not a confirmed 73 or 75.
2. **Category vs acquisition:** categories are reliable at the system level, but the complete category assignment for every database row is not cross-checked. Do not infer a category from a stone's name.
3. **Name variants:** `Headsman's` / `Headman's`, `Black Blood` / `Blackblood`, `Magdalena's Memento` / `Magdalena's Momento`, and similar spellings appear across guides. Preserve the most legible local spelling and note that source naming can vary.
4. **Location variants:** Gloombound Stone and Parasitic Stone have differing locations across sources. The existing local Gloom-farm and Tarforge pages remain the local owners for their route context; do not overwrite them in Item 16.
5. **Patch drift:** Balance Patch 1 explicitly buffed several named Tarstones but gives no stable numeric values. Keep Bulwark, Grudge, Parasitic, Clerik's, and Squall in a “patch-sensitive” note rather than inventing numbers.
6. **“Best” claims:** use goal-based recommendations. Do not publish a universal tier list from editorial rankings.
