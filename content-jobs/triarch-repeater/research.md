# Item 48 research: Triarch Repeater Location

## Scope and evidence boundary

This research is for Item 48 only: `mortal-shell-ii/triarch-repeater`. It covers the Blackwell Cavern route, access requirements, pickup, weapon role, Resolve behavior, Tarstone / Tarforge context, trophy and achievement coverage, Balance Patch 1, practical dungeon advice, conflicting route descriptions, internal links, and source URLs.

No map coordinates, exact turn count, invented Ova threshold, or unverified weapon stat is asserted. The route uses named Beacons, elevators, bridge / cliff landmarks, cave features, and the broken wagon described by current sources. No shared hub, config, plan, deployment, IndexNow, unrelated item, or other shared file belongs to this checkpoint.

## Direct answer supported by the sources

- The **Triarch Repeater** is a fully automatic ranged **Sidearm** found on the ground inside **Blackwell Cavern** in the Mammon region. It is not a boss reward.
- Use **Castigator's Keep Beacon** as the practical route anchor. From the Beacon, descend by the wooden lift or the bridge-side drop, follow the cliffside route into the lower grassy area, find the cave mouth beneath / near a large tree, and take the mine lift into Blackwell Cavern.
- Continue through the dark, apparition / wraith-filled tunnel. The weapon is near a **broken wagon** in a lit end area; another checklist describes the same area as a room with light and broken wagons.
- The broader route is blocked by corruption until the player can use the corruption-cleansing progression. Sources describe that ability as coming after collecting and siphoning enough Ova, but this item checkpoint does not have a reliable universal Ova number. Follow the current in-game prompt rather than publishing an exact threshold.
- There is no checked Triarch Repeater-specific key, quest, NPC dialogue, or boss requirement. The practical requirement is access to the Mammon route, a survivable build for its stronger enemies, and enough Resolve to use the weapon according to the current build.
- Steam lists **Old Painless** — “Unlock the Triarch Repeater.” Collecting every Sidearm contributes to **Guns. Lots of Guns** — “Unlock all Sidearms.”

## Prerequisites and route

### What is required

The source-supported access checks are:

1. Reach the Mammon / Castigator's Keep route and activate or use **Castigator's Keep Beacon**.
2. Have the corruption-cleansing ability needed to open the southern Mammon approach. Current route reporting ties this to the Ova / Siphon progression, but does not establish a safe item-specific number.
3. Bring a primary melee weapon and a build that can handle or bypass the stronger Mammon enemies. This is practical preparation, not a documented Triarch Repeater stat gate.

No source supports a mandatory boss kill, exact level, exact Resolve total, key, quest, or dialogue choice for the pickup.

### Conservative route from Castigator's Keep

The common route anchors are:

1. Fast travel to **Castigator's Keep Beacon**.
2. Move to the nearby battlement / cliff edge and use the **wooden elevator**. One route says to ride it; another says the player can drop from the bridge or platform before continuing downward.
3. Follow the cliffside path into the lower grassy area. Current descriptions mention Grisha Hunters, flying gargoyles, and axe-wielding enemies; sprinting past them is reported as viable if the route is open.
4. Look for the cave mouth set into the cliff beneath / near a large tree. Enter **Blackwell Cavern** and take the mining lift deeper into the dungeon.
5. Push through the dark tunnel and apparition / wraith encounters. The layout is described as a main route toward the reward, with a side loop back toward the entrance.
6. Continue until the passage opens into a lit area. Find the **broken wagon / broken wagons** and interact with the Triarch Repeater on the floor.
7. Hold the pickup prompt if required and confirm the **New Sidearm Acquired: Triarch Repeater** banner or inventory entry before leaving.

### Why the route wording differs

The checked sources describe the same approach at different levels of detail:

- Destructoid places Blackwell Cavern below the broken bridge leading to Castigator's Keep and offers either the elevator to the right of the Beacon or a drop from the bridge.
- GameSpot gives a Beacon-to-cliffside route: go down the steps, travel east-southeast, take the wooden lift, circle the grassy field, then find the cave.
- AllThingsHow describes a Beacon battlement, wooden elevator, two-level cliffside descent, grassy area, and a torchlit cave beneath a large tree.
- Gamer Guides identifies the pickup by the broken wagons in the lit room.

These are compatible landmark descriptions, not a reliable coordinate system. The article should not claim that north, east-southeast, elevator, or bridge-drop is the only valid turn sequence.

## Dungeon hazards and practical insights

- **Blackwell Cavern is extremely dark.** GameSpot says visibility is limited to only a few feet; AllThingsHow says the cavern is almost pitch black.
- **Wraiths / apparitions can drain or distort vision.** AllThingsHow reports that approaching them pushes the screen toward black and white. Lock on to track their positions, then handle them one at a time or move past them when safe.
- The reward route is reported as mostly direct. Do not search for a loose pickup in the upper entrance area; keep moving toward the light and broken wagon.
- The Mammon approach has stronger enemies than earlier regions. Upgrade or prepare as needed, but do not turn a guide's general difficulty warning into a required level.
- After the weapon is acquired, AllThingsHow reports that the player can finish the dungeon or quit to the main menu to avoid the return route. This is a convenience observation, not a required interaction and not a universal platform guarantee.

## Weapon, Resolve, and reward details

- The Triarch Repeater is described as a **fully automatic projectile sidearm** / minigun. Its sustained fire is useful for pressure against groups of weaker enemies, but the weapon trades mobility for its firing pattern according to current coverage.
- Current item coverage lists compatible Tarstones including **Enfeebling Stone, Rupturing Stone, Unstable Stone, Voltaic Amber, Frostshard Stone, Marksman's Stone, Deadeye Stone, Siegebreaker's Stone, and Spite Stone**. The article can link the local Tarstones guide and describe Tarstone customization without claiming a best universal build.
- AllThingsHow reports that an earlier/currently observed behavior required at least **50 Resolve** before firing and could store up to **100**. Cold Symmetry's official Balance Patch 1 note explicitly says **Caged Hystrix and Triarch Repeater no longer have minimum Resolve requirements to fire**. The article must make the patch change the controlling current note and label the 50 / 100 figures as older or source-reported behavior, not current universal rules.
- The article may use the weapon's practical role: automatic sustained ranged pressure, best when the player has time to hold aim and spend Resolve. It should avoid fixed damage, rate of fire, range, reload, capacity, or lock-on claims beyond the source-qualified 50 / 100 pre-patch observation.
- The pickup itself is the reward. No checked source reports a boss, currency payout, or separate quest reward attached to the Triarch Repeater.
- Sidearm enhancement is a shared Tarforge function. The local Tarforge guide identifies the **Obsidian Lathe** and Franz in Marrow Keep as the relevant unlock boundary; do not duplicate that system here.

## Conflicts and uncertainties to preserve

- **Approach direction:** sources use “below the broken bridge,” “to the right of Castigator's Keep Beacon,” “east-southeast along the cliff,” and “head north.” Use the Beacon, wooden lift, cliffside descent, grassy area, tree-side cave, and Blackwell Cavern as stable anchors; do not add coordinates.
- **Elevator versus drop:** some sources say ride the wooden elevator; others say drop from the bridge / battlement. Present both as route options where the terrain allows, not as a contradiction that needs a fabricated resolution.
- **Pickup wording:** sources say broken wagon, broken wagons, or a corpse / object in a lit room. The stable actionable instruction is to search the lit end area near the broken wagon(s) and interact with the floor pickup.
- **Prerequisite count:** “enough Ova” and the local corruption-cleansing progression are supported; a fixed Ova number for this item is not. Do not publish one.
- **Resolve threshold:** a current guide reports 50 minimum / 100 storage, while the official Balance Patch 1 removes the minimum firing requirement. Treat the official patch as the current rule and omit the capacity number unless clearly version-qualified.
- **Weapon limitations:** one current source reports lock-on drift and missed registration against some large swamp creatures. This is practical guide evidence, not a universal bug matrix; if included, attribute it and avoid promising that every enemy is affected.
- **Map coordinates, exact room count, and fixed enemy placement** are not supported by the checked sources.
