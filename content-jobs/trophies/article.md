Short answer
- Yes — you can unlock all exposed trophies/achievements for Mortal Shell II. As of the checked platform pages there are 53 entries total (PlayStation shows a 53-row trophy list including a Platinum; Xbox and Steam also expose 53 achievement rows but present them without a Platinum tier). Below is a conservative, practical roadmap that protects the currently reported one-time opportunities, preserves platform-name differences, and uses NG+ for memory cleanup.

Platform count and title caveats (read this before you start)
- The live PlayStation, Xbox, and Steam lists checked during this research each expose 53 rows. Some guides report 52 by subtracting the PlayStation Platinum row; do not assume that is definitive — use your platform UI if counts change.
- Several trophy/achievement titles differ between PlayStation and Xbox/Steam lists. Preserve platform wording in your UI. Notable mapped variants found in the source material:
  - PlayStation "Just the Tip" = Xbox/Steam "Point Taken"
  - PlayStation "Down with the Thickness" = Xbox/Steam "Thickness"
  - PlayStation "Ultimate Karen" = Xbox/Steam "Wheelie Good Time" (Magdalena boss slot)
  - PlayStation "Giga Chad" = Xbox/Steam "Ultimate Gainz" (Malborn Offspring boss slot)
  - PlayStation "Bag Holder" = Xbox/Steam "Baghead"
  - PlayStation "No Lifer" = Xbox Platinum; Xbox/Steam use "No Lifer" as the 100G / completion achievement
- Patch notes and hotfixes examined so far did not change the trophy list rows, but several behavioral warnings (Tarstone accessibility, Sester Genessa bond state, Slayer Seal achievement lock) are version-sensitive — treat those as current-launch warnings and re-check your build if you play after a patch.

Run 1 — protect one‑time opportunities and collect naturally (follow these numbered steps)
1. Start and keep a normal achievement-enabled save. Do not equip the Slayer Seal on that save (picking up the item is not the same as equipping it; see /mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/ for the achievement-lock warning).
2. During the opening Festival/tutorial, accept Marigold’s flower crown when she offers it. Missing this interaction is reported as a one-time loss on some saves.
   [IMAGE: Marigold offering the flower crown in the opening Festival]
3. In the same opening area, fight the tutorial Tar Golem and reduce it to its minimum/zero health before leaving the encounter. This reduction is reported as a one-time opportunity and may not reappear in NG+ for some builds.
   [IMAGE: Tutorial Tar Golem at low health]
4. Claim your first Shell, Weapon and Sidearm, reach Marrowkeep, and watch your first Shell Memory. These early actions unlock the main early progression trophies.
5. As you progress the world the first time, pick up Shells, weapons, sidearms, Ova, map fragments, Tarstones, and Beacons whenever they are reachable on your route. Play discovered Lute Tracks immediately rather than leaving them for an end sweep.
6. Before killing the Nameless Captive boss, attempt the seven‑hit headspin Perfect Parry with the Untarnished Seal (see /mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/). If you miss the full sequence you can retry as long as the boss is alive; once the boss is dead you will likely need NG+ to try again.
   [IMAGE: Untarnished Seal and Nameless Captive headspin setup]
7. Clear named bosses you can reach. The checked sources do not prescribe a single mandatory boss order; follow the world progression that opens to you.
8. Before wrapping the first run, pick up the Temple of Vatra Tarstone if you can and advance Sester Genessa’s bond during the first run as a precaution against reported bonding state bugs (these are launch-sensitive reports).
   [IMAGE: Temple of Vatra Tarstone pickup]
9. Finish the reachable first-run sweep: all reachable Shells, Sidearms, Weapons, Ova, Tarstones, Map Fragments, Beacons, Lute Tracks, Egon/Feed Me interaction, Mango/Ascension secret, Baghead/Bag Holder interaction, and any bosses still available.

NG+ and cleanup — where to spend later runs (numbered order)
1. Use the post-story free‑roam window to do as much cleanup as possible before starting NG+. Prioritize attempts at any remaining one-time boss attempts you missed (Peter’s Perfect Parry, tutorial Tar Golem, etc.).
2. Start NG+ cycles to finish memory bonding for Seeking the Past. Community estimates (not developer-published) say Shell memory Glimpse costs and Glimpse economy push full completion into multiple runs — treat those as estimates, not a guarantee. Bond a small number of Shells to completion each run rather than spreading Glimpses very thinly.
3. In later runs, focus on:
   - Opening every Shell Memory manually (complete each memory to secure Seeking the Past).
   - Maxing a single weapon at the Tarforge for the Over 9000 trophy (sources say “max out a Weapon at the Tarforge”; one guide mentions “+16” but that numeric cap was not independently verified here).
   - Collecting any remaining Beacons, Map Fragments, Tarstones, Ova, and Lute Tracks left from Run 1.
4. Retry any missed boss-specific attempts (Peter’s Perfect Parry, Nameless Captive special attempts) in NG+ if your build retains the same reset behavior.

Missables and save‑protection rules (preserve these warnings)
- The following three are currently reported as missable by checklist sources (treat them as one-time opportunities unless your build/patch indicates otherwise):
  1. Mid Summer? — accept Marigold’s crown in the opening Festival/tutorial.
  2. No, You Still Can't Win — reduce the tutorial Tar Golem to its minimum/zero health before leaving the encounter.
  3. Peter's Perfect Parry — perform the seven‑hit headspin parry with the Untarnished Seal before killing the Nameless Captive. See /mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/.
- Two additional progression traps reported (version-sensitive cautions):
  - Temple of Vatra Tarstone: reported to become inaccessible after leaving the area on some saves; handle the Tarstone before departing that region if you can.
  - Sester Genessa bond: some saves reportedly keep her in a tutorial state in NG+, interfering with Seeking the Past. Advance her bond as a first-run precaution.
- Slayer Seal achievement-lock: community/launch reporting indicates equipping the Slayer Seal can lock achievements on that save. Keep your completion save on the normal Seal path or use a separate save. See /mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/ for details.
- Night Mode: third‑party sources disagree about whether Night Mode affects achievement eligibility. The checked official/coverage framing treats Night Mode as optional; do not assume it blocks trophies without checking your current build.

Common Mistakes
- Equipping the Slayer Seal on your main achievement save (keep it on a secondary save).
- Skipping the Marigold crown interaction during the opening Festival.
- Leaving the tutorial Tar Golem before reducing it to minimum/zero health.
- Killing the Nameless Captive without attempting the seven‑hit headspin parry (you can retry while the boss is alive).
- Leaving the Temple of Vatra without picking up the Tarstone (reported to be inaccessible on some saves).
- Spreading Glimpses across many Shells in NG+ instead of fully bonding a few — cost estimates suggest slower progress that way (these estimates are community‑based).

What happens next (post‑completion and patch notes to watch)
- After your first completion you will likely need NG+ runs to finish Seeking the Past (memory bonding). Community guides estimate multiple cycles; these are estimates and may change with routing or patches.
- Check patch notes and your platform UI after any hotfix/patch. The launch-window sources flagged the following areas as patch-sensitive:
  - Temple of Vatra Tarstone accessibility.
  - Sester Genessa bond state in NG+.
  - Slayer Seal achievement menu lock (Hotfix 2.0 and other notes referenced the Slayer Seal menu behavior).
  - Glimpse economy and Tarforge/weapon caps; numbers reported in third-party guides were not all cross-verified by first-party sources for this pass.
- Re-check your platform achievement/trophy list if you return after a significant patch; platform name variants and counts can be presented differently per-UI.
