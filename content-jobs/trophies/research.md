## Scope and evidence boundary

This is a trophy/achievement checklist and completion-roadmap pass for Mortal Shell II. It does not attempt to invent an official trophy guide where the platform lists or launch guides disagree. Platform UI/list pages are the strongest evidence for names and counts; specialist guides are used for route advice, missable warnings, and practical cleanup. Exact collectible totals, patch behavior, and some platform-localized names remain version-sensitive below.

## Platform counts and naming caveats

- The current PlayStation list exposes **53 trophies**: 1 Platinum, 1 Gold, 10 Silver, and 41 Bronze. It reports 15 secret trophies.
- The current XboxAchievements list exposes **53 achievements / 1,000 Gamerscore**. It has no Platinum tier; `No Lifer` is the 100-point completion achievement.
- The current Steam global achievements page exposes **53 achievements**. It also has no Platinum tier and includes `No Lifer` as the completion achievement.
- Two independent guide articles describe Xbox/Steam as 52 because they subtract the PlayStation Platinum from the 53-row PlayStation list. That count conflicts with the current Steam and Xbox list pages, so the article should say **53 rows are currently exposed on all three checked platform lists, with the tier/title presentation differing by platform**, and tell readers to use their platform UI if the list changes.

## Missables and save-protection rules

The current Mortal Shell II Wiki checklist identifies three trophies as missable in the current cycle. The exact reset behavior is launch/version-sensitive, so use the conservative route:

1. **Mid Summer?** — accept Marigold's flower crown during the opening Festival/tutorial sequence. Ignoring the interaction is reported to remove the current-save opportunity; NG+ may skip the tutorial.
2. **No, You Still Can't Win** — reduce the tutorial Tar Golem to its minimum/zero health before leaving the opening encounter. The encounter is reported as a one-time opportunity and does not return in NG+.
3. **Peter's Perfect Parry** — attempt the Nameless Captive's seven-hit headspin sequence with the Untarnished Seal before killing the boss. The existing local [Peter's Perfect Parry guide](/mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/) records the same practical boundary: a failed attempt can be retried while the boss remains alive; after the kill, NG+ is the next reliable opportunity.

Two other warnings are **progression traps rather than formally confirmed missable trophy conditions**:

- Current checklist research reports that the Temple of Vatra Tarstone can become inaccessible after leaving and that `Stoned` should therefore be handled before departing that area. This is version-sensitive and should be stated as a precaution, not as a universal permanent lock unless reproduced on the target build.
- The same research reports that Sester Genessa's bonding interaction can remain on a tutorial state in NG+ for some saves, which can affect `Seeking the Past`. Maximize/advance the relevant Genessa bond in the first run when possible, but preserve the report as a current launch warning rather than a guaranteed bug.

**Slayer Seal warning:** the repository's existing difficulty guide cites Cold Symmetry's launch clarification and supporting coverage that switching/equipping the Slayer Seal locks achievements on that save. Achievement hunters should keep their completion save on the normal Seal path or use a separate save. Picking up the item is not the same action as equipping it. The effect of Night Mode is inconsistently described by third-party trophy coverage; the existing local difficulty guide treats Night Mode as optional and does not establish a trophy requirement. Do not state that Night Mode disables trophies without current in-game or first-party confirmation.

## Recommended completion order

### Run 1: protect the one-time opportunities and collect naturally

1. Stay on the normal achievement-enabled save. Do not equip the Slayer Seal.
2. In the opening sequence, accept Marigold's crown and reduce the tutorial Tar Golem to its minimum/zero health before leaving.
3. Claim the first Shell, weapon, and sidearm, reach Marrowkeep, and view the first Shell Memory. These should cover the early progression entries.
4. Follow the normal world progression, but pick up Shells, weapons, sidearms, Ova, map fragments, Tarstones, and Beacons as each route permits. Play every discovered Lute Track instead of leaving the audio collectible for a blind end sweep.
5. Clear the named bosses as their routes open. Boss order is not established as one universal mandatory sequence by the checked sources; do not hard-code an order that the sources do not support.
6. Before killing the Nameless Captive, make the Peter's Perfect Parry attempt. Keep the boss alive if the seven-hit sequence is missed.
7. Before committing to the endgame/NG+ transition, finish the first-run sweep: all reachable equipment and Shells, Ova, Tarstones, Map Fragments, Beacons, Lute Tracks, Egon, Mango, Baghead/Bag Holder interaction, and any remaining bosses.
8. Take the Temple of Vatra Tarstone before leaving that area and advance Sester Genessa's bond in the first run as a precaution against the reported route bugs.

### NG+ cleanup: reserve cycles for Shell Memories

The strongest cross-checked reason for NG+ is `Seeking the Past`, not an ordinary story requirement. The checked guides describe eight Shells with five memories each (40 total) and a total of 216 Glimpses if the reported 27-Glimpse maximum-bond cost is correct. They estimate roughly three fully bonded Shells per playthrough, leading to two full runs and part of a third. These are guide estimates, not a developer-published NG+ requirement, so the article must label them as such.

Use the post-story free-roam window for cleanup before starting NG+. In each later cycle, prioritize Glimpses from bosses and Beacons, bond a small number of Shells to completion rather than spreading currency thinly, and manually open every memory. Use NG+ to retry any missed current-cycle trophy, including Peter's Perfect Parry, if the platform/build retains the same reset behavior.

## Conflicting or uncertain claims to preserve

1. **52 vs 53 platform count:** current Steam and Xbox list pages expose 53 entries, while some guide articles say 52 by excluding the PlayStation Platinum. Use the live platform list as the operational source and explain the counting convention.
2. **Platform names:** Black Needle, Lazlo, Magdalena, Malborn, and the Baghead/Bag Holder oddity use different titles across checked lists. Preserve the variants in the page rather than asserting that one is a universal title.
3. **Missable implementation:** three missables are consistently flagged by the current checklist research, but patch/build-specific recapture behavior has not been independently reproduced here.
4. **Collection totals:** one current guide reports 196 Ova, 73 Tarstones, 11 Map Fragments, and 46 Beacons; those totals were not independently established from first-party data in this pass. Do not put those numbers in the answer-first summary until corroborated against the target build.
5. **Feed Me threshold:** checked secondary sources report different cumulative Gloom thresholds (10,000 versus 20,000). The trophy condition is “cause Egon to pass away”; omit a numeric threshold until verified in-game.
6. **Over 9000 upgrade cap:** sources agree on maxing one weapon at the Tarforge; a +16 number appears in one current guide but is not required for the platform-list description. Keep the page's requirement at “max one weapon” unless the target build confirms the cap.
7. **Night Mode:** third-party pages disagree about whether it affects achievement eligibility. The existing local difficulty guide and the official difficulty framing treat it as an optional harder world state; do not classify it as a trophy blocker without stronger evidence.
8. **All memories in one run:** the current guide consensus is that the Glimpse economy pushes completion into NG+ and likely part of NG++, but the exact run count is an estimate and can change with patches or player routing.
