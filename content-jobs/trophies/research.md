# Mortal Shell II — trophies and achievements research dossier

Research date: 2026-08-24

Item: 03

Slug: `mortal-shell-ii/trophies`
Scope: current retail launch-window trophy/achievement list, missable-risk audit, and a conservative platinum/100% roadmap. This dossier is for Item 03 only. It does not claim a later patch has fixed launch-window progression issues.

## Executive answer

Mortal Shell II has 53 entries in the current official Steam Community achievement list. The current Xbox mirror also reports 53 achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore, while the PS5 list reports 53 trophies: 1 Platinum, 1 Gold, 10 Silver, and 41 Bronze. Several guides say “52” for Steam/Xbox because they count the non-Platinum objectives; the official Steam list is the stronger count for the Steam platform and should be used as the platform checkpoint.

The safe completion plan is:

1. Protect the two tutorial opportunities before leaving the prologue: accept Marigold’s flower crown for **Mid Summer?**, and take the tutorial Tar Golem to its achievement trigger for **No, You Still Can’t Win**.
2. Do not equip the **Slayer Seal** on the completion save; current player/review evidence says it disables achievement tracking.
3. Before leaving the Temple of Vatra after taking the Heart of Vatra, collect the Berserker’s Stone Tarstone. This is the strongest current missable-collection warning for **Stoned**.
4. Use the Untarnished Seal and complete all seven required headspin Perfect Guards against the Nameless Captive for **Peter’s Perfect Parry** before ending that attempt. Treat this as an early, deliberate boss challenge.
5. Finish shells, equipment, Ova, map fragments, Beacons, Tarstones, lute tracks, NPC interactions, upgrades, and boss trophies before selecting **Send Ova** after Zmey. The post-final-boss free-roam window is useful for cleanup.
6. Plan remaining Shell Memories around NG+ and possibly part of NG++; current guides report eight Shells with five memories each, while Glimpse availability is limited. Max Sester Genessa’s bond in the first playthrough as a launch-build workaround for a reported NG+ bond/tutorial trap.

## Platform scope and naming caveats

- **Steam:** the official Steam Community achievement page says `Total achievements: 53` and exposes the full launch list. Steam uses **Baghead**, **Point Taken**, **Wheelie Good Time**, and **Ultimate Gainz** in the current page.
- **Xbox Series:** Exophase reports 53 achievements, 25,155 obtainable EXP, and 1,000 Gamerscore. Its list substantially matches the Steam list.
- **PS5:** PlayStationTrophies.org reports 53 trophies: 1 Platinum, 1 Gold, 10 Silver, and 41 Bronze, with 15 secret trophies. Some third-party PS naming differs from the Steam/Xbox wording, including **Bag Holder** for the Baghead reward, **Just the Tip** for the Black Needle reward, and **Giga Chad** for the Malborn Offspring reward. Use the platform UI label when writing a platform-specific checklist.
- The official Steam page and Exophase disagree with several guide articles that state “52” for Steam/Xbox. Preserve that disagreement in editorial copy, but do not downgrade the official Steam count: the current Steam page explicitly reports 53.
- Trophy/achievement descriptions are often hidden for boss and secret entries. The table below uses the official platform names where visible and labels guide-derived conditions as such.

## Recommended platinum / 100% roadmap

### First save: protect the irreversible opportunities

1. In the opening Festival, accept Marigold’s flower crown.
2. During the tutorial Tar Golem encounter, use the current in-game trigger for **No, You Still Can’t Win**. Because guides differ between “minimum health,” 1 HP, and zero-health UI, verify the notification before leaving the sequence.
3. Reach Marrowkeep and claim the first Shell, weapon, and Sidearm. Watch a Shell Memory through the Shellkeeper/bond menu so **Haunting Times** is not left to an ambiguous automatic-memory trigger.
4. Keep Slayer Seal unequipped on the completion save.

### Main exploration sweep

5. In each newly opened region, collect Shell bodies, weapons, Sidearms, Map Fragments, Beacons, Ova, and Tarstones as they become available. Use the platform tracker and the site’s dedicated location pages; do not rely on a disputed global Tarstone count.
6. Before leaving Temple of Vatra after taking the Heart of Vatra, verify the Berserker’s Stone is in the inventory.
7. Complete NPC-specific requirements while the regions are open: Smert’s unlock route, Gragu’s Heart of Vatra hand-in, Egon’s cumulative Gloom interaction, Baghead’s empty-handed sequence, and the current lute tracks.
8. Use the Tarforge to max one weapon and the Shellkeeper to max one Shell. Keep a written checklist for all equipment and all Shell Memories; pickup-only assumptions are not enough for the memory trophy.

### Boss and ending pass

9. Defeat the six Corrupted Gate bosses and the three Unfound Path guardians in the route order that suits the run; boss trophy entries do not require a source-proven fixed order.
10. On the Nameless Captive attempt, equip Untarnished Seal and complete Peter’s Perfect Parry before ending the fight. If the sequence fails, use the platform-appropriate quit/reload or postpone to a later cycle; do not claim a universal recovery method without verifying the build.
11. Defeat Zmey. Remain in free-roam and finish all collection, NPC, upgrade, and memory cleanup before selecting Send Ova.
12. Select the completion interaction after cleanup to trigger **This is Not an Achievement**. The PS5 No Lifer Platinum or platform meta achievement should follow once every other entry is registered.

### NG+ and memory cleanup

13. Enter NG+ only after the first-playthrough checklist is complete, especially Temple of Vatra, Sester Genessa, and tutorial entries.
14. Use NG+ for missing Shell bonds, repeated Beacons/bosses, and remaining Shell Memories. Current guides report eight permanent Shells and 40 memories; limited Glimpse income may require part of NG++.
15. Treat the exact number of cycles as an estimate, not a hard requirement: guides range from two complete runs plus a partial third to NG++ planning, and current player reports conflict about whether Genessa’s NG+ interaction is fixed.

## Conflicting or uncertain claims to preserve

- **53 vs. 52 platform count:** the official Steam Community page says 53; Exophase says 53 Xbox achievements; several guides say 52 for Steam/Xbox by counting non-Platinum objectives. Use 53 as the current platform-list count and explain the counting convention if a reader sees 52 elsewhere.
- **Baghead vs. Bag Holder:** Steam/Xbox use Baghead in the reviewed lists; PS5 sources use Bag Holder. Treat them as the same reward only after matching the description and interaction, not by title alone.
- **Point Taken vs. Just the Tip; Ultimate Gainz vs. Giga Chad; Wheelie Good Time vs. Ultimate Karen:** these are observed cross-source naming differences. Do not merge them silently in a platform-specific table.
- **Tarstone total:** current guides report 73 and 75. The official achievement text says only “all Tarstones.” Do not publish a hard total without a platform/build-specific inventory audit.
- **Tutorial Tar Golem trigger:** official text says minimum health; guides/player posts describe 1 HP or zero-health behavior and launch timing quirks. Phrase the guide around the visible achievement notification, not an invented exact number.
- **Sester Genessa NG+ bug:** multiple launch guides report a bond/parry-tutorial trap, while player reports suggest some builds may allow Genessa progression in NG+. Keep the first-playthrough workaround and label it version-sensitive.
- **Exact cycle count:** limited Glimpse availability and 40 reported Shell Memories make NG+ / partial NG++ planning prudent, but a mandatory universal cycle count is not independently proven.
- **Ascension, Baghead, and other optional interactions:** current guides use “missable” broadly. Only the tutorial entries and Temple of Vatra Tarstone have strong lockout evidence in the reviewed sources; label the others optional or route-sensitive unless the installed build confirms a one-time lockout.
