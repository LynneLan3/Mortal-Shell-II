Short answer — how to complete the Unfound Path
- Enter the Unfound Path after the game opens the Gloom Siphon / Ova gate at Marrow Keep. Clear all three tendril branches (Bridge of Procession, Obsidian Vault, Faithful’s Shelter), perform the required post-boss interaction in each branch, then use the opened central platform to reach and defeat Zmey. Cleanse the Hidden Nave for the Endless Core before the Zmey fight if you want the Tarforge upgrade limit removed. Save the “Send Ova” decision until you’re ready to begin New Game Plus.

Step-by-step route (do these in order to progress; branch order is flexible)
1. Finish the pre-endgame Ova progression and watch for the in-game Gloom Siphon / Ova prompt at Marrow Keep. (Sources agree the Unfound Path only becomes available after enough Ova; the exact Ova total is disputed—follow the in-game prompt and see the Ova guide.)
2. Enter the Unfound Path from Marrow Keep once the gate opens. Activate or cleanse any encountered Beacon so you can return after a failed attempt. See Beacons & Fast Travel for Beacon usage.
3. Note the central platform is initially blocked by three tendril branches around it. All three tendrils must be removed before the center is reachable (sources concur). The branches and their bosses are:
   - Bridge of Procession → Orrem, the Discarded Golem (aka the Reclaimed)
   - Obsidian Vault → The Monolith
   - Faithful’s Shelter → Malborn Offspring
4. Choose a branch and clear it. Sources explicitly say the branches can be taken in any order, but a practical first-run recommendation (difficulty-oriented) is: Orrem → Monolith → Malborn Offspring. This is a recommendation, not a requirement.
5. Bridge of Procession (Orrem)
   1. Use the Bridge of Procession Beacon approach described on the Orrem page.
   2. Fight Orrem as a two-form encounter; conserve healing for the Reclaimed (second) form. After killing Orrem, perform any post-boss interaction in the branch arena that registers the tendril as severed before leaving.
6. Obsidian Vault (Monolith)
   1. Use the Obsidian Vault Beacon and the Monolith guide for approach and tactics (jump-slam recovery loop; beware the later beam phase).
   2. After the Monolith kill, perform the required branch interaction — the “sever red gland” action — before exiting the arena to ensure the tendril is counted as fallen.
7. Faithful’s Shelter (Malborn Offspring)
   1. Use the Faithful’s Shelter Beacon and follow the Malborn Offspring page for the stairs/bridge approach and arena route.
   2. Survive the delayed strings and phase-two lightning; use stagger windows and the slow resurrection window to finish the kill.
   3. Extract the gland/tendril interaction at the back of the arena before leaving so the branch registers as cleared.
8. Open the center
   1. Once all three tendrils are removed, the central platform becomes reachable. If it is still blocked after defeating the three bosses, revisit each branch arena and confirm you completed the post-boss interaction there (don’t assume a missing coordinate or hidden lever).
   2. If you find the center still blocked and you’ve confirmed all three interactions, consult Beacons/branch pages for missed prompts—sources emphasize the post-kill interactions, not hidden switches.
9. Cleanse the Hidden Nave before facing Zmey
   1. The Hidden Nave contains the Endless Core, which removes the Tarforge upgrade limit; GameSpot and other guides advise claiming it before the final boss. Treat this as a required pre-Zmey checklist item if you want the Endless Core benefit.
10. Fight Zmey and decide carefully about Send Ova
   1. Follow the Zmey final-arena guide for the approach and fight.
   2. Defeat Zmey. The “Send Ova” / New Game Plus transition is a separate decision — your current save remains playable until you actually select Send Ova. Only choose Send Ova when you are ready to start New Game Plus.

Common mistakes
- Leaving a branch arena immediately after killing the boss without performing the post-boss interaction (severing or extracting the gland). That will leave the tendril active and keep the center blocked.
- Assuming a fixed Ova number (150, 167, or 168) will always be correct. Sources conflict; use the in-game Gloom Siphon / Ova prompt and the Ova guide for the current progression cue.
- Treating the branches as forced in one order. The Faithful’s Shelter guide explicitly states the branches can be taken in any order; the Orrem→Monolith→Malborn order is a difficulty-based recommendation only.
- Missing the Hidden Nave cleanse before Zmey if you want Endless Core benefits.
- Selecting Send Ova immediately after Zmey without realizing it commits you to New Game Plus — you can continue cleaning up the current save until you choose Send Ova.

What happens next
- After all three tendrils are cleared and you reach the center, you fight Zmey. Claim any desired Hidden Nave reward (Endless Core) before the fight if you want the Tarforge upgrade limit removed.
- Defeating Zmey does not automatically force New Game Plus. The “Send Ova” choice at Marrow Keep is separate; your current save remains available until you confirm Send Ova. Consult the New Game Plus and Endings pages for consequences and planning.

Notes and remaining uncertainties (preserved from sources)
- Entry threshold: multiple sources agree the Unfound Path unlocks after “enough Ova” from the preceding gate progression, but reported totals vary (150 vs ~167–168). Follow the in-game Siphon prompt and the Ova guide for the current threshold.
- Prior gate count: some sources describe six Corrupted Gates; one guide claims a different structure. This guide avoids repeating disputed gate counts and uses the operational phrasing “enough Ova from the preceding gate progression.”
- Branch rewards (Orrem’s Head, Monolith Fragment, Offspring Trophy) are reported by walkthroughs but are not restated here as exact currency or Gloom totals. See the linked boss pages for reward details.
