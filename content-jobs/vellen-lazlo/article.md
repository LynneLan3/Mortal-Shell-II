Short answer — how to get Vellen and unlock Lazlo
1. From the Outskirts of Mammon Beacon (or the High Lord's Courtyard Beacon once you’ve activated it), follow the keep/crypt route into the underground arena area (named in guides as the High Lord's Courtyard / royal crypt).  
2. Defeat Vellen, High Lord of Mammon. Do not leave the area after the kill.  
3. Go into the chamber or down the stairs behind the arena, interact with Lazlo’s remains, choose the inhabit action if prompted, and complete Lazlo’s first Memory. Completing that Memory is the conservative boundary for treating Lazlo as permanently unlocked (sources: GameSpot, Gaming ProMax, AllThings.How).

Route (step‑by‑step)
1. Fast travel to Outskirts of Mammon Beacon if you have not yet opened the courtyard route. If you have activated it, use High Lord’s Courtyard Beacon. (GameSpot, AltChar)  
2. Head toward the keep/crypt entrance that leads under the courtyard. Guides variously describe this as a stairwell, long hallway, or underground cathedral; they agree at the area level but differ on exact rooms (Gaming ProMax, AltChar, AllThings.How).  
3. Proceed along the underground passage toward the large boss arena. Expect armored knights, coffin-eyebeholders, or similar approach enemies; clear or bypass them. (AllThings.How, Gaming ProMax)  
4. Rest at the nearest Beacon or otherwise refill healing and Resolve before committing to the boss.

Conservative boss‑approach checklist (prepare these before entering)
1. Ensure enough healing/Resolve to survive an unblockable warning attack and to heal during a boss recovery.  
2. Bring a Break‑damage option and a Riposte tool if you use them—several guides recommend these as useful burst tools (Gaming ProMax).  
3. Consider Hardening or parry options if you are comfortable using them; a guide mentions parrying as viable but does not define every move (Thumb Wars).  
4. Clear or isolate nearby foes — luring extras into Vellen can work situationally but is not required (GameSpot, Gaming ProMax).

Fight guidance (conservative, readable approach)
1. Make the encounter readable — watch for a full attack string and wait for a clear recovery window before punishing. Multiple sources emphasize patience rather than greedy interrupts.  
2. One guide identifies a long‑wind‑up overhead slam; a side dodge that keeps punish range is recommended as a useful tell (Gaming ProMax). Treat this as a specific tell rather than a universal script.  
3. Disengage from attacks that show an unblockable/warning animation and use that recovery to heal or reposition (Thumb Wars).  
4. Use Break damage and a Riposte during safe recovery windows for burst damage (Gaming ProMax).  
5. Do not rely on a single weapon/tempo description — sources conflict on Vellen’s weapon and exact pacing, so use the tell/recovery principles above rather than a rigid phase script (conflict noted in sources).

Lazlo unlock — exact post‑fight steps (follow in order)
1. Defeat Vellen. The kill does not by itself equal a permanent Shell unlock (GameSpot, AllThings.How).  
2. Immediately continue into the room or down the stairs behind the arena — do not fast‑travel away or quit to menu first. (GameSpot, Gaming ProMax, AltChar)  
3. Locate and interact with Lazlo’s corpse/remains in that chamber. If an inhabit prompt appears, choose the inhabit action (AltChar, Gaming ProMax).  
4. Complete Lazlo’s first Memory sequence. Sources indicate completing that Memory is the conservative completion boundary for permanent unlock status (AllThings.How).  

Troubleshooting if you don’t see Lazlo’s body/prompt
1. Inspect the post‑arena chamber/stairs thoroughly before assuming the Shell unlocked automatically (GameSpot, AltChar).  
2. If no prompt or body is visible, do not immediately quit; re‑check the chamber layout and nearby alcoves. The guides all point to a post‑arena location rather than an immediate post‑kill menu grant.

Common mistakes
- Leaving the arena or fast‑travelling away immediately after the kill and assuming the Shell is unlocked. The body interaction and Memory completion step are required for the conservative unlock boundary.  
- Trying to punish during an apparent pause in an attack string without confirming recovery — leads to damageable frames.  
- Expecting a single fixed weapon type, tempo, or phase order; sources conflict on those details.  
- Treating enemy‑lure tactics as guaranteed — luring extras into Vellen is situational, not required.
