# Item 34 research: Vellen, High Lord of Mammon / Lazlo Unlock

- Scope: Item 34 only (`mortal-shell-ii/vellen-lazlo`)
- Audience: players looking for Vellen's location, a conservative boss approach, and the exact post-fight steps that unlock Lazlo
- Checked: 2026-08-24
- Exclusions: no new topics, no map coordinates, no shared-file changes, no image download, no writer/provider call

## Direct answer

Reach the **High Lord's Courtyard** route in Mammon, using the **Outskirts of Mammon Beacon** as the broad approach anchor or the **High Lord's Courtyard Beacon** once activated. Follow the keep/crypt route to the underground arena and defeat **Vellen, High Lord of Mammon**. Do not leave after the kill: continue through the chamber or stairs behind the arena, interact with **Lazlo's remains**, choose the inhabit action if shown, and complete Lazlo's first Memory sequence. The Memory is the conservative completion boundary for treating Lazlo as permanently unlocked.

## Practical article guidance

### Route

1. Start from **Outskirts of Mammon Beacon** if the Courtyard route is not yet open.
2. Work toward **High Lord's Courtyard** and activate the **High Lord's Courtyard Beacon** when available.
3. Enter the keep/crypt route and follow the named underground passage toward the arena. Current guides mention Armored Knights, Coffin Eye Beholders, a long hallway, or an underground cathedral; these descriptions are compatible at the area level but not a canonical room-by-room map.
4. Clear or bypass approach enemies, refill before committing, and preserve enough healing/Resolve for the boss.

### Fight

- Keep the encounter readable: watch the complete string and take a short punish after recovery rather than attacking during an apparent pause.
- One current guide specifically identifies a long-wind-up overhead slam and recommends a side dodge that keeps punish range. Present this as a useful tell, not a universal attack script.
- Another guide recommends Hardening/Parrying against normal attacks and using a warning/unblockable attack's recovery to heal or reposition. Because the guide descriptions conflict, the article should say to disengage from warning attacks rather than promise a single counter.
- Break damage and a Riposte are supported as useful burst tools by Gaming ProMax. Eredrim is an optional existing-link suggestion only; do not make it a requirement.
- Luring nearby enemies into Vellen is reported as possible by GameSpot and Gaming ProMax, but it is situational and should not be presented as the intended route.

### Lazlo unlock

The evidence-backed completion sequence is:

1. Defeat Vellen.
2. Continue into the last chamber/room behind the arena or down the nearby stairs.
3. Interact with Lazlo's corpse/remains and select the inhabit action if the prompt appears.
4. Complete Lazlo's first Memory sequence. Only then should the page describe the Shell as permanently available.

The article must distinguish “Vellen defeated” from “Lazlo permanently unlocked.” If the body or prompt is not visible, the first troubleshooting action is to inspect the post-arena chamber rather than assuming the boss automatically granted the Shell.
