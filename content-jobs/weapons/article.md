Quick answer — which weapon to pick (by supported use case)
- Best first dual‑wield route: Axe & Dagger — early Shrine of Trials pickup; practical to test with an early Tiel run. See /mortal-shell-ii/axe-dagger/.
- Best early heavy route: Great Martyr’s Blade — early Martyr’s Prison heavy option; Patch 1 raised its attack damage by 20%. See /mortal-shell-ii/great-martyrs-blade/.
- Best early experimentation / morph route: Axatana — a Mammon detour with dual-mode design; useful for stance/mode testing. See /mortal-shell-ii/axatana/.
- Best reach option: Black Needle — long spear identity with Needle Storm; Patch 1 improved light-attack tracking. See /mortal-shell-ii/black-needle/.
- Best impact / stagger tool: Obsidian Hammer — slow, heavy, stagger-focused with Heavy Stomps. See /mortal-shell-ii/obsidian-hammer/.
- Best route‑adjacent pickup (efficiency): Veteran’s Battle Axe — lootable in King’s Crypt when passing Blackridge/Proxima routes. See /mortal-shell-ii/veterans-battle-axe/.
- Best late‑route reward: Clockwork Scythe — tied to the Chamber of Becoming / full Sariel route; pick it if you’re already pursuing Sariel objectives. See /mortal-shell-ii/clockwork-scythe/.
- Best guaranteed starter: The Iconoclast — given in the Prologue with the Harros Shell (no dedicated Iconoclast route page exists on the site).

Overview and what “best” means here
- This hub uses “best” to mean best-supported use case or route fit (early dual‑wield, heavy impact, reach, morph experimentation, route convenience, or late-route reward). There is no evidence in this pass to support a universal damage tier ranking across all weapons; do not treat the above as an S/A/B damage list.

Acquiring each primary weapon — step-by-step (use the linked route pages for full walkthroughs)
- Axe & Dagger — Chapel Key → Shrine of Trials
  1. Obtain the Chapel Key required for the chapel area.
  2. Travel to the chapel near Mushroom Village (the Shrine of Trials location given in launch coverage).
  3. Complete the Shrine of Trials puzzle/combat sequence.
  4. Claim the Axe & Dagger from the Shrine of Trials pedestal.
  Note: older Open Beta coverage placed this weapon at Mushroom Village Gate Beacon; prefer the launch-route description but be aware of that conflict. Link: /mortal-shell-ii/axe-dagger/

- Veteran’s Battle Axe — King’s Crypt (Blackridge/Proxima route)
  1. Reach King’s Crypt along the Blackridge Pass / Proxima exploration route.
  2. Clear the final reward area and loot the Veteran’s Battle Axe from the reward chest/space described on the local page.
  Link: /mortal-shell-ii/veterans-battle-axe/

- Great Martyr’s Blade — Martyr’s Prison (Holding Cells / Widow’s Overlook)
  1. Progress to Martyr’s Prison and reach the Holding Cells / Widow’s Overlook area.
  2. Pick up the Great Martyr’s Blade in the designated pickup location.
  Observation: one checked run showed the blade at +4 on pickup; that is an observation only, not guaranteed. Link: /mortal-shell-ii/great-martyrs-blade/

- Obsidian Hammer — Obsidianite Mines (Mammon)
  1. Enter the Obsidianite Mines through the Mammon route.
  2. Defeat the Prophet of Profane Infinities encounter in the mines.
  3. Loot the Obsidian Hammer from the reward area.
  Identity: the local page documents a slow, heavy, impact/stagger identity with Heavy Stomps. Link: /mortal-shell-ii/obsidian-hammer/

- Black Needle — Sester’s Bastion / Sisters’ Gate
  1. Progress the Bell route through Sester’s Bastion / Sisters’ Gate as described on the local page.
  2. Complete the Sester encounter and collect the Black Needle reward.
  Notes: this spear has Needle Storm; Balance Patch 1 improved light-attack tracking (no exact numbers provided). Link: /mortal-shell-ii/black-needle/

- Axatana — Forgotten Tower (Mammon)
  1. Take the Mammon route toward the Forgotten Tower.
  2. Open the final gate in the tower and clear the local room encounter.
  3. Pick up the Axatana from the room’s reward position.
  Observation: one run showed Axatana at +5 on pickup; that is an observed state only. The checked evidence does not conclusively establish a mandatory kill requirement for the pickup. Link: /mortal-shell-ii/axatana/

- Clockwork Scythe — Chamber of Becoming (Sariel route)
  1. Complete the multi-stage Sariel route (the Chamber of Becoming sequence) and permanently defeat Sariel as required by that route.
  2. Claim the Clockwork Scythe from the Chamber of Becoming reward area.
  Notes: Sariel, the Shell, and the Caged Hystrix are separate rewards in that route; do not conflate them. Patch 1 improved light-attack tracking for the scythe. Link: /mortal-shell-ii/clockwork-scythe/

- The Iconoclast — Prologue starter
  1. Obtain the Harros Shell during the Prologue.
  2. The Iconoclast is given as the Prologue starting weapon with Harros.
  Note: there is no dedicated /mortal-shell-ii/iconoclast/ page on the site; this hub preserves the short prologue fact only.

Upgrade and Tarforge flow — practical steps
1. Keep or retrieve the weapon you plan to use; not all eight weapons are available at the same progression point.
2. Return the Muradean Actuator to Franz in Marrow Keep to unlock primary weapon enhancement at the Tarforge. Link: /mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/
3. Use weapon enhancement once you’ve assessed route timing and materials; the checked evidence does not publish a complete per-weapon cost or level table.
4. After you obtain the Foundry Stone, use Smelt Equipment to reclaim upgrade materials from gear you no longer need.
5. Balance Patch 1 reduced Smelt Equipment’s Gloom cost by 75%, making respec/recovery more practical. Link: /mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/
6. The Endless Core removes the normal upgrade cap; the local evidence does not publish a fixed final maximum level.
7. Observed pickup upgrade states: Great Martyr’s Blade was seen at +4 in one run and Axatana at +5 in one run — treat these as single-run observations only.

Patch notes and platform caveat
- Balance Patch 1 (launch-window) highlights relevant to weapons:
  - Great Martyr’s Blade attack damage increased by 20%.
  - Light‑attack tracking improved for Axatana, Black Needle, and Clockwork Scythe.
  - Smelt Equipment Gloom cost reduced by 75%.
- The patch summary does not include exact numeric values for tracking changes. Platform rollout timing may vary; consult the patch tracker page for platform-specific availability. Link: /mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/

Common mistakes (what to avoid)
- Treating observed pickup levels (+4, +5) as guaranteed for every save or run.
- Assuming the Iconoclast is a missing late-game route — it is Prologue starter gear with Harros.
- Equating “16 weapons” with 16 primary melee weapons — current sources separate eight primary weapons and eight sidearms; do not call the total “16 weapons” without qualifying the split.
- Believing creator footage or loading-screen images proves a weapon is a Shell’s signature or the universally best choice.
- Expecting exact damage/tracking numbers from Patch 1 — the patch notes in evidence record improvements but not detailed numeric deltas.
- Assuming a hard kill requirement for Axatana or other pickups where the evidence only shows a cleared-room observation in footage.

Conflict and uncertainty (preserve these for readers)
- Axe & Dagger location conflict: older Open Beta sources placed it at Mushroom Village Gate Beacon; launch-route sources put it in the Shrine of Trials behind the Chapel Key. Prefer launch-route descriptions but be aware of the discrepancy.
- Route vs. boss wording: checklist sources differ on whether a weapon is a boss drop or a post-encounter pickup. The hub follows the local route pages’ narrower wording.
- Observed upgrade states (+4, +5) are single-run observations only, not guaranteed starting levels.
- Axatana pickup: footage shows the room enemy cleared on some runs; checked sources do not establish a mandatory kill requirement.
- No evidence supports a universal damage tiering of all eight weapons in this pass — use-case recommendations only.

What happens next (after you pick or upgrade a weapon)
1. Test the weapon in the build and route you’re pursuing; several recommendations above are route- or early-use-case focused (for example, Axe & Dagger with an early Tiel run).
2. If you commit, return to the Tarforge after the Muradean Actuator unlock to enhance the weapon — the site’s Tarforge page covers the enhancement menu and the functions of Foundry Stone and Endless Core. Link: /mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/
3. Use Smelt Equipment (cheaper after Patch 1) if you need to recover upgrade materials later; this makes respec less punishing. Link: /mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/
4. Keep an eye on the patch tracker for future tuning that could change practical weapon choices. Link: /mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/
