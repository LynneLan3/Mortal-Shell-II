Opening

The Week 1 Update was released August 29, 2026 with an official Steam announcement. This patch changes core economy rules, enables a Shell respec path, applies combat and targeting adjustments, adds new Beacons, and bundles many crash and stability fixes.

Who this affects: all PC players immediately; console players only once a rollout is confirmed. This page summarizes the largest changes and tells you what to do now and which existing guides to consult.

Quick answer — what to do first

1. Check Merrick and your Collector for Tarstones, Tarcores, and related items (Merrick now sells Tarstones, Mether's Severance, Glimpse Stones, Egon's Stones, and Tarcores and restocks with progression).
2. If you want to change a Shell's progression, give Mether's Severance to Zhirelle to respec — the update refunds Glimpses spent on that Shell's Bond.
3. If you have an upgraded save, look for compensatory Tarcores and verify Glimpses/Tarcore counts; then follow the guide updates below.

Biggest gameplay changes

A — Tarstones, Tarcores, and Glimpses (economy)

What changed

- Glimpses and Tarcores are no longer finite. All Shells and Tarstones can be upgraded in one playthrough. Fragile Tarstones can appear repeatedly. Breaking Tarstones yields more Tarcores.
- Upgraded saves can receive compensatory Tarcores.
- The Collector can now carry Tarcores.
- Merrick sells Tarstones and Mether's Severance and will restock with progression items (Tarstones, Mether's Severance, Glimpse Stones, Egon's Stones, Tarcores at relevant states).

Who this affects

- Completionist and upgrade-focused players: you can now reach full upgrades without being blocked by resource scarcity in a single playthrough.
- Players who previously saved Fragile Tarstones: they may now find additional Fragile Tarstones and Tarcores.

What to do now

- Visit Merrick and the Collector early to check stocks.
- If you relied on save farming for Tarcores, check upgraded saves for compensatory Tarcores.

B — Shell respec (Mether's Severance)

What changed

- Mether's Severance can be used to reset a Shell's progression when given to Zhirelle. The update refunds Glimpses spent on that Shell's Bond.

Who this affects

- Players who want to reallocate progression on a Shell without starting a new playthrough.

What to do now

- If you want to change a Shell build, obtain Mether's Severance (Merrick sells it per the update) and give it to Zhirelle to perform the respec.

C — Riposte scaling and combat refinements

What changed

- A riposte scaling pass and other immunity/combat refinements were applied. Enemy targeting and magnetism adjustments were made where officially listed.

Who this affects

- All players in combat; especially those relying on ripostes and precision timing.

What to do now

- Expect different riposte behavior and some enemy targeting changes; review our combat and difficulty guides for updated tactics and test builds in low‑risk encounters before committing changes.

D — Exploration and Beacons

What changed

- New Beacons were added and navigation pacing was adjusted in places.

Who this affects

- Players who use Beacons or follow specific route timings; navigation between areas may feel different in places where pacing was adjusted.

What to do now

- Check updated fast‑travel/Beacon routes before re‑routing a long run. See our Beacons and fast travel guide for route details.

E — Performance, crashes, and stability

What changed

- The update contains extensive crash and stability fixes, improved diagnostics, and changes to some graphics settings implicated in driver crashes.

Who this affects

- Players experiencing crashes or driver-related instability on PC; diagnostics improvements may help future troubleshooting.

What to do now

- If you experienced crashes, retest after the update and consult our crashing PC page if problems persist.

Week 1 Update FAQ

Q: When was this update released?
A: August 29, 2026 (official Steam announcement).

Q: Is the update live on consoles?
A: The update is confirmed live on PC per the Steam post; PS5/Xbox rollout is not confirmed in this record.

Q: Can I now fully upgrade every Shell in one playthrough?
A: Yes. The update makes Glimpses and Tarcores non‑finite so all Shells and Tarstones can be upgraded in one playthrough.

Q: How do I respec a Shell?
A: Obtain Mether's Severance (Merrick sells it per the update) and give it to Zhirelle. This resets the Shell's progression and refunds the Glimpses spent on that Shell's Bond.

Q: Did the update fix every crash?
A: No. The update includes extensive crash and stability fixes, improved diagnostics, and changes to some graphics settings implicated in driver crashes, but it does not claim to fix every crash.
