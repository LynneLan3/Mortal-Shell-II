Short answer: From the Mammon overworld, use the Gate of Mammon Beacon to enter Withered Shoals, then follow the interior route through Ossinite Falls Beacon → Nameless Pass Beacon → drop into the fog to fight Hexapod, and finally activate/cleanse Hexapod's Burrow Beacon in the arena. Along the way clear the Broodshell Nest at Ossinite Falls, check the waterfall for the optional Torpor Stone, inspect the Odious Crevice and nearby chest at Nameless Pass for a Charged Stone and Ova, and optionally pick up the Depleted Gland before the final drop. If you need full Hexapod fight mechanics, see the Hexapod guide (/mortal-shell-ii/hexapod/).

Route (step-by-step)
1. Reach the Gate of Mammon Beacon in the Mammon overworld and cleanse it if the Withered Shoals Corrupted Gate is blocked. (Sources differ on which overworld checkpoint players arrive from—Into Indie Games starts from Mammon Outskirts; GameSpot gives a High Lord’s Courtyard approach—so use the Gate of Mammon label and the visible Corrupted Gate as your access check.)
2. Enter Withered Shoals. Move past the early eel and snail enemies and follow the wooden platforms and the skeletal-remains landmark. Ignore the optional right-hand Strange Grisha side route unless you want that encounter; the main route continues straight through these landmarks. (Into Indie Games and AllThingsHow agree on this general path but disagree on the precise branching point for the Strange Grisha side path.)
3. Activate and cleanse Ossinite Falls Beacon. Cleanse it by clearing the Broodshell Nest enemies, collect the returned Ovum/Ova, then treat the Beacon as complete. After the Beacon cleanse, the waterfall area contains an optional Torpor Stone Tarstone — explore the falls if you want the Tarstone but it is not required for progression. (AllThingsHow and Into Indie Games confirm the Broodshell Nest and the post-cleanse waterfall item.)
4. Leave the waterfall area and follow the cliff/platform route through the crack in the wall and into the descending cavern. Use large snail shells, head enemies, and the deep rocky path as navigation checks until Nameless Pass Beacon appears.
5. Activate and cleanse Nameless Pass Beacon. Confirm you’re in the right place by locating the throne and the route behind it into Odious Crevice; there is a tunnel with Ova below and a nearby chest reported to contain a Charged Stone. Collect those optional items before returning to the Beacon. (Into Indie Games and AllThingsHow describe the throne → Odious Crevice sequence and the Charged Stone chest.)
6. From Nameless Pass follow the path toward the Ruk landmark and the hollowed tree / tube structure that precedes the final drop. Before you commit to the final drop into the foggy arena, inspect the lower landmark cluster (the underside of the hollowed tree / tube, around Ruk) for the optional Depleted Gland. Sources agree the Depleted Gland is in this final-approach area but give different micro-descriptions of its exact position, so treat it as an optional check rather than a required step.
7. If you want help, summon Proxima if her sign is present (she costs a Glimpse, according to boss coverage). Then jump into the void/fog to trigger the Hexapod boss. For full fight mechanics, strategies, and disputed phase details, use the dedicated Hexapod guide: /mortal-shell-ii/hexapod/.
8. After defeating Hexapod, search the misty arena thoroughly for the post-boss Beacon labeled in-game (commonly listed in guides as Hexapod's Burrow Beacon). Activate and cleanse Hexapod's Burrow Beacon and collect any Ova/Ovum before fast-traveling away. (Probonk, Gaming ProMax, and other sources identify this post-boss Beacon.)

Quick practical notes and checks
- Cleanse each Beacon when the menu offers the cleanse interaction; cleansing requires clearing the local corrupted nest (see /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/ for Beacon mechanics).
- Do not assume side routes are mandatory: Strange Grisha (optional) sits near Ossinite Falls; Torpor Stone (optional) is in the waterfall; Depleted Gland (optional) is in the final-approach landmark cluster.
- Use landmarks—wooden platforms, skeletal remains, waterfall, cliff/wall crack, throne → Odious Crevice, hollowed tree/tube, Ruk, foggy arena—instead of hard compass turns; sources do not share a calibrated room-by-room map.

Common mistakes
- Mistaking a large snail shell or a distant environmental object for Nameless Pass Beacon—AllThingsHow warns the snail shell can be mistaken for the Beacon at a distance.
- Trying to “cleanse” Ossinite Falls without first clearing the Broodshell Nest (the cleanse sequence requires enemy clearing).
- Treating the Depleted Gland or Torpor Stone as required progression items; the checked sources list them as optional.
- Skipping a full sweep of the foggy arena after the boss and leaving without activating Hexapod’s Burrow Beacon or collecting post-boss Ova/Ovum.
- Expecting exact reward totals (Gloom or Ova amounts) from this route—sources disagree on reward numbers, so do not rely on a fixed value here.

Conflicts and uncertainties preserved from sources
- Overworld access: Into Indie Games and GameSpot describe different Mammon overworld approaches. Use Gate of Mammon as the stable in-game access check, not a specific overworld coordinate.
- Exact Ossinite route turns and Strange Grisha branch points vary between guides; follow environmental landmarks rather than step-by-step left/right instructions.
- Depleted Gland position is described differently (below Ruk, tube structure, underside of hollowed tree); inspect the final-approach landmark cluster and treat the item as optional.
- Beacon naming varies in external write-ups—this guide uses the in-game label Hexapod's Burrow Beacon and tells you to follow the Beacon’s in-game label.
- Reward totals (Gloom/Ova) are inconsistent across sources; consult the Ova/Ovum progression guide for broader questions: /mortal-shell-ii/ova/.
