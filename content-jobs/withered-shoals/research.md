## Evidence boundary

The reliable route is a named-Beacon and landmark route through the Mammon Corrupted Gate: **Gate of Mammon** access → **Ossinite Falls Beacon** → **Nameless Pass Beacon** → the Hexapod approach and arena → the post-boss **Hexapod's Burrow Beacon**. Sources agree on this chain and on Hexapod being the Withered Shoals boss, but they do not provide one audited room-by-room map or a shared coordinate system.

The article should therefore use Beacon labels, wooden platforms, skeletal remains, waterfalls, the Odious Crevice, Ruk, the hollowed tree/tube structure, and the foggy arena as navigation checks. It must not publish external map coordinates or create a new marker. The dedicated [Hexapod guide](/mortal-shell-ii/hexapod/) owns detailed boss mechanics; this route page should give only a short handoff and link to it.

No image is proposed. The route is clearer through named checkpoints and environmental landmarks, and no new screenshot is necessary to answer the route safely.

## Answer-first route

1. Reach **Gate of Mammon Beacon** in the Mammon overworld and cleanse it if the Withered Shoals Corrupted Gate is still blocked. Sources describe the overworld approach from nearby Mammon checkpoints differently; use the Beacon label and the visible Corrupted Gate rather than a compass coordinate.
2. Enter Withered Shoals. Defeat or bypass the early eel and snail enemies, follow the wooden platforms and skeletal remains, and keep the right-hand Strange Grisha route optional. The main route continues forward.
3. Activate and cleanse **Ossinite Falls Beacon**. The cleanse is an enemy-clear sequence; after the Beacon, the waterfall contains the optional **Torpor Stone** route.
4. Leave the waterfall area and follow the cliff/platform route through the crack in the wall and the descending cavern. Use the large snails, head enemies, and the deep rocky path as checks until **Nameless Pass Beacon** appears.
5. Activate and cleanse **Nameless Pass Beacon**. Its route runs behind the throne through **Odious Crevice**. Check the Ova below and the nearby chest for the source-reported **Charged Stone** before returning to the surface.
6. From Nameless Pass, follow the path toward **Ruk** and the hollowed tree / tube structure. Before dropping into the void or fog, check the lower landmark cluster for the optional **Depleted Gland**. Sources agree it is near this approach but differ on the exact drop and torch/tube position.
7. If the summon sign is present, **Proxima** is optional assistance and costs a Glimpse in checked boss coverage. Jump into the void/fog to trigger **Hexapod**; use the dedicated [Hexapod guide](/mortal-shell-ii/hexapod/) for the full fight.
8. After the kill, search the misty arena for **Hexapod's Burrow Beacon**, activate/cleanse it, and collect the associated Ova/Ovum before leaving. Use [Ova and corruption progression](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/) for the wider handoff.

## Source-backed facts

### Access to the gate

- **Withered Shoals** is a Mammon Corrupted Gate and Hexapod is its major boss.
- GameSpot's Mammon Beacon route says the **Gate of Mammon Beacon**, reached from the Mammon overworld, grants access to the Withered Shoals Corrupted Gate once cleansed.
- Into Indie Games describes the approach from **Mammon Outskirts** toward the southwest and then the Gate of Mammon. Gamer Guides treats the three Mammon gates as route-flexible and uses Withered Shoals as a practical early Mammon choice, not a universal mandatory order.
- The page should say “cleanse Gate of Mammon if the gate is blocked,” not claim that Withered Shoals must be the first or second gate for every save.

### Interior route and Beacons

- Into Indie Games gives the clearest continuous route: early eel/snail section and wooden platforms; optional Strange Grisha branch; brigand/bear-trap and skeletal-remains area; **Ossinite Falls Beacon**; waterfall; cliff route and wall crack; descending cavern; **Nameless Pass Beacon**; Odious Crevice; hollowed-tree drop; Hexapod.
- AllThingsHow's Ossinite Falls guide independently confirms that the Beacon cleanse is a short corrupted-nest clear, followed by an Ovum return. It also places the optional Strange Grisha route after the Beacon and wooden-bridge section.
- Probonk's Beacon inventory lists **Ossinite Falls** with **Broodshell Nest**, **Nameless Pass** with **Odious Crevice**, and **Hexapod's Burrow** as the post-boss Beacon hidden in the arena mist.
- Gaming ProMax's Beacon list cross-checks the three Withered Shoals interior Beacons: two main-route Beacons and a post-boss Beacon. It does not require the article to state a fixed universal Ova amount.

### Ossinite Falls and optional rewards

- Cleansing Ossinite Falls requires clearing enemies in the Broodshell Nest, cleansing the corruption, collecting the Ovum, and returning to the Beacon.
- The waterfall beyond Ossinite Falls contains the **Torpor Stone** Tarstone according to Into Indie Games and 100% Guides. Present this as optional exploration, not as a mandatory boss prerequisite.
- The Strange Grisha route is optional. The available descriptions place it on a side path near Ossinite Falls; the main route continues forward. The page should not turn a side-route turn into a universal compass instruction.

### Nameless Pass and the final approach

- Nameless Pass is the second Withered Shoals Beacon and sits shortly before the Hexapod arena in AllThingsHow, Into Indie Games, PatchCrazy, and Gaming ProMax coverage.
- Into Indie Games describes a throne with a Dorsalite stone, a route behind it into Odious Crevice, a tunnel, Ova below, and a Charged Stone chest to the right before cleansing/returning.
- AllThingsHow places the optional **Depleted Gland** near Nameless Pass, with the player dropping only one level into a tube structure before the boss-room commitment. Other guides describe checking below Ruk or around the underside of the hollowed tree. Treat these as the same final-approach landmark cluster, not as one guaranteed turn sequence.
- The Depleted Gland is useful for the Seedbearer's Scripture side objective, but the checked sources do not prove it is required to enter or defeat Hexapod. Do not make it mandatory.

### Hexapod handoff and post-boss completion

- Hexapod is the final boss of Withered Shoals. The encounter begins after the player follows the Nameless Pass approach and drops into the arena/void.
- Proxima is an optional associated summon. Checked boss coverage reports a Glimpse cost and ranged support; neither the summon nor a particular Shell/weapon is required by the route evidence.
- After Hexapod, a Beacon appears in the large foggy arena. Sources call it **Hexapod's Burrow** or describe it generically as the post-boss Beacon. Cleanse/activate it and collect the Ova/Ovum before leaving.
- The existing Hexapod page covers the red warning, Squid Surge, later burrow/dive behavior, rewards, and achievement. The Withered Shoals article should not duplicate its full attack table.

## Conflicts and uncertainty to preserve

1. **Overworld starting point:** Into Indie Games starts from Mammon Outskirts and moves southwest; GameSpot describes reaching Gate of Mammon from the High Lord's Courtyard/elevator/jump-gate side. These are different approach descriptions around Mammon, not proof of two Withered Shoals entrances. Use Gate of Mammon as the stable access check and omit coordinates.
2. **Exact Ossinite route turns:** sources agree on wooden platforms, enemies, the waterfall, and the cliff/wall-crack continuation but do not share a calibrated turn-by-turn map. Prefer these landmarks over hard-coded left/right instructions.
3. **Strange Grisha placement:** the side route is optional and associated with the Ossinite Falls section, but the checked walkthroughs narrate the branch at different points. Say “take the side path if you want Strange Grisha; continue forward for the main route.”
4. **Depleted Gland position:** sources variously describe a lower drop below Ruk, a tube structure, a torch, or the underside of the hollowed tree. Keep the item optional and tell the player to inspect the lower landmark cluster before the final drop; do not invent a coordinate or exact turn.
5. **Beacon naming:** local and external pages use **Hexapod's Burrow**, **Hexapod’s Burrow Beacon**, or “post-boss Beacon.” Use **Hexapod's Burrow Beacon** while telling the player to follow the in-game label.
6. **Rewards:** checked Hexapod sources disagree on exact Gloom totals (38,753 versus 54,250), and post-boss Ova counts are source-specific. The route page should omit fixed totals and send progression questions to the Ova guide.
7. **Boss phases and mechanics:** existing Hexapod research records a two-phase versus three-stage conflict. Keep fight advice to “see the Hexapod guide” and do not restate a disputed phase count here.

## Practical route guidance

- Cleanse each named Beacon when the menu offers it; resting, discovering, cleansing, and travelling are distinct Beacon interactions. Link the existing [Beacons and fast travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/) for that distinction.
- At Ossinite Falls, clear the Beacon dungeon before treating the checkpoint as complete. The post-cleanse waterfall item is optional.
- At Nameless Pass, use the throne, Odious Crevice, tunnel, Ova, and nearby chest as confirmation that the route is correct. Do not mistake a large snail shell for the Beacon at a distance; this warning is reported by Into Indie Games.
- Before the final drop, decide whether to collect the Depleted Gland and summon Proxima. Neither is a progression requirement in the checked evidence.
- After the boss, search the entire foggy arena for the new Beacon before fast travelling. Cleanse it and collect the Ova/Ovum before leaving.
