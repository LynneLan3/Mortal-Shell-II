Answer first
- Follow this route: Finish the Prologue → establish Marrow Keep → Fainweald (clear its three Corrupted Gates) → siphon Ova at Marrow Keep to unlock Mether’s Breath → Ruins of Mammon (clear its three Corrupted Gates) → deposit enough Ova to open the Unfound Path → clear the Unfound Path’s three branches → defeat Zmey → optionally Send Ova at the Gloom Siphon to begin NG+. See the numbered walkthrough below for concrete Beacons, bosses, and preparation notes.

Full walkthrough (step‑by‑step)
0. Prologue → Marrow Keep (do this first)
1. Finish the Prologue sequence and reach Marrow Keep.
2. Treat Marrow Keep as your persistent hub: use it for Shell discovery, the Tarforge, Gloom Siphon (Ova handling), and resupply. Speak with the hub NPCs before leaving.
3. Use the upper‑Keep elevator / travel mechanism to reach the first open region, Fainweald.
[IMAGE: Marrow Keep upper travel mechanism / elevator]

1. Fainweald — orientation and establishing early power
1. Travel to and activate the Widow's Overlook Beacon; use it as your early anchor.
[IMAGE: Widow's Overlook early-route anchor]
2. Recommended (not mandatory) route: head south from Widow’s Overlook to pick up Tiel (Shell), the Gloombound Flame pickup, and the Revered Gland; clear Mushroom Village and activate its Beacon to build a stable setup.
3. From Mushroom Village, return north to Blackridge Pass and toward Proxima / the Shattered Beacon route. Collect Ruk map fragments when convenient to reduce navigation fog — they’re helpful but not confirmed required for story progression. See /mortal-shell-ii/map-fragments/ and /mortal-shell-ii/shells/.
[IMAGE: Mushroom Village / Blackridge Pass Beacon chain]

2. Beacons, Ova, and the first major travel unlock
1. Activate every Beacon you find (this “tags” the location for travel). When you enter and clear a Beacon’s short dungeon you “cleanse” it — cleansing is a separate action that unlocks the full Beacon menu and removes local corruption. See /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/.
2. Major Corrupted Gate bosses drop large Ova rewards and unlock post‑boss Beacons. Treat post‑boss Beacons as high‑value rewards.
3. After collecting and siphoning Ova at the Marrow Keep Gloom Siphon you can obtain Mether’s Breath, which enables direct Beacon‑to‑Beacon travel and clears some overworld corruption. Sources disagree on the exact early Ova counts for Mether’s Breath (see Conflicts below). For endgame Ova thresholds, see /mortal-shell-ii/ova/.
[IMAGE: Post-boss Ova Beacon]

3. Fainweald Corrupted Gates (recommended southern-first orientation)
Note: the order of these three is flexible; the southern-first path is a recommendation to reduce backtracking.
1. Clear Glutted Mire — boss: Magdalena, Lady of the Woods. Collect the post‑boss Beacon and Ova.
2. Clear Sanguine Caverns — boss: The Lost Child. In the final Beacon/inner chamber check for a Blackmarrow Key before leaving; see /mortal-shell-ii/blackmarrow-keys/.
3. Clear Prisoners’ Domain — boss: The Nameless Captive. Before treating this boss as done, check the Peter’s Perfect Parry condition (see /mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/).
4. After each major boss return to Marrow Keep as desirable to siphon Ova, upgrade the Tarforge, change Shells, and retool loadout.

4. Use Mether’s Breath → Ruins of Mammon
1. Siphon enough Ova at Marrow Keep to obtain Mether’s Breath and clear the corrupted teleporter route to Mammon.
2. Arrive at Outskirts of Mammon and activate nearby Beacons; choose the Mammon gate that fits your current Shell, weapon, and upgrades. Mammon is commonly reported as a step up in difficulty — use the hub to respec, upgrade, or swap Shells if you struggle.
[IMAGE: Mammon transition / gate overview]

5. Mammon Corrupted Gates (three‑gate working model)
Note: sources conflict (one guide claims two Mammon gates), but GameSpot and the repository list three — treat three as the working campaign model and preserve the discrepancy (see Conflicts).
1. Conquered Temple — boss: Droeg the Conqueror. Collect the Conqueror’s Hoard Beacon and Ova. See /mortal-shell-ii/droeg-the-conqueror/.
2. Withered Shoals — boss: Hexapod. Collect Hexapod’s Burrow Beacon and Ova. See /mortal-shell-ii/hexapod/.
3. Faded Citadel — boss: Isaac, the Scholar Prince. Collect the Scholar‑Prince’s Chamber Beacon and Ova. See /mortal-shell-ii/isaac-the-scholar-prince/.
4. After each major win, return to Marrow Keep as needed to siphon Ova, adapt builds, and cleanse Beacons.

6. Unfound Path → final guardians → Zmey
1. Deposit enough Ova at the Gloom Siphon to open the Unfound Path central area (use the in‑game Gloom Siphon prompt as the live gate; reported endgame totals are conflicted — see Conflicts and /mortal-shell-ii/ova/).
2. Enter the Unfound Path and clear all three branch guardians (branch order is not fixed):
   - Bridge of Procession (Orrem / Orrem, the Reclaimed)
   - Obsidian Vault (The Monolith)
   - Faithful's Shelter (Malborn Offspring)
3. Cleanse the Hidden Nave Beacon and collect the Endless Core before the final engagement if you want to remove the Tarforge upgrade limit.
4. After clearing the three branches, take the opened central route to Zmey the Unbidden. See /mortal-shell-ii/orrem-discarded-golem/, /mortal-shell-ii/monolith/, /mortal-shell-ii/malborn-offspring/, and /mortal-shell-ii/zmey/.
[IMAGE: Unfound Path final preparation]

7. Post‑Zmey cleanup and NG+ decision
1. Defeating Zmey does not appear to forcibly begin New Game Plus. The strongest current reporting says you can remain in the current cycle and clean up remaining content.
2. When you are ready, choose Send Ova at Marrow Keep’s Gloom Siphon to deliberately begin the ending / NG+ flow. See /mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/ and /mortal-shell-ii/endings/.

Common mistakes and missable warnings
- Skipping the Prologue and hub sequence: Prologue‑bound achievements (example names: Mid Summer?, No, You Still Can't Win) are reported missable if you skip prologue content. Warn before skipping; see /mortal-shell-ii/endings/ and the trophy pages for details.
- Treating Beacon “tagging” as cleansing: activate (tag) every Beacon but remember cleansing is a separate action required for the full checkpoint, local corruption removal, and some achievements. See /mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/.
- Peter’s Perfect Parry: this specific trophy/condition is tied to The Nameless Captive’s head‑spin sequence — address it before leaving the Prisoners’ Domain. See /mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/.
- Sending Ova prematurely: do not use the Gloom Siphon Send Ova option until you have finished current‑cycle cleanup you care about (Beacons, Shells, Tarstones, keys, NPC scenes).
- Relying on a single route order: the three gates in Fainweald and Mammon are not strictly ordered by the game; the guide’s southern‑first recommendation reduces backtracking but is not mandatory.
- Assuming fixed Ova totals: sources disagree (see Conflicts). Use the in‑game Gloom Siphon prompt and /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ rather than a single offsite number.

What happens next (endgame checklist)
- Optional: clean remaining Beacons and collect their Ova (AllThingsHow reports Beacons are cleanup‑legal after Zmey).
- Finish any optional Shell, weapon, Tarstone, key, or NPC routes you care about.
- If you want the Tarforge upgrades unlimited, make sure to cleanse Hidden Nave and collect the Endless Core before the final push.
- When satisfied, choose Send Ova at Marrow Keep’s Gloom Siphon to start NG+. See /mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/ and /mortal-shell-ii/endings/.
- Note: a Steam community report highlights that tutorial Beacons may matter for the all‑Beacons achievement; the safe rule is to cleanse Prologue Beacons during your first run.

Conflicts and outstanding uncertainties (preserved from sources)
- Mammon gate count: GameSpot + repository list three Mammon gates (Droeg, Hexapod, Isaac); AltChar claims two. The route retains three as the working model and flags the two‑gate claim as unresolved.
- Ova thresholds:
  - Early Mether’s Breath unlock is variably reported (AllThingsHow: “five additional” vs. “roughly seven to eight total” including starting stock). Use the in‑game prompt for precise unlocking.
  - Endgame Send Ova totals are reported as 168 (AllThingsHow / repository) vs 167 (trophy checklist). The route links to /mortal-shell-ii/ova/ and recommends using the current Gloom Siphon prompt as authoritative.
- Mandatory gate count for endgame: safest completion route clears all six Corrupted Gates; some reports say the 168 Ova threshold can be reached without every gate. Treat all six as the reliable full‑completion route and use the game’s siphon prompt as the live effect.
- Beacon totals and achievement behavior: AllThingsHow reports 49 Beacons (46 cleanseable) and that Beacons are cleanup‑legal after Zmey; a community report flags tutorial Beacons for the all‑Beacons achievement. Preserve the conservative early‑cleanse warning.
