# Writer Smoke Test Research

## Topic

A fictional game route called the Lantern Trial in Harbor of Echoes.

## Confirmed notes

- The route starts at North Gate.
- The player must speak with the NPC Mira before leaving North Gate.
- The Blue Lantern is collected at Ash Wharf.
- The Tide Warden is the boss at the end of the route.
- The listed reward is the Saltglass Key.

## Uncertainty to preserve

- One note places the Saltglass Key in the reward chest immediately after the Tide Warden fight.
- Another note says the key is handed over by Mira after the player returns to North Gate.
- The research does not establish which reward sequence is correct.
