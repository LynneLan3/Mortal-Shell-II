Short answer
Zmey, the Unbidden, is the mandatory final boss at the end of the Unfound Path. To reach it: open the Marrow Keep Gloom Siphon (sources report 167–168 Ova; check the in‑game prompt), enter the Unfound Path, clear the three guardian branches (Orrem the Reclaimed, the Monolith, and Malborn Offspring), cleanse the Hidden Nave Beacon, then take the updraft to Zmey’s arena. The fight is built around Cosmic Flame arena control: read tells, survive the full animation sequence, take a short punish, and avoid chasing retreats. (Route and trophy sources: AllThingsHow, Nerd’s Chalk, GameSpot, GamingProMax.)

Route to Zmey (step-by-step)
1. Gather Ova until the Marrow Keep Gloom Siphon route becomes available. The sources report 167–168 Ova; check the Gloom Siphon prompt in your game rather than relying on a single number (AllThingsHow; Nerd’s Chalk).  
2. Enter the Unfound Path from beneath Marrow Keep through the opened Gloom Siphon (AllThingsHow).  
3. Defeat the three tendril guardian branches: Orrem the Reclaimed, the Monolith, and the Malborn Offspring. One source lists the order Orrem → Monolith → Malborn; another says the branches can be tackled in any order. All three must be cleared to open the central route (AllThingsHow; GamingProMax).  
4. Reach and cleanse the Hidden Nave Beacon before the final approach. GameSpot reports the cleansed Beacon contains the Endless Core (removes the Tarforge upgrade limit). GamingProMax reports an updraft behind the Beacon that carries you to the arena — cleanse the Beacon then use the updraft to reach Zmey (GameSpot; GamingProMax).  
5. Enter the final arena and initiate the fight.

Prep checklist (what to consider before the arena)
- Cleanse the Hidden Nave Beacon (see above) and, if you want the final Tarforge upgrade first, pick up the Endless Core reported by GameSpot.  
- Consider Seedbearer’s Scripture for Cosmic Disease mitigation; GamingProMax recommends it, but the Scripture’s exact mitigation values conflict across sources — see the local Seedbearer’s Scripture page for the repository’s documented values before treating external percentages as settled (/mortal-shell-ii/seedbearers-scripture/).  
- Shell choices reported by guides: Tiel for mobility/stealth (recommended by Nerd’s Chalk) and Genessa for safety/aggro control (Nerd’s Chalk and GameSpot). These are recommendations, not requirements; pick a Shell you are comfortable with (/mortal-shell-ii/tiel/, /mortal-shell-ii/sester-genessa/).  
- Use your upgraded, familiar weapon loadout rather than chasing a specific “best” weapon. Guides mention multi-hit setups mid‑game but don’t establish a Zmey‑specific best weapon (/mortal-shell-ii/weapons/).  
- GamingProMax reports you can summon Genessa or Tiel before this encounter; that is a single-source report and optional.  
- Balance Patch 1 checked material does not identify any direct Zmey health/damage/animation change; future patches may still affect the fight (/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/).

Zmey fight — core procedure (what to do, in order)
1. Start the encounter and keep the camera on Zmey’s body and active flame areas; avoid hugging the legs where tells can be hidden (consolidated recommendation from sources).  
2. Read each tell fully. The consistent loop reported across sources: read the tell → survive the full sequence → take a short punish → reset. Do not chase retreats automatically.  
3. When Zmey retreats or jumps away: do not chase to force a punish; wait to see the follow-up animation (GamingProMax; Nerd’s Chalk). The retreat itself is not a punish.  
4. For Blade Slam / straight-line Cosmic Flame: dodge sideways rather than straight back. Re-enter only after the flame line ends and take a short punish.  
5. For Cascading Flare (flare field around the player): move out immediately and wait for the area to clear. One source calls the rooted cast a free‑damage window; another advises waiting it out — treat the “rooted-window = free damage” claim as source‑reported and use only a short, confirmed punish (GamingProMax; Nerd’s Chalk).  
6. For close-range red-icon attacks: stop extending combos and evade away from the active hitbox while preserving lateral space. Sources disagree on a single “best” dodge direction; avoid trading into the red attack (Nerd’s Chalk; conflicting guidance).  
7. For Flamebreath/wave-like Cosmic Flame pressure: move laterally to exit the attack path; do not chase through the waves. Re-engage after the effect ends.  
8. For sword/tail melee sequences: stay to the side where possible, dodge the sequence in full, then take a short one- or two‑hit punish after a clear recovery (exact combo lengths are not confirmed).  
9. Keep punishes short (two or three hits) and mobile — the boss’s model makes openings look larger than they are.

Attack tells, source-backed responses, and punish windows
- Retreat / jump away — Do not chase; wait for follow-up. Punish window: none during the retreat (GamingProMax; Nerd’s Chalk).  
- Blade Slam — Retreat + straight flame line; dodge sideways (not straight back). Punish: short window after flame line ends.  
- Cascading Flare — Flames erupt around the player for several seconds. Move out immediately. Punish: sources conflict; GamingProMax calls a rooted cast a free window, Nerd’s Chalk says wait it out. Use only a short, confirmed punish after danger clears.  
- Close-range red-icon attack — Stop combos and evade away from the active hitbox while keeping lateral space. No immediate punish; reset after the animation. (Direction guidance is a source conflict — preserve evasive space.)  
- Flamebreath / wave-like Cosmic Flame — Move laterally until the effect ends; re-engage afterward. Exact wave count/timing not established.  
- Sword / tail melee pressure — Side positioning + full-dodge of the sequence. Punish: one or two hits after recovery; exact combo length not confirmed.

Phases and phase uncertainty
- GamingProMax reports two phases and a phase transition around roughly half health, adding a flaming sword/Burn layer in phase two. This two-phase description is a single-source report for phase detail; the exact transition percentage and per‑hit effects are not independently confirmed. Treat phase descriptions as reported rather than definitive.

Rewards, trophy, and missable actions
- Trophy: defeating Zmey awards the “It’s over now?” trophy — reported consistently across sources (AllThingsHow; Nerd’s Chalk).  
- Loot: Nerd’s Chalk and GamingProMax report no unique named item reward from the fight. AllThingsHow’s route guide describes extracting “Ora” after the cutscene but does not confirm a named boss loot item. Conclude: no universally confirmed named weapon/item is guaranteed by the cross-checked boss guides; post-fight extraction wording varies by source.  
- Missable action: defeating Zmey is not itself reported as missable. The irreversible action is selecting Send Ova at the Gloom Siphon after the fight — AllThingsHow and GamingProMax say choosing Send Ova begins New Game Plus without a confirmation prompt. Finish any desired collection/exploration before returning to the siphon.

Common mistakes (what players repeatedly get wrong)
- Chasing every retreat — many animations that look like openings are follow-ups; chasing leads to avoidable hits.  
- Greedy/full combos — attempting long combos in the boss’s flame zones or after partial recovery often gets you hit. Prefer short two‑ or three‑hit punishes.  
- Standing directly in front or under the boss — leaves you exposed to straight-line flame and breath attacks; side positioning is safer.  
- Hugging the legs — hides tells (especially the red close-range warning) and reduces your lateral escape options.  
- Skipping Beacon cleanse — not cleansing Hidden Nave can cause you to miss the Endless Core (if you want that Tarforge upgrade) or the updraft reported by GamingProMax.  
- Relying on a hard Ova number — sources disagree between 167 and 168 Ova; check the in-game Gloom Siphon prompt.

What happens next (after you win)
1. Let the post-fight cutscene finish and return to Marrow Keep / your save. (AllThingsHow.)  
2. If you want more time in the current cycle, continue optional exploration and collection before committing to New Game Plus. Several guides warn that Send Ova is the irreversible action.  
3. When you are ready, return to the Gloom Siphon and choose Send Ova to begin New Game Plus. AllThingsHow and GamingProMax report that Send Ova starts NG+ without a confirmation prompt.

Conflicting / uncertain claims to preserve (summary)
1. Ova threshold: 167 (AllThingsHow trophy guide) vs. 168 (AllThingsHow Zmey guide and Nerd’s Chalk). Check the Gloom Siphon prompt in‑game.  
2. Guardian order: AllThingsHow lists a specific order; GamingProMax reports branches can be cleared in any order. Only that all three must be cleared is consistent.  
3. Phase details: GamingProMax reports two phases with a ~half‑health transition and a flame sword/burn layer in phase two; not independently confirmed.  
4. Seedbearer’s Scripture mitigation percentage: an external “80%” claim conflicts with the repository’s local Seedbearer’s Scripture page; link to the local page for documented values (/mortal-shell-ii/seedbearers-scripture/).  
5. Reward wording: no unique item vs. post-fight “Ora” extraction — sources differ; the trophy is consistent.  
6. Name variant: “Zmey, the Unbidden” is the majority name; one guide uses “Uninvited.”

Patch note
