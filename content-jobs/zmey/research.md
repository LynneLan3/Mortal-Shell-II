## Evidence summary

The sources agree that Zmey, the Unbidden, is the final mandatory boss at the end of the Unfound Path. The safest stable facts are the gated route, the three guardian branches before the final arena, the Cosmic Flame patterns, short punish windows, the boss trophy, and the fact that defeating Zmey is separate from selecting Send Ova for New Game Plus.

Exact progression numbers and some build/phase details conflict across current launch coverage. Those conflicts are preserved below and must remain qualified in the page.

## Location, nearest Beacon, and route

- **Location:** Zmey is at the end of the **Unfound Path**, the final dungeon reached from beneath **Marrow Keep**. AllThingsHow and Nerd’s Chalk agree on the end-of-Unfound-Path placement.
- **Nearest practical Beacon:** The final route source and GameSpot identify the **Hidden Nave Beacon** before the final arena. Cleanse it before proceeding; GameSpot says it contains the **Endless Core**, which removes the Tarforge upgrade limit. GamingProMax reports an updraft behind the Beacon that carries the player to the arena.
- **Route:** Gather enough Ova to open the Marrow Keep Gloom Siphon route; enter the Unfound Path; defeat the three tendril guardians (**Orrem the Reclaimed, the Monolith, and Malborn Offspring**) so the central route opens; reach and cleanse Hidden Nave; take the updraft to Zmey.
- **No coordinates:** No x/y map coordinates are needed or supported by the reviewed sources.

## Prerequisites and route conflicts

- AllThingsHow and Nerd’s Chalk report **168 Ova** as the Unfound Path threshold.
- AllThingsHow’s trophy guide reports **167 Ova** for reaching the Unfound Path. This is a direct conflict. The page should say **“the sources report 167–168 Ova; check the Gloom Siphon prompt rather than treating one number as universal”** or avoid a hard number in the quick answer.
- The sources agree that the Gloom Siphon room in Marrow Keep is the entry point, but do not establish a single mandatory order for the three guardians. AllThingsHow lists Orrem → Monolith → Malborn; GamingProMax says the three branches can be tackled in any order. Preserve the order as a source-reported route, not a requirement.
- The boss name is **Zmey, the Unbidden** in the plan and most sources. One guide uses “Uninvited”; treat that as a naming variant, not a separate boss.

## Fight mechanics and phases

- Zmey is a large dragon whose main threat is **Cosmic Flame** arena control.
- GamingProMax explicitly describes **two phases**, with a transition around half health and a flaming sword/Burn layer in phase two. The exact phase percentage and whether every sword hit receives the same effect are not independently confirmed; do not present the percentage as a hard rule.
- The stable fight loop is: read the tell, survive the full sequence, take a short punish, then reset. Multiple sources warn that chasing retreats and extending long combos causes avoidable hits.
- Stand to the side when practical rather than directly in front or underneath. Keep space for lateral movement and avoid being trapped by the arena edge.

## Attacks, tells, and punish windows

| Attack / tell | Response supported by the sources | Punish window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Retreat / jump away** | Do not automatically chase; wait to see the follow-up. | The retreat itself is not a punish. |
| **Blade Slam** | A jump-back setup sends Cosmic Flames in a straight line. Dodge sideways, not straight backward. | Re-enter after the flame line ends and take a short punish. |
| **Cascading Flare** | Flames erupt around the player for several seconds. Move out immediately and wait for the area to clear. | GamingProMax calls the rooted cast a free-damage window; Nerd’s Chalk advises waiting it out. Use only a short, confirmed punish after the danger clears, and label the rooted-window claim as source-reported. |
| **Close-range red-icon attack** | Stop extending the combo and evade; do not trade into a red attack. | None during the red warning. Reset after the animation. |
| **Flamebreath / wave-like Cosmic Flame pressure** | Move laterally until the area attack ends; do not stand in the line or chase through it. | Re-engage after the effect ends. Exact wave count/timing is not established. |
| **Sword / tail melee pressure** | Stay on the side where possible and dodge the complete sequence. | One or two hits after a clear recovery; exact combo length is not confirmed. |

The sources differ on whether the close-range red attack should be dodged backward or sideways. The guide should avoid a universal direction for that attack and say to evade away from the active hitbox while preserving lateral space.

## Shell, weapon, and preparation recommendations

- **Seedbearer’s Scripture:** GamingProMax recommends it for Cosmic Disease resistance. The local Seedbearer’s Scripture page documents different mitigation values (15% passive and a 50% active effect), so the external “80%” claim must not be repeated as settled fact. Link the existing guide and describe the item as a Cosmic Disease mitigation option.
- **Shell:** Nerd’s Chalk recommends Tiel for mobility/stealth; it also lists Genessa for safety/aggro control. GameSpot says its writer used a powered-up Tiel. These are recommendations, not requirements or proof of a best Shell.
- **Weapon:** AllThingsHow mentions an Axatana/Naylshotte Clone Genessa setup for multi-hit play, but explicitly tunes it for mid-game. It is not a Zmey-specific best weapon proof. Recommend the player’s upgraded, familiar weapon and link the existing weapons guide; do not invent a required weapon.
- **Endless Core:** GameSpot reports that the cleansed Hidden Nave Beacon contains the final Tarforge upgrade. Treat this as route preparation, not a requirement to win the fight.
- **Summons:** GamingProMax reports that Genessa and Tiel can both be summoned before this encounter. This is single-source detail and should be labeled as a reported option, not required preparation.

## Rewards, trophy, and missable status

- The cross-checked trophy/achievement is **“It’s over now?”**, awarded for defeating Zmey. AllThingsHow’s trophy guide lists the same condition; no special weapon, difficulty, or finishing technique is established.
- Nerd’s Chalk and GamingProMax report **no unique item reward** from the fight. AllThingsHow’s dedicated route guide says the player extracts “Ora” after the cutscene, but does not establish that as a named boss loot item. Keep this as an uncertainty: **no named weapon/item reward is confirmed by the cross-checked boss guides; the post-fight extraction wording varies by source**.
- Defeating Zmey itself is not reported as missable. The actual irreversible action is selecting **Send Ova** at the Gloom Siphon after the fight; AllThingsHow and GamingProMax agree that this starts New Game Plus without a confirmation prompt. Finish optional exploration and collection first if desired.

## What to do after the fight

1. Let the post-fight cutscene complete and return to Marrow Keep/current save.
2. Continue optional exploration and unfinished collection in the current cycle if needed.
3. Only when ready to begin New Game Plus, return to the Gloom Siphon and choose **Send Ova**. Treat the no-confirmation warning as source-backed and do not imply that killing Zmey automatically starts NG+.

- The reviewed external Zmey guides do not cite a Zmey-specific patch adjustment. The safe wording is: **no direct Zmey change was identified in the checked patch material; future platform/version differences remain possible**.

## Practical player insights

- The most repeatable advice across sources is to stop treating every retreat as an opening: wait for the next animation, especially after a jump-back.
- Sideways movement is consistently recommended for Blade Slam and straight-line flame pressure.
- Short two- or three-hit punishes are safer than full combos. The boss’s large model makes openings look larger than they are.
- Move first when Cosmic Flames appear. Do not finish a greedy combo inside a growing flame zone.
- Keep the camera on the boss’s body and the active flame area; do not hug the legs so closely that the red tell is hidden.

## Conflicting / uncertain claims to preserve

1. **Ova threshold:** 167 (AllThingsHow trophy guide) versus 168 (AllThingsHow Zmey guide and Nerd’s Chalk). Use a qualified range or advise checking the in-game Gloom Siphon rather than guessing.

3. **Phase details:** two phases and an approximate half-health transition are reported by GamingProMax; exact trigger and all phase-two effects are not independently confirmed.
4. **Cosmic Disease mitigation:** the external 80% claim conflicts with the repository’s existing Seedbearer’s Scripture page. Link the local page and omit the exact external percentage.
5. **Reward wording:** named item/no item/extraction wording varies. State the trophy confidently and label any post-fight extraction as source-dependent.
6. **Name variant:** “Unbidden” is the plan/source majority; “Uninvited” is a variant used by one guide.
