Mortal Shell II Guide

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Mortal Shell II is a standalone action-RPG sequel to Mortal Shell, developed by Cold Symmetry and published by Playstack. It expands the original with stamina-unrestricted combat, deeper weapon upgrades, possessable warrior Shells, and a compact interconnected open world. The game is scheduled for a worldwide digital release on August 20, 2026 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam.

What is Mortal Shell II?

Mortal Shell II is a standalone sequel — you do not need the original Mortal Shell to play. Official materials describe it as a dark action-RPG where you play as the Harbinger, prophesied to reclaim the Undermether’s stolen Ova from creatures that guard her unborn.

Compared with the first game, Cold Symmetry and Playstack position Mortal Shell II around freer exploration, more weapons and Shells, and combat that is no longer limited by a stamina gauge. Players discover dormant warrior Shells in the world, possess them, and unlock unique abilities and story fragments through lost memories.

Mortal Shell II Release Date

Mortal Shell II launches worldwide on August 20, 2026 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam. This date is listed on the official site (mortalshell2.com), the Steam store page, and console store listings.

Players who pre-purchase the Devout Edition receive up to 72 hours of advanced access, starting August 17, 2026.

Platforms

Based on official store and publisher listings, Mortal Shell II is confirmed for:

  • PC (Steam) — August 20, 2026; pre-purchase available
  • PlayStation 5 — August 20, 2026; digital listings live; physical Revered Edition announced for PS5
  • Xbox Series X|S — August 20, 2026; optimized for Xbox Series X|S

Nintendo Switch / Switch 2 versions are not yet officially confirmed. Multiplayer modes beyond single-player are also not officially confirmed on current store pages.

Gameplay Overview

Official Steam and store copy highlight several core systems:

  • Shell possession — find and awaken warrior Shells with unique abilities
  • Stamina-unrestricted combat — break enemy posture and land critical strikes
  • Weapons & sidearms — deeper weapon design with extensive upgrade options
  • Compact open world — interconnected exploration across temples, forests, icy graves, and bone citadels
  • Dungeons — Steam currently describes more than 80 dungeons between landmarks

Official materials also state there are eight playable Shells. Detailed weapon lists, boss names, and full progression trees beyond what store pages publish are not fully confirmed in public materials.
