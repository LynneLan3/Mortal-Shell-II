Mortal Shell II Release Date

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Mortal Shell II comes out on August 20, 2026 worldwide for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam.

Confirmed launch date

Worldwide digital release: August 20, 2026

This date appears on the official Mortal Shell II website, the Steam store page (app ID 2584270), PlayStation Store listings, and Xbox store listings published by Playstack / Cold Symmetry.

Devout Edition advanced access

Pre-purchasing the Devout Edition unlocks up to 72 hours of advanced access, starting August 17, 2026 — three days before the full launch.

Official materials describe the Devout Edition as a digital deluxe version that includes an exclusive Obsidian skin set for all eight playable Shells. Exact regional store copy may also list pre-order bonus Harbinger skins; check your platform storefront for the current bonus wording.

Platforms

PC (Steam) August 20, 2026 — confirmed; pre-purchase available
PlayStation 5 August 20, 2026 — confirmed digital release; physical Revered Edition announced for PS5
Xbox Series X|S August 20, 2026 — confirmed; optimized for Xbox Series X|S
Nintendo Switch / Switch 2 Not yet officially confirmed

Physical edition note

The official site announces a collector’s Revered Edition coming to PS5, featuring a physical artbook, steelcase, fine art prints, and digital extras. A separate street date for physical stock beyond the digital August 20 launch is not clearly confirmed on the materials checked for this page — verify on the retailer or storefront you use.
