## Does Mortal Shell 2 beta progress carry over?

**No — your full Open Beta save does not carry over to the full game.**

Complete inventory and normal progression reset at launch. Only explicit beta launch benefits remain: **The Flayed Harbinger** cosmetic for playing the Open Beta, and an optional **Prologue skip** if you progressed beyond **Marrow Keep**.

| Beta progress / reward | Full release |
| --- | --- |
| Currency | ❌ Resets |
| Weapons | ❌ Resets |
| Shells | ❌ Resets |
| Collectibles | ❌ Resets |
| Other discovered items | ❌ Resets |
| The Flayed Harbinger cosmetic | ✅ Available at launch after playing the beta |
| Prologue skip | ✅ Unlocked if you progressed beyond Marrow Keep |

**Your beta save itself is not transferred; these are launch rewards or unlocks, not restored beta progression.**

For how to download the preview and what it contains, see the [Open Beta guide](/mortal-shell-ii/open-beta/).

## What carries over from the beta?

Official Steam and Xbox Open Beta listings confirm two launch benefits:

1. **The Flayed Harbinger** — exclusive cosmetic, claimable in the full game at launch if you played the Open Beta.
2. **Prologue skip** — optional unlock if you progress **beyond the Marrow Keep** during the Open Beta.

The listings describe “select progress carrying forward,” then spell out these limits. They do not say your Open Beta save file becomes your launch save.

## What resets when the full game launches?

Steam and Xbox both state that the following **reset at launch**:

- Currency
- Weapons
- Shells
- Collectibles
- Other items discovered during the Open Beta

Do not farm Open Beta resources on the assumption that the inventory will transfer. Official copy frames the reset as intentional, to keep the full release balanced.

GamesRadar’s reporting of the same rules adds a practical note: items picked up after the Prologue in the first open region are among the things that reset. That matches the store language. This page does not turn that into an item-by-item database.

## Mortal Shell 2 beta rewards: The Flayed Harbinger

Playing the Open Beta unlocks **The Flayed Harbinger**, an exclusive cosmetic for the full game.

What the official Steam listing confirms:

- It is earned by playing the Open Beta.
- It is available to **claim in the full game at launch**.
- It is described as a cosmetic reward, not a combat upgrade.

Steam does not publish a playtime threshold. Play the Open Beta on the account you will use at launch, then claim the cosmetic when Mortal Shell II releases on August 20, 2026.

## How the Marrow Keep Prologue skip works

Players who progress **beyond the Marrow Keep** unlock the option to skip the Prologue in the full game.

Confirmed from official listings:

- The skip is an **option**, not a forced start point.
- The requirement is progress **beyond** the Marrow Keep, not merely launching the beta.

GamesRadar reports that the skip returns you to your first visit to the Keep rather than erasing the opening entirely, and that this point comes after the Tar Golem fight. That is secondary reporting around the official Marrow Keep condition. This Guide treats “beyond the Marrow Keep” as the rule that matters.

Reaching Magdalena is **not** the stated requirement for the skip. Magdalena is a later Open Beta encounter; the carry-over condition named by the store is Marrow Keep.

## Do beta saves transfer to the full game?

No. Treat **beta save transfer** and **beta launch benefits** as different things.

Your Open Beta save does **not** become a full launch save. Expect a **new save** for the full release. Until launch-day patch notes say otherwise, do not assume extra transfer for levels, map completion, or dungeon unlocks — those details are not confirmed in the current store listings.

What carries forward are launch entitlements only: the Flayed Harbinger cosmetic (if you played the beta) and the optional Prologue skip (if you progressed beyond Marrow Keep).

Mortal Shell II’s full release is scheduled for **August 20, 2026**. See the [release date guide](/mortal-shell-ii/release-date/) for platforms and edition context.

## FAQ

### Does Mortal Shell 2 beta progress carry over?

No. Full Open Beta progression does not carry over. Only The Flayed Harbinger cosmetic and the Marrow Keep Prologue-skip option are documented as launch benefits.

### Does the Mortal Shell 2 beta save transfer?

No. Official listings do not transfer your beta save file into the full game. Start a new save at launch and claim any unlocks there.

### What carries over from the Mortal Shell 2 beta?

Playing the Open Beta unlocks The Flayed Harbinger cosmetic at launch. Progressing beyond Marrow Keep unlocks an optional Prologue skip. Nothing else is confirmed as carrying over.

### What resets after the Mortal Shell 2 beta?

Currency, weapons, Shells, collectibles, and other discovered items reset. Do not expect your beta inventory to appear in the full release.

### Do Mortal Shell 2 beta rewards carry over?

Yes for the documented rewards: The Flayed Harbinger cosmetic (play the beta) and the Prologue skip (progress beyond Marrow Keep). Those are launch unlocks, not a restored save.

### Does Mortal Shell 2 demo progress carry over?

Searchers sometimes call the Open Beta a “demo.” The current official product is the **Open Beta**, and the same rule applies: full progress does not transfer to the full game. Only the confirmed launch benefits above apply.

### Do I keep my Shells or weapons?

No. Official copy lists **Shells** and **Weapons** among the things that reset. Discovering a Shell such as Tiel in the [Open Beta](/mortal-shell-ii/open-beta/) does not mean it carries into launch as possessed progress.

### How do I unlock the Prologue skip?

Progress **beyond the Marrow Keep** during the Open Beta. The skip is optional at full release. Defeating Magdalena is not the stated requirement.
