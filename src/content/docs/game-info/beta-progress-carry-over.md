## Does Mortal Shell II beta progress carry over?

**Only select benefits carry forward.** Official Steam and Xbox Open Beta listings say the preview includes “select progress carrying forward into the full release,” then spell out the limits.

To preserve the balance of the full release, **beta save progress will not carry over in full**. That is the official rule. You keep specific participation rewards, not your Open Beta inventory or character save.

This page is only about carry over, rewards, and skipping the Prologue. For how to download the preview and what it contains, use the [Open Beta guide](/mortal-shell-ii/open-beta/).

## What you keep at launch

Official store copy currently confirms two benefits:

| Benefit | Official condition | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Flayed Harbinger** | Play the Open Beta | Exclusive cosmetic, claimable in the full game at launch |
| **Skip the Prologue** | Progress **beyond the Marrow Keep** during the Open Beta | Optional Prologue skip when starting the full game |

Those are the two documented progression-related benefits. The listings do not say your Open Beta save file becomes your launch save.

## What does not carry over

Steam and Xbox both state that the following **reset at launch**:

- Currency
- Weapons
- Shells
- Collectibles
- Other items discovered during the Open Beta

Do not farm Open Beta resources on the assumption that the inventory will transfer. Official copy frames the reset as intentional, to keep the full release balanced.

GamesRadar’s reporting of the same rules adds a practical note: items picked up after the Prologue in the first open region are among the things that reset. That matches the store language. This page does not turn that into an item-by-item database.

## Beta rewards: The Flayed Harbinger

Playing the Open Beta unlocks **The Flayed Harbinger**, an exclusive cosmetic for the full game.

What the official Steam listing confirms:

- It is earned by playing the Open Beta.
- It is available to **claim in the full game at launch**.
- It is described as a cosmetic reward, not a combat upgrade.

Steam does not publish a playtime threshold. Play the Open Beta on the account you will use at launch, then claim the cosmetic when the full game releases on August 20, 2026.

## Skip prologue: the Marrow Keep condition

Players who progress **beyond the Marrow Keep** unlock the option to skip the Prologue in the full game.

Confirmed from official listings:

- The skip is an **option**, not a forced start point.
- The requirement is progress **beyond** the Marrow Keep, not merely launching the beta.

GamesRadar reports that the skip returns you to your first visit to the Keep rather than erasing the opening entirely, and that this point comes after the Tar Golem fight. That is secondary reporting around the official Marrow Keep condition. This Guide treats “beyond the Marrow Keep” as the rule that matters.

Reaching Magdalena is **not** the stated requirement for the skip. Magdalena is a later Open Beta encounter; the carry-over condition named by the store is Marrow Keep.

## Do Mortal Shell II Beta Saves Carry Over?

Official listings treat **beta save transfer** and **beta launch benefits** as different things.

### Beta save / normal progression

Your Open Beta save does **not** become a full launch save. Expect a **new save** for the full release. Currency, weapons, Shells, collectibles, and other discovered items **reset**. Until launch-day patch notes say otherwise, do not assume extra transfer for levels, map completion, or dungeon unlocks.

### Beta benefits / entitlements

What does carry forward are launch entitlements, not a restored inventory:

1. Claim **The Flayed Harbinger** cosmetic at launch if you played the Open Beta.
2. Optionally **skip the Prologue** if you progressed beyond the Marrow Keep.

Save transfer ≠ beta reward / launch benefit.

## FAQ

### Does any of my beta save transfer?

Not as a full save. Official listings transfer select rewards and a Prologue-skip option, then reset discovered items.

### Do I have to finish Magdalena to keep anything?

No official listing ties The Flayed Harbinger or the Prologue skip to defeating Magdalena. The named skip condition is progress beyond the Marrow Keep.

### Can I skip the Prologue without playing the beta?

The Open Beta listings only describe the skip as a beta unlock. They do not say the full game will offer the same skip to players who never installed the preview.

### Will I keep Tiel or other Shells?

Official copy lists **Shells** among the things that reset. Tiel, the Acolyte, is available to discover in the [Open Beta](/mortal-shell-ii/open-beta/); that does not mean the possessed Shell carries into launch.

### When can I claim The Flayed Harbinger?

Official copy says it is available to claim in the full game **at launch**. The worldwide release date is [August 20, 2026](/mortal-shell-ii/release-date/).
