## What is the Mortal Shell II Open Beta?

The **Mortal Shell II Open Beta** is a free, limited preview of Cold Symmetry and Playstack’s sequel. The official Steam listing describes it as the opening three hours of the game, with select progress carrying forward into the full release.

It is listed as a separate demo/beta application, not as the full game. The official website currently points players to “Play the Beta” alongside pre-order messaging for the August 20, 2026 launch.

This page covers how to play the current Open Beta. It is not a complete game guide, and it does not claim the preview represents the finished campaign.

## When is the Open Beta available?

Checked on 2026-08-11:

- Steam lists the Open Beta with a **June 5, 2026** release date.
- The console beta was reported as going live on **July 31, 2026** for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That timing comes from press coverage of the rollout, not from a dated Xbox or PlayStation store confirmation saved with this Guide.
- An Xbox Store listing for *Mortal Shell II Open Beta* is currently available. Push Square reports a matching free listing on the PlayStation Store.

The Steam Open Beta page still presents the build as available. It does **not** publish a confirmed shutdown date. The full game remains scheduled for **August 20, 2026**. Until Cold Symmetry or Playstack announce otherwise, treat availability as current but time-limited.

## How to play Mortal Shell II beta

No invite code is required on the store listings reviewed for this page. Download the standalone Open Beta, then launch it from your library.

| Platform | How to access |
| --- | --- |
| PC | Download [Mortal Shell II - Open Beta](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4711740/Mortal_Shell_II__Open_Beta/) on Steam |
| PlayStation 5 | Search the PlayStation Store for **Mortal Shell II Open Beta** and install the free listing |
| Xbox Series X\|S | Download [Mortal Shell II Open Beta](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/mortal-shell-ii-open-beta/9N1NPQRFBGTX) from the Xbox Store |

You do not need to pre-purchase the full game to try the preview. The beta and the [full release listing](/mortal-shell-ii/release-date/) are separate store products.

Steam’s Open Beta page also notes that this is an in-development build. Bugs, performance issues, and other rough edges are expected; the listing asks players to report problems through the dedicated Discord channel.

## What content is in the Open Beta?

Official store copy for the current preview is consistent across Steam and Xbox:

- Access to the **opening hours**, from the **Prologue** into the **first explorable region**
- Roughly the first **three hours** of the game, per Steam’s short description
- Possessing fallen **Shells** and beginning the Harbinger’s journey
- Cleansing beacons, optional dungeons, hidden mini-bosses, and challenges
- A route that presses onward toward **Magdalena, the Lady of the Woods**
- Discovery of **Tiel, the Acolyte**, described as one of eight playable Shells in the full game

The Open Beta is therefore an early slice of the interconnected world, not a full-game unlock. Optional dungeons and mini-bosses are mentioned by the store pages; this Guide does not turn that list into an invented map or boss roster.

For combat context around the named encounter, see the current [Magdalena Open Beta guide](/mortal-shell-ii/magdalena/).

## How the Beta relates to the full game

- The full game is scheduled to release worldwide on **August 20, 2026** for PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S.
- The Open Beta is a limited preview of the opening hours, not an Early Access version of the complete campaign.
- Official listings say **select progress** carries forward. They also say beta save progress will **not** carry over in full.

Details about rewards, what resets, and skipping the Prologue belong on the dedicated [beta progress carry over](/mortal-shell-ii/beta-progress-carry-over/) page. Players who progressed **beyond Marrow Keep** during the beta can also use the optional [Prologue skip in the full game](/mortal-shell-ii/skip-prologue/) — see that guide for who qualifies, what the skip changes, and whether to skip.

## FAQ

### Is Mortal Shell II Open Beta free?

Yes. Steam lists it as a free demo/beta. The Xbox listing is also free. PlayStation coverage describes a free PlayStation Store download.

### Do I need a beta key?

The current Steam and Xbox listings do not require a key or waitlist. Search for the Open Beta by name and install it.

### Is the Open Beta the same as Mortal Shell 2 beta?

Yes. Storefronts and press use **Mortal Shell II**, **Mortal Shell 2**, **Open Beta**, and **beta** for the same preview build.

### Does Mortal Shell II beta progress carry over?

Only in a limited way. Official listings unlock a cosmetic reward for playing, and they unlock a Prologue-skip option if you progress beyond the Marrow Keep. Currency, weapons, Shells, collectibles, and other discovered items reset at launch. For the full breakdown, see [does Mortal Shell II beta progress carry over](/mortal-shell-ii/beta-progress-carry-over/).

### Will the Beta stay up until August 20?

That has not been confirmed on the Steam Open Beta page. The preview is still listed as available as of 2026-08-11, and the full game launches on August 20, 2026.
