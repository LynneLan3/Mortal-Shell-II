## When does Mortal Shell II release?

Mortal Shell II is scheduled for worldwide release on **August 20, 2026**.

## Confirmed platforms

The reviewed official and store listings identify these platforms:

- PC via Steam
- PlayStation 5
- Xbox Series X|S

## Developer and publisher

- **Developer:** Cold Symmetry
- **Publisher:** Playstack

The official website and publisher announcement also provide edition and launch context. This page does not turn that context into an unverified price comparison; store pricing can change and is best checked at the source.

A separate free Open Beta is currently listed on Steam, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. See [how to play the Mortal Shell II Open Beta](/mortal-shell-ii/open-beta/).
