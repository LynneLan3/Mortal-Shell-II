## Can you skip the Prologue in Mortal Shell 2?

**Yes, if you earned the unlock during the Open Beta.** It is optional, not a forced start.

Who can skip:

- Players who progressed **beyond the Marrow Keep** during the Mortal Shell II Open Beta
- The skip is an **option** in the full game, including current Devout Edition Advanced Access and the August 20 Standard Edition launch

Who cannot skip, based on current official copy:

- Players who never played the Open Beta
- Players who did not progress **beyond** Marrow Keep

Official listings do not document a later in-game alternative for unlocking the skip after the beta.

## How do you unlock the Prologue skip?

**Progress beyond Marrow Keep during the Mortal Shell II Open Beta.**

Official Steam and Xbox Open Beta listings state the same unlock:

- Progress **beyond the Marrow Keep** during the Open Beta
- The skip is an **option** in the full game, not a required start point
- Your complete Open Beta save does **not** carry over into the full release

The named condition is Marrow Keep progress. Official copy does not list a later Open Beta encounter as the unlock.

A developer-marked Steam answer (August 13, 2026) confirms you will be able to skip the prologue, and that save data still does not transfer. The store listing is more specific about the Marrow Keep requirement, so this Guide treats **beyond the Marrow Keep** as the unlock rule.

Official listings do not publish the exact in-game prompt. They describe it as an option when you start the full game.

For how to download and play the preview, see the [Open Beta guide](/mortal-shell-ii/open-beta/).

## What happens when you skip the Prologue?

Official listings confirm that progressing beyond the Marrow Keep unlocks the **option to skip the Prologue** in the full game. They do not publish a scene-by-scene list of what is omitted.

**Officially confirmed:** the skip is optional, and the unlock condition is progress beyond the Marrow Keep.

**Secondary reporting:** GamesRadar writes that the skip returns you to your first visit to the Keep rather than removing the opening entirely, and that this point comes after the Tar Golem fight. That is reporting around the official Marrow Keep condition, not additional store copy.

This Guide treats “beyond the Marrow Keep” as the rule that matters.

## Do you lose Gloom or currency by skipping?

**Your Open Beta currency does not carry over.** That is separate from using the skip itself.

Official Steam and Xbox Open Beta listings say **currency** resets at launch, along with weapons, Shells, collectibles, and other discovered items. Players looking for “Gloom” should treat that as part of the official **currency** reset: beta Gloom does not transfer into the full game.

Official store copy and the developer-marked Steam answer reviewed for this page **do not** say that using the Prologue skip grants an amount of Gloom or currency equal to playing that section. They also do not say the skip removes extra currency beyond the documented beta reset.

This page will update if Cold Symmetry, Playstack, or an official listing confirms a skip-specific Gloom or currency grant.

## Does your beta inventory carry over?

**No.** The Prologue skip is a launch unlock, not a transferred beta save.

Your Open Beta save, weapons, Shells, collectibles, and other discovered items reset. If you met the Marrow Keep condition, you can use the skip when starting the full game — that is an entitlement, not restored inventory.

For the full reset list, Flayed Harbinger reward, and save-transfer rules, see [Does Mortal Shell 2 beta progress carry over](/mortal-shell-ii/beta-progress-carry-over/).

## Can everyone skip the Prologue?

**No.** Current official copy only documents the Open Beta unlock: progress **beyond the Marrow Keep**.

There is no confirmed post-beta or launch-day alternative on the listings reviewed for this page. If you did not play the Open Beta, or did not go beyond Marrow Keep, this Guide does not treat the skip as available.

## Do you need to beat Magdalena?

**No — Magdalena is not the stated requirement.**

The official condition is progress **beyond Marrow Keep**. Magdalena is a later Open Beta encounter and is not named as the Prologue-skip unlock.

## Does skipping the Prologue affect achievements?

That is currently not confirmed. Official listings and the developer Steam answer reviewed for this page do not say whether using the skip blocks, grants, or changes achievements.

There is not enough reliable official confirmation for a yes or no. This page will update when official copy is clear.

## What else do you get from the Open Beta?

Playing the Open Beta also unlocks **The Flayed Harbinger**, an exclusive cosmetic you can claim in the full game at launch. Reward details, the reset list, and save-transfer rules belong on the [beta progress carry-over](/mortal-shell-ii/beta-progress-carry-over/) page.

## FAQ

### How do I skip the prologue in Mortal Shell 2?

Progress beyond Marrow Keep during the Open Beta to unlock the optional Prologue skip in the full game. Your full beta save does not transfer.

### Do I need to reach Marrow Keep?

You need to progress **beyond** the Marrow Keep. That is the wording on the official Steam and Xbox Open Beta listings. Arriving at the Keep without going beyond it is not the stated condition.

### Do I lose Gloom if I skip the Prologue?

Open Beta currency, including Gloom, does **not** carry over — that reset is official. Official copy does not currently confirm a skip-specific Gloom grant or extra Gloom loss in the full game.

### Does my beta save carry over?

No. The Prologue skip is a launch unlock, not a transferred save. See the [beta progress carry-over](/mortal-shell-ii/beta-progress-carry-over/) page for the reset rules.

### Is the Prologue skip mandatory?

No. Official listings describe it as an **option** to skip the Prologue.

### Can I unlock the Prologue skip after the beta?

That is currently not confirmed. Official copy only documents the unlock from Open Beta progress beyond Marrow Keep. No post-beta or launch-day alternative is stated on the listings reviewed for this page.
