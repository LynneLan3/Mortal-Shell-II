## How do you unlock the Prologue skip in Mortal Shell 2?

**Progress beyond Marrow Keep during the Mortal Shell II Open Beta to unlock the optional Prologue skip in the full game. Your full beta save does not transfer.**

Official Steam and Xbox Open Beta listings state the same unlock:

- Progress **beyond the Marrow Keep** during the Open Beta
- The skip is an **option** in the full game, not a required start point
- Your complete Open Beta save does **not** carry over into the full release

The named condition is Marrow Keep progress. Official copy does not list a later Open Beta encounter as the unlock.

For how to download and play the preview, see the [Open Beta guide](/mortal-shell-ii/open-beta/).

## What does the Prologue skip actually skip?

Official listings confirm that progressing beyond the Marrow Keep unlocks the **option to skip the Prologue** in the full game. They do not publish a scene-by-scene list of what is omitted.

**Officially confirmed:** the skip is optional, and the unlock condition is progress beyond the Marrow Keep.

**Secondary reporting:** GamesRadar writes that the skip returns you to your first visit to the Keep rather than removing the opening entirely, and that this point comes after the Tar Golem fight. That is reporting around the official Marrow Keep condition, not additional store copy.

This Guide treats “beyond the Marrow Keep” as the rule that matters.

## Do you need to beat Magdalena?

**No — Magdalena is not the stated requirement.**

The official condition is progress **beyond Marrow Keep**. Magdalena is a later Open Beta encounter and is not named as the Prologue-skip unlock.

## Does beta progress need to carry over?

No. Treat the Prologue skip as a **launch unlock**, not restored beta progression.

Your Open Beta save itself does not carry over. If you met the Marrow Keep condition during the beta, the skip is an entitlement you can use when starting the full game — it is not a transferred save file.

For what resets and what else carries forward, see [Does Mortal Shell 2 beta progress carry over](/mortal-shell-ii/beta-progress-carry-over/).

## What else do you get from the Open Beta?

Playing the Open Beta also unlocks **The Flayed Harbinger**, an exclusive cosmetic you can claim in the full game at launch. Reward details, the reset list, and save-transfer rules belong on the [beta progress carry-over](/mortal-shell-ii/beta-progress-carry-over/) page.

## FAQ

### How do I skip the prologue in Mortal Shell 2?

Progress beyond Marrow Keep during the Open Beta to unlock the optional Prologue skip in the full game. Your full beta save does not transfer.

### Do I need to reach Marrow Keep?

You need to progress **beyond** the Marrow Keep. That is the wording on the official Steam and Xbox Open Beta listings. Arriving at the Keep without going beyond it is not the stated condition.

### Do I need to beat Magdalena?

No. Magdalena is not the stated requirement. The official condition is progress beyond Marrow Keep.

### Does my beta save carry over?

No. The Prologue skip is a launch unlock, not a transferred save. See the [beta progress carry-over](/mortal-shell-ii/beta-progress-carry-over/) page for the reset rules.

### Is the Prologue skip mandatory?

No. Official listings describe it as an **option** to skip the Prologue.

### Can I unlock the Prologue skip after the beta?

That is currently not confirmed. Official copy only documents the unlock from Open Beta progress beyond Marrow Keep. No post-beta or launch-day alternative is stated on the listings reviewed for this page.
