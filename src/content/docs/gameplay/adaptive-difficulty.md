## Quick Answer

Adaptive Difficulty is an **Experimental** toggle added in the Sep. 5 update. It defaults to **OFF** and can be enabled or disabled in **Game > Game Settings > Adaptive Difficulty**. When enabled, it raises enemy damage, health, and break resistance after sustained success and lowers them after repeated deaths. See the [Sep. 5 update](/mortal-shell-ii/updates/september-5-update/) for the official context.

## How to Turn Adaptive Difficulty On or Off

1. Pause the game and open the Game menu.
2. Go to **Game > Game Settings**.
3. Find **Adaptive Difficulty** and toggle it ON or OFF.

It is optional and defaults to OFF. If you cannot find it, confirm that the Sep. 5 update is installed; see the [Updates hub](/mortal-shell-ii/updates/).

## How Adaptive Difficulty Works

Officially confirmed behavior:

- Sustained winning increases enemy damage, health, and break resistance.
- Sustained deaths reduce those values and limit the maximum share of player health a single attack can remove.
- Difficulty shifts are gradual and move back toward normal balance over time.
- The announcement does not provide exact multipliers, numeric caps, or a complete interaction table for every mode or build.

## Does It Go Above Normal Difficulty?

Yes. Sustained success can push enemies beyond the game's original highest standard difficulty. The update does not describe infinite scaling or publish a numeric ceiling, so the exact upper limit is unknown.

## Does Adaptive Difficulty Make the Game Easier?

It can after repeated deaths: enemy damage, health, and break resistance reduce, and one attack is limited in how much of your health it can remove. The system is bidirectional, so continued success can raise the challenge again.

## Adaptive Difficulty and Night Mode

Night Mode is a separate challenge state. The official announcement says Night Mode does **not** become easier when Adaptive Difficulty scales downward. Do not treat Adaptive Difficulty as a way to remove Night Mode's baseline challenge.

## Break Resistance and Parries

When the system scales upward, enemy break resistance increases. Some players report that this changes the feel of parry- or break-focused play, but that is an observation rather than an official multiplier or universal result.

## Should You Enable It?

Editorial guidance:

- Enable it if you want a changing challenge that responds to performance, including an experienced or NG+ run.
- Keep it OFF for fixed, repeatable difficulty, build testing, speedrun practice, or controlled comparisons.
- Keep expectations qualitative: the official announcement does not publish exact enemy values or guaranteed win/death thresholds.

These are recommendations, not additional official mechanics.

## FAQ

### Is Adaptive Difficulty on by default?

No. It defaults to **OFF**.

### How do I toggle it?

Use **Game > Game Settings > Adaptive Difficulty**.

### Will sustained winning make the game harder?

Yes. Sustained success increases enemy damage, health, and break resistance.

### Will repeated deaths make it easier?

Yes. Those values reduce and one attack is limited in how much of your health it can remove.

### Does it interact with Night Mode?

Night Mode does not become easier when Adaptive Difficulty scales downward.

### Can it exceed the normal maximum?

Yes. It can exceed the original highest standard difficulty after sustained success, but exact caps and multipliers are not published.
