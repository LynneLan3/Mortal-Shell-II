## Where is the Axatana in Mortal Shell 2?

The **Axatana** is in the **Forgotten Tower**.

In the launch build covered here, the early pickup route starts from **Marrow Keep Beacon**, continues through the **Outskirts of Mammon**, passes **Castigator's Keep**, and ends inside the Forgotten Tower. This is not a Prologue weapon and it is not handed out by an NPC.

Use these landmarks:

- **Marrow Keep Beacon**: the supported starting point for this early route
- **Outskirts of Mammon**: the first major overworld stretch after the south-side shortcut
- **Castigator's Keep**: a mid-route landmark on the showcased path
- **Forgotten Tower**: the final area containing the weapon pickup

For broader combat, Shell, and exploration context, see the [Mortal Shell II gameplay guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gameplay/). For another early dual-wield option in Fainweald, see the [Axe & Dagger location guide](/mortal-shell-ii/axe-dagger/).

## Can you get the Axatana early?

**Yes, but early has conditions.**

You can reach the Axatana relatively early once you have access to **Marrow Keep Beacon** and the south-side route. Current gameplay evidence supports that route, but it does **not** support these stronger claims:

- not during the **Prologue**
- not immediately after starting a **New Game**
- not at the literal first minute of play

Creator titles such as “FROM THE START” or “Right from the Start” are marketing language, not in-game UI facts. Joe Hammer Gaming's transcript also notes the pickup is **not immediately out of the gate**, even though it can be done before the first dungeon with enough early beacon progress.

If the route feels too dangerous, the optional [Slayer Seal](/mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/) in Marrow Keep is one way to make early combat easier. That is a player-choice recommendation, not a requirement for the weapon itself.

## How to get the Axatana from Marrow Keep

This is the compressed route supported by the current gameplay footage and transcript evidence:

1. Once you have access to **Marrow Keep Beacon**, start there.
2. From the top of Marrow Keep, unlock the **south-side teleporter / shortcut** and travel to the **Outskirts of Mammon**.
3. From the Outskirts beacon, run the main path **straight ahead** through the broken alcove and across the bridge. Sprint past or dive through the early enemies rather than stopping to farm them.
4. Use the route's **jump / teleport gates** carefully. Stop in front of each gate when needed, face the target, then jump through.
5. Continue past the **gates**, **ramps**, and **left-hand bridge** sections until the route reaches **Castigator's Keep**, then keep pushing toward the **Forgotten Tower**.
6. Enter the **Forgotten Tower**, go **left up the stairwell**, turn **right** through the **broken wall**, and continue through the next gate / teleporter section.
7. At the final gated room, interact with the **switch / button** beside the door to open it.
8. Enter the room, deal with the **strong enemy** if needed, and pick up the **Axatana** on-site.

## Outskirts of Mammon route

The early route uses the in-game location name **Outskirts of Mammon**.

After the Marrow Keep south-side shortcut, fast travel or teleport to this beacon and treat it as the main running segment. From here, the supported path is a long sprint through enemy pressure, broken stonework, bridges, and multiple jump / teleport gates.

This section is enemy-heavy. The checked transcripts recommend sprinting through, healing if you get hit, and using dive / dodge to avoid one-shot pressure rather than clearing every pack.

## How to reach the Forgotten Tower

After the Outskirts of Mammon stretch, the route continues through gate sections, ramps, bridges, and more teleport jumps. The showcased gameplay also passes **Castigator's Keep** before the final tower approach.

The Forgotten Tower also has a separate Night Mode objective: after collecting Depleted, Thawed, and Faded Gland, you can offer all three at the top of the tower for [Seedbearer's Scripture](/mortal-shell-ii/seedbearers-scripture/). This is separate from the Axatana pickup.

Inside the Forgotten Tower, both checked transcripts agree on the same late-route shape:

1. Enter the tower and go **left** up the stairwell.
2. Turn **right** toward the **broken wall / broken area**.
3. Continue through the next **teleporter / gate** section.
4. Reach the final **gated room** with the nearby **switch / button**.

Joe Hammer Gaming's transcript notes the final gate may start closed and must be opened from the side switch. NewDengs shows the same door-side button setup before the pickup room.

## How to get the Axatana inside the Forgotten Tower

The weapon is a **direct pickup** in the final room. It is not an NPC reward, quest payout, or chest unlock shown in the current evidence.

What the footage supports:

1. Use the **switch / button** beside the final door.
2. Enter the room behind the gate.
3. A **strong enemy** is present in that room.
4. Pick up the **Axatana** on-site.
5. The game UI displays **`NEW WEAPON ACQUIRED — AXATANA`**.

The inventory screen in the checked Gamer Guru footage also confirms the weapon name as **AXATANA** and shows that copy equipped at **+5**. Treat that upgrade level as **observed in this footage**, not as a guaranteed drop state for every player.

## Do you have to defeat the enemy?

**Not confirmed.**

- NewDengs clears the room enemy before picking up the weapon.
- Joe Hammer Gaming says he is **not entirely certain** whether defeating that enemy is strictly required.

For safety, most players should assume the room is dangerous and prepare to fight or run cleanly past the enemy. This page does **not** state a confirmed hard requirement to kill the guardian before the pickup appears.

## Is the Axatana worth getting early?

It is a legitimate early weapon detour once the Marrow Keep south-side route is open.

What the evidence supports:

- It is a named weapon you can pick up before much of Mammon is fully explored.
- Launch creators built dedicated early-location routes for it.
- The checked Gamer Guru footage shows the pickup and inventory confirmation clearly.

What is **creator opinion**, not game UI fact:

- “best weapon in the game”
- “ overpowered”
- “FROM THE START” timing claims

NewDengs describes it as strong for **dual wielding** and shows the moveset after pickup, but those are creator impressions from the video, not official weapon-tier documentation. If the Forgotten Tower gauntlet is too punishing, come back after more beacon progress or after picking up easier early tools such as the [Slayer Seal](/mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/).

## FAQ

### Where is the Axatana?

In the **Forgotten Tower**, reached from the early Marrow Keep route through the **Outskirts of Mammon**.

### Can you get the Axatana early?

Yes, relatively early once you have access to **Marrow Keep Beacon** and the south-side route, but not during the Prologue and not immediately from a fresh New Game.

### Where should I start?

**Marrow Keep Beacon.**

### Does the route go through the Outskirts of Mammon?

Yes. The in-game UI confirms that location name on the supported route.

### Do I have to kill the enemy in the final room?

Not confirmed. NewDengs defeats the enemy first; Joe Hammer Gaming did not verify whether the kill is mandatory.

### Is the Axatana +5?

The Gamer Guru footage used for this guide shows an acquired / equipped Axatana at **+5**, but that is not proof every player or state will always show +5 on pickup.
