## Quick Answer

- **Weapon:** Axe & Dagger (also listed as Axe and Dagger), a dual-wield axe-and-dagger pair.
- **Launch location:** pedestal / slab at the end of the **Shrine of Trials**.
- **Nearest Beacon:** Mushroom Village Beacon.
- **Required key:** yes — the **Chapel Key** (older guides still call this the Mushroom Village Key).
- **Puzzle:** face the king statue, then move backwards into the illusory wall; after that, park sliding stone blocks on pressure plates.
- **Beta warning:** Open Beta pages that put the weapon on a table inside **Mushroom Village Gate Beacon** do not match the current launch route.

## Where is the Axe & Dagger in Mortal Shell 2?

In the **2026-08-17 / 2026-08-18 launch and Advanced Access build**, Axe & Dagger is an early dual-wield weapon inside the **Shrine of Trials** dungeon.

That shrine is not a separate overworld pin you walk up to. It sits **inside the locked chapel** by **Mushroom Village Beacon** in Fainweald. The chapel door is chained shut until you have the Chapel Key.

You do not need a specific Shell, named boss kill, or extra ability just to pick it up. You do need Mushroom Village. Merrick in Marrow Keep sells an optional 50-gold cloth map that marks the weapon; skip it if you are already heading to the chapel.

If this part of Fainweald is still fogged, use the [map fragment guide](/mortal-shell-ii/map-fragments/). For Beacon travel, see the [gameplay overview](/mortal-shell-ii/gameplay/).

## Did the Axe & Dagger location change from the Open Beta?

**Yes. Do not follow late Open Beta weapon pages that send you to Mushroom Village Gate Beacon.**

The still-visible Fextralife weapon page (last edited **16 July 2026**) sends players southeast to **Mushroom Village Gate Beacon** and a **round table** inside that beacon. That is preview-build documentation.

Launch / Advanced Access coverage puts the weapon at the **end of Shrine of Trials**, behind the Chapel Key door, on a **pedestal / slab** after the statue riddle and pressure plates.

The key pickup is the same shape as preview coverage (bonfire, illusory arch, statue rope). The **weapon’s resting place** is what changed. If a June/July video still says “it’s on the table in the Gate Beacon,” that is why you are in the wrong room.

## Where to find the Chapel Key

**Nearest Beacon:** Mushroom Village Beacon.

**Landmark:** a large bonfire / pyre with dancing enemies (often described as witches or old women; one of them plays music). Current location notes also place this stretch northeast of the Ritual Grounds dungeon.

The Chapel Key is **not** sitting on the chapel door. It is a hidden statue pickup. Launch item text calls it Chapel Key; GamesRadar’s **12 August 2026** article and older wiki pages still say **Mushroom Village Key**. Treat those as the same key. It is **not** the separate **Cage Key** sold by the nearby Hunter.

1. Start at **Mushroom Village Beacon**.
2. Leave on the **right / east** path rather than the Hunter-chapel side.
3. Descend to the lower level and **cross the rope bridge** toward the loud bonfire.
4. Clear the dancing enemies around the pyre if they are in the way (not required to spawn the key, but it makes the arch readable).
5. Find the **stone archway** that looks out of place beside the pyre. Look **through** it toward the fire — a statue that is not visible from the other side should appear.
6. Pass through the arch from the side with the steps / from the side that reveals the statue.
7. Take the Chapel Key from the statue (it hangs on the rope the statue holds).

If you reach the arch and see no statue, you are on the wrong face of the portal. Turn around and walk through from the other side instead of circling the stonework.

## How to reach the Shrine of Trials

**Shrine of Trials** is the dungeon behind the chapel door. The chapel is still anchored on **Mushroom Village Beacon**, on the opposite side from the bonfire.

1. Return to **Mushroom Village Beacon**.
2. Take the **west / left** path through the short passage.
3. You should enter the Hunter clearing: Grisha Hunter NPCs, a caged Grisha Pup, and a **dilapidated chapel** with a **chained door** behind them.
4. Interact with that chapel door while the Chapel Key is in your inventory. You do not equip the key.
5. Enter. Current launch wiki notes a **lift down** into the shrine interior; other launch writeups start you in the first hall with a statue and a handful of enemies.

You are now in the shrine. The next room is the riddle, not the weapon.

## How to solve the Shrine of Trials puzzle

There are **three** puzzle beats after the door: the king-statue riddle, a one-block pressure plate, then a two-block pressure plate. After that, a knight guards the pickup.

Clear enemies before you start sliding blocks. Cultists in the first hall can heal each other, so interrupt the staff-raise if a fight starts dragging.

### 1. Gaze Upon Your King (illusory wall)

1. In the statue hall, interact with the plaque. Current launch quotes are: **“Gaze upon your king so true. Back and back you shall pass through.”**
2. Stand so you are **looking at the statue’s face**. Do not turn statues, and do not start hitting walls.
3. Move **backwards** toward the wall opposite the statue while keeping the statue in view.
4. That far wall is illusory. It yields when you back into it. If walking back does not open it, **dodge backwards twice** while still facing the statue — that input matches the “back and back” line in launch footage writeups.

Success: the wall opens into the next puzzle room.

### 2. First stone-block room (one plate)

1. Clear the wraiths.
2. Find one sliding stone block and one floor plate. Standing on the plate yourself opens the door, but it **closes when you step off**.
3. Read the **grooves on the floor** around the block. They show the only legal slide directions.
4. Hit the block along those grooves until it rests on the plate (one launch sequence: hit it forward, then onto the plate to the left).
5. Leave the block on the plate so the doorway stays open.

### 3. Second stone-block room (two plates)

Launch guides agree on the **goal** more tightly than on one universal hit order: both plates must stay pressed at the same time.

1. Clear the remaining enemies.
2. Identify two sliding blocks and two plates.
3. Slide each block along its floor grooves onto a plate.
4. If a wide light attack shoves **both** blocks, switch to a **heavy attack** so only one moves.
5. One documented launch sequence: move the left block left, then forward, then onto the right-hand path; put the right block onto the first plate; then slide the first block back onto the second plate.

Success: the last shrine door stays open.

Current sources do not describe a dedicated reset lever or a punishment for a wrong slide. If a block is off-path, keep hitting it along the grooves until both plates are occupied.

## What does "Gaze Upon Your King" mean?

It is an instruction for **your facing**, not a lore quiz and not a multi-statue rotation puzzle.

- **King** here is the **statue with the plaque** in the first shrine hall. Launch guides do not require you to know a named lore king to solve it.
- The object that must “gaze” is **you**. Keep looking at that statue.
- The action is **back up** (walk or dodge) into the wall opposite the statue.
- Success is visual: that wall becomes an opening. Hitting walls or standing on random floor tiles is the wrong read.

There is only one gaze statue for this riddle. The later rooms are pressure plates, not extra kings.

## How to get the Axe & Dagger after solving the puzzle

1. Go through the last opened door.
2. Fight the knight / mini-boss in that room (launch writeups call it a spear- or two-handed-weapon knight).
3. Interact with the **pedestal / slab** in the same room.
4. Claim **Axe & Dagger**.

The weapon does not drop from the knight and does not appear back at the chapel door. If you solved the plates and still have no weapon, you are not in the final room yet.

After pickup, you can leave the dungeon. Current shrine notes treat that as enough to mark the dungeon complete on the map.

## Is the Axe & Dagger worth getting early?

Yes, if you want a **faster dual-wield moveset** than the heavier starters. Launch roundups treat it as an early Mushroom Village detour: axe in one hand, dagger in the other, good at weaving in and out, and a common pairing for an early Tiel run. No late-game upgrade is required first.

If the shrine enemies are deleting you, run a nearby [Gloom farm loop](/mortal-shell-ii/gloom-farm/) and come back. The dungeon is short.

Current launch guides do **not** document a permanent miss for the Chapel Key, shrine, or Axe & Dagger. Access is gated by reaching Mushroom Village. Treat it as **progression-gated, not proven permanently missable**.

## FAQ

### Where is the Axe & Dagger in Mortal Shell 2?

On a pedestal at the end of the Shrine of Trials, inside the locked chapel by Mushroom Village Beacon.

### Where is the Chapel Key?

East / right of Mushroom Village Beacon, at the bonfire. Walk through the illusory arch and take it from the statue’s rope.

### Where is the Shrine of Trials?

Behind the chained chapel door on the west / Hunter side of the same beacon.

### How do you solve the Shrine of Trials puzzle?

Face the king statue and back into the opposite wall, then park stone blocks on the pressure plates. A knight guards the final pedestal.

### What does Gaze Upon Your King mean?

Keep looking at the plaque statue and move backwards. The far wall is fake. You are not rotating multiple statues.

### Did the Axe & Dagger location change after the beta?

Yes. Gate Beacon table pickups are preview-build. Launch coverage puts it in Shrine of Trials.

### Can you get Axe & Dagger early?

Yes, as soon as Mushroom Village is reachable. No special Shell is required.

### Is Axe & Dagger missable?

Current launch guides do not list a permanent miss. The village can be gated by progression, but no point of no return is documented.
