Balance Patch 1 is the August 20, 2026 economy and combat rebalance. For the newer post-launch changes — renewable Glimpses and Tarcores, Shell respec, and Fragile Tarstones — see the [Week 1 Update](/mortal-shell-ii/updates/week-1-update/). For all patches in one place, use the [Updates hub](/mortal-shell-ii/updates/).

Balance Patch 1 moves Shell-map reveals from Glimpses to Gloom, refunds Glimpses previously spent on those map reveals in Blackmarrow, cuts Tarforge weapon smelting cost by 75%, rebalances several enemies and bosses, and buffs multiple weapons and Tarstones. The official [Balance Patch 1 notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2584270/discussions/0/582805931178489108/) are the source for the changes below.

## What should you do first after Balance Patch 1?

There are three immediate changes worth checking:

1. **Collect your refunded Glimpses in Blackmarrow.** If you spent Glimpses on Shell-map reveals before the patch, that spending is now refunded at the Shell Keeper's alcove.
2. **Recheck the Tarforge if you want to change upgrades.** Smelt Equipment now costs 75% less Gloom.
3. **Revisit weapons affected by the patch.** Great Martyr's Blade and Troubadour's Lute received direct damage buffs, while Axatana, Black Needle, and Clockwork Scythe received improved light-attack tracking.

For the resource change itself, see the [Mortal Shell 2 Glimpses guide](/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/). For the upgrade station, use the [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/).

## How to collect your refunded Glimpses

The refund applies specifically to **Glimpses previously spent on Shell-map reveals**.

Go to the **Shell Keeper's alcove in Blackmarrow** to collect the refunded Glimpses. After Balance Patch 1, Shell-map reveals use **Gloom instead of Glimpses**, so future map reveals no longer consume this part of your Glimpse supply.

If you are still looking for individual Shell routes, use the [Shells guide](/mortal-shell-ii/shells/) rather than spending time retracing already documented paths.

## Economy changes: Gloom, Gold and Tarforge smelting

Balance Patch 1 changes three parts of the launch economy.

### Shell maps now cost Gloom

Shell-map reveals now use **Gloom instead of Glimpses**. Glimpses previously spent on those map reveals are refunded in Blackmarrow.

### Gold drops increased

Enemy Gold drops were increased globally, and **Mammon enemies now drop twice as much Gold**.

### Tarforge smelting is 75% cheaper

The Gloom cost of **Smelt Equipment** at the Tarforge was reduced by **75%**.

That makes the Foundry Stone's Smelt Equipment function more practical when you want to recover weapon or sidearm upgrade materials. The [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/) covers the five parts that unlock and extend the station.

If you need a separate route for ordinary Gloom income, use the [Gloom farming guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gloom-farm/).

## Boss and enemy nerfs

Two named bosses received direct damage or health reductions.

### [The Lost Child](/mortal-shell-ii/lost-child/)

- Base Damage reduced by **10%**
- Maximum Health reduced by **15%**

### Monolith

- Base Damage reduced by **19%**
- Maximum Health reduced by **10%**
- Some attack timings were adjusted

Several regular enemies also received health reductions:

- **Mace Cultist:** HP reduced by 21.1%
- **Caerinid Spider:** HP reduced by 33.3%
- **Infested Stalker:** HP reduced by 52.4%
- **Rusted Knave:** HP reduced by 20.8%

Rusted Knave also received a fix for its overhead hit detection.

The official notes also say that enemies featured in the beta were restored to their beta HP values, except the Caerinid Spider in Magdalena's area. The named percentages above are the explicit listed changes; the broader beta-HP statement should not be read as a promise that every enemy has the same reduction or that every launch behavior matches the beta.

## Weapon buffs

Several weapons and sidearms were changed in Balance Patch 1.

- **Great Martyr's Blade:** attack damage increased by **20%**
- **Troubadour's Lute:** attack damage increased by **100%**
- [**Caged Hystrix**](/mortal-shell-ii/caged-hystrix/): minimum Resolve requirement to fire removed
- **Triarch Repeater:** minimum Resolve requirement to fire removed
- **Axatana:** light-attack tracking improved
- [**Black Needle**](/mortal-shell-ii/black-needle/): light-attack tracking improved
- [**Clockwork Scythe**](/mortal-shell-ii/clockwork-scythe/): light-attack tracking improved

Players have debated whether these changes make the game easier or simply rebalance underused tools. That is community sentiment, not a verified mechanical claim.

Use the [Weapons guide](/mortal-shell-ii/weapons/) for the current weapon index and the [Sidearms guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sidearms/) for the Sidearm roster. The [Great Martyr's Blade guide](/mortal-shell-ii/great-martyrs-blade/) and [Axatana guide](/mortal-shell-ii/axatana/) cover their individual acquisition routes.

## Tarstone and Guard changes

Five [Tarstones](/mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/) were buffed:

- Parasitic Stone
- Grudge Stone
- Bulwark Stone
- Clerik's Stone
- Squall Stone

The supplied patch information confirms the buffs but does not provide exact percentages, so this page does not assign numbers to them.

**Guard can also now be used while walking.**
