The **Ballistazooka** is awarded after you defeat the **Subjugated Guardian** at the bottom of **Sentry's Grave**. Start from the **Gate of Mammon** area, find the stairs / doorway / portal into Sentry's Grave inside the Lonesome Spire route, descend through the dungeon, and collect the sidearm after the fight. The route sources use slightly different names for the entrance transition, so follow the named landmarks rather than a map coordinate.

## Route from Gate of Mammon to Sentry's Grave

Use this conservative route once the Mammon area is open:

1. Reach **Gate of Mammon Beacon**.
2. Follow the nearby stairs into the fort / Lonesome Spire interior. Depending on the route description, you may be looking for stairs, a doorway, or a portal leading to the dungeon.
3. Enter **Sentry's Grave** and keep descending. The current guides describe the dungeon as largely linear.
4. Expect repeated **Ballista Head / crossbowhead** enemies. Their ranged fire can make the approach more dangerous than the pickup itself.
5. Continue to the lowest section and enter the **Subjugated Guardian** arena.

AllThingsHow describes the entrance as stairs down inside Lonesome Spire. Thumb Wars describes two stair flights, a heavy knight and archers, then a right-side doorway. Nerd's Chalk calls the transition a portal from Lonesome Spire. These are conflicting descriptions of the local route, not evidence for a fixed coordinate or universal turn count.

## Defeat the Subjugated Guardian

You do not need the Ballistazooka for this fight—the Guardian is the encounter that awards it.

1. Bring a melee weapon and the Shell abilities you normally use for boss fights.
2. At the arena, watch for the two elevated ranged enemies reported on the side ledges. Use the stairs on either side to reach them, or reposition so their shots are less likely to cross your path.
3. Close distance on the Subjugated Guardian when it is safe. One current guide reports that this reduces its ranged pressure and leaves head-slam attacks that can be blocked or parried.
4. If bolts are active, use short attack strings and reset instead of committing to a long combo.
5. After the Guardian dies, collect or confirm the **Ballistazooka** reward before leaving.

The sources differ on whether the Guardian's bolts should be described as explosive or simply heavy ranged attacks. The shared practical advice is to avoid standing in the firing lane, remove the surrounding ranged pressure, and fight at close range when you have an opening.

## What the Ballistazooka does

The Ballistazooka is a **heavy ranged sidearm**. It is best used to open an encounter, punish an enemy after it commits to an attack, or finish a weakened target from a safer distance. Treat it as a deliberate ranged complement to your primary melee weapon, not as a fast weapon to fire continuously while moving.

Current guides identify Tarstones as part of sidearm customization and describe sidearms as upgradeable through the Tarforge system. The local [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/) identifies the **Obsidian Lathe** as the component that unlocks sidearm enhancement at Franz's Tarforge in Marrow Keep.

No reliable cross-checked stat sheet establishes a universal damage value, reload time, ammunition capacity, range, cost, or best Shell pairing for this weapon. Use the weapon's role—slow, deliberate ranged pressure—to decide whether it fits your build.

## Ballistazooka trophies and achievements

The official Steam achievement list includes:

- **Speared** — unlock the Ballistazooka.
- **Guns. Lots of Guns** — unlock all Sidearms.

The Ballistazooka's individual achievement is tied to the unlock. The checked sources do not establish an extra kill count, Tarforge upgrade, or separate quest requirement. For the eight-sidearm checklist, use the [Sidearms guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sidearms/). For the wider completion route, see [Trophies and Achievements](/mortal-shell-ii/trophies/).

## Patch notes and route warnings

Cold Symmetry's August 20, 2026 **Balance Patch 1** lists changes to other weapons, removes minimum Resolve requirements from Caged Hystrix and Triarch Repeater, and changes the economy and enemies. The checked official weapon section does not list a direct Ballistazooka damage, cost, or behavior change. That is a dated patch snapshot, not a guarantee about every later build or platform.

The patch also changes broader Mammon economy details, including increased gold drops. Do not turn that into a Ballistazooka-specific price claim. Check the current in-game weapon and Tarforge menus if your build differs from the launch window. See the local [Balance Patch 1 guide](/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/) for the wider note.

## Common mistakes

- Searching Sentry's Grave's upper approach for a loose weapon pickup. The Ballistazooka is tied to the bottom Guardian encounter.
- Treating one source's stairs, doorway, or portal wording as a universal map instruction.
- Trying to use the Ballistazooka before defeating the Guardian.
- Ignoring the ranged enemies on the arena ledges while focusing only on the boss.
- Publishing or relying on an exact damage, reload, ammunition, or reward number that the checked sources do not consistently support.
- Assuming the reported Gloom / Glimpse reward is a fixed universal amount. The weapon reward is the stable fact; currency details differ between guides.

After the Guardian falls, verify the Ballistazooka in your sidearm inventory and check whether **Speared** has triggered.
