The **Black Needle** is a long-reaching primary spear found in **Sester's Bastion** in Mammon. Start from the **Sester's Gate Beacon**, solve the dungeon's **five-bell puzzle** by hitting the bells with a Sidearm to raise stone walkways, then complete the final Sester encounter to obtain the weapon.

## Where is the Black Needle?

The Black Needle is in **Sester's Bastion**, reached from the **Sester's Gate Beacon** in Mammon.

The acquisition route has two important progression steps:

1. solve the five-bell walkway puzzle
2. complete the final encounter involving the **Twin Sesters / Stray Sester**

The Black Needle is obtained after that supported final encounter.

## How to reach Sester's Gate

Use the **Sester's Gate Beacon** as the route anchor and continue into **Sester's Bastion**.

This page keeps its scope on the dungeon and weapon route rather than inventing an exact Ova threshold or another unsupported access requirement.

For the site's wider Beacon coverage, use the [Beacons and fast travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).

## How the five-bell puzzle works

Sester's Bastion uses **five bells** to control stone walkways.

Hit the bells **from range with a Sidearm**. Activating the bells raises the walkways that let you continue through the dungeon.

The important search problem is usually not understanding the mechanic; it is finding the bell that was missed.

## Where to find all five bells

The supported bell layout breaks down like this:

### First section

- One bell is on the **left**.
- A second bell is **higher on the right wall**.

### Deeper bridge room

The remaining **three bells** are in the deeper bridge-room section.

One of them is positioned **below the bridge** and is particularly easy to miss. If you have activated the obvious bells but the route still has not opened as expected, check below bridge level before retracing the entire dungeon.

This guide intentionally avoids pretending the bells have coordinate-perfect positions. Use the left / high-right / bridge-room structure to scan each section methodically.

## Twin Sesters and Stray Sester boss route

After the bell puzzle opens the route forward, continue to the final encounter involving the **Twin Sesters / Stray Sester**.

Completing that supported encounter is part of the Black Needle acquisition route.

This page does not expand the fight into an unsupported boss strategy. The search intent here is reaching the weapon: solve the bells, continue through the Bastion, complete the Sester encounter, and claim the Black Needle.

## How to get the Black Needle

Once you have completed the five-bell progression and the final Sester encounter, the **Black Needle** becomes obtainable.

It is a **primary melee spear** with long reach. Its weapon ability is called **Needle Storm**.

For upgrading primary weapons, see the [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/). The [Weapons guide](/mortal-shell-ii/weapons/) covers the site's broader launch weapon index.

## What Balance Patch 1 changed for Black Needle

[Balance Patch 1](/mortal-shell-ii/updates/balance-patch-1/) improved **Black Needle light-attack tracking**.

That is the confirmed Patch 1 change this guide uses. It does not infer additional changes to damage, range, scaling, or Needle Storm.
