**Caged Hystrix** is a Sidearm found near the **Chamber of Becoming exit** after completing the Sariel route from **Silent Steps**. It is a separate manual pickup: after the Sariel reward / Memory sequence, head up the rear / exit stairs and check the final exit area before leaving the dungeon.

## Where is Caged Hystrix?

Caged Hystrix is in the **Chamber of Becoming** in Mammon.

It is on the same broader route as **Sariel** and the **Clockwork Scythe**, but it is not the same reward.

The important distinction is that Caged Hystrix must be collected separately near the dungeon exit.

## What do you need to do before Caged Hystrix appears?

Complete the Sariel route through the Chamber of Becoming first.

In the final arena, Sariel's revival mechanic is tied to **four stone tablets / revival markers**. Destroy them before the permanent defeat.

After that sequence, the route gives access to the Sariel / Clockwork rewards and continues toward the exit where Caged Hystrix is found.

For the full revival and Shell route, use the [Sariel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sariel/).

## Where to go after defeating Sariel

After the Sariel Memory / Shell sequence, continue toward the **rear / exit stairs**.

Head upward toward the dungeon exit and check the final exit area instead of leaving immediately.

Caged Hystrix is a manual pickup near that route.

## How to avoid missing Caged Hystrix

The easiest mistake is assuming the Sidearm came with the other Sariel rewards.

It does not.

Treat the Chamber rewards as three separate objectives:

- Sariel's Shell
- [Clockwork Scythe](/mortal-shell-ii/clockwork-scythe/)
- **Caged Hystrix**

After finishing the first two parts of the reward sequence, continue up the exit stairs and look for the Caged Hystrix pickup before leaving.

The [Sidearms guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sidearms/) tracks Caged Hystrix as one of the eight launch Sidearms.

## What Balance Patch 1 changed for Caged Hystrix

[Balance Patch 1](/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/) removed the **minimum Resolve requirement to fire Caged Hystrix**.

That is the confirmed Patch 1 change used by this guide. It does not imply a new damage value, rate of fire, scaling curve, or upgrade table.

## How to unlock the Spiked trophy

Acquiring **Caged Hystrix** is associated with the **Spiked** trophy / achievement.

The practical completion step is therefore the same as the location objective: finish the Sariel route and remember to collect the Sidearm near the Chamber exit.

## Caged Hystrix FAQ

### Where is Caged Hystrix?

Near the **Chamber of Becoming exit** after the Sariel route.

### Does Sariel directly give you Caged Hystrix?

No. Caged Hystrix is a separate pickup farther along the exit route.

### Is it the same reward as Clockwork Scythe?

No. Clockwork Scythe and Caged Hystrix are separate rewards in the same broader dungeon route.

### Can you miss the pickup on the way out?

Yes. Because it is a separate exit-side pickup, it is easy to leave the Chamber after the Sariel sequence without collecting it.

### What did Balance Patch 1 change?

It removed the minimum Resolve requirement to fire Caged Hystrix.

### How do you unlock Spiked?

Caged Hystrix acquisition is associated with the **Spiked** trophy / achievement.
