The **Clockwork Scythe** is obtained through the **Sariel the Endless** route in the **Chamber of Becoming**, reached from **Silent Steps Beacon** in Mammon. In the final arena, destroy all four revival tablets so Sariel can be defeated permanently; the Clockwork Scythe is then obtained in the reward sequence.

## Where is the Clockwork Scythe?

The Clockwork Scythe is tied directly to **Sariel the Endless** and the **Chamber of Becoming**.

Use **Silent Steps Beacon** as the route anchor, progress through Sariel's outer encounter, enter the Chamber behind the large statue, and continue to the final arena.

The weapon is not a separate overworld pickup elsewhere in Mammon.

## How to reach the Chamber of Becoming

Start from **Silent Steps Beacon**.

Use the nearby **Bone Gate / launch route** to reach Sariel's outer encounter area. After advancing that part of the route, access behind the large statue leads into the Chamber of Becoming.

Continue through the Chamber until you reach the final Sariel arena.

For the full Shell route and revival explanation, use the [Sariel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sariel/).

## How to stop Sariel from reviving

The final arena contains **four revival tablets / markers** that sustain Sariel's revivals.

Destroy **all four**. They can be destroyed in any order.

Once all four are gone, Sariel can be defeated permanently and the weapon reward becomes available.

## How to get the Clockwork Scythe

Complete the final Sariel encounter after destroying all four revival markers.

The **Clockwork Scythe** is obtained in the post-fight reward sequence.

Sariel's Shell is a nearby separate reward, and **Caged Hystrix** is another separate pickup farther along the exit route. Do not treat defeating Sariel as automatically collecting every reward in the dungeon.

## How to unlock Like Clockwork

Acquiring the **Clockwork Scythe** is associated with the **Like Clockwork** trophy / achievement.

The weapon-acquisition route and the trophy intent therefore point to the same Chamber of Becoming objective.

## What kind of weapon is the Clockwork Scythe?

The Clockwork Scythe is a **mechanical primary scythe** with long, sweeping melee reach.

Its named ability is **Clockwork Chainsaw**.

This page keeps the focus on acquisition rather than inventing an upgrade curve, damage table, tier ranking, or best build.

For the launch primary-weapon roster, see the [Weapons guide](/mortal-shell-ii/weapons/). For enhancement access, see the [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/).

## What else is in the Chamber of Becoming?

The same route also contains:

- **Sariel's Shell**, unlocked through the nearby Unknown Shell / Memory sequence
- **Caged Hystrix**, found separately near the exit after the Sariel sequence

Use the [Sariel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sariel/) for the Shell unlock and the [Caged Hystrix guide](/mortal-shell-ii/caged-hystrix/) for the exit pickup.

## Clockwork Scythe FAQ

### Where is the Clockwork Scythe?

In the **Chamber of Becoming** on Sariel's route from Silent Steps.

### Who is the Clockwork Scythe tied to?

**Sariel the Endless**.

### Why does Sariel keep reviving?

Four revival tablets / markers sustain the final-arena revival mechanic. Destroy all four.

### How many revival tablets are there?

Four.

### Does the Clockwork Scythe come from a chest?

The approved route ties it to the post-Sariel reward sequence rather than a separate ordinary chest pickup.

### How do you unlock Like Clockwork?

Acquire the Clockwork Scythe. Its acquisition is associated with the **Like Clockwork** trophy / achievement.

### Is Caged Hystrix in the same dungeon?

Yes. Caged Hystrix is a separate Sidearm pickup farther along the Chamber of Becoming exit route.
