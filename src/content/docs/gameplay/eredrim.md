## Evidence boundary

This page covers the known Eredrim location and unlock interaction. It is an Exploration Page, not a complete Shell database.

The current evidence supports the Citadel of Penance route, The Warden encounter, and the option to inhabit Eredrim after the fight. It does not support a complete Eredrim ability table, build guide, or Shell tier list.

## Where is Eredrim?

Eredrim is found through the **Citadel of Penance** route. Follow that area until you reach **The Warden**, the encounter connected to the Shell's unlock sequence.

The [Shells Guide](/mortal-shell-ii/shells/) tracks the wider Shell discovery boundary. Use this page when the search is specifically about Eredrim's location and unlock.

## Citadel of Penance route

The launch-safe route is area-first rather than a fabricated turn-by-turn map:

1. Enter the **Citadel of Penance**.
2. Continue through the route until you reach **The Warden**.
3. Defeat The Warden.
4. After the encounter, locate Eredrim and interact with the Shell.
5. Choose to **inhabit Eredrim** when the prompt appears.

If the Shell prompt does not appear immediately after the fight, finish the encounter state and inspect the nearby interaction before leaving the route. This page does not add unverified room names or landmark directions.

## Defeat The Warden and inhabit Eredrim

The Warden is the progression gate described by the current Eredrim guides. The unlock action is not a separate purchase: after defeating the encounter, interact with Eredrim and select the inhabit option.

The [Sester Genessa unlock guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sester-genessa/) covers another specific Shell route. These pages are intentionally separate from the provisional roster and use-case coverage in the [Shells Guide](/mortal-shell-ii/shells/).

## Do you need to spend Glimpses?

No location-information purchase is required for the known Eredrim route. You can follow the Citadel of Penance path directly to The Warden instead of spending Glimpses to reveal a Shell location hint.

That makes Eredrim a useful example of the resource choice covered by the [Glimpses Guide](/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/): location information can be convenient, but it is not automatically necessary when a route is already documented.
