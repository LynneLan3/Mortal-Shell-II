The **Forgotten Crossbow** is a Sidearm inside **Flooded Village** in Fainweald. Start from **Blackridge Pass Beacon**, find the boarded stone arch leading into the dungeon, descend into the village, collect the **Damp Key** from the rear section, then backtrack to the locked room and claim the Crossbow.

## Where is the Forgotten Crossbow?

The Forgotten Crossbow is behind a locked door inside **Flooded Village**.

The door requires the **Damp Key**, which is found deeper in the same dungeon. This makes the route a simple two-part objective:

1. reach the lower village and find the Damp Key
2. return to the upper locked room and use it

The Forgotten Crossbow is one of the eight weapons tracked by the [Sidearms guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sidearms/).

## How to enter Flooded Village from Blackridge Pass

Use **Blackridge Pass Beacon** as the practical route anchor and travel northwest toward the Flooded Village entrance.

Look for a **boarded stone arch**.

Break the boards, then use the arch / viewing angle to reveal or unblock the stone-building entrance beyond it.

Enter and continue until a metal gate stops your progress.

For broader navigation, use the [Beacons and fast travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).

## How to open the metal gate inside the dungeon

At the blocked gate, check the **left side** of the room.

Break the crates there to reveal a **wall plate / button**, then use it to open the gate.

Continue forward and take the elevator down into the Flooded Village.

The locked Crossbow room is part of the upper route you will return to after obtaining the key.

## Where to find the Damp Key

Explore toward the **rear / back-left section** of the flooded village.

Look for an **elevated house with torches near its entrance**. The Damp Key is around that house, on the porch / near a brazier or container.

You do not need a more precise object description than that: use the elevated house and torches as your landmark, then check the immediate porch area for the key.

## How to return to the locked Crossbow room

Once you have the Damp Key, backtrack toward the earlier **scaffolding / upper section** of Flooded Village.

Return to the locked door you passed on the way down.

Use the Damp Key to open it.

## How to get the Forgotten Crossbow

Enter the unlocked room and collect the **Forgotten Crossbow**.

For Sidearm enhancement, use the [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/).

## Forgotten Crossbow FAQ

### Where is the Forgotten Crossbow?

Inside **Flooded Village** in Fainweald, behind a locked door that requires the Damp Key.

### Which Beacon should I use?

**Blackridge Pass Beacon** is the practical route anchor.

### Where is the Flooded Village entrance?

Northwest of Blackridge Pass, at a boarded stone arch. Break the boards and use the arch / viewing angle to reveal the entrance.

### Where is the Damp Key?

In the rear / back-left part of Flooded Village around an elevated house with torches near its entrance.

### What does the Damp Key open?

The locked room containing the Forgotten Crossbow.

### Is a boss part of this route?

The documented acquisition route centers on entering Flooded Village, finding the Damp Key, and opening the locked Crossbow room; this guide does not use a boss requirement for the pickup.

### Is Forgotten Crossbow one of the Sidearms?

Yes. It is part of the eight-Sidearm launch collection.
