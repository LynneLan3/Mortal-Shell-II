## Quick answer: what should you do first?

For a first launch-build run, use this order:

1. Finish the **Prologue** once instead of skipping it.
2. Settle **Marrow Keep**: activate the Beacon, inspect the Tarforge, speak with the Shellkeeper and vendors, and learn what the hub can upgrade.
3. Take the clear upper-Keep route into **Fainweald**, then activate **Widow's Overlook**.
4. Claim one early Shell, test one Seal, and improve one weapon before chasing distant rewards.
5. Explore in Beacon-sized loops, return **Ova** to the hub, and unlock direct travel/corruption clearing when the in-game prompt becomes available.
6. Push into harder dungeons or Mammon only after the current enemy patterns and defensive timing feel repeatable.

“First 10 hours” is a planning frame, not a sourced completion time. The game allows optional detours, and the exact first dungeon or boss order is not established as mandatory.

## Recommended first-hours route

### 1. Play the Prologue deliberately

The Prologue teaches the basic combat loop and leads to Marrow Keep. Players who progressed far enough in the Open Beta may have a full-game **Skip Prologue** option, but it is best treated as a convenience for a replay. The local [Skip Prologue guide](/mortal-shell-ii/skip-prologue/) records two Prologue-bound achievement opportunities: **Mid Summer?** and **No, You Still Can't Win**.

The skip itself is not the same as beta-save transfer, and official material does not prove that every opening collectible or interaction can be recovered later. If this is your first completion run, play the opening normally.

### 2. Use Marrow Keep as your reset point

Before travelling, activate the Keep Beacon and inspect the services around the hub:

- **Tarforge:** check which weapon, sidearm, and Tarstone functions are available before selling materials.
- **Shellkeeper:** use Glimpses to Bond with a Shell after you know you like its playstyle.
- **Vendors:** check Merrick's healing, upgrade, map, and Tarstone stock before taking a long detour.
- **Upper travel mechanism:** use the clear route overlooking Fainweald when the hub sequence opens it.

The route toward the Ruins of Mammon is blocked by corruption early on. Do not treat that southern path as the intended first region.

### 3. Start in Fainweald at Widow's Overlook

The first practical open-world anchor is **Widow's Overlook**. Rest there, collect the nearby Glimpse if it is still present, and use the Beacon as the starting point for nearby exploration.

From this area, the most useful early landmarks are:

- **Tiel's** torch-marked grave and Memory route;
- the **Great Arbiter of Flesh** area, where current launch coverage places a Revered Gland and the Gloombound Flame nearby;
- **Martyr's Tomb**, which provides an early Tarforge-related item and Parasitic Stone route;
- the route onward through **Blackridge Pass**, Mushroom Village, and Mushroom Village Gate.

The Great Arbiter area is optional. Current route coverage shows that players can prioritize nearby pickups without treating the miniboss as a prerequisite, but it remains dangerous if you are underprepared.

### 4. Claim an early Shell and stabilize one weapon

The clearest early permanent Shell choices are:

- **Tiel**, near Widow's Overlook, for a mobile/evasive style. Use the [Tiel route](/mortal-shell-ii/tiel/) for the grave and Rash Memory landmarks.
- **Proxima**, through Blackridge Pass and the Shattered Beacon, for an early utility option. Use the [Proxima route](/mortal-shell-ii/proxima/) for the Unknown Shell Memory sequence.

Harros is the Prologue Shell and is not a permanent post-Prologue option. The current roster model is **nine Shells total, eight permanent**; some guides say “eight Shells” when counting only the equipable permanent roster.

Do not wait for a perfect build before upgrading. Keep one weapon you can control and learn its spacing. For route-based options, see the [weapons overview](/mortal-shell-ii/weapons/), [Axe & Dagger](/mortal-shell-ii/axe-dagger/), [Great Martyr's Blade](/mortal-shell-ii/great-martyrs-blade/), and [Axatana](/mortal-shell-ii/axatana/) guides. Axatana is a long, risky Mammon detour, not a fresh-game reward.

### 5. Build a Beacon-sized exploration loop

Activate and rest at each Beacon you reach, even when you are not ready to cleanse its attached dungeon. A tagged Beacon is a checkpoint and later travel destination; a cleansed Beacon is a completed route that can provide Ova and open nearby corruption-gated content. These are different actions.

Resting restores Health and Resolve but respawns ordinary enemies. The full checklist and fast-travel rules belong on the [Beacons and Fast Travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).

When you find a Ruk on a landmark, collect its [map fragment](/mortal-shell-ii/map-fragments/) when convenient. A map helps you make a loop; it is not a reason to cross a dangerous region with a large unspent Gloom balance.

### 6. Return Ova to the hub before pushing deeper

The early Fainweald route commonly continues through Blackridge Pass, Mushroom Village, and Mushroom Village Gate. Cleansing the Gate route yields enough Ova in current launch coverage to make the return to Marrow Keep worthwhile, where the Gloom Siphon/Ova Nursery can unlock **Mether's Breath**.

Sources disagree on the exact early threshold: reports include four returned Ova, five additional Ova, and totals that include starting stock. Follow the live in-game prompt rather than treating one number as universal. Mether's Breath is the progression that clears corruption and enables faster travel between tagged Beacons; see the [Ova guide](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/) for the larger progression context.

## Combat fundamentals

Mortal Shell II's official combat framing is stamina-unrestricted, but enemies still punish poor timing, greedy attacks, and bad positioning. Learn one defensive system before trying to rotate between all of them.

### Seals, Break, and Riposte

- **Untarnished Seal:** block/guard; a well-timed guard can add Break pressure.
- **Vatra's Seal:** Harden to nullify the next incoming hit; good timing can also contribute Break damage.
- **Infinite Seal:** parry for stronger Break pressure at greater timing risk.
- **Slayer Seal:** an optional easier combat modifier with stronger stagger/riposte tools. Equipping it locks achievements on that save according to current launch coverage; read the [Slayer Seal and Difficulty guide](/mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/) first.

Fill an enemy's **Break** meter, then take the critical/riposte opening. If your defense staggers you instead, you cannot safely heal or continue attacking for that recovery window. Against a new enemy, watch one attack, defend once, and punish only after you understand the recovery.

### Resolve and Shell separation

Melee attacks build Resolve for abilities and sidearms, while sidearm use consumes it. Do not spend every Resolve charge before a dangerous room if the sidearm or weapon ability is your safest answer.

When a Shell's health is depleted, the Harbinger can be separated from it. Keep attacking carefully to refill the Shell meter and re-enter at low health. If the Harbinger dies, you return to the last tagged Beacon and must recover lost Gloom on a corpse run.

### Exploration habits that prevent avoidable deaths

- Pull groups into open space instead of fighting around a doorway.
- Shoot bells or ropes and inspect suspicious glyph-marked walls; they can reveal shortcuts.
- Rest before a long optional branch.
- If a route suddenly feels far harder than the surrounding area, mark the Beacon and return later rather than forcing it.

## Resources and early spending

| System | What it does | Sensible first-hours use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gloom** | Harbinger progression, Shell-map reveals after Balance Patch 1, and other costs; lost on death with a recovery chance | Spend some at Marrow Keep and avoid carrying an unnecessary bank into an unknown route. |
| **Glimpse** | Shellkeeper Bonding, Shell Memories, and Shell-related actions | Test a Shell, then deepen one or two Bonds instead of scattering every Glimpse. |
| **Ova/Ovum** | Beacon rewards and UnderMether progression | Return to the Gloom Siphon after meaningful progress; use the in-game threshold. |
| **Coins** | Vendors, maps, consumables, and upgrades | Buy a clear healing or upgrade improvement before novelty purchases; sell only clearly tradeable junk. |
| **Revered Glands** | More Mether's Pulse charges | Prioritize when healing charges are the reason a route fails. |
| **Synaptic Vessels** | More healing per Mether's Pulse charge | Prioritize when the existing charge count is adequate but each heal is too small. |
| **Tarstones** | Support, Combat, Infusion, and Ability effects | Check weapon/sidearm compatibility before investing; see the [Tarstones guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/). |

The [Glimpses guide](/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/) and [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/) cover the systems without forcing a universal build. Optional early leads include Gorf's Marsh Pearls, leech Tarstones, the Troubadour's Lute, and the Grisha Remnant; they are useful detours, not mandatory objectives.

## Pitfalls and patch-sensitive details

- **Do not confuse beta and launch routes.** Balance Patch 1 changed Shell-map spending from Glimpse to Gloom, refunded eligible old spending, increased gold drops, reduced weapon-smelting cost, changed enemy/boss values, improved several weapon behaviors, and changed Guard/Tarstone behavior. The [Balance Patch 1 guide](/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/) has the detailed list.
- **Do not equip the Slayer Seal casually.** Picking it up is different from switching to it; the achievement consequence is tied to equipping/switching in current launch coverage.
- **Buy from time-sensitive vendors before leaving their quest branches.** Current launch guides report that some events can remove vendors, but they do not establish one universal schedule.
- **Leave Night Mode for later unless you want the challenge.** The Gloombound Flame → Marrow Keep lantern → Thestus chain is optional; use the [Gloombound Flame guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gloombound-flame/) for the exact route.
- **Do not claim a universal best Shell or weapon.** Tiel, Eredrim, specific Tarstones, and Axatana are recommendations tied to playstyle or outlet opinion, not official rankings.
- **Do not hard-code the Mether's Breath threshold.** Current launch reports conflict, so the in-game prompt is the authority.
