## Where is the Gloombound Flame in Mortal Shell 2?

The **Gloombound Flame** is in **Fainweald**, near the **Widow's Overlook** beacon, in the **Great Arbiter of Flesh** arena.

The pickup is not just somewhere in the wider region. Video and transcript evidence place it near the Great Arbiter, beside a **ritual stone / stone marker** with a visible glowing pickup. Esoterickk's gameplay footage shows the interact prompt at that marker, and the Gamer Guides transcript describes the Flame as being near Great Arbiter on a ritual stone.

## How to get the Gloombound Flame

Follow only the route supported by the current video and transcript evidence:

1. Complete the Prologue and reach **Marrow Keep**.
2. Use the beacon / teleport route north from Marrow Keep to **Widow's Overlook**.
3. Move from Widow's Overlook into the nearby **Great Arbiter of Flesh** arena.
4. Look for the **ritual stone / upright stone marker** with the glowing Gloombound Flame pickup.
5. Pick up the **Gloombound Flame**, then leave or return to Marrow Keep.

The available footage does not support a more precise turn-by-turn path through every doorway or slope, so this guide keeps the route to the confirmed landmarks.

## What does the Gloombound Flame do?

The Gloombound Flame is the key item used to unlock and activate **Night Mode**, Mortal Shell II's harder world state.

It is separate from the **Slayer Seal**. The Slayer Seal is the easier combat option; the Gloombound Flame leads to Night Mode, which increases challenge and adds night-related rewards or encounters. For the broader difficulty comparison, see the [Slayer Seal and difficulty guide](/mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/).

For general combat, Shells, weapons, and exploration context, see the [Mortal Shell II gameplay guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gameplay/).

## How to Unlock Night Mode

1. Go to Fainweald → Widow's Overlook Beacon → Great Arbiter of Flesh arena. The Gloombound Flame sits next to a ritual stone/stone marker inside the arena. [IMAGE: Gloombound Flame beside ritual stone in Great Arbiter arena]
2. Pick up the Gloombound Flame. (Launch footage shows this is possible even if the Great Arbiter is still alive — gameplay-observed/video-supported.)
3. Return to the top of Marrow Keep.
4. Interact with the `LIGHT EXTINGUISHED LANTERN` and use the Gloombound Flame to light it. This is required before Thestus will appear.
5. Summon Thestus at the lantern; when he appears choose `CALL FORTH THE NIGHT` (the prompt reads “Enable increased difficulty.”) Night Mode is then active.

## What Changes in Night Mode

Confirmed / source-supported:

- Night Mode increases overall difficulty and adds extra hazards and encounters. This is supported by launch coverage and the existing page evidence.
- Night Mode can yield more rewards / Gloom and adds night-only content (extra encounters and a night-only merchant are source-supported in launch coverage). Exact multipliers and exact Gloom amounts are not published; avoid treating those numbers as confirmed.
- The Shrine of Sorrows dungeon is a Night Mode-only dungeon per launch coverage.
- Seedbearer's Scripture is a separate Night Mode-only objective requiring Depleted, Thawed, and Faded Glands at the Forgotten Tower — see the existing Seedbearer's Scripture page (/mortal-shell-ii/seedbearers-scripture/) for its route.

Community-reported discoveries (unconfirmed / anecdotal):

- Players on community forums report additional night-only small dungeons near Mushroom Village, a night vendor near the abandoned outpost selling Glimpses / Tarstones / thorium, and more overworld enemies or Glimpses around Shell locations. Treat these as player-reported and not developer-confirmed.
- Some players report unquantified increases to currency/rewards and tougher enemy behavior (reduced stagger, extra moves). These reports conflict and remain anecdotal.

## How to Return to Day Mode

- Return to the top of Marrow Keep and interact with Thestus. The community and secondary launch coverage indicate Thestus can reverse Night Mode; the specific prompt to `SEEK DAYLIGHT` is supported by secondary sources but was not locally video-verified. Treat reversal via Thestus as source-supported but label the exact UI text and behavior as secondary-source supported.

## Is Night Mode worth using?

Use Night Mode if you want a harder run, more reward pressure, or night-related encounters and content. It makes the most sense once you are comfortable with baseline combat and can survive tougher enemies.

Wait before using it if this is your first exploration pass, if normal combat already feels demanding, or if your only goal is to collect the Gloombound Flame for later. This is an editorial recommendation, not an official rule.

## FAQ

### Where is the Gloombound Flame?

Near **Widow's Overlook** in the **Great Arbiter of Flesh** arena. Look for the glowing pickup beside a ritual stone / stone marker.

### Do I have to kill Great Arbiter of Flesh?

Current launch gameplay video evidence says no. The pickup is shown while Great Arbiter is still alive, so you can run in and grab the Gloombound Flame. This is video-supported, not official developer documentation.

### How do I activate Night Mode?

Take the Gloombound Flame back to the top of Marrow Keep, interact with `LIGHT EXTINGUISHED LANTERN`, summon Thestus, then choose `CALL FORTH THE NIGHT`.

### What does Light Extinguished Lantern mean in Mortal Shell 2?

It means the lantern at the top of Marrow Keep has not been lit yet, so you need the **Gloombound Flame** to interact with it. After lighting it and speaking with Thestus, choose `CALL FORTH THE NIGHT` to enter **Night Mode**.

### Where is Thestus?

Thestus appears at the top of Marrow Keep after you light the extinguished lantern with the Gloombound Flame.

### Can you turn Night Mode off?

Yes, Night Mode is toggleable through Thestus. Current secondary launch guides report the return-to-day option as `Seek Daylight`; this exact option was not locally video-verified in the checked Esoterickk footage.

### What does Night Mode change?

It increases difficulty, makes enemies more threatening, increases rewards / Gloom, and adds night-related hazards, encounters, merchants, quests, or dungeon-style content. Exact stat and reward numbers are not treated as official on this page.
