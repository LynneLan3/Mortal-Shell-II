**Gragu** is found inside the **One-Legged Wolf Tavern** in eastern Fainweald. Speak to him until he asks for the **Heart of Vatra**, travel east to the **Temple of Vatra**, take the Heart from the corrupted kneeling statue at the top, escape by the alternate route, and bring the Heart back to Gragu to unlock his Shell.

## Where is Gragu in Mortal Shell 2?

Gragu is inside the **One-Legged Wolf Tavern** in eastern Fainweald.

The tavern is both the start and end of his unlock route: first talk to Gragu there, then return after collecting the Heart of Vatra.

## How to get Gragu to ask for the Heart of Vatra

Talk to Gragu and continue through his dialogue until he asks you to bring him the **Heart of Vatra**.

Do not rely on a fixed interaction count. The useful rule is simply to exhaust the relevant dialogue until the Heart request appears.

Once the request is active, head east toward the Temple of Vatra.

## Where is the Heart of Vatra?

The **Heart of Vatra** is at the **Temple of Vatra**, east of the One-Legged Wolf Tavern.

At the top of the Temple, look for a **corrupted kneeling statue**. The Heart is taken from that statue.

The Heart is the quest item you need to bring back to Gragu.

## How to reach the Temple of Vatra

From the One-Legged Wolf Tavern, continue east.

A supported route uses the **Bone Gate / chasm crossing** to reach the Temple side of eastern Fainweald.

If corruption is still blocking the eastward route, continue your wider progression and return when the relevant access / **Mether's Breath** progression allows you through. This guide does not use an invented fixed Ova threshold for that gate.

## How to get the Heart and escape the temple

Reach the corrupted kneeling statue at the top of the Temple and take the **Heart of Vatra**.

Picking up the Heart activates nearby enemies and blocks the obvious return path.

Instead of trying to force your way back through the same route, look for the **side / alternate exit** and use it to leave the Temple.

From there, return to the One-Legged Wolf Tavern.

## How to give Gragu the Heart and unlock his Shell

Talk to Gragu again and give him the **Heart of Vatra**.

The resulting event leaves Gragu's body available to inhabit. Interact with it to unlock Gragu as a playable Shell.

The unlock is associated with the **Heartless** trophy / achievement.

For the broader Shell index, see the [Shells guide](/mortal-shell-ii/shells/).

## Optional: Berserker's Stone in the Temple of Vatra

The Temple also contains the **Berserker's Stone**, but it is a separate optional reward.

Its chest is tied to a **three-fire / three-candle offering puzzle**.

You do **not** need Berserker's Stone to obtain the Heart of Vatra or unlock Gragu. Keep the two objectives separate if you only want to finish the Shell quest.

For the site's broader stone coverage, see the [Tarstones guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/) and [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/).

## Gragu FAQ

### Where is Gragu?

Inside the **One-Legged Wolf Tavern** in eastern Fainweald.

### How many times do I need to talk to Gragu?

There is no fixed interaction count used by this guide. Keep talking to him until he asks for the Heart of Vatra.

### Where is the Heart of Vatra?

At the **Temple of Vatra**, east of the tavern, held by a corrupted kneeling statue at the top.

### Is Berserker's Stone required?

No. It is a separate optional Temple reward and is not required for Gragu's unlock.

### What if the route east is blocked by corruption?

Continue your wider progression and return when the relevant route access / Mether's Breath progression opens the way. Do not use a fixed Ova number for this route.

### What happens when you give Gragu the Heart?

The resulting event leaves Gragu's body available to inhabit, which unlocks the Shell.

### How do you unlock Heartless?

Complete the Heart of Vatra route and unlock Gragu. That unlock is associated with the **Heartless** trophy / achievement.
