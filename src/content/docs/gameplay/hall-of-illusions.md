The **Hall of Illusions** is the Beacon dungeon at **Mushroom Village Gate** in Fainweald. Its route uses perspective-based illusion doorways, a breakable hidden wall, and a final encounter with **Vrannic the Grand Illusionist**. Clearing the dungeon also opens the route toward **Glutted Mire**.

## Where is the Hall of Illusions?

Hall of Illusions is entered through the **Mushroom Village Gate Beacon**, east / southeast of Mushroom Village in Fainweald.

Use the Beacon as your route anchor. The dungeon is tied to the progression route toward Glutted Mire.

For the wider Beacon network, see the [Beacons and fast travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).

## How to enter from Mushroom Village Gate

Enter the Beacon dungeon from **Mushroom Village Gate** and continue until you reach the first freestanding illusion doorway / frame.

Hall of Illusions does not treat every doorway like a normal passage. The route can change depending on the side and angle from which you view the frame, so the main puzzle rule is to reposition before assuming a doorway is blocked.

## How to solve the first illusion doorway

At the first lone doorway:

1. Approach the frame.
2. Move around to the far side.
3. Look back through the doorway from that side.
4. When the route changes, pass through the frame.

The important action is viewing the doorway from the opposite side before walking through it.

## How to solve the second doorway and hidden wall

A second illusion doorway continues the same perspective-based progression.

After passing it, you reach a room that appears to have no obvious exit. The next route is behind a **breakable / illusory wall**.

Descriptions place the wall **opposite the entrance / beside the tree**. Use that as the search area rather than expecting a precise coordinate marker.

Break the hidden wall and continue into the next section.

## How to trigger the Vrannic fight

The next room can also look sealed at first.

This time, **look upward**.

Vrannic is positioned on a **raised wooden platform** above the room. Spotting him advances the route toward the boss encounter.

If you are searching only at ground level, this is the step that is easiest to overlook.

## How to identify the real Vrannic

**Vrannic the Grand Illusionist** uses both ranged projectiles and staff / melee attacks.

During the clone phase, multiple Vrannic copies appear. The useful behavioral difference is:

- the **real Vrannic moves and uses melee attacks**
- the **clones are more stationary and fire projectiles**

Use movement and melee behavior to identify the real target instead of guessing from appearance alone.

The clones can be dispelled very quickly, so clearing a fake target does not have to stop the fight for long.

## What you get for clearing Hall of Illusions

Defeating Vrannic rewards the **Volatile Fragment** Tarstone.

For the broader Tarstone system, see the [Tarstones guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/).

Clearing the Hall of Illusions also opens / cleanses the route toward **Glutted Mire**, which is the larger progression reason to complete the Beacon dungeon.

## Hall of Illusions FAQ

### Where is Hall of Illusions?

At the **Mushroom Village Gate Beacon** in Fainweald, east / southeast of Mushroom Village.

### How do the illusion doorways work?

Reposition around the doorway and view it from the other side. The route changes based on the viewing side / angle.

### Where is the hidden wall?

After the second illusion doorway, search the sealed room for the breakable wall described as opposite the entrance / beside the tree.

### How do I start the Vrannic fight?

After breaking the hidden wall, continue into the next sealed-looking room and **look upward** for Vrannic on the raised wooden platform.

### Which Vrannic is real?

The real Vrannic moves and uses melee attacks. The clones are more stationary and rely on projectiles.

### What does Vrannic drop?

The **Volatile Fragment** Tarstone.

### Does Hall of Illusions open Glutted Mire?

Yes. Completing the Beacon dungeon opens / cleanses the route toward Glutted Mire.
