## What the two upgrade types do

- **Revered Gland:** increases the number of healing items you can carry.
- **Synaptic Vessel:** increases how much each healing use restores.

Current launch coverage reports **21 total upgrades: 11 Revered Glands and 10 Synaptic Vessels**. Two are purchases from Merrick; the remaining 19 are route pickups, dungeon rewards, or boss rewards.

## The gated pickups

### Thieves Retreat — Nocthean Gate

From **Nocthean Gate Beacon**, head north and drop one level. Use the switch to open the dungeon route, then follow the invisible walkways. The suspended bodies are the reported visual landmarks for the route to the Revered Gland.

### Holding Cells — Mushroom Village

From **Mushroom Village Beacon**, go northwest to the route that enters Holding Cells. Use **Mether's Breath** on the corruption blocking the jump pad, enter through the well, and find the button behind the boxes to open the Revered Gland room.

### Pale Grisha — One Legged Wolf

Reach **Grisha Hunting Grounds** northwest past the ruins from the One Legged Wolf area. The Revered Gland is the reported reward for defeating **Pale Grisha**.

### Citadel of Penance

For the Revered Gland, go southeast of the Beacon to the southwestern outer-castle corner and break the wooden wall. For the Synaptic Vessel, find and press the button on the wall opposite Eredrim's former position to open the nearby gate.

### Abbey Entrance

Enter the cave east of town and destroy all three glowing tombstones. The door to the Revered Gland opens after the third tombstone.

### Sunken Village

Cleanse the **Sunken Village Beacon** first. Then turn around, walk down the hill, and check the container behind you.

### Prisoners' Domain

Follow the main path to the giant statue. Destroy the pots at its base to topple it, then pass the iron-maiden room to reach the Revered Gland.

## How to check whether you collected one

There is no reported collection counter for these upgrades. Fast-travel back to the named location: if the pickup is gone, it was collected. Your healing item should also show the result—more charges for a Revered Gland or greater restoration for a Synaptic Vessel.

LootMap separately reports a 15-marker “Healing Upgrades” layer and 12 “Devout's Chalice” artifacts. Do not add those artifact chalices to this 21-item Gland/Vessel checklist; current coverage treats them as a different map-layer category.

## Are healing upgrades missable?

Current launch coverage reports that none of the 21 are missable and that uncollected upgrades remain available during free roam after the final boss. Treat that as current guide reporting rather than a permanent guarantee for every future patch.

## Common mistakes

- Counting Devout's Chalices as Revered Glands or Synaptic Vessels.
- Forgetting the 1,500-coin Merrick purchases in Marrow Keep.
- Entering Holding Cells without first clearing the corruption at the jump pad.
- Leaving the Sunken Village route before cleansing its Beacon.
- Missing the Citadel button because the Vessel is not behind the same wooden wall as the Gland.
- Expecting a collection counter to track the 21 pickups.
