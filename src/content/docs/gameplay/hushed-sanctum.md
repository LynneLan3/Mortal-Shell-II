Hushed Sanctum is the separate cleanse dungeon you enter by selecting **Cleanse Beacon** at the **Citadel Annex Beacon** inside the Faded Citadel. Sources report a multi-room sequence that ends when you interact with a corrupted crystal core; the game then shows **Beacon Cleansed**.

## Location and how to enter

- Hushed Sanctum is **not** Ashen Sanctum. It is the distinct cleanse instance triggered from the Citadel Annex Beacon inside the Faded Citadel.
- Route anchor: reach the Citadel Annex Beacon via the Faded Citadel interior (Gate of Infinity → Ashen Sanctum approach → Citadel Annex). For broader route context, see the [Faded Citadel walkthrough](/mortal-shell-ii/faded-citadel/) — this page does not duplicate that full route.
- At the Citadel Annex Beacon, choose **Cleanse Beacon** to load Hushed Sanctum.

## Reported cleanse sequence

Sources (AllThingsHow and local reporting) list the following sequence when you pick Cleanse Beacon at Citadel Annex. Use it as a checklist while playing:

1. Narrow bridge with a ranged caster
2. Tunnel with melee enemies and spiders
3. Stairs with a teleporting lightning caster
4. Large pillared spider room
5. Platforming / drop into a circular arena
6. A red rift to a high ledge (mage + chest)
7. Interact with the **corrupted crystal core** to complete the cleanse

When completion registers you should see a **Beacon Cleansed** message. The reported reward is **Stillblade's Stone** (a Tarstone).

## Stillblade's Stone

Multiple sources report Stillblade's Stone as the reward for the Citadel Annex cleanse. See the [Tarstones database](/mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/) for the item entry.

## How to finish the cleanse — corrupted core requirement

- The cleanse does not appear to complete simply by reaching a chest or leaving the instance. Local coverage reports that taking the chest and leaving **without** interacting with the corrupted crystal core does not register the Beacon as cleansed.
- You must interact with the corrupted crystal core inside Hushed Sanctum. That interaction is the final step that triggers the **Beacon Cleansed** confirmation.

## Why a Beacon might be stuck

If the Citadel Annex Beacon does not mark as cleansed after you run Hushed Sanctum:

1. Confirm you interacted with the corrupted crystal core (not just looted a nearby chest).
2. Rest at the Citadel Annex rest point and attempt **Cleanse Beacon** again to reload the sequence.
3. Cross-check [Beacons and fast travel](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/) for general Beacon behavior.
4. For Isaac-related combat or blue-flame damage issues, see [Isaac, the Scholar Prince](/mortal-shell-ii/isaac-the-scholar-prince/).

Do not assume platform-specific bug fixes beyond verifying the core interaction unless confirmed by official patch notes.

## Common mistakes

- **Leaving after looting the chest:** taking the chest without interacting with the corrupted crystal core does not complete the cleanse.
- **Confusing Ashen Sanctum with Hushed Sanctum:** Hushed Sanctum is the Citadel Annex cleanse instance and is separate from Ashen Sanctum.
- **Skipping the core interaction:** the reported final trigger is the corrupted crystal core — ensure you completed that step.
