## Quick answer

Lazlo the Justiciar is a later permanent Shell in **Mammon**. Use the **High Lord's Courtyard Beacon** as your route anchor, reach the keep or crypt beyond it, and defeat **Vellen, Lord of Mammon**. After the fight, continue into the last chamber and interact with Lazlo's remains or the inhabit prompt, then complete the first Memory sequence so the Shell is permanently available.

For the build, think **armored bruiser with a timed burst window**. Keep Lazlo's armor and Heat Meter working, generate Resolve with melee attacks, use **Retribution** to heat the armor and release shockwaves, then trigger **Overheat** when you have a safe opening. Overheat delivers the bigger Burn/shockwave payoff but temporarily turns the armor off, so use the exposed window for critical hits or ranged pressure instead of standing in front of a boss and trading blindly.

## Where to find Lazlo

The stable location claim is **the Mammon High Lord's Courtyard / keep route, beyond Vellen**. GameSpot describes the approach from the Outskirts of Mammon toward High Lord's Courtyard. Other current guides name the destination the **Royal Crypt**, **High Lord's Keep**, or the crypt below the keep.

These names may describe connected landmarks, but the published bearings are not identical. Use the in-game beacon and encounter names rather than a fabricated map coordinate.

## Lazlo route and unlock

1. Reach and activate the **High Lord's Courtyard Beacon**. If you do not have it, current guides describe working westward from the **Outskirts of Mammon** through the courtyard approach.
2. Follow the keep/crypt route beyond the beacon. Expect a hostile underground approach with Armored Knights, Coffin Eye Beholders, or a Knight of Mammon in current guide reports.
3. Continue to the arena or underground cathedral and defeat **Vellen, High Lord of Mammon**.
4. Do not stop at the boss reward state. Enter the chamber beyond or behind Vellen and inspect **Lazlo's body**.
5. Interact with the Shell and complete the first Memory sequence. The wider [Shells Guide](/mortal-shell-ii/shells/) tracks the distinction between finding a body and making a Shell permanently usable.

### Location uncertainty

The sources agree on the beacon, keep/crypt route, Vellen gate, and post-fight body. They differ on whether the final route is described as west or south from the beacon, and one guide uses a different regional label. That is why this page stops at named landmarks and does not publish compass coordinates or a rigid room-by-room route.

## How Lazlo's build works

Current launch guides describe two linked systems:

- **Fortified Plate:** Lazlo's armor supplies damage reduction while it is active. The exact values below are publisher-reported and can be patch-sensitive, so use the armor as a defensive state rather than relying on one permanent percentage.
- **Retribution and Heat:** Retribution heats the armor and releases a shockwave. Repeating the cycle fills the Heat Meter; the next trigger can cause **Overheat**, a stronger shockwave that applies Burn but temporarily disables the armor.

That trade-off is the build. Armor-on is your safe pressure state. Overheat is your damage conversion state. Armor-off is a short danger window in which you should use a critical opening, create distance, or fire a sidearm rather than continue taking clean hits.

## A practical Lazlo starting build

This is a source-backed starting template, not a universal tier-list verdict.

### Weapon

Use the **Axatana** as the default primary. Both current Lazlo build guides recommend it because its fast light attacks help generate Resolve and its speed makes the Overheat critical window easier to use. The [Axatana guide](/mortal-shell-ii/axatana/) covers its location and the early route to it.

If you prefer slower, wider swings in crowded dungeons, the [Veteran's Battle Axe](/mortal-shell-ii/veterans-battle-axe/) is a reasonable alternative reported by AllThings.How. It changes the rhythm; it is not required for Lazlo.

### Perks and Bond priorities

Work toward the following Heat-cycle package as your Bond rises through the Shellkeeper. Higher Bond tiers require Glimpses; see the [Glimpses Guide](/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/) before committing scarce resources.

- **Worthy Armor** to reduce Retribution's Resolve pressure.
- **Warmed Up** to reward keeping the Heat Meter high.
- **Incandescence** to help refill Heat when the armor returns.
- **Temperament** to improve shockwave output and radius.
- **Furnace** for full-Heat defensive and Burn pressure.
- **Inflamed** to make the Overheat cycle less punishing at higher investment.
- **Rush** to turn the armor-off period into a critical-hit opportunity.

The important order is not “buy every damage perk first.” Stabilize the loop: reduce the cost of Retribution, keep Heat available, shorten or soften the armor-off gap, then invest in the burst payoff. If Overheat leaves you exposed too often, **Adaptation** is a reasonable comfort alternative before the full damage package.

### Sidearm: two supported choices

Current guides do not agree on one best sidearm:

- Choose **Caged Hystrix** if you want a large-ammo ranged option while Lazlo's armor is offline. Its dedicated [Caged Hystrix guide](/mortal-shell-ii/caged-hystrix/) covers the pickup route.
- Choose **Ballistazooka** if you want to turn the Overheat/Rush critical window into a more deliberate ranged burst. The [Sidearms Guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sidearms/) keeps its location in the wider collection checklist.

Both recommendations solve the same problem: armor is temporarily unavailable after Overheat. Pick the sidearm you can actually supply and aim consistently; neither is an established mandatory Lazlo item.

### Tarstones and Seal

Use the [Tarstones Guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/) for current effect and collection boundaries. The build evidence favors **critical chance, critical damage, and Resolve generation** over stacking more defense that Lazlo's armor already provides. **Duality Stone** is especially relevant to the melee/Resolve loop in the current build reports; crit-oriented support stones are the natural companions for Rush.

**Vatra's Seal** is a useful optional fit because Hardening lets Lazlo absorb a key hit while he is standing his ground. That is a current guide recommendation, not a requirement. For the broader Seal and difficulty trade-off, see [Slayer Seal & Difficulty](/mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/).

## Simple combat rotation

1. Start with armor active and build Heat without spending every resource on the approach.
2. Use light melee strings to restore Resolve and keep pressure on the target.
3. Fire Retribution when it is safe; do not trigger Overheat automatically in the middle of an enemy combo.
4. When Overheat opens the burst window, use the stronger shockwave and convert the armor-off period with critical melee hits or your chosen sidearm.
5. Reposition until the armor returns, then rebuild the Heat loop.

This rotation is a practical synthesis of the overlapping current guides. It is not a developer-published combo or a promise of a specific damage result.

## FAQ

### Where is Lazlo in Mortal Shell 2?

Reach the **High Lord's Courtyard Beacon** in Mammon, follow the keep/crypt route, defeat Vellen, and inspect the chamber beyond the encounter for Lazlo's remains.

### Do you have to defeat Vellen to get Lazlo?

Current location guides consistently treat **Vellen, High Lord of Mammon** as the boss gate to Lazlo's body.

### Does defeating Vellen unlock Lazlo immediately?

Not by itself in the safe route description. Continue into the post-fight chamber, interact with Lazlo's Shell, and complete the first Memory sequence.

### Is Axatana the best weapon for Lazlo?

It is the clearest shared recommendation in current build guides, especially for fast Resolve generation and the Rush critical window. “Best” remains a playstyle judgment; the Veteran's Battle Axe is a slower alternative.

### Which sidearm should Lazlo use?

Current guides split between **Caged Hystrix** and **Ballistazooka**. Use Caged Hystrix for safer ranged pressure during armor downtime, or Ballistazooka for a more deliberate burst setup.

### Is Lazlo a tank?

His armor-and-Heat kit is built for close-range pressure and damage mitigation, but Overheat temporarily removes that protection. He is a timing-based armored bruiser, not permission to ignore every attack.
