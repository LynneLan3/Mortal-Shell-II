**Lucian the Thirsting Knight** is the boss tied to the **Hidden Keep** and cleansing **Stonebled Gate Beacon**. The route begins at Stonebled Gate, passes through a hidden passage inside the Keep, and ends with a fight that changes after Lucian retreats to higher ground and begins attacking while invisible.

## Where is Lucian the Thirsting Knight?

Use **Stonebled Gate Beacon** as the route anchor.

The associated Beacon dungeon is **The Hidden Keep**. Enter the cleanse route from Stonebled Gate and progress through the Keep until you reach Lucian.

Defeating him allows the Stonebled Gate progression / cleanse to continue.

## How to reach Lucian through the Hidden Keep

Inside the Hidden Keep, continue until you reach a hallway with **destructible crates** at the end.

Break the crates and look for a **highlighted stone**.

Interact with the stone to open the hidden passage, then follow the newly opened route to Lucian.

For the site's broader Beacon coverage, see the [Beacons and fast travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).

## Lucian first phase: what to watch for

Lucian's first phase is built around **fast sword attacks**, including slash and stab combinations.

The main takeaway is to respect the speed of his melee strings rather than treating him like a slow heavy boss.

This guide does not assign frame-perfect punish windows or a required weapon. Use your normal defensive timing and avoid overcommitting into a fast follow-up.

## Where Lucian goes after the first phase

After the first part of the fight, Lucian **retreats toward higher ground**.

Follow him to continue the encounter.

A supported route uses an **opening on the left beside a wall torch**, where you squeeze through to reach the next section.

That is the important navigation step if the fight appears to have stopped after the first phase.

## How to survive Lucian's invisible phase

In the later phase, Lucian can **turn invisible and attack while unseen**.

You do not need a special reveal item for the supported route.

A safe current tactic is to use **Guard** against incoming invisible attacks while paying close attention to your **Break meter**. Do not let repeated blocked attacks exhaust the meter and leave you open.

Keep the focus on surviving the unseen attacks and taking the next safe opening rather than searching the arena for an invented anti-invisibility mechanic.

## What happens after defeating Lucian?

Defeating Lucian allows the **Stonebled Gate** cleanse / progression to continue.

Supported named rewards include:

- **Serpent Stone**
- a **Glimpse**

This guide does not use an exact Gloom reward because launch guides do not consistently report the same amount.
