## Evidence boundary

This is a launch-window **Exploration Page** for the Ova/Ovum progression blocker. It explains where Ova fits in the Beacon and Corrupted Gate loop, why the 168 requirement matters, and why Send Ova should be treated as an endgame commitment.

It is not a complete endgame walkthrough and does not replace the [New Game Plus guide](/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/). NG+ explains character carry-over and world reset; this page explains the Ova resource and the point at which the current run moves toward endgame.

## What is Ova?

Ova is the progression resource tied to cleansing and advancing through corrupted world routes. You collect it through the Beacon and **Corrupted Gate** loop, then return to Marrow Keep to feed the relevant siphon progression.

The system connects three things that are easy to confuse:

- **Beacons** provide route, rest, and progression anchors.
- **Corrupted Gates** create the blocked-route and cleanse objective around the world.
- **Ova/Ovum** is the resource brought back into the Marrow Keep siphon progression.

For the broader Beacon travel system, see the [Beacon locations and fast travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/). This page keeps its focus on Ova rather than repeating every Beacon location.

## How to get Ova

Use the current progression loop:

1. Discover and activate the relevant Beacon.
2. Identify the Corrupted Gate or corrupted route associated with the area.
3. Advance through the cleanse or combat route.
4. Collect the Ova reward when the route is completed.
5. Return to Marrow Keep and use the Gloom Siphon progression.
6. Continue opening the next corrupted route or endgame progression step.

Not every Beacon is identical, and not every travel node should be treated as a guaranteed Ova source. The important relationship is between eligible corrupted routes, Beacon progression, and the return to the siphon.

If fast travel is the immediate blocker, first check whether Mether's Breath is available in the Beacon system. If endgame progression is the blocker, check your Ova route and the Gloom Siphon instead of assuming that another ordinary Gloom farm will solve it.

## Corrupted Gates, Beacons, and Ova

Corrupted Gates are the route obstacles that make Ova progression meaningful. They are not simply another collectible marker.

Use Beacons as the navigation framework:

- rest at discovered Beacons so the route is recoverable,
- note which nearby path is blocked by corruption,
- cleanse the eligible route when your progression allows it,
- collect Ova and return to Marrow Keep,
- revisit the world after the siphon unlocks the next stage.

The [Gloombound Flame guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gloombound-flame/) covers a separate Widow's Overlook and Night Mode route. Do not confuse Gloombound Flame with Ova or treat Night Mode as the Ova upgrade system.

## What is the 168 Ovum requirement?

Current endgame research identifies a **168 Ovum** requirement in the final Ova progression sequence.

Treat 168 as an endgame gate, not as a universal claim about every earlier Ova threshold. Earlier Beacon and siphon steps may appear before this final requirement, and the exact menu wording can vary by progression state.

When the game presents the 168 requirement, stop and check your optional-content list before committing to the next action. The number tells you that the run is approaching its endgame transition; it is not a reason to invent a complete Ova farming table.

## What does Send Ova do?

**Send Ova** is the practical point of no return for the current run.

Choosing it moves the campaign into the endgame progression after the Ova requirement is satisfied. It should be treated differently from depositing or siphoning ordinary progression resources: once you send the required Ova, optional routes may no longer be available in the same pre-endgame form.

Before choosing Send Ova, consider completing:

- known weapon routes such as Great Martyr's Blade, Axatana, and Axe & Dagger,
- Shell discoveries and Shell progression,
- Beacon activation and map work,
- Tarstone experiments or upgrades you want for the current run,
- Boss preparation and any trophy attempts with a missable retry window,
- optional quests and exploration routes you do not want to repeat.

The [Peter's Perfect Parry guide](/mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/) is a useful example of why this matters: some encounter trophies need to be attempted before a Boss is killed, and NG+ may be the next opportunity.

## When does Ova progression enter endgame?

The endgame transition arrives when the siphon/Ova progression presents the final requirement and the game offers the Send Ova action. The 168 Ovum requirement is the clearest current research marker for that stage.

Do not infer a specific final Boss name from inconsistent launch wording. The practical decision is the resource commitment and the loss of the normal pre-endgame route, not the label attached to the final encounter.

If you are not ready, continue exploring and upgrading before selecting Send Ova. If you have already sent Ova, use the [New Game Plus guide](/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/) to plan what character-bound progress can carry into the next cycle and what world state will reset.

## Ova preparation checklist

Before the 168 requirement becomes an immediate decision:

1. Activate useful Beacons and make sure you can navigate back to important areas.
2. Complete weapon and Shell routes that matter to your current playstyle.
3. Spend time on Tarstones if you want to test their XP and upgrade progression.
4. Attempt any trophy or Boss challenge that becomes unavailable after a kill.
5. Check your optional quest and exploration list.
6. Only then choose Send Ova when you are ready for the endgame transition.

## FAQ

### How do you get Ova in Mortal Shell 2?

Progress through eligible Corrupted Gates and Beacon-related cleanse routes, collect Ova, and return it through the Gloom Siphon progression in Marrow Keep.

### Are Ova and Ovum the same progression?

This page uses Ova for the resource and Ovum for the wording used in the endgame requirement. Follow the exact term shown by the current in-game prompt.

### What is the 168 Ovum requirement?

It is the researched endgame requirement associated with the final Ova progression sequence before Send Ova.

### What is Send Ova?

Send Ova is the practical point of no return that moves the current run into endgame progression after the required Ova/Ovum threshold.

### Should I send Ova immediately?

No, not if you still want optional weapons, Shells, Beacons, Tarstone tests, quests, or missable Boss trophies in the current pre-endgame route.

### Does Send Ova start New Game Plus?

No. Send Ova is an endgame commitment in the current run. NG+ is a separate later cycle covered by the [New Game Plus guide](/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/).

### Do Corrupted Gates give Ova?

Current research connects Ova rewards to the Corrupted Gate and Beacon cleanse progression. Treat individual Gate rewards as launch-build evidence rather than a complete universal table.
