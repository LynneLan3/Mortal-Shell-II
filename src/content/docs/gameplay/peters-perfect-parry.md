## Evidence boundary

This is a focused **Exploration Page** for the Peter's Perfect Parry trophy and achievement intent. It is not a complete trophy guide and does not attempt to catalogue every missable achievement.

The current launch research supports the Nameless Captive, the Untarnished Seal, the second-phase seven-hit headspin target, the retry window after a failed attempt, and the New Game Plus consequence after the Boss is killed. It does not add unverified damage values, frame timings, or a complete Boss weakness table.

## What is Peter's Perfect Parry?

**Peter's Perfect Parry** is the trophy/achievement challenge associated with the **Nameless Captive** encounter and the **Untarnished Seal**.

The important point is that this is not a normal “win the fight” objective. The required parry sequence happens during the encounter, and the attempt is tied to recognizing the correct combo rather than repeatedly parrying every attack at random.

If you are preparing a broader combat route, the [Slayer Seal and difficulty guide](/mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/) explains how Seal-based combat modifiers work. Keep that general difficulty advice separate from this specific trophy condition.

## Nameless Captive and the Untarnished Seal

The challenge uses the **Nameless Captive** encounter and the **Untarnished Seal** context recorded by the current launch research.

Before you commit to the fight:

1. Confirm that you are at the Nameless Captive encounter rather than a similar-looking combat sequence.
2. Have the Untarnished Seal requirement or setup ready according to the current in-game prompt.
3. Treat the encounter as a trophy attempt, not only as a Boss clear.
4. Do not kill the Boss until you are satisfied that the required parry condition has been completed.

The goal is missable in the current run because defeating the Boss removes the immediate opportunity to try the challenge again.

## The second-phase seven-hit headspin

The key target is the **seven-hit headspin** in the encounter's second phase.

The safest way to approach it is to identify the correct combo first, then count the sequence rather than reacting to isolated visual noise:

1. Reach the second phase without spending the whole fight attacking on autopilot.
2. Watch for the headspin combo that belongs to the trophy attempt.
3. Start counting the consecutive hits in that sequence.
4. Parry the full **seven-hit** headspin pattern.
5. Confirm the trophy/achievement result before finishing the Boss if the game provides the notification.

Do not treat a single successful parry, a different attack animation, or an interrupted combo as the seven-hit requirement. The challenge is about the recognized sequence, not simply surviving Phase Two.

## How to identify the correct combo

Combo identification matters more than trying to parry everything in the arena.

- **Stay patient in Phase Two.** The trophy sequence is a specific attack pattern, so wait for the recognizable headspin rather than forcing an attempt during every opening.
- **Count the hits.** Use the repeated animation and audio rhythm to keep a seven-hit count. Losing count is a reason to reset your attempt rather than assume it worked.
- **Keep the Boss alive.** A low-health Boss is still an opportunity; a dead Boss is the end of the current-run retry window.
- **Separate a miss from a kill.** Missing the parry does not mean you must abandon the save. The important lockout happens when the Nameless Captive is killed before the trophy condition is completed.

The current evidence does not justify publishing exact frame-perfect timing or claiming that one build, Shell, or weapon is mandatory. Use the attack pattern shown by the live encounter and keep the attempt controlled.

## What happens if you fail?

A failed parry attempt can be continued or retried while the Nameless Captive remains available.

After a miss:

1. Do not panic and finish the Boss immediately.
2. Create space and wait for the encounter to present the relevant combo again.
3. Re-identify the Phase Two headspin.
4. Attempt the seven-hit sequence again.

The retry rule is the main reason to avoid rushing the kill. A failed attempt is not the same as a permanently failed trophy, provided the Boss is still available for another try.

## What if I already killed the Boss?

If the Nameless Captive has already been killed, current research indicates that the next opportunity to attempt Peter's Perfect Parry requires **New Game Plus**.

This makes the trophy effectively missable in the current run. Use the [New Game Plus guide](/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/) to understand which character progress carries over and which world state is rebuilt before planning the retry.

Do not assume that reloading an ordinary Beacon, returning to the arena, or changing difficulty will recreate the encounter after the kill. The current guidance treats NG+ as the next reliable opportunity.

## Practical preparation

Before attempting the trophy, prepare for consistency rather than speed:

- make sure the Untarnished Seal setup is available,
- use a weapon and Shell you can control without changing your parry rhythm,
- avoid optional difficulty changes that make the pattern harder to read,
- leave enough healing and space for repeated Phase Two attempts,
- do not commit the Boss kill until the trophy condition is confirmed.

The [Gloombound Flame guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gloombound-flame/) covers Night Mode separately. It should not be treated as a requirement for Peter's Perfect Parry unless the current in-game prompt says otherwise.

## FAQ

The challenge is associated with the **Nameless Captive** encounter.

The Untarnished Seal is the Seal context tied to this trophy attempt. Follow the current in-game requirement and do not substitute an unverified Seal setup.

The Phase Two headspin target is **seven hits**.

Yes, while the Nameless Captive remains alive and available. A failed attempt is not the same as killing the Boss.

No. This page covers only the Nameless Captive challenge and does not claim to be a complete achievement guide.
