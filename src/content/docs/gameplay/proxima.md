## Quick Answer

- **Shell:** **Proxima** is a new Shell discovered at the **Shattered Beacon**, north of **Blackridge Pass**.
- **Timing:** Proxima can be unlocked **shortly after completing the Prologue**. Current evidence does not show any boss, key, Ova, Mether's Breath, specific weapon, or specific Shell requirement.
- **Route:** Marrow Keep → northern transporter → Widow's Overlook → Blackridge Pass → Shattered Beacon.
- **Unlock:** Enter the Shattered Beacon, interact with the Unknown Shell, complete the short memory sequence, and Proxima is yours.

---

## Where Is Proxima?

Proxima is found inside the **Shattered Beacon**, a dungeon located north of the **Blackridge Pass Beacon** in an elevated area. You reach the Shattered Beacon by traveling north from Marrow Keep through Widow's Overlook and Blackridge Pass.

---

## Can You Unlock Proxima Early?

Yes. Proxima can be unlocked early after completing the Prologue. The route from Marrow Keep to the Shattered Beacon is available in the open world without additional story progression in the checked evidence.

:::note[Single-source note]
The creator of the source video describes Proxima as "the first Shell that we can unlock after completing the Prologue." This guide treats the claim as transcript-supported rather than confirmed by multiple independent sources.
:::

---

## How to Reach Blackridge Pass

Starting from the **Marrow Keep Beacon**:

1. Make your way **up to the top of Marrow Keep** and launch into the open world.
2. Take the **northern transporter** to reach the **Widow's Overlook Beacon**.
3. From Widow's Overlook, head **north** until you reach the **Blackridge Pass Beacon**.

The Widow's Overlook area is also used for the [Great Martyr's Blade](/mortal-shell-ii/great-martyrs-blade/) weapon route and the [Axatana](/mortal-shell-ii/axatana/) route — if you have already visited those areas, you may already know the path.

## How to Find the Shattered Beacon

From the **Blackridge Pass Beacon**, continue **north and to the left**. You are looking for an **elevated area** with a **Shattered Beacon** near an enemy.

The Shattered Beacon is a dungeon entrance, not a standard cleansable beacon.

![Shattered Beacon dungeon entrance in Mortal Shell 2. Third-party gameplay footage / evidence capture.](/images/proxima/proxima-shattered-beacon.png)

## How to Unlock Proxima Inside the Shattered Beacon

1. **Enter** the Shattered Beacon.
2. **Interact with the Unknown Shell** inside.
3. A short memory / cutscene will begin. During the memory, perform these interactions in order:
   - **Take the Offering**
   - **Use Biosampler on stranger**
   - **Answer the Stranger**
   - **Inform Merrick**
4. **Exit the memory.** You will see the notification: **NEW SHELL DISCOVERED — PROXIMA**.
5. You can **immediately switch to Proxima** after the discovery.

![New Shell Discovered — Proxima confirmation in Mortal Shell 2. Third-party gameplay footage / evidence capture.](/images/proxima/proxima-unlocked.png)

:::caution[Evidence boundary]
The four interaction steps above follow a single video walkthrough. The guide does not confirm whether alternative dialogue choices also lead to unlocking Proxima.
:::

## Do You Need to Cleanse Blackridge Pass Beacon?

The guide route does not require cleansing the Blackridge Pass Beacon before continuing toward Proxima. The transcript explicitly states that cleansing is optional for this path. You can cleanse the beacon if you choose, but it is not a prerequisite for reaching the Shattered Beacon or unlocking Proxima.

---

## What Happens After You Unlock Proxima?

After the memory sequence, Proxima becomes available as a Shell. The game offers a **SWITCH TO PROXIMA?** prompt immediately.

According to the source video, Proxima's Shell ability functions as a lightning lance that can:

- **Pull smaller enemies** toward Proxima, or
- **Pull Proxima toward larger enemies** that cannot be moved.
- The ability causes **lightning status buildup** on the target.

Proxima also has a **15% chance to mitigate incoming damage**, reducing damage by 50% and preventing stagger (transcript-stated values).

## FAQ

### Is Proxima missable?
The checked evidence does not indicate that Proxima is missable. The Shattered Beacon appears to remain accessible.

### Do I need a specific weapon or Shell to unlock Proxima?
No weapon or Shell requirement is shown in the checked evidence.

### Can I unlock Proxima during the Prologue?
No. The route requires reaching the open world, which becomes available after completing the Prologue.
