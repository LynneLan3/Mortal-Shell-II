**Revered Beacon** is a dungeon in the **Sester's Abbey / Sester's Gate** area of Mammon. Start from **Sester's Gate Beacon**, follow the lower route past the Rollerball courtyard and Bone Gate ledge, enter on the right, use solid cover against the tracking golden light, climb uphill to the tower, break the central light source, and collect **Cursed Child** in the upper area.

## Quick Answer

From **Sester's Gate Beacon**: take the northwestern stairs into the **Rollerball courtyard** → go onto the **western platform** → drop to the lower ledge near the **Bone Gate** → follow the path north and pass the **hammer-wielding enemy** → find the **Revered Beacon entrance on the right**. Inside, use walls for cover to avoid the rotating golden beam, move uphill generally counterclockwise toward the tower, attack the central pillar to disable the light, then collect the **Cursed Child** sidearm in the upper area.

## Revered Beacon Location

- **Area:** Sester's Abbey / Sester's Gate in Mammon.
- **Route anchor:** [Sester's Gate Beacon](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/) — use fast travel to reach it.
- **Landmarks on the approach:** northwestern stairs, Rollerball courtyard, western platform, Bone Gate lower ledge, hammer enemy, entrance on the right.

Do not treat this as a vague “somewhere near the Abbey” pickup. The entrance is on the documented lower route north of the Bone Gate ledge.

## How to Reach Revered Beacon

1. Fast travel to **Sester's Gate Beacon**.
2. Take the **northwestern stairs** from the Beacon up into the courtyard.
3. Enter the courtyard with the large **rolling / Rollerball enemy** and move onto the **western platform**.
4. Drop to the **lower ledge near the Bone Gate**.
5. Follow the path **north** and pass the **hammer-wielding enemy**.
6. The **Revered Beacon dungeon entrance** is on the **right**.

An alternate approach from **Abbey Entrance** can reach the same general area, but the Sester's Gate route above is the clearest documented path. For wider Beacon travel context, see [Beacons and fast travel](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).

The nearby [Black Needle guide](/mortal-shell-ii/black-needle/) also uses Sester's Gate as an anchor, but it leads to **Sester's Bastion** and a primary spear — not Revered Beacon.

## How the Golden Light Works

- A **rotating / tracking golden light** sits in the central / upper area of Revered Beacon.
- Remaining **exposed in the beam drains health continuously** while you are in line of sight.
- The light **tracks the player** and sweeps to follow exposed movement.
- **Solid walls and terrain cover break line of sight** and provide safe cover.
- The beam **can also damage enemies**, but documented reports say it does not kill foes quickly enough to rely on as a primary tactic.

Recommended movement logic: **wait behind cover → watch the beam sweep → move during a safe window → reach the next wall → continue uphill**.

Do not assume dodging grants immunity or that a specific resistance negates the beam unless you have confirmed that from another source.

## Revered Beacon Walkthrough

The interior traversal shape:

- entrance
- first safe cover
- move between walls
- uphill progression
- upper tower / light source

Use these landmark-based steps inside the dungeon:

1. **Entrance:** step inside and immediately seek a solid wall or alcove that blocks the central beam's sightline.
2. **First safe cover:** hold behind cover and observe the beam's rotation until a clear window opens.
3. **Move between walls:** when the beam sweeps away, move to the next piece of terrain or wall that breaks line of sight.
4. **Uphill progression:** continue moving generally uphill toward the taller tower area. The clearest documented route works **generally counterclockwise and uphill toward the tower**, but it is not the only possible path — use cover and sightlines to guide movement.
5. **Upper tower / light source:** when you reach the upper area, you will find the central pillar or structure emitting the golden light.

The main player question inside is “where do I go next?” If you lose orientation, return to cover, watch the beam sweep, and look for the next solid wall on the uphill path.

## How to Destroy the Beacon Light

- At the upper area, **attack the central structure / pillar / light source** repeatedly until the golden light is disabled.
- Do not expect a hidden interaction prompt or a mandatory weapon type — damage the structure until the beam stops.
- Once disabled, the hazardous beam no longer tracks or damages you in that area.

## Revered Beacon Reward

Clearing Revered Beacon and shutting off the light makes the **Cursed Child Sidearm** collectible in the upper area.

## Common Mistakes

- Standing in the open and trying to tank the beam — the light continuously drains health while in sight.
- Moving without watching the sweep — move only during a safe window after watching the beam rotate.
- Relying on the beam to kill regular enemies quickly — it damages foes but is not a reliable kill method.
- Ignoring route landmarks — use the Rollerball courtyard, western platform, Bone Gate ledge, and hammer enemy as navigation cues.
- Confusing Revered Beacon with **Sester's Bastion** — same Beacon anchor, different dungeon. See [Black Needle](/mortal-shell-ii/black-needle/) for the Bastion route.
