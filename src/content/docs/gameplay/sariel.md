**Sariel the Endless** is tied to the **Chamber of Becoming** route from **Silent Steps Beacon** in southern / southeastern Mammon. Progress through Sariel's outer encounter, enter the Chamber behind the large statue, then destroy all **four revival tablets** in the final arena so Sariel can be defeated permanently. After the fight, interact with the nearby Unknown Shell and complete Sariel's Memory to unlock the Shell.

## Where is Sariel in Mortal Shell 2?

Use **Silent Steps Beacon** as the main route anchor for Sariel.

The route leads from Silent Steps toward an outer Sariel encounter and then into the **Chamber of Becoming**.

Sariel appears more than once along this progression, but this guide does not force a disputed total encounter count. The important distinction is between the outer route and the final Chamber arena where the revival mechanic must be solved.

## How to reach Sariel from Silent Steps

Start at **Silent Steps Beacon** in southern / southeastern Mammon.

Use the nearby **Bone Gate / launch route** to reach the upper area where Sariel's outer encounter takes place.

Advance that encounter and continue until access opens or becomes available behind the **large statue**. That route leads into the Chamber of Becoming.

## How to enter the Chamber of Becoming

After progressing the outer Sariel encounter, move behind the large statue and continue into the Chamber.

Keep following the interior route until you reach the final arena.

This is the point where simply reducing Sariel's health is not enough: the four revival markers must also be dealt with.

## Why does Sariel keep reviving?

Sariel's revivals in the final arena are sustained by **four stone tablets / gravestones / revival markers**.

As long as the revival system is still active, defeating Sariel does not end the encounter permanently.

The solution is to destroy **all four markers**.

## How to destroy the four revival tablets

The four revival tablets can be destroyed in **any order**.

You do not need a numbered sequence. Focus on breaking all four before Sariel's final defeat.

Once all four are gone, Sariel can be defeated permanently and the Chamber reward sequence can continue.

## How to unlock Sariel's Shell

After Sariel is permanently defeated, the **Clockwork Scythe** is obtained in the reward sequence.

A nearby **Unknown Shell** is a separate Sariel reward. Interact with it to trigger Sariel's Memory, then complete the Memory / inhabit prompt to register **Sariel the Endless** as a playable Shell.

Defeating the boss alone is not the full Shell-unlock step; do not leave without interacting with the Unknown Shell.

For the weapon route itself, use the [Clockwork Scythe guide](/mortal-shell-ii/clockwork-scythe/).

## What else should you collect before leaving?

The Chamber of Becoming route has three distinct rewards worth separating:

- **Sariel's Shell** — unlocked through the Unknown Shell / Memory sequence
- **Clockwork Scythe** — the primary weapon tied to the Sariel reward sequence
- **Caged Hystrix** — a separate Sidearm pickup farther along the exit route

Caged Hystrix does not simply come with the Sariel reward. Use the [Caged Hystrix guide](/mortal-shell-ii/caged-hystrix/) before leaving the dungeon if you want the Sidearm as well.

The [Sidearms guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sidearms/) tracks the full launch Sidearm roster.

## Sariel FAQ

### Where is Sariel?

On the **Silent Steps → Chamber of Becoming** route in southern / southeastern Mammon.

### Which Beacon should I start from?

Use **Silent Steps Beacon**.

### Why does Sariel revive?

Four revival tablets / markers in the final arena sustain the revival mechanic.

### How many revival tablets are there?

Four.

### Do the tablets have to be broken in order?

No. They can be destroyed in any order; all four must be destroyed before the permanent defeat.

### Does defeating Sariel automatically unlock the Shell?

No. After the fight, interact with the nearby Unknown Shell and complete Sariel's Memory / inhabit sequence.

### What rewards are in Chamber of Becoming?

The route contains three distinct rewards: **Sariel's Shell, Clockwork Scythe, and Caged Hystrix**.
