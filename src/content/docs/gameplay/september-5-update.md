The **Sep. 5 update** (official Steam announcement: [New Update Out Now](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2584270/discussions/0/581681621355213340/)) introduces an Experimental Adaptive Difficulty option plus balance changes, bug fixes, and stability work described as **“Several crash fixes and optimisations.”** This page explains what changed and where to go next.

## Quick answer

- Adaptive Difficulty is optional and defaults to **OFF**.
- Toggle it at **Game > Game Settings > Adaptive Difficulty**.
- Sustained winning increases enemy damage, health, and break resistance.
- Sustained deaths reduce those values and limit the maximum share of player health a single attack can remove.
- The system can push enemies beyond the original highest standard difficulty after sustained success, but the announcement does not call this infinite scaling.
- The update does not claim that every crash, freeze, or performance issue is fixed.

## How Adaptive Difficulty changes the challenge

Adaptive Difficulty is a two-way dynamic challenge system. Repeated success can make encounters harder; repeated deaths can ease some pressure. Changes are gradual and move back toward normal balance over time.

The official announcement does not provide exact multipliers, numeric caps, or a complete interaction table for every mode or build. Community questions about Night Mode, boss damage, and break resistance are useful context, but player observations do not define official mechanics.

For the full explanation and editorial guidance, see [Mortal Shell 2 Adaptive Difficulty — How It Works](/mortal-shell-ii/adaptive-difficulty/).

## Balance, combat, and bug-fix status

The update contains balance changes and combat-related fixes. The official brief does not provide a new numeric stat table, so do not infer specific multipliers or boss-specific values from the announcement.

The stability wording is limited to **“Several crash fixes and optimisations.”** That indicates targeted fixes and performance work, not a universal crash or freeze fix. If you are seeing crashes, save-loading failures, stutter, or FPS degradation, use the [current PC crash guide](/mortal-shell-ii/crashing-pc/).

## Which guides may now be outdated?

Any guide that assumes completely fixed enemy stats may need a note about Adaptive Difficulty. Keep it OFF when you need fixed, repeatable behavior for build testing, speedrun practice, or controlled comparisons.

## FAQ

### Is Adaptive Difficulty on by default?

No. It is optional and defaults to **OFF**.

### Where do I toggle it?

Open **Game > Game Settings > Adaptive Difficulty**, then switch it on or off.

### Does it make Night Mode easier?

No. Night Mode does not become easier when Adaptive Difficulty scales downward.

### Can it exceed the normal maximum difficulty?

Yes. Sustained success can push enemies beyond the original highest standard difficulty. Exact caps and multipliers are not published, and the announcement does not claim infinite scaling.

### Did the update fix all crashes?

No. It includes several crash fixes and optimisations, but does not claim to resolve every crash, freeze, save/load failure, or performance problem. See the [PC crash guide](/mortal-shell-ii/crashing-pc/) for symptom-specific routing.
