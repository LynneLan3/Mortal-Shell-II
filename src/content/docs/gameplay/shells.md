## Quick answer: how NPC questlines advance

Mortal Shell II does not consistently present side quests as a complete journal. The reliable loop is: **exhaust the current dialogue, record the exact requested item or choice, rest once at a Beacon, return to the NPC, and confirm the next state before resting again**. The launch coverage below supports full or near-full routes for Gorf and Hilga, the Grisha Pup camp, Gragu, Baghead, Egon, and Genessa. Vlas has a verified opening request but a disputed later route, so this page stops rather than importing an unverified walkthrough.

| NPC or chain | Start point | Completion checkpoint | Reward, consequence, or uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Gorf + Hilga + Grom** | Gorf north of Widow's Overlook; Hilga's camp east of Mushroom Village Beacon | Give the **Poisoned Dagger** to Hilga, give Gorf **Common Moonshine**, then use the final Beacon-rest visits until the toads appear | Up to five **Marsh Pearls** are reported; do not skip Hilga's earlier scenes before the dagger handoff |
| **Grisha Pup + Hunter** | Hunter camp in Mushroom Village | Buy what you need, open the cage, return after a rest, and collect the follow-up reward | **Sheephead Totem** immediately and two **Glimpses** later; buy the Hunter's **Grisha Remnant** first because the camp's shop state becomes unsafe to rely on |
| **Gragu** | One-Legged Wolf Tavern | Retrieve the **Heart of Vatra** from the Temple of Vatra and hand it back | Unlocks Gragu's Shell; collect optional temple rewards before taking the Heart |
| **Baghead** | Desolate Peak, by the Illusionist's Cache route | Inspect the Special Moonshine and note, put it back, then return **empty-handed** and confirm the “nothing” choice | **Bag Holder** secret ending/credits sequence; treat it as an ending trigger, not a normal mid-run hand-in |
| **Egon** | Central tower of Castigator's Keep | Feed Gloom in repeated offerings until the completion scene | Current coverage conflicts on the threshold; 20,000 total and a 40,000 return are the conservative figures, but confirm the in-game prompt |
| **Sester Genessa** | Marrow Keep, then the Sester Secundus route | Recover the required item, defeat Sester Secundus, return it to Genessa, and complete her Memory | Item name conflicts as **Sester's Censer / Sester's Chalice**; the handoff and Memory are the stable parts |
| **Vlas** | Injured beside a broken cart in the Outskirts of Mammon | Only the missing-cat request is consistently documented in the bounded sources | Later movement, key, cat, and reward steps conflict; keep this route explicitly incomplete |
| **Zhirelle / Thestus** | Marrow Keep | These are system-state interactions rather than ordinary item questlines | Zhirelle handles Shell discovery/Bonding; Thestus toggles Night Mode after the Gloombound Flame sequence |

## Safe order for NPC questlines

Use this order when you want to reduce avoidable lockouts and skipped scenes:

1. **Before opening the Grisha Pup's cage**, buy the Hunter's Grisha Remnant and any other stock you want. Sources differ on whether the trader is described as dead or simply unavailable afterward; the stable rule is not to rely on the shop after the camp turns hostile.
2. **Finish Hilga and Grom's available dialogue before giving Hilga the Poisoned Dagger.** Current walkthroughs report that the handoff advances her story and can skip scenes that have not been viewed. Rest once between checks rather than stacking multiple rests.
3. **For Gragu**, take the Berserker's Stone and other optional Temple of Vatra rewards before removing the Heart of Vatra from the statue. The Heart handoff is the Shell unlock; the optional loot is a separate objective.
4. **Treat Baghead as an ending choice.** Activate a nearby Beacon and finish any current-cycle work you care about before confirming the empty-handed request.
5. **Do not spend all Gloom on Egon until the prompt is clear in your build.** The total reported by current sources is inconsistent, even though the repeated-donation loop and completion scene agree.
6. **Finish active NPC routes before Send Ova / New Game Plus.** Current progression coverage treats world-side quests as current-cycle work; do not assume an unfinished NPC state will carry over.

### The universal reset rule

If an NPC appears stuck, exhaust every line, rest at the nearest Beacon once, and return. Walking away without resting is not a reliable reset for Gorf, Hilga, or other stateful encounters. After a hand-in, check the NPC's new location or note before performing another reset. Preserve the exact item name shown by the game; similarly named keys, weapons, censers, and chalices are not interchangeable by default.

## Gorf, Hilga, and Grom: the linked Fainweald chain

Start with **Gorf**, the coughing toad north of **Widow's Overlook Beacon**, then work through **Hilga and Grom's** camp east of **Mushroom Village Beacon**. This is one linked chain, not two independent errands.

### Gorf's visits

The stable sequence across current coverage is:

1. Exhaust Gorf's dialogue and answer the first item request, beginning with a **Weltcap** in the checked launch guides.
2. Rest at the nearby Beacon and return after each state change. Later visits use **Common Moonshine** in the current evidence set; the exact number of early mushroom/moonshine requests is described differently across walkthroughs.
3. Continue checking the clearing until Gorf's injury sequence produces the **Poisoned Dagger**. Do not sell or discard it: the item description points to a knowledgeable local, which is Hilga.

Current reporting describes up to **five Marsh Pearls** across the full route, with each Pearl selling to Merrick for 2,000 Coins. Treat that as launch-guide evidence rather than a formal in-game reward table; confirm each inventory change before the next reset.

### Hilga's handoff and the ending state

Visit Hilga and Grom in the fenced camp east of Mushroom Village. Exhaust their available dialogue and scenes before handing over the Poisoned Dagger. Give Hilga the dagger, rest once, and return to find her note or changed position. Follow it back to Gorf, give him the requested **Common Moonshine**, then rest and revisit the clearing.

The reported completion state is: Gorf recovers, Hilga changes form and joins him, and a final rest reveals **four young toads** in the clearing. That scene is the practical confirmation that the paired chain has stopped advancing. If the chain stalls, check the nearby corruption/travel gate and the exact prompt; one current guide reports that access near Hilga can depend on having enough Ova for Mether's Breath, but this page does not convert that report into a fixed universal threshold.

## Grisha Pup: shop first, cage second

The **Grisha Pup** is in a Hunter camp in **Mushroom Village**, near the Hall of Illusions route. Before opening the cage, speak with the Hunter trader and buy the **Grisha Remnant** and any consumable stock you want. The cage can be opened by buying the key, defeating the Hunters for it, or breaking the padlock with a melee attack; the free lock-break route is the lowest-cost option, but it turns the camp hostile.

Interact with the freed Pup to receive the **Sheephead Totem**, then rest at a Beacon and return. Current launch coverage reports a follow-up interaction that gives **two Glimpses** and places the Grisha Matron in the camp. Do not attack before collecting that follow-up reward. The sources disagree in how they describe the trader's final state, so the actionable certainty is simply: **buy the shop's unique stock before opening the cage**.

## Gragu: Heart of Vatra delivery quest

Speak to **Gragu** inside the **One-Legged Wolf Tavern** until he asks for the **Heart of Vatra**. Travel to the **Temple of Vatra** east of the tavern, collect the optional rewards first, take the Heart from the corrupted statue, survive the ambush through the side route, and return to Gragu. Handing over the Heart completes the NPC handoff and unlocks Gragu's Shell.

The existing [Gragu guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gragu/) owns the route-specific details. This page keeps the questline boundary clear: the Berserker's Stone and other Temple rewards are optional loot, while the Heart is the actual Gragu objective. The exact corruption/travel gate for reaching the eastern route remains deliberately unquantified in the existing evidence.

## Baghead: the empty-handed secret ending

Find **Baghead** at **Desolate Peak**, on the route in front of the **Illusionist's Cache** dungeon. Exhaust his dialogue, sit with his followers when prompted, inspect the **Special Moonshine** and the nearby note, and then put the Special Moonshine back. Return to Baghead with empty hands and confirm that you are giving him **nothing**.

That choice triggers the **Bag Holder** joke ending and rolls the credits. Current coverage reports that the run can continue afterward, but this is still an ending trigger rather than a routine reward hand-in. Make a Beacon/save checkpoint and finish any other current-cycle NPC, Shell, or trophy work first. See the [Endings guide](/mortal-shell-ii/endings/) for the separate main-ending boundary.

## Egon: repeated Gloom offering

**Egon** is in the central tower of **Castigator's Keep**. He accepts repeated **1,000-Gloom** offerings and stores progress between visits. The strongest convergent current reporting says that reaching **20,000 Gloom total** causes Egon to pass away and returns **40,000 Gloom**, for a net gain of 20,000.

There is a real launch-source conflict: one guide reports a **10,000-Gloom** cap, while newer guides report **20,000**. Do not treat either number as an official patch note. Use 20,000 as the conservative amount to plan for, make offerings in increments, and trust the completion scene/prompt in the build you are playing. The related [trophies guide](/mortal-shell-ii/trophies/) keeps the reward threshold marked as source-dependent.

## Vlas: verified opening, unverified later chain

The bounded sources place **Vlas** injured beside a broken cart in the **Outskirts of Mammon**. He asks for help finding his missing cat. The unofficial [Mortal Shell II database](https://mortalshelldb.com/quests/vlas-quest) labels the chain **A Sweet Boy** and begins with recovering Vlas's wares, while another current walkthrough claims a later cage-and-key route. Those accounts do not establish one reproducible, corroborated sequence.

Therefore, the reliable instruction is limited: exhaust Vlas's opening dialogue, note the exact request and location, rest and return when the game gives you a reason to recheck him, and do not promise a cat location, final reward, missable cutoff, or completion state from the conflicting guides. This page intentionally stops at the verified opening rather than importing first-game Vlas behavior or a single-source sequel route.

## Genessa, Zhirelle, and Thestus: NPC systems with quest consequences

### Sester Genessa

Genessa is both an NPC and a permanent Shell. Follow the [Sester Genessa guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sester-genessa/) to the **Sester Secundus** encounter, recover the required handoff item, return it to Genessa in Marrow Keep, and complete her first Memory. Current sources call that item **Sester's Censer** or **Sester's Chalice**; keep both names visible and follow the in-game prompt instead of treating the discrepancy as two separate requirements.

Genessa's Bonding interaction is also an exception in current coverage: check Genessa/Stray Sester in person rather than assuming every Shell uses the standard Zhirelle flow. The route is complete when the Shell prompt and first Memory are available, not merely when Sester Secundus is defeated.

### Zhirelle the Shellkeeper

**Zhirelle** is a system NPC, not a conventional item quest. She can reveal most Shell locations and handles Bonding for the permanent Shell roster. After [Balance Patch 1](/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/), Shell-location reveals use Gloom rather than Glimpses in the checked PC coverage, while Glimpses remain the scarce Bond resource. The existing Shell roster and Bonding guidance below remains intact.

### Thestus and Night Mode

**Thestus** likewise changes the world state instead of paying out a normal side-quest reward. The existing [Gloombound Flame guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gloombound-flame/) documents the bounded sequence: obtain the Gloombound Flame, place it at the Marrow Keep lantern/brazier, then speak with Thestus to **Call forth the Night**. Night Mode is reversible through Thestus and is not a universal prerequisite for NPC questlines.

## Quick answer: how many Shells are there?

There are **nine Shells in Mortal Shell II** if you count the prologue Shell: **Harros, Tiel, Proxima, Gragu, Eredrim, Smert, Lazlo, Sariel, and Genessa**. **Harros** is available during the prologue but is taken away when that opening section ends. The other **eight Shells are permanent unlocks**.

For every permanent Shell, the practical loop is:

1. Reach the Shell's body or encounter.
2. Interact with it and complete the first Shell Memory or unlock sequence.
3. Return to the Shellkeeper at Marrow Keep when you want to raise that Shell's Bond.
4. Spend **Glimpses** on Bonding to open higher perk tiers. Shell-map reveals are a separate **Gloom** expense after [Balance Patch 1](/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/).

This page is a route and decision guide, not an objective S/A/B combat tier list. Current sources support a few playstyle and timing recommendations, but not a complete, tested stat comparison for every Shell.

## Fainweald Shell routes

### Tiel: Widow's Overlook and the Rash Memory

Start from **Widow's Overlook Beacon** and follow the local landmarks toward the wooden enclosure and large tree. The shallow grave is marked by **two torches**. Interact with Tiel, then enter the nearby building through its side window and take the flask from the desk to complete the **Rash Memory**.

The exact compass description varies between current guides, so use the beacon, enclosure, tree, torches, and grave as the route checks. The local [Tiel location guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tiel/) does not establish defeating the nearby Great Arbiter as a requirement.

Tiel is the clearest early **mobility/evasion** recommendation in the current evidence: the local guide describes a mobile, Shadow-focused identity, and an independent launch guide places Tiel among the earliest Shells and recommends him for early-to-mid game. That is a use-case recommendation, not proof that Tiel wins every build matchup.

### Proxima: Shattered Beacon

After the prologue, travel from **Marrow Keep** through the northern transporter to **Widow's Overlook**, continue to **Blackridge Pass**, and look for the elevated **Shattered Beacon** route. Enter the dungeon and interact with the Unknown Shell.

The locally checked Memory sequence is:

- **Take the Offering**
- **Use Biosampler on stranger**
- **Answer the Stranger**
- **Inform Merrick**

The game then displays **NEW SHELL DISCOVERED — PROXIMA** and allows an immediate switch. The local [Proxima guide](/mortal-shell-ii/proxima/) supports the route as an early post-prologue option and does not show a specific weapon, boss, key, Ova, Mether's Breath, or Shell requirement. It also records Proxima's lightning-lance pull utility and a transcript-stated damage-mitigation value; those details come from one walkthrough and should not be treated as a complete balance-tested build.

### Gragu: Heart of Vatra

Find **Gragu inside the One-Legged Wolf Tavern** and exhaust his dialogue until he asks for the **Heart of Vatra**. Travel east to the **Temple of Vatra**, take the Heart from the corrupted statue at the top, use the alternate exit when the route becomes hostile, and return to the tavern.

Giving Gragu the Heart triggers the unlock event and leaves his body available to inhabit. The [Gragu guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gragu/) separates the optional Berserker's Stone from the Shell objective: it is not required for Gragu.

The route may be inaccessible until wider Fainweald corruption and travel progression has opened. Current local evidence intentionally does **not** assert a fixed Ova count, so do not treat a reported number as a universal requirement.

### Eredrim: Citadel of Penance

Follow the **Citadel of Penance** route until **The Warden** encounter. Defeat The Warden, finish the encounter state, and interact with Eredrim to choose the inhabit option. The [Eredrim guide](/mortal-shell-ii/eredrim/) supports this direct route without requiring a paid Shell-location hint.

Independent coverage describes Eredrim as a mid-to-late game choice, but the current evidence does not justify filling in a full stat table or calling him objectively strongest. Use Eredrim when the route, timing, and your tested Bond upgrades fit your run; keep the broader build claim provisional.

### Smert: Prophet's Rest

Use **Outskirts of Nochte Beacon** as the route anchor for **Prophet's Rest**. Activate all **three blood pools** around the ritual area, then interact with the Unknown Shell to start **The Convert**. Complete the Memory route and defeat the **Hierarch** to unlock Smert.

The local [Smert guide](/mortal-shell-ii/smert/) records two supported ways to activate a pool: kill an enemy inside it or make a Blood Offering. A Blood Offering can eject you from your Shell, so recover it before attempting another sacrifice. Check the pools on arrival rather than assuming all three start empty.

Smert is the clearest current **Miracle/time-control** use case, but the site does not claim a complete Miracle table or a universal high-risk/high-reward tier position.

## Mammon Shell routes

### Lazlo: Vellen and the High Lord's Courtyard crypt

In Mammon, use **High Lord's Courtyard** as the route anchor. Reach the keep and defeat **Vellen, Lord of Mammon**; Lazlo's remains are in the crypt or final chamber beyond that encounter. Interact with the remains and finish Lazlo's first Memory before treating the Shell as permanently unlocked.

No standalone Lazlo route page is present in this site checkout yet, so this hub keeps the claim at region, landmark, boss, and crypt level. It does not invent a room coordinate, an exact compass path, or Lazlo's stats/build.

### Sariel: Chamber of Becoming

Start at **Silent Steps Beacon** and follow the outer Sariel route into the **Chamber of Becoming**. In the final arena, destroy all **four revival tablets/markers** before expecting a permanent defeat. After the fight, interact with the nearby Unknown Shell and complete Sariel's Memory; defeating the boss alone is not the complete Shell unlock.

The route also contains the **Clockwork Scythe** and **Caged Hystrix**, which are separate rewards. Use the [Sariel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sariel/), [Clockwork Scythe guide](/mortal-shell-ii/clockwork-scythe/), and [Caged Hystrix guide](/mortal-shell-ii/caged-hystrix/) when you want all three objectives.

### Genessa: Sester Secundus and the return handoff

Genessa starts as an NPC in **Marrow Keep**. Follow the unlock route to the Mammon-side encounter with **Sester Secundus**, defeat that boss, and return the required item to Genessa in Marrow Keep. Complete Genessa's Memory and then use the Shell prompt in the training grounds.

Current sources disagree on the returned item's name: some call it **Sester's Censer**, while others call it **Sester's Chalice**. The local [Sester Genessa guide](/mortal-shell-ii/sester-genessa/) preserves that conflict. Follow the in-game prompt in the build you are playing rather than assuming the two labels describe separate prerequisites.

Genessa is also the clearest example of a route whose Bonding interaction differs in current coverage: independent launch guides say to raise her Bond by talking to Genessa rather than using the ordinary Shellkeeper flow. Treat that as a supported route-specific instruction, and verify the prompt after the handoff.

## Shell Memories, Bonding, and resources

Finding the body is not always the same as unlocking the Shell. Current launch guides agree on the following progression model:

- Interact with the remains or post-encounter Shell prompt.
- Complete the first Shell Memory or route-specific Memory sequence.
- The permanent Shell becomes available after that completion, not merely after reaching the landmark.
- Use **Glimpses** to raise the Shell's **Bond** and open higher perk tiers.
- Shell-specific abilities/weapons are not the same as shared equipment. Current coverage says Seals, Tarstones, and other shared items can be evaluated across Shells, but local pages do not validate a full cross-Shell build matrix.

The standard Bonding interaction is associated with the Shellkeeper at **Marrow Keep**. Current coverage treats **Genessa** as the exception to check in person: her Bonding is described through dialogue with Genessa. See the [Glimpses guide](/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/) for the resource boundary and do not spend Glimpses as if they were ordinary Gloom.

## Missables and conflict notes

### Harros is the real permanent missable

Harros is a prologue Shell, not one of the eight permanent collection Shells. Current launch guides describe losing him when the prologue ends as irreversible. If you are seeking an all-permanent collection, do not count Harros as a permanent unlock.

For the other eight, current evidence supports persistent collection after the first Memory, but this hub does not claim that every optional dialogue choice, Bond prompt, or alternate ending interaction has identical outcomes. Finish the Memory and confirm the Shell menu before leaving a route.

### Do not confuse a route gate with a fixed number

Gragu's eastern Fainweald route may depend on corruption-clearing and broader travel progression. Current local evidence avoids a fixed Ova threshold. Similarly, a paid location reveal and the physical Shell route are different things.

### Compass wording is not a coordinate

Tiel and Proxima descriptions differ slightly between sources on whether the first turn is described as north, southeast, or a turn from the Beacon. Use the named Beacon, dungeon, enclosure, grave, and boss landmarks. This page does not invent map coordinates.

### Genessa's item name is unsettled in current coverage

Keep **Sester's Censer / Sester's Chalice** as a deliberate uncertainty until the in-game prompt or a stronger primary source resolves the naming. The unlock dependency—Sester Secundus, return to Genessa, Memory—is the stable part.

## Balance Patch 1 and Shell planning

Balance Patch 1 moved **Shell-map reveals from Glimpses to Gloom** and refunds Glimpses previously spent on those reveals at the Shell Keeper's alcove in Blackmarrow. That matters when planning a collection: map discovery no longer competes with Bonding for the same Glimpse budget.

The patch also changed enemy, weapon, Tarstone, and economy balance. Therefore, old beta or pre-patch “best Shell” claims should be treated as dated opinions unless their test conditions are clear. See [Balance Patch 1](/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/) and [Glimpses](/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/) for the site's current resource notes.

## FAQ

### How many Shells are in Mortal Shell 2?

There are **nine total Shells**: Harros is the prologue-only Shell, and Tiel, Proxima, Gragu, Eredrim, Smert, Lazlo, Sariel, and Genessa are the eight permanent Shells.

### Which Shell should you get first?

Start with **Tiel or Proxima** after the prologue because both have early Fainweald routes. Tiel has the clearest supported mobile/evasive use case; Proxima has the clearest locally documented lightning utility. Neither is confirmed as universally strongest.

### Is Harros permanently missable?

Harros is available in the prologue and is lost when the prologue ends. Current launch coverage treats that loss as irreversible. He is not part of the eight permanent Shell collection.

### Do I have to complete a Shell Memory?

Yes, for the permanent Shell loop. Finding or reaching a body is not the whole unlock: interact with the Shell and complete its first Memory or route-specific unlock sequence, then confirm the Shell becomes available.

### How does Shell Bonding work?

Spend **Glimpses** to raise a Shell's Bond and unlock higher perk tiers. The standard interaction is at the Shellkeeper in Marrow Keep; current coverage describes Genessa as a dialogue-based exception to check with Genessa herself.

### Is there a verified Shell tier list?

No. This page gives evidence-bounded use cases and progression timing rather than an S/A/B combat ranking. Stats, builds, and performance can change with Bonding, weapons, Seals, Tarstones, and patches.

### Do Shell-map reveals still cost Glimpses?

No. After Balance Patch 1, Shell-map reveals cost **Gloom** instead, and eligible Glimpses spent on those reveals are refunded in Blackmarrow.
