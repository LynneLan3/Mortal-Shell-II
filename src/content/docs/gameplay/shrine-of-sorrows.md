The **Shrine of Sorrows** is a **Night Mode-only** dungeon near Mushroom Village. Start from **Mushroom Village Beacon**, accept the invitation at the cocoon-like entrance, collect the **Moonlight, Sunlight, and Starlight Sonatas**, solve the mounted-head / frozen-world puzzle, then use the resulting **Alluring Mushrooms** to reach the basement sequence. Completing the route rewards a **Blackmarrow Key** and **Tooth of Nochte**.

## How to unlock the Shrine of Sorrows

Shrine of Sorrows is only available while **Night Mode** is active.

If you reach the area during the day, the location gives you a clue that the secret is hidden by daylight rather than opening the dungeon route.

Use the [Gloombound Flame and Night Mode guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gloombound-flame/) if you have not enabled Night Mode yet.

## How to reach Shrine of Sorrows from Mushroom Village

With Night Mode active:

1. Fast travel to **Mushroom Village Beacon**.
2. Take the path to the right.
3. Pass the dancing villagers.
4. Cross the first wooden bridge.
5. Pass the bonfire.
6. Cross the second wooden bridge.
7. Approach the **cocoon-like structure**.
8. Interact with it and accept the invitation.

That opens the Shrine of Sorrows route.

For general travel routing, use the [Beacons and fast travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).

## Optional: how to open the three-offering Dorsalite chest

Before entering the house, you can open an optional chest by activating **three candle offerings** around the exterior area.

Look for them:

- near the **broken shed** beside the chest
- farther along the path **past the shed**
- near the **well**

Activate all three offerings, then return to the chest to collect **Dorsalite**.

This chest is optional and is separate from the three-Sonata progression inside the house.

## Where to get the Tempered Iron Key

Inside the house, find the seated figure and read the nearby note.

Then interact with the **cauldron** to obtain the **Tempered Iron Key**.

Use that key on the first locked room.

## Where to find Moonlight and Sunlight Sonata

The first two books are inside the house.

- **Moonlight Sonata** — in the first room opened with the Tempered Iron Key, on the corpse / table area.
- **Sunlight Sonata** — in the second room.

Collect both before moving on to the mounted-head puzzle for the third book.

## How to solve the head puzzle and get Starlight Sonata

After the banging / headless-corpse event, leave the house and head toward the tree.

Read the note associated with the **mounted head**.

The puzzle uses repeated full rotations:

1. Re-check the mounted head.
2. Make a full **360-degree turn**.
3. Re-check the head again.
4. Repeat until the environment shifts into the **frozen version** of the area.
5. Find **Starlight Sonata** near the gravestone.
6. Repeat the head / rotation sequence to return.

Do not rely on a fixed universal spin count. The practical rule is to keep repeating the full-turn and head-check sequence until the environment changes.

## What to do with all three Sonatas

Return to the house with:

- Moonlight Sonata
- Sunlight Sonata
- Starlight Sonata

Place all three into the box.

This rewards **Alluring Mushrooms**.

Take the Alluring Mushrooms to the cauldron, place them inside, and drink the resulting concoction.

You are moved outside into a darker transformed version of the area.

## Where to find the Basement Key

After drinking the mushroom concoction, return to the house.

The **Basement Key** is now available on the table.

Take it, unlock the basement, and descend.

The basement contains the final witches' ritual sequence that completes the Shrine of Sorrows route.

## Shrine of Sorrows rewards

Completing the dungeon rewards:

- **Blackmarrow Key**
- **Tooth of Nochte**

The Blackmarrow Key belongs to the wider Shade-chest collection system in Marrow Keep. See the [Blackmarrow Keys guide](/mortal-shell-ii/blackmarrow-keys/) for how those keys are used.

**Tooth of Nochte** can be sold to **Merrick** for Gold.

## Shrine of Sorrows FAQ

### Why can't I enter Shrine of Sorrows during the day?

The dungeon is tied to **Night Mode**. Enable Night Mode first, then return to the Mushroom Village route.

### Which Beacon should I use?

Use **Mushroom Village Beacon**.

### Where is the Tempered Iron Key?

Inside the house. Read the note by the seated figure, then interact with the cauldron to obtain it.

### Where are all three Sonata books?

- Moonlight Sonata: first room opened with the Tempered Iron Key
- Sunlight Sonata: second room
- Starlight Sonata: frozen version of the exterior area, near the gravestone

### How does the head / frozen-world puzzle work?

Use repeated full 360-degree turns while re-checking the mounted head. Continue until the environment shifts, collect Starlight Sonata, then repeat the sequence to return.

### What do Alluring Mushrooms do?

Place all three Sonatas into the box to receive them, then put them into the cauldron and drink the concoction to advance the dungeon into its final state.

### Where is the Basement Key?

After the mushroom sequence, return to the house and collect the Basement Key from the table.

### Does Shrine of Sorrows give a Blackmarrow Key?

Yes. Completing the Shrine route rewards a **Blackmarrow Key**.
