Mortal Shell 2 has **eight Sidearms** in the launch collection: **Naylshotte, Forgotten Crossbow, Salvaged Trebuchaxe, Troubadour's Lute, Triarch Repeater, Ballistazooka, Cursed Child, and Caged Hystrix**. Collecting all eight is associated with the **Guns. Lots of Guns.** trophy / achievement.

## How many Sidearms are in Mortal Shell 2?

There are eight:

1. Naylshotte
2. Forgotten Crossbow
3. Salvaged Trebuchaxe
4. Troubadour's Lute
5. Triarch Repeater
6. Ballistazooka
7. Cursed Child
8. Caged Hystrix

This Hub owns the roster and compact location checklist. Detailed dungeon walkthroughs can live on individual Sidearm pages as those spokes are added.

## Naylshotte

**Naylshotte** is the Prologue / starter Sidearm acquisition.

It is the roster entry you should already have from the opening progression before hunting the seven world-location Sidearms.

## Forgotten Crossbow

The **Forgotten Crossbow** is in the **Flooded Village**.

Use the **Damp Key** for the locked pickup room. **Blackridge Pass** is a practical route anchor for this objective.

Use the detailed [Forgotten Crossbow guide](/mortal-shell-ii/forgotten-crossbow/) for the Flooded Village route, Damp Key landmark, and return path to the locked room.

## Salvaged Trebuchaxe

The **Salvaged Trebuchaxe** is in **Ravaged Hideout**, south / southeast of **Gloomshade Grove**.

Access is tied to the wider **Bloodcursed Lithopod** route.

## Troubadour's Lute

The **Troubadour's Lute** is inside the **One-Legged Wolf Tavern**, around the stage area.

The tavern is also where the [Gragu guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gragu/) begins, so the two objectives can share the same visit.

## Triarch Repeater

The **Triarch Repeater** is in **Blackwell Cavern** in Mammon.

Use **Castigator's Keep** as a practical route anchor.

[Balance Patch 1](/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/) removed the minimum Resolve requirement to fire the Triarch Repeater.

## Ballistazooka

The **Ballistazooka** is tied to **Sentry's Grave** near the **Gate of Mammon**.

Its reward is associated with the **Subjugated Guardian** encounter.

## Cursed Child

The **Cursed Child** is on the **Revered Beacon** route near the **Sester's Gate / Abbey** area.

This Hub keeps that location at the route-summary level rather than inventing a longer dungeon path.

Use the detailed [Cursed Child guide](/mortal-shell-ii/cursed-child/) for the Revered Beacon golden-light route and upper light-source puzzle.

## Caged Hystrix

The **Caged Hystrix** is in the **Chamber of Becoming** after the Sariel route.

It is a separate pickup near the dungeon exit, not something to assume was collected with Sariel or the Clockwork Scythe.

Use the dedicated [Caged Hystrix guide](/mortal-shell-ii/caged-hystrix/) for the post-Sariel pickup route.

Balance Patch 1 also removed the minimum Resolve requirement to fire Caged Hystrix.

## How to upgrade Sidearms at the Tarforge

**Obsidian Lathe** unlocks Sidearm enhancement at the **Tarforge**.

Use the [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/) for the station and its five upgrade components.

This Hub does not assume that every Sidearm follows an identical upgrade path.

For primary melee weapons, use the separate [Weapons guide](/mortal-shell-ii/weapons/).

## How to unlock Guns. Lots of Guns.

The **Guns. Lots of Guns.** trophy / achievement is associated with collecting all eight Sidearms:

- Naylshotte
- Forgotten Crossbow
- Salvaged Trebuchaxe
- Troubadour's Lute
- Triarch Repeater
- Ballistazooka
- Cursed Child
- Caged Hystrix

Use the checklist above to identify the missing entry in your collection.

## Sidearms FAQ

### How many Sidearms are there?

Eight in the launch collection.

### What is the first Sidearm?

**Naylshotte**, acquired during the Prologue / starter progression.

### Which Sidearm is in One-Legged Wolf Tavern?

**Troubadour's Lute**.

### Which Sidearm needs the Damp Key?

**Forgotten Crossbow** in the Flooded Village.

### Which Sidearm comes from Sariel's route?

**Caged Hystrix**, found separately near the Chamber of Becoming exit.

### What unlocks Sidearm upgrades?

The **Obsidian Lathe**, which unlocks Sidearm enhancement at the Tarforge.

### What did Balance Patch 1 change for Caged Hystrix and Triarch Repeater?

It removed the minimum Resolve requirement to fire both Sidearms.

### How do you unlock Guns. Lots of Guns.?

Collect all eight Sidearms. The full collection is associated with the **Guns. Lots of Guns.** trophy / achievement.
