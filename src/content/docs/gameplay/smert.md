**Smert the Apostate** is found at **Prophet's Rest** in western Fainweald, near the **Outskirts of Nochte** route. To unlock Smert, activate all three blood pools around the ritual altar, interact with the Unknown Shell to start **The Convert**, then complete the Memory and defeat the **Hierarch**.

## Where is Smert in Mortal Shell 2?

Smert's unlock starts at **Prophet's Rest** in western Fainweald.

Use **Outskirts of Nochte Beacon** as the practical route anchor for the final approach. At Prophet's Rest, look for the ritual area built around a central altar / corpse area with **three circular blood pools**.

Your goal is to make sure all three pools end in their active red state.

## Fast route from Outskirts of Nochte

Start from **Outskirts of Nochte Beacon** and follow the western Fainweald route back toward Prophet's Rest.

The most useful landmark at the destination is the ritual itself: three circular pools arranged around the central altar area.

Once you reach the site, check the pools before doing anything else. A supported route may already show one pool active, so do not assume you must personally fill three pools from empty.

For broader travel coverage, use the [Beacons and fast travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).

## How the three blood pools work

There are **three blood pools**, and all three must be active before the Smert unlock can move forward.

You have two supported ways to activate an inactive pool:

- kill an enemy while it is inside the circle
- make a **Blood Offering**

If one pool is already red when you arrive, leave it alone and focus on the inactive circles.

Once all three pools are active, the ritual area / altar changes red, indicating that the next interaction is ready.

## How to use Blood Offerings safely

A Blood Offering can activate a pool, but it also ejects you from your current Shell and leaves you exposed.

If you use this method, recover your Shell before attempting another self-sacrifice.

You do not need to rely only on Blood Offerings. Enemy kills inside the circles are also a supported way to activate the ritual pools.

## How to start The Convert Memory

After all three pools are active, go to the central altar and interact with the **Unknown Shell**.

This starts **The Convert**, Smert's Shell Memory.

The blood-pool ritual is the setup; completing The Convert is the actual unlock challenge that follows.

## How to finish The Convert and unlock Smert

Progress through **The Convert** until you reach the **Hierarch**.

Defeat the Hierarch to complete the supported Memory route and unlock **Smert the Apostate** as a playable Shell.

This unlock is associated with the **My Brether** trophy / achievement.

Use the [Shells guide](/mortal-shell-ii/shells/) for the site's wider Shell index.

## What kind of Shell is Smert?

Smert is a higher-risk Shell built around **time-control and Miracle-focused gameplay**.

That identity is useful for deciding whether you want to unlock him early, but this location page does not turn the route into a full Smert build or stat guide.

If you want other documented Shell routes first, see [Tiel](/mortal-shell-ii/tiel/) or [Proxima](/mortal-shell-ii/proxima/).

## Smert FAQ

### Where is Smert?

At **Prophet's Rest** in western Fainweald, near the Outskirts of Nochte route.

### Which Beacon should I use?

Use **Outskirts of Nochte Beacon** as the practical route anchor for Prophet's Rest.

### How many blood pools are there?

Three. All three need to be active before you can start The Convert.

### Do all three pools start empty?

Not necessarily. A supported route may show one pool already active when you arrive. Check the ritual and activate only the pools that are still inactive.

### Can enemies activate the blood pools?

Yes. Killing an enemy while it is inside a pool is a supported activation method.

### What happens when you make a Blood Offering?

It can activate a pool, but it ejects you from your current Shell. Recover the Shell before attempting another offering.

### What is The Convert?

The Convert is the Shell Memory that begins after the blood-pool ritual is ready and you interact with the Unknown Shell.

### How do you unlock My Brether?

Complete Smert's unlock route through The Convert. The Smert unlock is associated with the **My Brether** trophy / achievement.
