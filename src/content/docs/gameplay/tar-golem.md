## How to Beat the Tar Golem and Get No, You Still Can't Win

The **Tar Golem** is the major scripted encounter at the end of Mortal Shell II's Prologue. Its missable trophy, **No, You Still Can't Win**, requires you to **take the Tar Golem to its minimum health**. If that opportunity is missed on the Prologue run, New Game Plus does not replay the encounter, so a fresh save is the clean retry route.

## Where is the Tar Golem?

The Tar Golem is reached through the **Disciple's Grotto** route near the end of the Prologue.

Use **Disciple's Grotto Beacon** as the practical route anchor, then continue through the rocky path toward the arena.

## How to reach the Tar Golem from Disciple's Grotto

From Disciple's Grotto Beacon:

1. Continue onward / down from the Beacon.
2. Use the **updraft / launch route** to reach the higher path.
3. Continue through the rocky route toward the arena.
4. Pass through the wider **Bone Gate** section.
5. Enter the arena and use the encounter trigger that awakens the Tar Golem.

The encounter is part of the Prologue progression. The trophy condition, however, is a separate objective you can miss.

## How to get No, You Still Can't Win

The trophy requirement is:

> **Take the Tar Golem to his Minimum Health**

Use that wording literally.

Your goal is to reduce the Tar Golem to its **minimum-health state** during the encounter. Do not assume the trophy requires a conventional permanent boss kill, an extra finishing hit, or a specific universal HP number.

The Prologue continues after the Tar Golem sequence whether or not you satisfy the trophy condition, so achievement hunters should treat the fight as a one-time objective for that run.

## How to fight the Tar Golem safely

The Tar Golem uses heavy axe attacks and punishes long, greedy offense.

A safer approach is:

- use **Guard / Perfect Guard** against attacks that can be guarded
- dodge attacks that are clearly unblockable or too dangerous to defend normally
- keep enough stamina / defensive resources available to react
- use short punish windows rather than long attack strings
- prioritize reaching the minimum-health state over trying to force a fast finish

This guide does not assign frame-perfect parry timings or a mandatory weapon / Shell.

If you are also working on a later parry trophy, keep that separate from this encounter; the [Peter's Perfect Parry guide](/mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/) covers the Nameless Captive challenge.

## What happens if you fail the attempt?

If the Tar Golem sequence ends without **No, You Still Can't Win** unlocking, the same Prologue encounter is no longer available to replay normally on that save.

The clean recovery method is:

1. create a **fresh save / character**
2. replay the Prologue
3. return to the Tar Golem
4. take it to minimum health before the sequence ends

Some players may use platform-specific save backups for repeated attempts, but this guide does not make save manipulation the default method.

## Can you retry the Tar Golem in New Game Plus?

**No. New Game Plus does not replay the Prologue Tar Golem encounter.**

NG+ begins at **Marrow Keep**, after the Prologue section that contains this fight.

That means starting NG+ is not a replacement for a missed Tar Golem trophy attempt. Use a fresh separate save instead.

For the wider reset / carry-over rules, see the [New Game Plus guide](/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/).

## Should you fight Tar Golem before skipping the Prologue?

If you care about achievements or trophies, do not rely on the Prologue skip for the only save you want to use for opening completion goals.

The skip bypasses two confirmed Prologue trophy opportunities:

- **Mid Summer?** — accept the flower crown interaction and enter the Festival
- **No, You Still Can't Win** — take the Tar Golem to minimum health

You can still recover either by replaying the Prologue on a fresh save, but NG+ does not restore these opening encounters.

See the [Skip Prologue guide](/mortal-shell-ii/skip-prologue/) for the full missable-trophy decision.

## Tar Golem FAQ

### Where is the Tar Golem?

At the end of the Prologue on the **Disciple's Grotto** route.

### Is the Tar Golem encounter part of progression?

Yes. The Tar Golem sequence is part of the Prologue route, while the **No, You Still Can't Win** trophy condition is optional.

### What does minimum health mean for the trophy?

Follow the requirement exactly: take the Tar Golem to its **minimum-health state**. This guide does not assign a universal HP number.

### Do I need to kill the Tar Golem?

The trophy requirement is not worded as a conventional kill. It requires taking the Tar Golem to minimum health.

### Can I retry if I miss the trophy?

Not through normal return travel on that Prologue save. The clean retry is a fresh save / character and another Prologue run.

### Does NG+ include the Tar Golem?

No. NG+ begins at Marrow Keep and does not replay this Prologue encounter.

### What trophy is tied to the Tar Golem?

**No, You Still Can't Win.**

### Should achievement hunters skip the Prologue?

Not before securing the two confirmed opening trophy opportunities, unless you are comfortable replaying the Prologue on a separate fresh save.
