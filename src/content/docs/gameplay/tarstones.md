## What are Tarstones in Mortal Shell 2?

Tarstones are equipment items that add build effects to a Shell's weapon, sidearm, or support loadout. They are separate from ordinary Gloom leveling, weapon upgrades, and the Gloombound Flame used to activate Night Mode.

The system has four categories:

| Category | What it generally does | Slot/use boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Support** | Adds a passive utility or combat modifier | Launch guides report four Support slots. |
| **Combat** | Triggers an effect from attacks, hits, or firing | Usually assigned to a main weapon or sidearm; compatibility varies. |
| **Infusion** | Activates a weapon or sidearm status effect | Usually consumes Resolve and may be restricted to specific equipment. |
| **Ability** | Activates a special Tarstone ability | Usually consumes Resolve and may be restricted to specific equipment. |

GamesRadar reports up to **ten equipped Tarstones**: four Support stones plus two each from Combat, Infusion, and Ability. GameSpot also warns that many Combat, Infusion, and Ability stones only fit particular weapons or sidearms. Read the compatibility text before planning a build around one stone.

## How to unlock Tarstone upgrades

Tarstone upgrades are called **Tempering** at the Tarforge. The normal route is:

1. Reach the **Glutted Mire** in the southeast Fainweald route.
2. Progress through the area, activate the Sunken Village Beacon, and defeat the **Tarblighted Shepherd**.
3. Continue through the gap and wooden walkways until the chest near **Ruk**, before the Magdalena encounter.
4. Take the **Etching Needles** from the chest.
5. Return to **Franz** at the Tarforge in Marrow Keep and hand over the item.
6. Select **Temper Tarstones** at the Tarforge.

This is the normal component path. The [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/) owns the full five-part station checklist, including weapon, sidearm, smelting, and Endless Core functions.

## How Tarstone XP, coins, and Tarcores work

Current launch guides agree on the important part of the loop:

1. Slot a Tarstone into an **equipped compatible weapon or sidearm**.
2. Kill enemies while that stone is equipped so it gains XP.
3. At **10,000 XP**, use the Tarforge and pay **100 coins** for the next level; the reported reward is one **Tarcore**.
4. At the next **20,000-XP** threshold, the reported higher/max step costs **six Tarcores**.

An inventory Tarstone does not earn XP merely because you own it. If you are testing a new stone, equip it before the route or fight that should level it. Because Tarcores are limited, a practical strategy is to raise several low-investment stones to the first threshold, then spend six Tarcores on the stones that actually fit your build.

These are launch-window reported values, not a promise that every future patch will preserve the same thresholds or costs.

## Missable and special-mode warnings

### Berserker's Stone: check Temple of Vatra early

[AllThings.How reports](https://allthings.how/mortal-shell-2-every-tarstone-location-for-the-stoned-trophy/) that the **Berserker's Stone** is in a Temple of Vatra offering chest and can become inaccessible after you take the Heart of Vatra and leave. Treat this as a third-party collection warning rather than a fully verified game rule: collect and confirm it before leaving the region if you are pursuing the collection trophy.

### Night-only merchant records

The same checklist reports that **Weeping Stone, Infused Stone, Fulminant Stone, and Infested Stone** are sold by The Collector during the Night cycle at Abandoned Outpost. If your final inventory is short, check this merchant before assuming a dungeon pickup was missed.

### Puzzle-gated records

Some source-reported entries do not appear until a setup is completed:

- **Clockwork Shardstone:** launch the Makeshift Projectile with the Outskirts of Mammon trebuchet, then check the Alchemist's Workshop.
- **Curseblood Stone:** use the Grimorium Obscurum in Forbidden Archives, then defeat the Gloombound Wraith and cleanse the route.
- **Frostshard Stone:** pray at the Conqueror's Hoard statue to freeze the water, then cross to the sarcophagus.
- **Inflamed Clawstone:** obtain the Feeding Room Key in Gloomshade Grove, open the large door, and defeat the Rabid Grisha Pack.
- **Auspicious, Siegebreaker's, and Berserker's Stones:** source reports describe candle-offering chests; search the surrounding platforms and side paths for all three candles.

## FAQ

### How many Tarstones are in Mortal Shell 2?

Launch sources conflict: GamesRadar reports 75, while AllThings.How reports 73 and says two inventory slots remain empty. This page uses a source-reported database until the in-game count and naming are independently reconciled.

### How do I unlock Tarstone upgrades?

Find the Etching Needles in Glutted Mire after the Tarblighted Shepherd and before Magdalena, then return them to Franz at the Marrow Keep Tarforge to unlock **Temper Tarstones**.

### Do Tarstones gain XP in my inventory?

No. Current launch guides report that a Tarstone gains XP from enemy kills only while it is slotted in an equipped compatible weapon or sidearm.

### What is Tarcore used for?

Tarcore is part of the higher Tarstone tempering economy. The reported launch loop is one Tarcore after the 10,000-XP/100-coin step, then six Tarcores for the next 20,000-XP step.

### What is the best early Tarstone?

Choose by goal: Gloombound Stone for Gloom farming, Unwieldy Stone for early sustain, Auspicious or Grudge for melee crits, Duality for compatible Axe & Dagger/Axatana light combos, and Enfeebling for a compatible sidearm Fragile setup. None is a verified universal best.

### Is Berserker's Stone missable?

AllThings.How reports that the Temple of Vatra gates close after taking the Heart of Vatra and leaving. If you are collecting every entry, check the offering chest before progressing out of that area; keep the warning labeled as third-party until directly verified.

### Is this a fully verified complete list?

No. It is a launch-window, source-reconciled database of reported names and acquisition routes. Exact totals, category assignments for every row, some spellings, and some locations still need in-game verification.
