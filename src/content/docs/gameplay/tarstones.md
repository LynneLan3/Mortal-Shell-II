## Evidence boundary

This is a launch-window **Exploration Page** for the Tarstones progression cluster. It explains the current known system, the upgrade path, and practical early choices without pretending to catalogue every Tarstone.

The current evidence supports the broad Tarstone types, Tarforge progression, Etching Needles, XP from use, and the relationship between Tarcore, Tempering, and higher upgrades. It does not yet support a complete list of every Tarstone, exact percentage values for every effect, or a universal best-Tarstone ranking.

For the related leveling loop, see the [Gloom farming guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gloom-farm/). For weapon and Shell context, use the [Weapons Guide](/mortal-shell-ii/weapons/) and [Shells Guide](/mortal-shell-ii/shells/).

**Volatile Fragment** is a named Tarstone reward from defeating Vrannic in the [Hall of Illusions](/mortal-shell-ii/hall-of-illusions/).

## What are Tarstones in Mortal Shell 2?

Tarstones are Shell equipment that add a specific progression, combat, infusion, or ability effect. They are separate from weapons, ordinary leveling currency, and the Gloombound Flame used to activate Night Mode.

The practical loop is:

1. Find or obtain a Tarstone.
2. Equip it to a Shell when you want its effect.
3. Use that Tarstone during play so it can build progression XP.
4. Return to the Tarforge path once the relevant upgrade access is available.
5. Spend the required upgrade resources, including coins where the current upgrade step asks for them.

Do not read the word “Tarstone” as a promise that every effect is a passive stat bonus. The type and the individual description matter.

## Tarstone types

The current launch research groups known Tarstones into four broad types.

### Support Tarstones

Support Tarstones improve a practical loop or utility goal. The clearest current example is the **Gloombound Stone**, which is associated with increasing Gloom drops from slain foes while equipped.

Use a Support Tarstone when your immediate goal is farming, exploration efficiency, or another repeatable progression task rather than a direct damage test.

The [Gloom farm guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gloom-farm/) covers the Gloombound Stone boundary in more detail. Its reported percentage is intentionally not treated as a universal number because launch sources do not consistently verify the same first-rank value.

### Combat Tarstones

Combat Tarstones are the category to investigate when you want an effect that changes how a fight plays. The current page does not publish a complete combat roster, damage table, or “best combat Tarstone” claim.

Treat creator language such as “best” or “overpowered” as a player opinion unless the in-game description and repeatable launch evidence support a narrower statement.

### Infusion Tarstones

Infusion Tarstones are associated with changing or adding an infusion-style interaction. The category is useful for searching and organizing the system, but the current evidence does not justify a complete element-by-element table or universal damage comparison.

If you are testing one, record the exact Tarstone description, the weapon or Shell context, and the observed result separately. Do not turn a single run into a global build rule.

### Ability Tarstones

Ability Tarstones are associated with ability-focused effects. They belong in the same progression system, but the exact ability interactions and best pairings remain outside this first exploration pass unless the current in-game description confirms them.

For now, choose by the goal you are testing: utility, combat behavior, infusion experiment, or ability interaction.

## How to upgrade Tarstones

The current upgrade path has several connected pieces rather than one simple “upgrade at the menu” action.

### Tarforge and Franz

Current launch research identifies **Franz** and the **Tarforge** as the path for Tarstone upgrades. The Tarforge is the relevant upgrade station; it is not the same thing as equipping a Tarstone to a Shell.

When the upgrade option is not visible, check whether the Tarforge progression and the required unlock item have been reached. Do not assume that finding a Tarstone automatically unlocks every upgrade tier.

### Etching Needles unlock Tarstone upgrades

**Etching Needles** are the key unlock item identified by the current research for opening Franz/Tarforge Tarstone upgrades.

This means there are two separate questions:

- **Can I equip the Tarstone?** That is an equipment question.
- **Can I upgrade the Tarstone at Tarforge?** That depends on the relevant Tarforge progression and Etching Needle unlock.

If the Tarforge only shows a limited option, continue the progression that unlocks Etching Needles instead of spending time looking for a missing generic upgrade menu.

### Tarstone XP comes from actual use

Tarstones do not simply gain progress because they are stored in the inventory. Current research indicates that a Tarstone builds XP through **actual use**.

Equip the Tarstone you want to develop before the activity that should feed its progression. If you swap to a different Tarstone, do not assume the inactive item receives the same XP. The exact XP thresholds are not catalogued here.

This is also why a low-cost early test is useful: equip one Tarstone, use it through a repeatable combat or farming loop, and check the progression rather than relying on a guide's assumed percentage.

### Coins, Tarcore, and Tempering

Coins are part of the later Tarstone upgrade economy in the current research boundary. The exact cost of every rank is not published here, so keep enough coins available when an upgrade path becomes visible rather than assuming the upgrade is free.

**Tarcore** and **Tempering** belong to the higher-level progression vocabulary. They are relevant to advanced Tarstone upgrades, but this page does not claim a complete map of which item, rank, or recipe is required for every Tarstone.

Use the in-game upgrade prompt as the immediate authority for the current item and coin requirement. Treat third-party cost tables as provisional until they agree with the launch build.

For the five components that unlock and extend the station, see the [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/).

## Best early Tarstone picks

There is no verified universal best Tarstone. Choose by the activity you are about to repeat.

1. **Gloombound Stone for early Gloom farming.** Equip it before a farming loop if your goal is more Gloom from slain foes. The [Gloom farming guide](/mortal-shell-ii/gloom-farm/) explains the current evidence boundary.
2. **A Support Tarstone for progression utility.** Use this category when you are trying to make a repeatable route safer or more efficient.
3. **A Combat Tarstone for a controlled fight test.** Change one variable at a time and avoid calling the result a tier ranking.
4. **An Infusion or Ability Tarstone for a playstyle experiment.** Pair the effect with a known weapon or Shell route, then record what is observed rather than assuming every combination works identically.

If a weapon route is the immediate problem, first choose the weapon from the [Weapons Guide](/mortal-shell-ii/weapons/) and Shell from the [Shells Guide](/mortal-shell-ii/shells/). Tarstones should support that plan, not replace it with an unverified build claim.

## FAQ

### How do Tarstones work?

Equip a Tarstone to a Shell and use it during play. Tarstones are grouped into Support, Combat, Infusion, and Ability types, with each individual description determining the actual effect.

### How do you upgrade Tarstones?

Use the Tarforge progression associated with Franz. Current research identifies Etching Needles as the unlock item for Tarstone upgrades; later steps can require coins.

### Do Tarstones gain XP when stored?

Current research supports XP from actual use. Equip the Tarstone you want to develop instead of assuming inventory ownership is enough.

### What is Tarcore?

Tarcore is associated with higher-level Tarstone progression and advanced upgrades. This page does not claim a complete Tarcore recipe or rank table.

### What is Tempering?

Tempering is part of the higher Tarstone upgrade vocabulary. Use the current Tarforge prompt for the exact requirement shown in your launch build.

### What is the best early Tarstone?

There is no verified universal best pick. Gloombound Stone is the clearest early Support choice when your goal is Gloom farming; other picks should follow your combat or playstyle experiment.

### Is this a complete Tarstone list?

No. This is an exploration guide covering known mechanics and early choices, not a complete 75-Tarstone database.
