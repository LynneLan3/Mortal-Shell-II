**Tiel the Acolyte** is found southeast of **Widow's Overlook Beacon**, in the wider Martyr's Tomb / Ritual Grounds direction. Look for a large tree and wooden enclosure, then find the shallow grave marked by **two torches**. Interacting with Tiel starts the **Rash Memory**; complete it by entering the nearby building through a side window and collecting the flask from the desk.

## Where is Tiel in Mortal Shell 2?

Start from **Widow's Overlook Beacon** and travel generally southeast.

Use the wider **Martyr's Tomb / Ritual Grounds** direction to orient yourself. The **Great Arbiter** can also serve as a route landmark, but this guide does not treat defeating it as a requirement for reaching Tiel.

Near the target area, look for:

- a large tree
- a wooden enclosure or rampart
- two torches marking the grave entrance

Tiel is in the shallow grave beside those landmarks.

## Fast route from Widow's Overlook

A simple route is:

1. Start at **Widow's Overlook Beacon**.
2. Head southeast toward the broader Martyr's Tomb / Ritual Grounds area.
3. Use the Great Arbiter area only as a visual landmark if it helps you orient yourself.
4. Look for the large tree and wooden enclosure.
5. Find the **two torches** marking the entrance to Tiel's shallow grave.
6. Interact with Tiel to begin the Rash Memory.

If you are already exploring this part of the map for Tarforge progression, the [Tarforge guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarforge/) also covers the Obsidian Lathe in the wider Martyr's Tomb route.

## How to find Tiel's grave

The grave is easier to identify from its immediate landmarks than from a precise map coordinate.

Once you are in the correct southeast area, search around the **large tree and wooden enclosure / rampart**. The grave entrance is marked by **two torches**.

Approach the shallow grave and interact with Tiel. That interaction begins **The Rash / Rash Memory**.

You do not need this guide to invent a boss gate or exact numerical requirement for the unlock route. The important steps are finding the grave and completing the memory that follows.

## How to complete Tiel's Rash Memory

After interacting with Tiel, the Rash Memory sends you to a nearby building.

Follow these steps:

1. Locate the building near the grave.
2. Do not rely on the direct front entry as your route.
3. Move around to the **side window**.
4. Enter the building through that window.
5. Find the **desk** inside.
6. Collect the **flask**.

Completing the memory unlocks **Tiel the Acolyte** as a usable Shell.

## What kind of Shell is Tiel?

Tiel is best described as a **mobile, evasive, Shadow-focused Shell**.

Compared with more tank-oriented Shell choices, Tiel is the more fragile option. That makes him a natural fit for players who prefer movement, positioning, and an evasive playstyle rather than simply absorbing pressure.

This location page does not turn that identity into a full build, skill table, or tier ranking. Use the [Shells guide](/mortal-shell-ii/shells/) for the broader Shell index.

## Should you get Tiel or Proxima first?

Both Tiel and [Proxima](/mortal-shell-ii/proxima/) have documented early routes on this site, so the better first target depends mainly on where you are already exploring.

Choose **Tiel** first if you are moving southeast from Widow's Overlook toward the Martyr's Tomb side of Fainweald and want a mobile, evasive Shell.

Choose **Proxima** first if you are already following the Blackridge Pass → Shattered Beacon route.

Neither route establishes that one Shell is objectively stronger than the other. You can use the two location guides to choose the shorter detour for your current run.

## Tiel FAQ

### Where is Tiel?

Tiel is southeast of **Widow's Overlook Beacon**, near a large tree and wooden enclosure in the wider Martyr's Tomb / Ritual Grounds direction. Two torches mark his shallow grave.

### Which Beacon should I start from?

Start from **Widow's Overlook Beacon**.

### Is Tiel inside Martyr's Tomb?

The supported route places Tiel in the wider Martyr's Tomb / Ritual Grounds direction. This guide uses the grave landmarks rather than treating Martyr's Tomb itself as the final destination.

### How do you start the Rash Memory?

Interact with Tiel in the shallow grave.

### How do you finish the Rash Memory?

Find the nearby building, enter through the **side window**, locate the desk, and collect the **flask**.

### Do you need to defeat Great Arbiter?

The supported evidence uses the Great Arbiter as a route landmark; it does not establish defeating it as a requirement for Tiel's unlock.

### Do Shell-map reveals still cost Glimpses?

No. After [Balance Patch 1](/mortal-shell-ii/balance-patch-1/), Shell-map reveals cost **Gloom instead of Glimpses**. The [Glimpses guide](/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/) covers the Patch 1 refund and the current Glimpse role.
