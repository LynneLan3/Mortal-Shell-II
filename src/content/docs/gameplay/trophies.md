Mortal Shell II's current platform lists expose **53 completion entries**. PlayStation presents 53 trophies with 1 Platinum, 1 Gold, 10 Silver, and 41 Bronze; Xbox and Steam present 53 achievements without a Platinum tier. Some third-party guides count Xbox/Steam as 52 by excluding the PlayStation Platinum, so use the live list on your platform if a future patch changes the presentation.

For a clean completion save, protect three current-cycle opportunities before you leave the opening content or kill their related boss: **Mid Summer?**, **No, You Still Can't Win**, and **Peter's Perfect Parry**. Keep the Slayer Seal off your trophy save, collect optional sets while routes are open, and leave NG+ mainly for Shell Memory cleanup.

## Before you leave the opening sequence

Do these checks on the save you intend to complete:

1. Accept Marigold's flower crown during the Festival/tutorial for **Mid Summer?**.
2. Take the tutorial Tar Golem to its minimum/zero health for **No, You Still Can't Win**.
3. Claim the first Shell, weapon, and sidearm, reach Marrowkeep, and view a first Shell Memory.
4. Do not equip the Slayer Seal on this save. The current repository evidence records the achievement lock as a save-level consequence after switching to it; use a separate save if you want that easier combat modifier.

The opening interactions are the safest place to protect the two tutorial opportunities. Do not assume that NG+ will replay the same tutorial state.

## Three missables to protect

| Trophy / achievement | What to do | Safe timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mid Summer?** | Accept Marigold's flower crown during the Festival | Opening tutorial, before moving on |
| **No, You Still Can't Win** | Reduce the tutorial Tar Golem to its minimum/zero health | Opening Tar Golem encounter |
| **Peter's Perfect Parry** | Perfect Guard the Nameless Captive's seven-hit headspin with the Untarnished Seal | Attempt it before killing the Nameless Captive; see the [dedicated guide](/mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/) |

The current checklist also warns about the Temple of Vatra Tarstone and Sester Genessa's bonding interaction. Those are practical progression risks rather than independently confirmed trophy names: collect the Tarstone before leaving that area and advance Genessa's bond during the first run when possible.

## Practical platinum / 100% roadmap

### First run: collect while the world is open

- Stay on the normal achievement-enabled save and avoid equipping the Slayer Seal.
- Pick up every Shell, weapon, sidearm, Ova, Tarstone, Map Fragment, and Beacon that your route exposes.
- Play every Lute Track when you find it instead of leaving `Heavy Metal` to an untracked end sweep.
- Clear the named bosses as their routes open. The checked sources do not establish one universal boss order, so do not wait for a rigid sequence.
- Attempt [Peter's Perfect Parry](/mortal-shell-ii/peters-perfect-parry/) while the Nameless Captive is alive. A failed attempt is safer to retry before the kill than after it.
- Before entering the endgame transition, sweep optional equipment, Shells, Beacons, Tarstones, Ova, map fragments, Lute Tracks, Egon's interaction, the Mango secret, and the Bag Holder/Baghead interaction.

### NG+ cleanup: spend Glimpses deliberately

The current guide consensus is that `Seeking the Past` is the main reason completion extends beyond one run. The eight Shells have five memories each—**40 memories total**—and current guides estimate 27 Glimpses per maximum Shell bond, or 216 across the roster. They estimate two full runs and part of a third for the required currency. These numbers are guide estimates, not a developer-published fixed run count.

Use the first post-story free-roam window for cleanup. In NG+, repeat the boss and Beacon activities that provide the Glimpses you still need, max Shell bonds methodically, and open each memory manually. Use NG+ to retry a missed current-cycle challenge if the target build retains the same reset behavior.
