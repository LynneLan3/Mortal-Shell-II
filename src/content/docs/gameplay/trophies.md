## Quick answer

Mortal Shell II's current official Steam achievement page lists **53 achievements**. The reviewed Xbox list also shows 53 achievements, while the PS5 list has **53 trophies: 1 Platinum, 1 Gold, 10 Silver, and 41 Bronze**. Some guides report “52” for Steam/Xbox because they count the non-Platinum objectives; use the platform's own counter when a guide and the in-game list disagree.

For a safe Platinum or 100% run:

1. Accept Marigold's flower crown in the opening Festival and complete the tutorial Tar Golem achievement check before leaving the prologue.
2. Keep the **Slayer Seal** unequipped on the completion save because current launch reports say it disables achievement tracking.
3. Collect the Temple of Vatra Tarstone before taking the Heart of Vatra and leaving that area.
4. Complete **Peter's Perfect Parry** with the Untarnished Seal during the Nameless Captive encounter.
5. Finish collections, NPC interactions, equipment, bosses, upgrades, and Shell Memories before selecting **Send Ova** after Zmey.
6. Plan NG+ and possibly part of NG++ for the limited Glimpse economy behind all Shell Memories.

## Platform names are not perfectly identical

This page uses the current Steam/Xbox names as the baseline. PS5 sources show some alternate labels for the same rewards:

- Steam/Xbox **Baghead** = PS5 **Bag Holder**.
- Steam/Xbox **Point Taken** may appear as **Just the Tip** in PS-oriented guides.
- Steam/Xbox **Ultimate Gainz** may appear as **Giga Chad** in PS-oriented guides.
- Steam/Xbox **Wheelie Good Time** may appear as **Ultimate Karen** in older/alternate guide tables.

If the platform notification differs from this page, follow the platform UI and use the requirement, not the joke title, as the identity check.

## Missables and launch-window traps

### Do these before leaving the tutorial

- **Mid Summer?** — accept Marigold's flower crown during the opening Festival sequence.
- **No, You Still Can't Win** — take the tutorial Tar Golem to its achievement trigger. The official wording says “minimum health”; current guides describe the exact trigger as 1 HP or zero-health UI behavior, so verify the notification rather than trusting a hard number.

New Game Plus skips the tutorial, so do not assume either entry can be cleaned up later.

### Protect the first-playthrough collection route

The current launch evidence repeatedly warns that one Tarstone in **Temple of Vatra** can be locked behind closing gates after taking the Heart of Vatra and leaving. Collect the Berserker's Stone before that point. The total Tarstone count is disputed across current guides, so use the platform inventory/checklist rather than a hard total.

For **Seeking the Past**, current guides report that Sester Genessa's bond interaction can become stuck on its NG+ tutorial state. Max Genessa's bond and watch her memories during the first playthrough as a conservative workaround; this warning is version-sensitive and may change with a patch.

### Avoid the Slayer Seal on the trophy save

Current launch reviews and player reports say equipping the Slayer Seal disables achievements. This is not stated in the official trophy list, but it is a high-cost risk: keep a separate experimentation save if you want to test it.

## Recommended Platinum roadmap

1. Finish the opening Festival check and Tar Golem check.
2. Reach Marrowkeep, claim the first Shell/weapon/Sidearm, and deliberately watch a Shell Memory through the Shellkeeper menu.
3. Explore each region as it opens. Collect Shells, weapons, Sidearms, Map Fragments, Beacons, Ova, and Tarstones instead of leaving all cleanup to the end.
4. Complete the Shell/NPC interactions for Tiel, Lazlo, Eredrim, Sariel, Proxima, Smert, Gragu, Sester Genessa, and Egon. Complete Baghead's empty-handed joke ending if possible before the final commitment.
5. Play the lute tracks, max one Shell bond, and max one weapon at the Tarforge.
6. Defeat the six Corrupted Gate bosses, the three Unfound Path guardians, and Zmey. Boss trophies do not have a source-backed fixed order.
7. During the Nameless Captive fight, equip the Untarnished Seal and Perfect Guard all seven required headspins for Peter's Perfect Parry. Treat this as a deliberate challenge rather than a passive boss trophy.
8. After Zmey, remain in free-roam and finish every collection, NPC, upgrade, and memory task. Select **Send Ova** only when the cleanup checklist is complete.
9. Use NG+ for remaining Shell bonds and memories. Current guides report eight permanent Shells and 40 memories; limited Glimpses may require part of NG++.

## What remains uncertain

- Current official Steam and Xbox pages show 53 entries, but multiple guides say 52 for Steam/Xbox. Treat this as a counting-convention/list-copy conflict, not as a reason to omit an entry.
- Tarstone totals are reported as 73 or 75. The official requirement is simply all Tarstones.
- The exact Tar Golem trigger is described as minimum health, 1 HP, or zero-health UI behavior across sources.
- The Sester Genessa NG+ problem may be patched or inconsistent across saves; max her bond in the first run and label the workaround launch-window specific.
- No universal mandatory playthrough count is proven. Plan for NG+, and possibly a partial NG++, because Shell Memories consume limited Glimpses.
