The complete **Unfound Path** route is: open the late-game entrance through the Marrow Keep **Gloom Siphon**, clear the three guardian branches, finish each branch's tendril interaction, cleanse **Hidden Nave**, and take the central route to **Zmey the Unbidden**. The required branches are **Bridge of Procession → Orrem**, **Obsidian Vault → The Monolith**, and **Faithful's Shelter → Malborn Offspring**.

The sources do not establish one mandatory order. For a practical first run, use **Orrem → The Monolith → Malborn Offspring**: the reviewed final-dungeon guide treats Malborn as the toughest of the three, so leaving it until your build is ready is sensible. This is a recommendation, not a quest lock.

## Before entering the Unfound Path

Reach the late-game Ova progression in Marrow Keep and return to the **Gloom Siphon** when you have collected enough Ova. Current guides agree on the Ova-driven gate but disagree on the exact total, with reports ranging from 150 to 167–168 depending on the source and progression framing. Use the prompt shown by your current Siphon instead of farming to an unverified fixed number; the [Ova guide](/mortal-shell-ii/ova/) tracks the conflict.

The preceding campaign spine is **Prologue → Fainweald → Mammon → Unfound Path**. For the wider route context, use the [World & Progression route hub](/mortal-shell-ii/routes/world-progression/). For the Beacon interaction and travel layer, see [Beacons & Fast Travel](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/).

## Complete Unfound Path walkthrough

### 1. Enter the final dungeon and establish a checkpoint

Use the opening beneath **Marrow Keep** once the Gloom Siphon has made the route available. The Unfound Path is organized around a central platform that is initially blocked by three tendrils. Explore the outer branches and activate or cleanse each named Beacon when the game presents it; do not treat the whole area as one uninterrupted boss run.

The exact interior turns vary across current route descriptions. The reliable navigation anchors are the branch names and their Beacons, not a compass direction, room count, or coordinate. The linked boss guides contain the branch-specific route details.

### 2. Clear Bridge of Procession: Orrem

Take the **Bridge of Procession** branch and use its Beacon before the arena. Continue to **Orrem, the Discarded Golem**, then survive the transition into **Orrem, the Reclaimed**.

The fight is two forms: the first uses flaming axes, melee strings, Cascading Fire, and a jumping slam; the Reclaimed form moves faster and adds tail/spine pressure and ranged flame or blood-bullet attacks. Save healing for the second form and take short punishes after complete attack sequences.

Use the [Orrem guide](/mortal-shell-ii/orrem-discarded-golem/) for the Beacon approach, summon choice, and full fight notes. After the encounter, confirm that the Bridge of Procession branch has advanced before leaving.

### 3. Clear Obsidian Vault: The Monolith

Take the **Obsidian Vault** branch, activate its Beacon, and continue to **The Monolith**. This is the slow, high-health fight: leave the landing area when it jumps, return to the exposed center after recovery, and take a short punish.

Around the later part of the health bar, beams cross the arena and can rotate through your position. Keep the beam in view and dodge when it reaches you rather than rolling on the first animation. The [Monolith guide](/mortal-shell-ii/monolith/) covers the branch route and beam timing.

After the kill, do not leave immediately. Walk to the arena's **red gland** and use the **sever** interaction. The boss death alone is not the complete Obsidian Vault branch handoff.

### 4. Clear Faithful's Shelter: Malborn Offspring

Take the **Faithful's Shelter** branch and use its Beacon. From there, go up the nearby stairs, cross the narrow bridge, and climb the long staircase to the circular arena.

Malborn Offspring uses delayed five- or six-hit cleaving strings. Watch the actual swing instead of counting a fixed rhythm, dodge every red-circle unblockable, and build the Stagger gauge with short, safe hits. When the first health bar empties, keep attacking during the slow resurrection; phase two adds lightning-infused attacks and electric projectiles.

Use the [Malborn Offspring guide](/mortal-shell-ii/malborn-offspring/) for the full route and encounter strategy. After the kill, go to the back of the arena and interact with the fleshy ribcage/tendril structure to extract the gland. Wait for the branch progression to complete before returning to the central route.

## 5. Open the central route

When all three branch tendrils are gone, the central platform becomes reachable. If it remains blocked, revisit each cleared arena and check for the required post-boss interaction—especially the red-gland or gland extraction step—before assuming the route is bugged.

The branch order is flexible in the current evidence. You may clear Faithful's Shelter first or switch branches after activating a Beacon; **Orrem → Monolith → Malborn** is only the conservative difficulty-oriented recommendation used on this page.

## 6. Cleanse Hidden Nave before Zmey

Before committing to the final boss, reach and cleanse the **Hidden Nave Beacon**. Current boss coverage identifies the **Endless Core** inside Hidden Nave; it removes the normal Tarforge upgrade limit. Treat this as the final preparation checkpoint, not as a replacement for clearing the three guardians.

Then follow the central route and the final approach to **Zmey the Unbidden**. The [Zmey guide](/mortal-shell-ii/zmey/) covers the Cosmic Flame tells, Blade Slam, Cascading Flare, and the final arena. A familiar upgraded Shell and weapon are safer than changing to an unfamiliar build for a single fight.

## 7. Finish the current cycle before Send Ova

Defeating Zmey does not automatically begin New Game Plus. Return to the current save and finish any Beacons, Shells, weapons, Tarstones, keys, or NPC scenes you still want. Only then return to Marrow Keep and choose **Send Ova** at the Gloom Siphon.

Treat **Send Ova** as the deliberate New Game Plus boundary. See [New Game Plus](/mortal-shell-ii/new-game-plus/) and [Endings](/mortal-shell-ii/endings/) before selecting it.

## FAQ

### What is the Unfound Path order in Mortal Shell 2?

There is no confirmed mandatory order for the three branches. A practical recommendation is **Orrem → The Monolith → Malborn Offspring**, saving the reported toughest guardian for last. All three branches must be cleared.

### How do you unlock the Unfound Path?

Progress the Ova/Gloom Siphon route in Marrow Keep until the current in-game prompt opens the late-game entrance. Guides disagree on the exact Ova total, so do not treat 150 or 167–168 as a universal fixed number.

### Which bosses are in the Unfound Path?

**Orrem** guards Bridge of Procession, **The Monolith** guards Obsidian Vault, and **Malborn Offspring** guards Faithful's Shelter. **Zmey the Unbidden** waits beyond the opened central route.

### Do you need to cleanse Hidden Nave?

You should cleanse it before Zmey if you want the **Endless Core**, which current coverage identifies as the item that removes the Tarforge upgrade limit. See the [Zmey guide](/mortal-shell-ii/zmey/) for the final preparation boundary.

### Does defeating Zmey start New Game Plus?

No. Defeat Zmey, complete current-cycle cleanup, and choose **Send Ova** at the Marrow Keep Gloom Siphon only when you are ready to begin New Game Plus.
