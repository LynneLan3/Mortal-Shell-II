The **Week 1 Update** is Mortal Shell II's first major post-launch patch. It changes how you plan resources, whether early Shell Bonding is permanent, and how much crash troubleshooting you should do before blaming your PC setup.

The biggest player-facing changes are the **resource economy** (Glimpses and Tarcores are no longer finite), **Shell respec** through Mether's Severance, **Fragile Tarstones** and expanded vendor stock, **exploration Beacons**, **Riposte and combat refinements**, and **extensive crash and stability fixes**. PC players can install this now; console rollout is not confirmed in this record. This page explains what changed, who it affects, and which guides to use now.

For the full update index, see [Mortal Shell II Updates & Patch Notes](/mortal-shell-ii/updates/).

## Quick answer

**When:** August 29, 2026 — [official Steam announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2584270/view/690892955941077484).

**PC status:** Live per the official post. PS5 and Xbox Series rollout is not confirmed in this site's evidence record.

**What to do first:**

1. If you limited Tarstone or Glimpse spending because resources felt finite, revisit your [Tarstones](/mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/) and [Glimpses](/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/) plans.
2. If you committed to a Shell Bond path you regret, look for **Mether's Severance** and the Zhirelle respec flow in the [Shells guide](/mortal-shell-ii/shells/).
3. If the game still crashes, confirm Steam installed this update before using the [PC crash guide](/mortal-shell-ii/crashing-pc/).

## Biggest gameplay changes

### A. Tarstones, Tarcores, and Glimpses

**Confirmed in the official Week 1 post:**

- **Glimpses and Tarcores are no longer finite.** You can upgrade all Shells and Tarstones in one playthrough.
- **Fragile Tarstones can appear repeatedly** — they are not one-time unique pickups.
- **Breaking Tarstones yields more Tarcores.**
- **Existing upgraded saves can receive compensatory Tarcores** for prior investment.
- **Merrick's inventory now includes progression-related upgrade resources**, including Tarstones, Mether's Severance, Glimpse Stones, Egon's Stones, and Tarcores at relevant progression states. The Collector can also carry Tarcores.

**Player impact:** Launch-era advice warning that Glimpses or Tarcores are permanently capped in a single run is **obsolete**. You no longer need to treat every Temper choice as irreversible because of a hard resource ceiling.

Use the [Tarstones guide](/mortal-shell-ii/tarstones/) for locations, Tempering, and the Week 1 economy block. For Glimpse versus Gloom spending, see [Glimpses & Resources](/mortal-shell-ii/glimpses/).

Do not invent exact Merrick restock timings or guaranteed first-visit availability — the official note confirms expanded stock with progression, not a fixed schedule.

### B. Shell respec

**Confirmed:**

- New item: **Mether's Severance**
- Give it to **Zhirelle**
- Resets **Shell progression**
- **Refunds spent Glimpses** tied to that Shell's Bond

**Player impact:** Early Shell Bond commitment is no longer permanent. If you invested Glimpses into a Shell you do not want to keep, respec is now a supported option rather than a New Game Plus workaround.

See the [Shells guide](/mortal-shell-ii/shells/) for Bonding context and roster planning. This update does not by itself change individual Shell Memory routes or unlock requirements.

### C. Riposte and combat

The official Week 1 notes include a **Riposte scaling pass**, combat immunity refinements, and enemy targeting or magnetism adjustments where listed in the developer post.

**Player impact:** Riposte-heavy builds and parry timing may feel different after the patch. Treat specific numerical changes as patch facts only where the official note names them; do not assume every enemy or weapon received the same adjustment.

For Slayer Seal and Riposte tooling, see [Slayer Seal Difficulty](/mortal-shell-ii/slayer-seal-difficulty/). Weapon-specific tracking changes from [Balance Patch 1](/mortal-shell-ii/updates/balance-patch-1/) remain separate dated changes.

### D. Exploration

The Week 1 Update adds **new Beacons** and adjusts navigation pacing in supported areas.

**Player impact:** Old launch walkthrough directions may differ slightly from current fast-travel and Beacon coverage. Do **not** claim every map route changed — use the [Beacons & Fast Travel guide](/mortal-shell-ii/beacons-fast-travel/) for the current Beacon checklist and Mether's Breath progression.

### E. Performance and crashes

The Week 1 Update includes **extensive crash and stability fixes**, improved diagnostics, and changes to some graphics-related settings implicated in driver crashes.

**Player impact:** Update before deep troubleshooting. Crashes can still occur after this patch — the update improves stability; it does not guarantee every crash scenario is resolved.

See the [PC crash guide](/mortal-shell-ii/crashing-pc/) for current symptom routing and the Week 1 status block at the top of that page.

## Week 1 Update FAQ

### Is the Week 1 Update live on PC?

Yes. The [official Steam post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2584270/view/690892955941077484) announces the Week 1 Update for the PC build checked on August 29, 2026.

### Are Glimpses still scarce?

They are no longer **finite per run**. You can still prioritize which Shell to Bond first, but the old hard cap that blocked full Shell and Tarstone upgrading in one playthrough is removed.

### How do I respec a Shell?

Obtain **Mether's Severance**, give it to **Zhirelle**, and follow the respec prompt. Spent Glimpses on that Shell's Bond are refunded per the official note.

### Did this fix all crashes?

No. The patch includes substantial crash and stability work, but continuing crashes are still possible. Confirm your build is updated, then use the [PC crash guide](/mortal-shell-ii/crashing-pc/).

### How does Week 1 relate to Balance Patch 1?

[Balance Patch 1](/mortal-shell-ii/updates/balance-patch-1/) (August 20, 2026) changed Shell-map costs, weapon balance, Tarforge smelting, and several bosses. Week 1 builds on top of that economy with renewable resources, respec, Fragile Tarstones, exploration Beacons, and additional fixes.
