## About Mortal Shell II

**Mortal Shell II** is the standalone action-RPG sequel from Cold Symmetry and Playstack. The official descriptions reviewed for this site emphasize high-stakes combat, possessable warrior Shells, deeper weapon upgrades, an interconnected open world, and single-player play.

This hub separates confirmed release, Open Beta, gameplay, and PC requirement information into focused Guides. It does not turn preview language into an invented roster, build system, quest route, or map database.

## Evidence boundary

The reviewed official sources do not provide stable, complete evidence for a full Shell roster, full-game boss order, build tier list, map, quest walkthrough, multiplayer mode, or definitive price comparison. Those topics remain omitted. The current Open Beta Magdalena Guide is limited to the preview build and will be updated on the same URL after launch.
